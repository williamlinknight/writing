# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：句型模仿——写“呼吁”只会 We should？跟FT学4个呼吁与分歧句型

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №89 — 希腊呼吁欧盟尽快推出疫苗护照（2021年2月，667词）

## 为什么学“呼吁与分歧”

倡议书、建议信、议论文的“呼吁段”，是学生在考场上最容易写空的一段。多数人只有三句：

*We should protect the environment. Everyone must take action. It is very important.*

这三句都停在“表态”层面，没有回答三个真正决定说服力的问题：**谁在呼吁、向谁呼吁**？**为什么是现在、不做会怎样**？**如果有人反对，他的理由是什么、你怎么回应**？《金融时报》的呼吁类报道，骨架恰好就是这三问：先写谁向谁呼吁、呼吁什么；再写方案要上哪个会讨论；接着写“谁支持、谁还在犹豫”；最后写“反对者担心什么、呼吁者如何澄清”。

今天从一篇希腊呼吁欧盟尽快推出疫苗护照的报道里，抽 4 个句型。它们解决一个具体问题：**怎么把一句口号，写成一个有人、有时机、有阻力、有回应的呼吁段**。

## FT 原文

> Greece's tourism minister has called on EU leaders to “move more quickly” to embrace the vaccine certificates that could allow mass travel to resume, as Athens seeks to repair a holiday industry that has been battered by the coronavirus pandemic.

> The introduction of vaccination passports that could allow leisure and business travellers to move between countries after being inoculated will be discussed at the EU summit that begins on Thursday.

> Member states led by Greece floated the idea last month but the bloc is divided and diplomats see early adoption of the proposed system as unlikely, because of fears they will set up a discriminatory two-tier system of citizens' travel rights.

> It has found support from member states with large tourism sectors or concerns about travel from neighbouring countries with higher infection rates, but countries including Germany and France have remain unconvinced.

> One fear is that vaccine passports would stoke public anger or even legal action by those who are prevented from travelling simply because they have not yet reached their turn in the inoculation queue.

> He also insisted that vaccinations would not be mandatory for travel and only meant that those who had been inoculated would not have to take a Covid-19 test to enter the country.

**中文大意：** 希腊旅游部长呼吁欧盟领导人“更快采取行动”，接受可以让大规模旅行恢复的疫苗接种证明——雅典方面正试图修复被疫情重创的旅游业。可以让已接种的休闲和商务旅客在各国之间通行的疫苗护照，将在周四开始的欧盟峰会上讨论。以希腊为首的一些成员国上月提出了这个想法，但欧盟内部存在分歧，外交官们认为这套拟议系统被尽早采纳的可能性不大，因为担心它会建立起歧视性的“两级”旅行权利体系。它得到了一些旅游业规模较大、或担心邻国感染率更高带来输入风险的成员国的支持，但包括德国和法国在内的国家仍未被打动。一种担忧是，疫苗护照会让那些仅仅因为还没轮到接种而被禁止旅行的人愤怒，甚至引发法律行动。塞奥哈里斯也坚称，接种疫苗不会成为旅行的强制要求，只是意味着已接种者入境时不必做新冠检测。

## 句型一：呼吁 + 目的 + as 背景 —— has called on sb to do ..., as [主体] seeks to ...

**结构公式**

`[人物], [身份], has called on [对象] to do [动作] to embrace [方案] that could allow [对象] to [结果], as [主体] seeks to repair [受影响的事] that has been battered by [原因]`

**FT 原文**

> Greece's tourism minister has called on EU leaders to “move more quickly” to embrace the vaccine certificates that could allow mass travel to resume, as Athens seeks to repair a holiday industry that has been battered by the coronavirus pandemic.

**为什么好**

`call on sb to do` 是**公开呼吁**，有明确的施压对象（EU leaders），比 *should* 多出一个“向谁呼吁”的行为主体；`that could allow mass travel to resume` 用定语从句交代方案的**能力**（它能让什么发生），而不是另起一句 It can make travel start again；句尾 `as Athens seeks to repair a holiday industry that has been battered by ...` 把动机压进同一句：as 引出的既不是原因也不是时间，而是**“他为什么现在要呼吁”**。学生写同意思要四句：The Greek government called on the EU. They want the EU to accept vaccine certificates. This can help travel. Greek tourism is badly hit.

**❌ 学生初版**

The school should ask students to protect the old street. The old street is important. It is badly damaged.

**✅ 升级版**

The history club has called on the school to “act now” to protect the old street that could give students a real sense of the past, as residents seek to save a lane that has been battered by years of heavy traffic.

**💡 效果**

一句里读到“谁在呼吁—向谁呼吁—呼吁什么—为什么此刻呼吁”四层信息，句尾的 as 从句顺手补上动机，语气从“抱怨”变成“行动”。

**模板 + 例句**

`[主体], [身份], has called on [对象] to do [动作], as [主体] seeks to [目的]`

- 环保话题（仿写示例）：The student union has called on the city council to move more quickly to open riverside paths that could allow residents to cycle to school, as campaigners seek to cut a street that has been choked by traffic for a decade.
- 教育话题（仿写示例）：Parent groups have called on the district to “act faster” to fund after-school reading rooms that could keep children off screens, as teachers seek to repair a habit that has been weakened by two years of online classes.

## 句型二：提案上桌 + 分歧压缩 —— will be discussed at ... / floated the idea ... but ...

**结构公式**

`The introduction of [方案] that could allow sb to do [动作] will be discussed at [会议] that begins on [时间]` ＋ `[一方] floated the idea [时间] but [组织] is divided and [观察者] see [结果] as unlikely, because of fears [担心]`

**FT 原文**

> The introduction of vaccination passports that could allow leisure and business travellers to move between countries after being inoculated will be discussed at the EU summit that begins on Thursday.

> Member states led by Greece floated the idea last month but the bloc is divided and diplomats see early adoption of the proposed system as unlikely, because of fears they will set up a discriminatory two-tier system of citizens' travel rights.

**为什么好**

第一句用**名词化主语** The introduction of ... 开头——提案不是“人提的”，而是“被提出来的”，一句话就把重心放在议程上；`will be discussed at the summit that begins on Thursday` 把时间和场合一并交代。第二句是全篇最值得模仿的**分歧压缩句**：`floated the idea`（试探性提出）→ `but` → `is divided`（内部分裂）→ `see X as unlikely`（专业判断）→ `because of fears ...`（顾虑本身）。一句完成“有人提、有人反对、反对的理由”。学生写对立只有 *Some countries agree. Some countries do not agree.*，读者看不到分歧的结构。

**❌ 学生初版**

Our class has an idea about a phone-free lunch. Many students agree and many students do not agree. It is difficult.

**✅ 升级版**

The introduction of a phone-free lunch hour that could allow students to talk face to face will be discussed at the class meeting that begins on Monday. The student council floated the idea last month but the class is divided and form teachers see early adoption of the proposed rule as unlikely, because of fears it will set up two sets of rules for boarders and day students.

**💡 效果**

“提案—场合—分歧—顾虑”四步写在一段里，读者立刻知道争议在哪一层，而不是只看到“有人赞成有人反对”。

**模板 + 例句**

`The introduction of [方案] that could allow [对象] to [结果] will be discussed at [场合] that begins on [时间]. [一方] floated the idea [时间] but [组织] is divided and [观察者] see early adoption of the proposed [方案] as unlikely, because of fears [顾虑].`

- 校园话题（仿写示例）：The introduction of morning reading that could allow students to start the day calmly will be discussed at the staff meeting that begins on Friday.
- 环保话题（仿写示例）：Town councillors floated the idea of a car-free centre last spring but the town is divided and planners see early adoption of the proposed ban as unlikely, because of fears it will push traffic into residential streets.

## 句型三：支持与保留 —— has found support from ..., but countries including ... remain unconvinced

**结构公式**

`It has found support from [赞成方 + 定语短语], but countries including [举例 1] and [举例 2] have remain unconvinced.`

**FT 原文**

> It has found support from member states with large tourism sectors or concerns about travel from neighbouring countries with higher infection rates, but countries including Germany and France have remain unconvinced.

（原文此处排印作 have remain，规范写法是 **have remained unconvinced**——直译“仍未被打动”。引用时请改回 remain 的过去分词形式。）

**为什么好**

`found support from` 是“获得……支持”，主语仍可以是方案本身（It），不必硬造 The scheme has supporters；后面的 `with large tourism sectors or concerns about ...` 用介词短语给支持者**画像**，而不是列举国名——读者明白他们是“因为利益相关才支持”。转折之后用 `including` 举例而不列全名单，既省字数，也暗示“不止这两个”；`remain unconvinced` 比 *do not agree* 更有余地：不是反对，是**还没被说服**——这正是满分作文需要的分寸感。

**❌ 学生初版**

Many people like this plan. But Germany and France do not like it.

**✅ 升级版**

It has found support from schools with crowded canteens or concerns about food waste, but leading families including those on the parent committee have remained unconvinced.

**💡 效果**

支持方和保留方各自带上“为什么”，对立就从情绪变成利益与顾虑；`remain unconvinced` 留住了谈判空间，而不是把对方推到对立面。

**模板 + 例句**

`It has found support from [对象] with [条件 A] or concerns about [条件 B], but [方面] including [举例] have remained unconvinced.`

- 科技话题（仿写示例）：It has found support from teachers with large classes or concerns about marking time, but departments including maths and physics have remained unconvinced.
- 社会话题（仿写示例）：It has found support from families with long commutes or concerns about road safety, but residents including those near the new junction have remained unconvinced.

## 句型四：担忧与澄清 —— One fear is that ... / insisted that ... would not be ... and only meant that ...

**结构公式**

`One fear is that [方案] would stoke [情绪] or even [后果] by those who [条件]` ＋ `[人物] also insisted that [方案] would not be [被误读的意思] and only meant that [真实边界]`

**FT 原文**

> One fear is that vaccine passports would stoke public anger or even legal action by those who are prevented from travelling simply because they have not yet reached their turn in the inoculation queue.

> He also insisted that vaccinations would not be mandatory for travel and only meant that those who had been inoculated would not have to take a Covid-19 test to enter the country.

**为什么好**

`One fear is that ...` 主动把**最尖锐的反对意见**摆到台面上（这是让步），再让 `would stoke public anger or even legal action by those who ...` 把后果具体化：不是 *people will be angry*，而是“会被激怒、甚至诉诸法律”，并且交代了**被激怒的是谁**（还没轮到接种的人）。第二句是**澄清误解**的标准句式：`would not be A ... and only meant that B`——先否掉最重的解读（不是强制接种），再给出真实边界（只是免检测）。学生被质疑时通常只会重复自己的观点，而 FT 的做法是：界定范围。

**❌ 学生初版**

Some students think the new rule is too strict. The teacher said the rule is not strict. It is only for safety.

**✅ 升级版**

One fear is that a phone ban would stoke resentment or even quiet rule-breaking by those who are simply using their phones to check on a sick grandparent. The head teacher also insisted that the rule would not be a ban on phones at school and only meant that they would stay in lockers during lessons.

**💡 效果**

先承认反对者“怕的是什么”，再界定“这条规则到底管到哪”，一段话就同时完成了让步、回应和收紧边界——这是议论文里最难写、也最能拉分的一步。

**模板 + 例句**

`One fear is that [方案] would stoke [情绪] or even [后果] by those who [条件]. [人物] also insisted that [方案] would not be [被误读的意思] and only meant that [真实边界].`

- 校园话题（仿写示例）：One fear is that the new timetable would stoke anxiety or even last-minute absences among those who are simply not early risers. The committee also insisted that the change would not add lessons and only meant that the first period would start half an hour later.
- 环保话题（仿写示例）：One fear is that a plastic charge would stoke anger or even informal markets by those who simply cannot afford reusable bottles. Officials also insisted that the charge would not ban bottled water and only meant that shops would list it separately on receipts.

## 练习建议

1. **呼吁四问**：写完任何一句“呼吁/建议”，回头补四样东西——向谁呼吁（call on sb）、方案能做到什么（that could allow ...）、为什么是现在（as ... seeks to ...）、谁还有保留（found support from ... but ... remain unconvinced）。四样补齐，口号就变成了提案。
2. **两个表达升级**：把 *The government should ...* 换成 `has called on [对象] to do ...`；把 *Some people disagree* 换成 `has found support from [方面], but [方面] have remained unconvinced`。读一遍，感受语体从“表态”到“报道”的变化。
3. **给反对意见留一个位置**：凡是写建议的地方，试着加一句 `One fear is that ... would stoke ... by those who ...`。能替对方把担忧说清楚的作文，才有说服力。
