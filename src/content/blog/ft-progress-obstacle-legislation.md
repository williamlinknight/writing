# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：句型模仿——写「怎么解决」只会 should？跟FT学4个「推进与受阻」句式

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №195 — 美国众议院民主党人推进3.5万亿美元支出方案（2021年8月，约600词）

## 为什么学「推进与受阻」

上一篇讲的是**主语放什么**：把「一个变化」放到句首，句子就从「谁觉得」变成「什么在发生」。这一篇往前走一步——主语放对了，句子还需要**动起来**。

学生作文里「解决问题」的那一段，几乎只有一个形状：

*The government should build more parks. Schools should give students less homework. This can solve the problem.*

三个句子，三个 should，读完你不知道这件事到底怎么推进、会遇到什么、卡在哪一环。真正的政策或方案从来不是「应该做」就结束了：它先被**通过**，然后**解锁**下一步，中间被某一方**卡住**，最后**实质上**改变了谁手里有权力。

今天这篇 FT 报道写的是美国众议院通过 3.5 万亿美元支出方案框架——一次典型的「立法往前挪了一格」。它把上面四个环节全写到了，正好当模板。这套句式在高考议论文的措施段、雅思 Task 2 的解决方案段、甚至中考书信体的建议段都能直接搬。

## FT 原文

> The 220-212 vote on the so-called budget resolution marked a crucial step towards enacting the president's economic agenda, which calls for huge investments in the US social safety net including education, child care and green energy.

> The framework will allow House lawmakers to proceed with a more detailed version of the legislation later in the year.

> A group of moderate Democrats, led by Josh Gottheimer from New Jersey, had threatened to hold up the package absent firm guarantees that a separate, $1tn bipartisan infrastructure package would be swiftly approved.

> This has in effect given veto rights on the plan to each faction of the Democratic party, leading to growing intraparty spats over the legislation that party leaders and White House officials have been trying to patch up.

中文大意：这场 220 票对 212 票、针对所谓预算决议的表决，标志着朝着落实总统经济议程迈出关键一步，该议程要求对教育、儿童照护和绿色能源等美国社会保障体系大举投资。这个框架将让众议员可以在今年晚些时候继续推进更详细的法案版本。一群温和派民主党人此前威胁要卡住整个方案——除非得到明确保证：另一份 1 万亿美元的两党基础设施建设方案会迅速获批。这实际上等于把对方案的否决权交给了民主党内的每个派系，导致党内围绕法案的争执不断加剧，党领袖和白宫官员一直在设法弥合。

## 句型一：表决作主语 —— marked a crucial step towards

**结构公式**

`[票数/表决/决定] marked a crucial step towards [V-ing], which calls for [内容]`

**FT 原文**

> The 220-212 vote on the so-called budget resolution marked a crucial step towards enacting the president's economic agenda, which calls for huge investments in the US social safety net including education, child care and green energy.

**为什么好**

注意主语：`The 220-212 vote`——不是「议员们通过了」，而是「这场表决」自己成为里程碑。`marked a crucial step towards` 把「通过了一项决议」升级成「在一条路上前进了一格」，读者立刻知道这件事有前后。后半句 `which calls for ...` 顺势解释这条议程要求什么，`including` 再补三个具体领域。学生写同类内容：*The government passed a plan. The plan is about education, child care and green energy.* 两句都在陈述「发生了什么」，没有一句在说「走到了哪一步」。

**❌ 学生初版**

Our school passed a new rule about phones. The rule is about using phones in class.

**✅ 升级版**

The 38-12 vote on the phone policy marked a crucial step towards reclaiming classroom attention, which calls for clear limits during lessons, defined break times and a place to store devices.

**💡 效果**

「通过了一条规定」变成「在夺回课堂注意力这件事上走了一步」，后面 which calls for 立刻把这条规定**要求什么**说清楚——三样具体做法，比一句 The rule is about phones 有信息量得多。

**模板 + 例句**

`[票数/表决/决定] marked a crucial step towards [V-ing], which calls for [内容A], [内容B] and [内容C]`

- 环境话题：The council's 9-3 vote marked a crucial step towards cutting single-use plastic, which calls for refill stations, reusable lunch boxes and a ban on plastic cutlery.
- 教育话题：The decision to open the library at weekends marked a crucial step towards equal access to quiet study space, which calls for longer staffing hours, a bus route and a rule on seat booking.

## 句型二：解锁句 —— will allow X to proceed with

**结构公式**

`[这一步] will allow [主体] to proceed with [下一步] later in the year / in the coming months`

**FT 原文**

> The framework will allow House lawmakers to proceed with a more detailed version of the legislation later in the year.

**为什么好**

`allow ... to proceed with` 是「解锁」的标准写法：前一步不完成，后一步动不了。学生常写 *After this, they can do more things.* ——「可以做更多事」不是信息，是空缺。这句还给了时间锚点 `later in the year`，让流程有了刻度。写建议段时，凡是「先做 A 才能做 B」的地方，都可以用这个句式把因果顺序交代清楚。

**❌ 学生初版**

First we should save money. After that we can do more things for the project.

**✅ 升级版**

A completed survey of every classroom will allow the student council to proceed with a pilot timetable in the coming months, starting with two year groups.

**💡 效果**

`will allow ... to proceed with` 明确了「前一步是后一步的前提」，`in the coming months` 给时间，`starting with` 给范围——一句话交代了顺序、时点和起点，读者知道这件事真的能推下去。

**模板 + 例句**

`[前一步] will allow [主体] to proceed with [下一步] in the coming months`

- 科技话题：A shared database of lesson plans will allow teachers to proceed with a school-wide reading programme in the coming months, without adding marking.
- 社会话题：The new night bus route will allow nurses on late shifts to proceed with a plan to cut taxi costs in the coming months.

## 句型三：阻力句 —— had threatened to hold up ... absent ...

**结构公式**

`[某一方], led by [代表人物], had threatened to hold up [对象] absent firm guarantees that [条件]`

**FT 原文**

> A group of moderate Democrats, led by Josh Gottheimer from New Jersey, had threatened to hold up the package absent firm guarantees that a separate, $1tn bipartisan infrastructure package would be swiftly approved.

**为什么好**

这句是整篇最值得学的一句。`threatened to hold up` 把「不同意」写成了「有能力让它停下来」——比 opposed 有力得多。`absent firm guarantees that ...` 是**条件前置的省略式**：absent 后面直接跟名词，等于 without，但更像法律语言，一句话交代了「什么条件下才不卡」。`led by ...` 又顺手交代了这群人是谁。学生写分歧只会 *Some people did not agree, so the plan was delayed.* 谁是阻力、阻力要什么、条件是什么，全没有。

**❌ 学生初版**

Some parents did not agree with the plan, so the school stopped it.

**✅ 升级版**

A group of parents, led by the head of the parents' committee, had threatened to hold up the reading scheme absent firm guarantees that homework would be reduced in the same term.

**💡 效果**

「有些家长不同意」变成「一群家长威胁要卡住这项计划」；`absent firm guarantees that ...` 把他们真正要的东西写出来——不是反对阅读，是要减作业。写分歧写到「要什么」，文章才有分析的样子。

**模板 + 例句**

`A group of [某一方], led by [人物], had threatened to hold up [对象] absent firm guarantees that [条件]`

- 校园话题：A group of teachers, led by the head of the science department, had threatened to hold up the tablet scheme absent firm guarantees that training time would be paid.
- 社会话题：A group of small shop owners, led by the market association, had threatened to hold up the pedestrian plan absent firm guarantees that delivery hours would be protected.

## 句型四：定性句 —— has in effect given ... leading to ...

**结构公式**

`This has in effect [动词] [权利/后果] to [对象], leading to [进一步后果]`

**FT 原文**

> This has in effect given veto rights on the plan to each faction of the Democratic party, leading to growing intraparty spats over the legislation that party leaders and White House officials have been trying to patch up.

**为什么好**

`in effect` 是「实质上等于」——它让作者可以对一件事**下定性判断**，而不是只叙述。`given veto rights to each faction` 把「每一派都能拦」翻译成一个政治学概念（否决权），`leading to` 用分词挂出后果，`that ... have been trying to patch up` 再补一句「有人在收拾」，把局面写成正在进行的状态。学生写结果只有 *This caused many problems.* —— 「很多问题」是空的，读者拿不到任何具体画面。

**❌ 学生初版**

This caused many problems, and the teachers tried to solve them.

**✅ 升级版**

This has in effect given each class a veto over the timetable, leading to growing arguments about which subject gives up a lesson that the year head has been trying to settle for weeks.

**💡 效果**

「造成很多问题」变成「等于给了每个班一票否决权」——一个具体的权力结构，比「问题很多」尖锐；`leading to` 后面的争执也有了内容（哪门课让出一节课），结尾的 `has been trying to settle` 说明这事还没完。

**模板 + 例句**

`This has in effect given [权利] to [对象], leading to [后果] that [谁] have been trying to [收尾动作]`

- 科技话题：This has in effect given every parent a veto over screen time, leading to rules that teachers have been trying to reconcile all term.
- 社会话题：This has in effect given each district the power to set its own recycling standard, leading to confusion that national retailers have been trying to absorb for years.

## 练习建议

1. **四步流程写作**：写措施段时强迫自己走完四步——谁通过了什么（句型一）、这解锁了什么（句型二）、哪一方可能卡住它、要什么条件（句型三）、实质上改变了什么权力关系（句型四）。四个句型就是四个空位，缺一步文章就少一层。
2. **把 should 换掉**：统计自己作文里 should 出现的次数。凡是「应该做」的地方，问一句「谁来做、做到哪一步、谁可能不愿意」——三问答完，句子自然从 should 句型变成流程句型。
3. **条件前置**：`absent + 名词` 这种省略式条件表达，改写时可以直接套。写「除非……」的地方，先试试 absent（或 without + 名词），再考虑 unless 从句——同义，但前者更像报道体。
