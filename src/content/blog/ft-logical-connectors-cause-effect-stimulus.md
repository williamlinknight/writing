论证类英语写作中，因果关系的表达往往决定了读者是否被说服。

很多学生在写"原因A → 结果B"时，只会用"because"和"so"，写出来的逻辑链僵硬得像说明书。但优秀的英文记者会用更丰富的手段——把因果关系融入伴随状语、现在分词结构、以及"with + 名词 + 分词"的句式里。

今天我们从FT一篇美国刺激法案报道中，学习三种**因果递进型逻辑衔接**结构。

## FT原文

> **来源：** FT双语阅读 #99 — 《美国国会通过拜登1.9万亿美元刺激法案》

> Joe Biden's $1.9tn coronavirus relief package received final backing from the US House of Representatives on Wednesday, **sealing congressional approval for a massive fiscal stimulus with far-reaching political and economic consequences that will last for years.**
>
> The House passed the bill in a narrow 220 to 211 vote, **with all but one Democrat voting in favour and every Republican voting against it, paving the way for Biden to sign it into law on Friday.**
>
> The final greenlight from Congress marks a big victory for the US president, **who took office in January with a mission to mitigate fallout from the coronavirus pandemic and reboot the US economy.** The bill reflects a belief among Biden and his top aides **that they can rapidly accelerate the recovery without triggering an unwanted jump in inflation.**
>
> In a statement from the White House **just moments after the House vote**, Biden said: "This legislation is about giving the backbone of this nation a fighting chance."

## 三个因果递进结构

### 1. V-ing 结果状语：把"因此"融入句子结构

**原文出处：**
> The House passed the bill..., **paving the way for Biden to sign it into law on Friday.**
>
> ...received final backing..., **sealing congressional approval for a massive fiscal stimulus.**

**为什么好：** 用现在分词（paving, sealing）直接引出结果。不说"Therefore, Biden could sign it"，而是把结果融进分词结构——更紧凑、更高级。这是FT记者最常用的因果衔接技巧。

**结构公式：**
> [主句: 某事件发生], [V-ing 结果: 由此导致的结果]

**❌ 学生初版**
> The House passed the bill. Therefore, Biden could sign it into law on Friday.

**✅ 升级版**
> The House passed the bill in a narrow 220 to 211 vote, paving the way for Biden to sign it into law on Friday.

**💡 效果**
分词结构把因果压缩到一句话里，且"paving the way for"（为...铺平道路）本身就是一个高级因果动词短语，比"led to"更生动。

**更多例子：**
> *The government announced a new stimulus package, boosting consumer confidence and driving a stock market rally.*

**模板：**
> [行动A], [V-ing 结果].

> *The company invested heavily in AI research, positioning itself as a market leader in the years ahead.*

### 2. Without + V-ing：兼顾两个目标

**原文出处：**
> ...they can rapidly accelerate the recovery **without triggering an unwanted jump in inflation.**

**为什么好：** "Without + V-ing" 结构表达了一种"做A同时避免B"的因果关系——既想要结果A（经济复苏），又要避免结果B（通胀飙升）。这种正反兼顾的表达，是议论文中展示权衡论证能力的关键。

**结构公式：**
> [主体] can [积极目标A] without [负面结果B]

**❌ 学生初版**
> They want to help the economy recover and they don't want inflation to rise.

**✅ 升级版**
> They can accelerate the recovery without triggering an unwanted jump in inflation.

**💡 效果**
"without triggering" 把"同时避免负面后果"打包成一个状语，结构简洁，意思完整。比用"and at the same time avoid"高级得多。

**模板：**
> [主体] [积极动作] without [负面结果].

> *Schools can expand access to technology without compromising the quality of in-person instruction.*

> *The company cut costs without laying off a single employee.*

### 3. With + 名词 + V-ing：用伴随状语补充原因

**原文出处：**
> The House passed the bill..., **with all but one Democrat voting in favour and every Republican voting against it...**

**为什么好：** "With + 名词 + V-ing（所有民主党都投赞成票，所有共和党都反对）" 用伴随状语补充投票细节。这个结构同时传达了两层信息：一是投票的具体数据（因果关系中的"原因"部分），二是其中蕴含的对比（两党对立）。

**结构公式：**
> [事件], with + [某人] + [动作].

**❌ 学生初版**
> The House passed the bill. All Democrats voted for it and all Republicans voted against it.

**✅ 升级版**
> The House passed the bill in a narrow 220 to 211 vote, with all but one Democrat voting in favour and every Republican voting against it.

**💡 效果**
"with" 把补充说明嵌入主句结构，既不打断主句逻辑，又能带出关键细节。

**模板：**
> [主要事件], with [某群体] [做什么/持什么立场].

> *The new policy was approved, with environmental groups praising its ambition and industry leaders warning about implementation costs.*

## 综合练习：三步升级法

尝试把下面这个基础句子，用今天学的三种因果递进结构升级：

**基础句：**
> The new law passed. It helped the economy. It did not cause inflation.

**第一步：用V-ing结果状语**
> The new law passed, helping the economy recover.

**第二步：同时加入without结构**
> The new law passed, helping the economy recover without causing a surge in inflation.

**第三步：加with伴随状语补充细节**
> The new law passed with broad support, helping the economy recover without causing a surge in inflation.

## 写作建议

因果递进结构的关键，是**把"and then"或"therefore"替换成更紧凑的语法手段**。每次写"Because..."或"So..."的时候，问问自己：能不能用分词结构（V-ing）替代？能不能把原因放进"with"伴随状语？

练熟了这三种结构，你的议论文就会从"说明书风格"变成"记者风格"——逻辑链条清晰，句式丰富自然。
