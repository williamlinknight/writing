很多同学写议论文开头，不是空洞的 "Nowadays..."，就是生硬的定义。今天我们从FT一篇以亲身经历切入、讲述澳大利亚抗疫成功的文章中，拆解3个可直接迁移到作文的提分技巧，让你的开头更生动、论证更有说服力。

## 原文选段

> When I came down with a nasty head cold last week, my first concern was not Covid-19, but that I would miss coaching my son's team for their first game of the cricket season.

> 上周，当我因重感冒病倒时，我首先担心的不是新型冠状病毒肺炎，而是我将无法指导我儿子的球队参加他们本赛季的第一场板球比赛。

> Efficient testing and contact tracing is at the forefront of Australia's virus response, and there have been just 905 deaths so far, compared with 45,365 in the UK and 1,885 in Ireland.

> 有效的新冠检测和接触者追踪是澳大利亚应对疫情的重心，迄今为止，澳大利亚只有905例死亡病例，而英国为45,365例，爱尔兰为1,885例。

> There have been mistakes: ... Yet recent surveys show a big increase in trust in government and public service...

> 但期间也犯过错误：……然而，最近的调查显示，民众对政府和公共服务的信任度大幅上升。

## 高分表达解析

### 1. came down with

**原文：** "When I came down with a nasty head cold last week..."

**中文解释：** 得了（病）、患上。come down with 多用于突发的轻中度疾病，如感冒、流感。

**为什么好：** 学生写"生病了"只会用 *be sick* 或 *have a cold*。*came down with* 更口语化、更具叙事感，用来写个人经历开篇（anecdote）非常自然，能让读者快速进入场景。

| 学生写法 | 升级写法 |
|---------|---------|
| I was sick last month and could not go to school. | I came down with a bad flu last month and missed a whole week of school. |
| He had a fever during the exam. | He came down with a fever on the day of the exam. |

**例句：** She came down with food poisoning the night before the presentation, but still managed to deliver it.

---

### 2. at the forefront of

**原文：** "Efficient testing and contact tracing is at the forefront of Australia's virus response..."

**中文解释：** ……的重点/前沿。forefront 指最前端、领先位置，at the forefront of 表示处于某领域的前沿或核心位置。

**为什么好：** 学生写"……是重点"只会用 *is important* 或 *is the focus of*。*at the forefront of* 自带"领先、核心"的意象，写教育、科技、环保等议题时显得更专业。

| 学生写法 | 升级写法 |
|---------|---------|
| Environmental protection is the focus of our work. | Environmental protection is at the forefront of our work. |
| New technology plays an important role in education. | New technology is at the forefront of educational reform. |

**例句：** AI technology is at the forefront of the transformation in modern education.

---

### 3. abundance of caution

**原文：** "...strong public compliance with government advice to act with an 'abundance of caution'."

**中文解释：** 充分谨慎、谨慎行事。abundance 这里指"充分、十足"，act with an abundance of caution 意为"采取十分谨慎的措施"。

**为什么好：** 学生写"小心谨慎"只会用 *be careful* 或 *cautious*。*abundance of caution* 是一个地道的正式搭配，常出现在公共卫生、安全、法律类议论文中，提升语体层次。

| 学生写法 | 升级写法 |
|---------|---------|
| We should be careful when using the internet. | We should act with an abundance of caution when sharing personal information online. |
| The government should be cautious about reopening schools. | The government should act with an abundance of caution before reopening schools. |

**例句：** In handling student data, schools must act with an abundance of caution to protect privacy.

## 给同学们的外刊阅读建议

这篇文章的写作结构值得模仿：**用一段个人经历切入**（came down with a head cold → miss coaching cricket）→**引出话题**（澳大利亚抗疫成功）→**用数据对比增强说服力**（905 vs 45,365 vs 1,885）→**最后做让步与转折**（There have been mistakes... Yet...）。

建议你下次写议论文时，试着用一个短小的个人经历做开头，再用一组数据对比支撑观点，最后加一句让步转折展示辩证思考。这三步组合，能让你的作文瞬间从"模版感"变成"有真实感"。
