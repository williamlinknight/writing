# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：定语从句——从逼空大战学「链条式定语从句」

## 你的句子为什么"一句一个意思"？

批改经济类、社会热点类作文时，最常见的写法是这样的：

**❌ 学生初版**
GameStop is a company. Its share price went up. Many investors lost money. The government was worried.

**❌ 学生初版**
There was a hedge fund. It bet against GameStop. Retail traders bought the shares. The fund lost money.

每个短句只装一个信息点，句子之间靠"下一句另起炉灶"推进。读起来像清单，不像文章。真正的问题不是语法——是**不知道定语从句可以一个接一个挂下去**。

《金融时报》这篇关于梅尔文资本(Melvin Capital)在"逼空大战"中亏损53%的报道，第一句就是教科书级的"链条式定语从句"：一个名词后面挂三层从句，把**基金是谁、散户做了什么、基金押注了谁**全部压缩进一句话。今天拆给你。

## FT原文示范

> Melvin Capital, the hedge fund that was wrongfooted by retail traders who drove up shares in GameStop and other companies it had bet against, lost 53 per cent in January, according to people familiar with the firm's results.

> Melvin became the target of retail traders who co-ordinated to drive up the share price of GameStop on online message boards such as Reddit, after the firm disclosed its bet against the company in regulatory filings.

> The GameStop saga marks a fall from grace for Melvin, which gained 52 per cent last year, ranking it among the best performing hedge funds.

中文对照：

> 知情人士透露，对冲基金梅尔文资本在1月亏损53%。此前该基金做空游戏驿站(GameStop)及其他一些公司，而散户们炒高了这些公司的股票，让该基金措手不及。

> 梅尔文资本在监管文件中披露其持有游戏驿站空头头寸后，成为了散户的靶子，他们通过Reddit等线上留言板联手炒高了游戏驿站的股价。

> 游戏驿站风波让梅尔文资本声名大降。该基金去年增长52%，是表现最好的对冲基金之一。

## 技巧1：三层嵌套——一个名词挂一串定语从句

**结构公式：** `[主体], the [身份] that [动作1] by [人群] who [动作2] [对象] it had [动作3], [主句结果]`

**FT例句：**
> Melvin Capital, the hedge fund that was wrongfooted by retail traders who drove up shares in GameStop and other companies it had bet against, lost 53 per cent in January.

这句话是定语从句的极限操作：`the hedge fund that was wrongfooted`（基金被搞）→ `by retail traders who drove up shares`（散户抬高股价）→ `companies it had bet against`（关系词省略，基金押注的公司）。三层从句像链条一样层层咬合，最后回到主句 `lost 53 per cent`。主谓之间插入了整整一层"背景信息块"，但读者完全不会迷路。

**❌ 学生初版**
There was a fund. It lost money. Retail traders bought its stocks. It bet against those stocks.

**✅ 升级版**
The company, a local chain that had been hit hard by online rivals who offered cheaper delivery, reported its first loss in a decade.

**💡 效果**
升级版把"公司是谁、遭遇了什么、谁造成的"全部塞进主谓之间，一句话讲完本来要四句的背景。阅卷老师看到这种"主谓间插入背景块"的写法，会立刻明白你的句子驾驭能力。

**考场模板：**
- `[主体], the [身份] that [动作1] by [人群] who [动作2], [主句结果]`
- 例：Our school, the campus that was built 50 years ago by volunteers who planted every tree, finally got a new library.

## 技巧2：定语从句做"对比转折"——which 从句补反差

**结构公式：** `[主句] marks a fall from grace for [主体], which [过去的辉煌], ranking it [定位]`

**FT例句：**
> The GameStop saga marks a fall from grace for Melvin, which gained 52 per cent last year, ranking it among the best performing hedge funds.

学生写"今昔对比"只会 Before, it was good. Now it is bad。FT记者用一个非限制性定语从句完成反差：主句说"跌落神坛"（fall from grace），`which gained 52 per cent last year` 补出"去年还是明星基金"——辉煌是定语从句给的，讽刺感瞬间拉满。

**❌ 学生初版**
The team lost the final. Last year it won the championship.

**✅ 升级版**
The defeat marks a fall from grace for the team, which swept every trophy last season, ranking it among the greatest in the league.

**💡 效果**
`fall from grace for X, which...` 是"先贬后褒"的高级写法：主句给结论，which 从句给反差的证据。雅思大作文讨论"盛衰""利弊"时，这个句式能把对比写进一个句子。

**考场模板：**
- `[事件] marks a fall from grace for [主体], which [昔日辉煌], ranking it [定位]`
- 例：The scandal marks a fall from grace for the bank, which once dominated the market, ranking it among the world's most trusted.

## 技巧3：定语从句藏"时间线"——who 从句里埋顺序

**结构公式：** `[主体] became the target of [人群] who [动作] after [前情]`

**FT例句：**
> Melvin became the target of retail traders who co-ordinated to drive up the share price of GameStop on online message boards such as Reddit, after the firm disclosed its bet against the company in regulatory filings.

学生写"先后顺序"只会 First... then... finally。FT记者把时间线直接埋进定语从句：`retail traders who co-ordinated to drive up the share price` 交代散户的动作，句尾 `after the firm disclosed its bet` 交代触发条件——谁先谁后，一个句子讲完。

**❌ 学生初版**
The company told people its plan. Then many customers were angry. They began to complain online.

**✅ 升级版**
The company became the target of angry customers who organised online boycotts after it announced the price rise in a press release.

**💡 效果**
升级版把"因果+先后"压缩成"主句定调 + who 从句行动 + after 从句前情"，时间线清清楚楚，还省掉了三个短句的重复主语。

**考场模板：**
- `[主体] became the target of [人群] who [行动] after [触发事件]`
- 例：The restaurant became the target of diners who posted negative reviews online after a video showed its kitchen.

## 针对性练习建议

1. **链条仿写**：用三层嵌套结构，把"我们学校是一所老学校，被一场台风毁掉了屋顶，学校一直想修缮"写成一个带定语从句的长句。
2. **今昔对比改写**：从你的作文里找一句"以前很好，现在很差"，用 `marks a fall from grace for..., which..., ranking it...` 改写成一句。
3. **时间线嵌入**：写"这家店被顾客抵制，顾客在网上差评，起因是涨价"，用 `became the target of... who... after...` 一个句子完成。
