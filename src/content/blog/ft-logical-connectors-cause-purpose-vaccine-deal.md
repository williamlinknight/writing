写"为什么"和"为了什么"，是英语议论文绕不开的两件事。

但很多同学写原因只会 `because`，写目的只会 `in order to`，结果就是：原因句堆在一起，目的句干巴巴，整段逻辑链像一串没扣好的珠子——读者能感觉到有关系，却看不到关系在哪里。

今天我们从FT一篇关于美国默克（Merck）与强生（Johnson & Johnson）疫苗生产合作的报道中，学习5种**因果目的型逻辑衔接**结构。

## FT原文

> **来源：** FT双语阅读 #95 — 《拜登：美国到5月底将有足够疫苗为每个成年人接种》

> The US would have enough doses of coronavirus vaccines for every adult by the end of May, Joe Biden said on Tuesday, **thanks in part to** an unusual production deal signed between two of the country's largest drugmakers.
>
> The US president said on Tuesday the deal would accelerate the production of Covid-19 vaccines **so that** enough would be available to vaccinate every adult within 90 days — two months earlier than previously announced.
>
> The Biden administration helped to engineer the deal between the competitors **after** J&J, which was granted an emergency use authorisation for its vaccine at the weekend, was beset by production delays.
>
> **Because** it is only one shot — **meaning** people do not have to return for a second appointment — the J&J vaccine should be easier to roll out than those from BioNTech/Pfizer and Moderna, and can be kept at normal refrigerator temperatures.
>
> Biden also announced that teachers in every state would now be eligible to sign up for a vaccine through their local pharmacy **as** the administration aims to get schools reopened by the end of the president's first 100 days in office.

## 五个因果目的结构

### 1. thanks to + 名词：把"原因"说得轻快自然

**原文出处：** "The US would have enough doses of coronavirus vaccines for every adult by the end of May, ... thanks in part to an unusual production deal signed between two of the country's largest drugmakers."

**为什么好：** `thanks to` 比 `because of` 更口语化、更有"归功于"的正面色彩，常放在句尾作补充说明，让原因成为锦上添花而非生硬的解释。加 `in part`（部分地）还能表达"多个原因之一"。

**❌ 学生初版**
Because of the cooperation between two companies, the US will have enough vaccines.

**✅ 升级版**
The US will have enough vaccines by May, thanks in part to a rare production deal between two industry rivals.

**💡 效果**
原因从"句首的交代"变成"句尾的惊喜"，信息重心落在结果上，原因轻轻带出——这是成熟写作者的节奏感。

### 2. so that + 从句：目的明确，逻辑不断层

**原文出处：** "The deal would accelerate the production of Covid-19 vaccines so that enough would be available to vaccinate every adult within 90 days."

**为什么好：** `so that` 连接"手段 + 目的"，比 `to` 或 `in order to` 更清楚地说明"这样做是为了达到什么效果"，尤其适合目的较长、需要从句展开的场景。

**❌ 学生初版**
The deal can speed up production. The government wants every adult to get vaccinated in 90 days.

**✅ 升级版**
The deal accelerates production so that every adult can be vaccinated within 90 days.

**💡 效果**
两个破碎短句合并成一个完整逻辑链，`so that` 明确宣告"前面是手段，后面是目的"，读者一眼看穿论证意图。

### 3. after + 完整句：时间顺序也是因果

**原文出处：** "The Biden administration helped to engineer the deal between the competitors after J&J, which was granted an emergency use authorisation for its vaccine at the weekend, was beset by production delays."

**为什么好：** `after` 引导的从句交代**时间背景**，而时间先后往往暗含因果——先有"生产延误"，后有"政府撮合"。用时间状语来暗示因果，比直接说 `because` 更含蓄、更专业。

**❌ 学生初版**
J&J had production problems. So the government helped them make the deal.

**✅ 升级版**
The White House helped engineer the deal after J&J was beset by production delays.

**💡 效果**
`after` 把"因"藏进时间状语里，句子的因果感由读者自己读出来——这正是新闻写作避免过度解释的高级笔法。

### 4. meaning + 解释：结果跟着原因走

**原文出处：** "Because it is only one shot — meaning people do not have to return for a second appointment — the J&J vaccine should be easier to roll out..."

**为什么好：** `meaning` 引出对前文的**即时解释**，相当于把"这意味着……"嵌入句中，用破折号隔开，既不影响主干，又补充了重要信息——是英文写作中极实用的插入式因果。

**❌ 学生初版**
The vaccine is only one shot. It means people don't need to go back for a second time.

**✅ 升级版**
It is a single-shot vaccine — meaning recipients do not need a second appointment.

**💡 效果**
破折号 + `meaning` 把解释压缩成一个插入成分，主干依然清晰，信息密度提升，读起来一气呵成。

### 5. as + 从句：原因藏在背景里

**原文出处：** "Biden also announced that teachers in every state would now be eligible to sign up for a vaccine through their local pharmacy as the administration aims to get schools reopened."

**为什么好：** `as` 在这里表示"因为/随着"，用背景信息带出原因，比 `because` 更柔和，适合解释政策动机——先给动作，再轻轻补一句"这么做是因为……"。

**❌ 学生初版**
Teachers can get vaccines. Because the government wants schools to open again.

**✅ 升级版**
Teachers became eligible for vaccination as the administration pushed to reopen schools.

**💡 效果**
`as` 把原因收进背景从句，主句信息先行，逻辑自然——避免了 `because` 开头常见的机械感。

## 写作建议

这一组结构的核心是：**不要总把原因摆在句子最前面。** `thanks to` 可以放句尾补充，`after` 可以藏时间线，`meaning` 可以插进句中，`as` 可以垫在背景里——因果的摆放位置，决定了你文章的成熟度。

建议同学们练习时，把同一个"因为A所以B"用这五种方式各写一遍，感受它们的细微差别；考试时按需取用，逻辑链条自然清晰。
