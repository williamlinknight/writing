# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：结尾技巧——别再用 In conclusion：从FT评论学三种「收得住」的结尾

批改到作文最后一段，我常看到这样的结尾：

**❌ 学生初版**
In conclusion, we should pay attention to this problem, because it is very important for everyone.

**❌ 学生初版**
All in all, we must take action to solve this problem.

问题在于：**这两句没有带来任何新信息**。读者读到最后一段，期待作者「给一个说法」——一个让步、一个方案、一个定性判断，而不是把开头的观点换个说法再抄一遍。

《金融时报》2021年6月有一篇评论，作者是伦敦帝国理工学院全球健康创新研究所联席主任，讨论「抗体筛查能否改变疫苗的分配」。这篇文章的结尾做了三件漂亮的事，正好对应议论文收尾最难的三步。

## FT原文示范

> Indeed, AstraZeneca has licensed its Covid-19 vaccine to be produced at cost by the Serum Institute of India and others. Moderna, meanwhile, announced in October 2020 that it would not enforce its patents covering Covid-19 vaccines based on its mRNA technology during the pandemic, so that others could benefit. That announcement was effectively a unilateral patent-enforcement waiver.

> 事实上，阿斯利康(AstraZeneca)已经授权印度血清研究所(Serum Institute of India)和其他机构以成本价生产新冠疫苗。与此同时，莫德纳于2020年10月宣布将不会在大流行期间执行基于其mRNA技术的新冠疫苗专利，以便让其他公司受益。该声明实际上是一项单方面的专利执行豁免。

> Governments and companies are scrambling to scale up the supply of vaccines as fast as possible; there is no disagreement that it is both necessary and desirable. But it will take time, meaning there will be scarcity in the short-run. Introducing antibody screening — so booster doses are distributed globally on the basis of need rather than nationality — is a strategy that could help end this terrible pandemic sooner.

> 各国政府和企业正争先恐后地尽快扩大疫苗供应；毫无疑问这是必要和可取的。但这需要时间，这意味着短期内会出现短缺。采用抗体筛查是一项有助于更快结束这一可怕疫情的战略，这样就可以根据需要而不是国籍在全球分发加强针。

## 技巧1：让步共识式结尾——先把「没人反对的事」说完，再用 But 转向

**结构公式：** `[各方] are [动作ing] as fast as possible; there is no disagreement that it is both [评价A] and [评价B]. But [真正的限制].`

**FT例句：**
> Governments and companies are scrambling to scale up the supply of vaccines as fast as possible; there is no disagreement that it is both necessary and desirable. But it will take time, meaning there will be scarcity in the short-run.

成熟的作者在结尾不会急着「说服」，而是先承认共识：**there is no disagreement that...（在这一点上没人有异议）**。承认共识的同时也划定了讨论边界——真正的问题不在「要不要做」，而在 But 后面那一句。

**为什么好：** 学生的结尾是「下结论」（So we must...），评论员的结尾是「缩小分歧、指出真正的难点」。前者是重复，后者是推进。

**❌ 学生初版**
So it is very important to use antibody tests. We must do this as soon as possible.

**✅ 升级版**
Hospitals are already working overtime to treat patients as fast as possible; there is no disagreement that more beds are both necessary and desirable. But they will take months to build, meaning prevention matters more than ever.

**💡 效果**
升级版先说「没人反对加床位」，再用 But 把真正的困难（要几个月）推出来，最后落到「预防更重要」。结尾不再是复述，而是把读者的注意力推到下一步。

**考场模板：**
- `[各方] are [动作] as fast as possible; there is no disagreement that [做法] is both [评价A] and [评价B]. But [限制], meaning [真正的重点]`
- 例：Students and teachers are revising as hard as possible; there is no disagreement that more practice is both useful and fair. But it takes time, meaning better methods matter more than more hours.

## 技巧2：动名词命名建议式结尾——给你的方案起一个名字

**结构公式：** `[V-ing 你的建议] — so [对象] are [动作] on the basis of [标准A] rather than [标准B] — is a strategy that could [结果].`

**FT例句：**
> Introducing antibody screening — so booster doses are distributed globally on the basis of need rather than nationality — is a strategy that could help end this terrible pandemic sooner.

这个结尾有三个动作：
1. **动名词做主语**：Introducing antibody screening（引入抗体筛查），把一整条建议压缩成一个名词短语，让它像一个「已经存在的方案」一样被讨论；
2. **破折号内嵌**：`on the basis of need rather than nationality`（依据需要，而不是国籍）——一句话把方案的判断标准讲清楚；
3. **`is a strategy that could...` 收尾**：不把话说满（could，而不是 will），反而更有专业感。

**❌ 学生初版**
I think we should use screening tests. In this way, poor countries can get more vaccines.

**✅ 升级版**
Introducing a weekly reading hour at school — so books are chosen on the basis of interest rather than exam value — is a strategy that could help more students finish a whole book this term.

**💡 效果**
同样的建议，加上「命名 + 标准对比 + could」，结尾立刻从「我的想法」变成「一个可讨论的方案」。

**考场模板：**
- `[V-ing 建议] — so [对象] are [动作] on the basis of [A] rather than [B] — is a strategy that could [结果]`
- 例：Introducing a fifteen-minute review before bed — so new words are revisited on the basis of memory rather than mood — is a strategy that could help students keep what they learn.

## 技巧3：事实定性式结尾——用一句短句给事实「贴标签」

**结构公式：** `[主体A] has [动作]. [主体B], meanwhile, [动作].... That [名词] was effectively a [定性名词短语].`

**FT例句：**
> Indeed, AstraZeneca has licensed its Covid-19 vaccine to be produced at cost by the Serum Institute of India and others. Moderna, meanwhile, announced in October 2020 that it would not enforce its patents covering Covid-19 vaccines based on its mRNA technology during the pandemic, so that others could benefit. That announcement was effectively a unilateral patent-enforcement waiver.

记者先摆两个事实（阿斯利康授权、莫德纳不执行专利），然后用一句话定性：**That announcement was effectively a unilateral patent-enforcement waiver.**（那项声明实际上是一项单方面的专利执行豁免。）

**为什么好：** effectively 是「实际上、等于说」的意思。它把一件具体的事，升格成一个可以讨论的概念（豁免 waiver）。学生写作文只会说 This is good / This is important；记者会告诉你**这件事「等于什么」**。

**❌ 学生初版**
Our school opened the library to others. This is a good thing.

**✅ 升级版**
Our school has opened its library to pupils from nearby schools; the local college, meanwhile, has shared its lab equipment with us. That arrangement was effectively a quiet redistribution of educational resources.

**💡 效果**
升级版用两个具体事实做铺垫，最后一句用 effectively 把这件事「命名」为一次资源的再分配——结尾有了结论，也有了新信息。

**考场模板：**
- `That [名词] was effectively a [定性名词短语]`
- 例：That decision was effectively a promise to the whole class.

## 结尾自检清单

写完最后一段，问自己三个问题：

1. **有没有新信息？** 如果最后一段只是把开头的观点换成同义词再说一遍，删掉重写。要么给让步（`No one disagrees that... But...`），要么给方案，要么给定性。
2. **方案有没有「名字」？** 你的建议是散在两三句话里的，还是被压缩成了一个动名词短语（Introducing...）？
3. **有没有一句「贴标签」的短句？** 找出全文最重要的那个事实，用 `That ... was effectively a ...` 给它一个概念上的名字。

## 针对性练习建议

1. **让步共识改写**：把你上一篇作文的最后一段改写成 `there is no disagreement that ... But ...` 结构；写完后圈出 But 后面的内容——那才是你真正的观点。
2. **方案命名练习**：用 `[V-ing 建议] — so [对象] are [动作] on the basis of [A] rather than [B] — is a strategy that could ...` 为「减少手机使用」「提高阅读量」「分担家务」三个话题各写一句结尾。
3. **定性标签练习**：从最近的新闻（学校的、社区的都可以）里挑一个具体事实，用 `That [名词] was effectively a [定性短语]` 给它贴一次标签。
