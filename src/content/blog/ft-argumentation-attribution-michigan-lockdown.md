# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：论证方法——归因论证，把"为什么"讲清楚

学生写议论文，一碰到"为什么"就只会一个词：because。Because the environment is polluted. Because technology develops fast. 归因单一、归因浅层——"因为污染所以污染"，绕了一圈等于没说。

真正高级的论证，会把"为什么"讲到具体层次：是**目的**使然，还是**时机**不对？是**机构角色**决定，还是**人与人的关系**在起作用？甚至是某种**心理机制**？今天我们从《金融时报》一篇关于美国疾控中心（CDC）与密歇根州博弈的报道里，学四种**归因论证**手法。

## FT原文

> The head of the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention has urged Michigan's governor to shut down the state instead of asking for more vaccines as a way to control a sudden jump in coronavirus cases.

> Rochelle Walensky, the CDC director, said on Monday she thought Michigan should impose fresh restrictions to combat the rapid spread of the disease, adding that a bumper delivery of vaccines would arrive too late to halt the uptick in cases.

> Her comments mark a rare political intervention from the US public health body, which has so far largely remained happy to issue guidelines and let states set their own lockdown rules. They also reflect growing tension between Gretchen Whitmer, the state's governor, and the federal government over how to quash a regional leap in cases which experts warn could sweep the whole country before enough people are vaccinated to stop it.

> Health officials believe the success of the vaccine rollout may have made even unvaccinated people feel overly-confident about the trajectory of the disease. Doctors in Michigan say the people filling up their wards are younger than during earlier waves.

## 中文对照

> 美国疾病控制与预防中心负责人敦促密歇根州州长关闭该州以控制新冠肺炎病例的突然激增，而不是索要更多疫苗。

> 疾控中心主任罗谢尔·瓦伦斯基表示，她认为密歇根州应该实施新的限制措施，以遏制该疾病的快速传播。她还说大量疫苗的交付时间会太晚，来不及阻止病例的增加。

> 她的言论标志着该美国公共卫生机构罕见的政治干预。迄今为止该机构大体上只愿发布指导方针，并让各州制定自己的封锁规则。这些言论也反映了该州州长格蕾琴·惠特默与联邦政府之间日益紧张的关系，双方在如何遏制该地区病例暴增的方式上存在分歧。专家警告称，可能在足够多人接种疫苗阻止其势头之前，病例激增就会席卷全国。

> 卫生官员认为，疫苗推广的成功可能让未接种疫苗的人也对该疾病的走势过于自信。密歇根州的医生表示，目前住满病房的病人比前几波疫情时更年轻。

## 手法一：目的归因（as a way to）

看这句：

> urged Michigan's governor to shut down the state instead of asking for more vaccines as a way to control a sudden jump in coronavirus cases

一句话完成三重归因：**动作**（shut down the state）、**目的**（as a way to control a sudden jump）、**对替代方案的否定**（instead of asking for more vaccines）。学生写"政府应该行动，因为情况很糟"，只说了动作和情绪；FT把动作、目的、对比三个信息压缩进一句话，归因立刻立体。

**❌ 学生初版**

The government should take action because the situation is bad.

**✅ 升级版**

The CDC urged Michigan's governor to shut down the state instead of asking for more vaccines as a way to control a sudden jump in cases.

**💡 效果**

"as a way to"把目的变成原因，"instead of"顺带否定替代方案——一句顶三句。

**模板**

[Authority] urged [X] to [动作A] instead of [动作B] as a way to [目的].

**例句**

The school urged students to read original books instead of relying on summaries as a way to improve their writing.

## 手法二：时机归因（too late to）

看这句：

> a bumper delivery of vaccines would arrive too late to halt the uptick in cases

为什么疾控中心反对"用疫苗解决问题"？不是疫苗没用，而是**来不及**。"too late to"把归因精确到时间维度：方案本身成立，但时机已过。这种"不是不行，是太晚"的归因，比笼统的"疫苗解决不了问题"高级得多——它承认了对手方案的部分合理性，再指出致命缺陷。

**❌ 学生初版**

Vaccines cannot solve the problem.

**✅ 升级版**

A bumper delivery of vaccines would arrive too late to halt the uptick in cases.

**💡 效果**

归因精确到"时间不够"，读者看到的是冷静的分析，而不是武断的否定。

**模板**

[方案] would arrive too late to [结果].

**例句**

Extra tutoring would arrive too late to rescue his grade before the final exam.

## 手法三：机构与关系归因（mark a rare…from / reflect growing tension between…and…over…）

看这句：

> Her comments mark a rare political intervention from the US public health body... They also reflect growing tension between Gretchen Whitmer, the state's governor, and the federal government over how to quash a regional leap in cases

这是归因论证里最见功力的一层：把现象归因到**机构角色**和**人际关系**。第一个动词 mark——"这标志着该机构罕见的政治干预"，归因到 CDC 一贯"只发指南、不插手"的角色传统，所以这次破例格外值得注意；第二个动词 reflect——"这反映了州长与联邦政府之间日益紧张的关系"，归因到两个权力主体之间的博弈。同一个现象，两个归因角度，论证立刻有了层次。

**❌ 学生初版**

The CDC did something unusual.

**✅ 升级版**

Her comments mark a rare political intervention from the US public health body. They also reflect growing tension between the state governor and the federal government over how to quash the surge.

**💡 效果**

"mark a rare…from"归因到角色，"reflect growing tension between A and B over C"归因到关系——两个动词撑起两重归因，深度立现。

**模板**

[事件] marks a rare [X] from [机构]. It also reflects growing tension between [A] and [B] over [议题].

**例句**

The principal's statement marks a rare intervention from the school administration. It also reflects growing tension between teachers and parents over homework loads.

## 手法四：心理归因（believe…may have made…feel）

看这句：

> Health officials believe the success of the vaccine rollout may have made even unvaccinated people feel overly-confident about the trajectory of the disease

归因到**心理机制**——疫苗推广太成功，反而让没打疫苗的人也放松警惕。这是整篇报道最深的一层归因。注意两个细节：一是"believe…may have made"，专家也只是推测，所以用了 may；二是"even unvaccinated people"，连没打疫苗的人都受影响，说明机制作用面之广。敢归因、又留余地，这是成熟论证的标志。

**❌ 学生初版**

People became careless.

**✅ 升级版**

Health officials believe the success of the vaccine rollout may have made even unvaccinated people feel overly-confident about the trajectory of the disease.

**💡 效果**

把"粗心"归因到一个具体机制（成功带来的错觉），并用 may 留出修正空间——既深且稳。

**模板**

Officials believe [原因] may have made [群体] feel [状态].

**例句**

Teachers believe the ease of online searches may have made some students feel overly-confident about their memory of facts.

## 写作建议

这四种手法的共同点是：**归因不是"because"换个说法，而是把"为什么"讲到具体层次**。下次写议论文，先别急着写 because，问自己四句话——我归的是目的、时机、机构关系，还是心理机制？我的归因动词够不够精确（urge…as a way to / too late to / mark / reflect / believe…may have made）？我敢不敢像"may have made"一样给归因留余地？议论文的深度，往往不取决于你说了什么，而取决于你把"为什么"讲到了第几层。
