## 为什么学"预警与因果链"

学生写"后果"类段落，最常见的写法是：`If we do not stop, the environment will be worse.` 或者 `This problem will cause many bad things.`——**只说"会变坏"，不说"怎么变坏"**。真正有说服力的后果描写，必须给出因果链条：因为 A，所以 B，B 又引发 C；还要给出时间（几十年内）、程度（多少、多高）、依据（数据范围）。

今天从 FT 一篇关于气候变暖可能引发海啸的科学报道里，抽出 4 个句型。它们解决一个具体场景：**怎么把"某件事将来会引发什么后果"写得有层次、有依据**。科技、环境、社会话题的议论文都能直接移植。

## FT 原文

> The world is underestimating the geological consequences of global warming, which could trigger catastrophic earthquakes and tsunamis as the melting of ice sheets reduces the weight on the crust below and unleashes intense seismic activity, a leading earth scientist has warned.
>
> Within decades, that could spark huge submarine earthquakes off the coast of Greenland, causing tsunamis with disastrous consequences for North America and probably Europe, he said.
>
> An offshore earthquake, triggered by the release of pressure after northern Europe's ice sheets had melted, set off a vast landslide of submarine sediments under the Norwegian Sea.
>
> Sensitive GPS instruments on coasts around the ocean are beginning to detect this uplift, which is taking place at a rate ranging from a few millimetres to 2.5cm a year.

中文大意：一位顶尖地球科学家警告，世界低估了全球变暖的地质后果——冰盖融化减轻地壳承受的重量，释放强烈地震活动，可能引发灾难性地震和海啸。几十年内，这可能引发格陵兰岛近海大型海底地震，对北美乃至欧洲造成灾难性后果。当年正是地震释放压力，触发挪威海海底大规模滑坡。海洋沿岸的敏感GPS仪器已开始探测到这种抬升，速度从每年几毫米到2.5厘米不等。

## 句型一：句尾倒装式警告

**结构公式**

`[现象/判断], which could trigger [后果] as [原因], [专家] has warned.`

**FT 原文**

> The world is underestimating the geological consequences of global warming, which could trigger catastrophic earthquakes and tsunamis as the melting of ice sheets reduces the weight on the crust below and unleashes intense seismic activity, a leading earth scientist has warned.

**为什么好**

学生写警告，只会 `A scientist said this is very dangerous`。FT 这个句子把四层信息一次装完：判断（世界低估了……）→ 后果（可能引发灾难性地震海啸）→ 原因（冰盖融化减轻压力、释放地震活动）→ 消息来源（一位顶尖科学家警告）。而且把 `a leading earth scientist has warned` 放在**句尾**——这是英文新闻的经典"句尾归因"结构：先抛结论和推理，最后才给出处，读者先被内容说服，再看到权威背书。

**❌ 学生初版**

A scientist said global warming is very dangerous. It will cause earthquakes and tsunamis.

**✅ 升级版**

The world is underestimating the risks of global warming, which could trigger earthquakes and tsunamis as melting ice reduces pressure on the crust, a leading scientist has warned.

**💡 效果**

判断、后果、原因、来源一气呵成，`has warned` 句尾收尾，比 "said it is dangerous" 有分量得多。

**模板 + 例句**

`[判断], which could trigger [后果] as [原因], [权威] has warned.`

- 科技话题：People are underestimating the risks of AI, which could replace millions of jobs as machines become better at creative work, a leading economist has warned.
- 环境话题：The city is underestimating the danger of flooding, which could paralyse the subway system as sea levels continue to rise, a city official has warned.

## 句型二：时间推演 Within decades

**结构公式**

`Within [时间], that could [动作1], causing [动作2/后果].`

**FT 原文**

> Within decades, that could spark huge submarine earthquakes off the coast of Greenland, causing tsunamis with disastrous consequences for North America and probably Europe, he said.

**为什么好**

学生写后果没有"时间坐标"，只会 `In the future, it will be bad`。FT 用 `Within decades` 把时间框死（几十年内），用 `could` 保持科学报道的谨慎（不是断言，是可能），再用 `causing + 动名词` 把第二层后果挂在句尾——第一层后果（海底地震）顺藤摸瓜带出第二层（海啸）。这就是因果链的写法：`A could spark B, causing C`。

**❌ 学生初版**

In the future, the ice will melt and there will be earthquakes and tsunamis.

**✅ 升级版**

Within decades, that could spark powerful earthquakes off the coast, causing tsunamis with disastrous consequences for coastal cities.

**💡 效果**

`Within + 时间段` 让预测有紧迫感，`could` 保留严谨，`causing` 让因果链自动延伸。

**模板 + 例句**

`Within [时间], [X] could [动作1], causing [后果].`

- 教育话题：Within a decade, online courses could replace many traditional lectures, causing a fundamental change in how students learn.
- 科技话题：Within a few years, self-driving cars could dominate city streets, causing traffic rules to be rewritten.

## 句型三：触发链 triggered by ... set off

**结构公式**

`[事件], triggered by [原因], set off [连锁反应].`

**FT 原文**

> An offshore earthquake, triggered by the release of pressure after northern Europe's ice sheets had melted, set off a vast landslide of submarine sediments under the Norwegian Sea.

**为什么好**

因果链条最专业的一环：**用被动分词 `triggered by` 把原因嵌进句中**。学生写因果只会 `Because A happened, B happened, and then C happened`——三个句子、三个主语、啰嗦。FT 用 `X, triggered by Y, set off Z` 一个句子呈现完整链条：地震被压力释放触发，又引发海底滑坡。`trigger` 和 `set off` 都是"触发/引发"的精准动词，比 `cause` 生动得多。

**❌ 学生初版**

Because the pressure was released, there was an earthquake. Then the earthquake caused a landslide under the sea.

**✅ 升级版**

The earthquake, triggered by the sudden release of pressure, set off a vast landslide on the seabed.

**💡 效果**

原因用 `triggered by` 压进逗号之间，结果用 `set off` 收尾，一句话走完"原因—事件—连锁反应"。

**模板 + 例句**

`[事件], triggered by [原因], set off [连锁反应].`

- 社会话题：The panic, triggered by a false rumour online, set off a rush to buy everyday goods.
- 校园话题：The argument, triggered by a misunderstanding over group work, set off a week of tension in the class.

## 句型四：数据范围 ranging from ... to ...

**结构公式**

`[指标], which is taking place at a rate ranging from [最小值] to [最大值].`

**FT 原文**

> Sensitive GPS instruments on coasts around the ocean are beginning to detect this uplift, which is taking place at a rate ranging from a few millimetres to 2.5cm a year.

**为什么好**

论证"后果有多严重"，不能只喊 "very fast" / "very big"。FT 用 `ranging from ... to ...` 给出精确范围（每年几毫米到2.5厘米），数据一出来，可信度立刻不同。`range from A to B` 是描述范围、程度、比例的标准句式，任何带数字的议论文都该掌握。

**❌ 学生初版**

The land is moving very fast, and it will cause many problems.

**✅ 升级版**

The sea level is rising at a rate ranging from 2mm to 4mm a year, and the speed is increasing.

**💡 效果**

`ranging from ... to ...` 把模糊的 "very fast" 变成可验证的数据范围，论证立刻有据可依。

**模板 + 例句**

`[指标] ... ranging from [最小值] to [最大值]`

- 教育话题：The time students spend on homework varies, ranging from one hour to four hours a day.
- 科技话题：The cost of the new technology is falling, ranging from 20 to 50 per cent cheaper than last year.

## 练习建议

1. **按"链"记忆**：把这 4 个句型当成一条因果链的四个零件——句型一定义问题，句型二推演时间后果，句型三压缩因果链条，句型四用数据收尾。写"后果段"时按顺序选。
2. **替换练习**：用同一个话题（比如"沉迷手机"）把 4 个句型各写一遍：underestimating → within a decade → triggered by → ranging from。同一话题连写四句，结构记忆最深。
3. **看数据说话**：凡是作文里出现 "very much / very big / very fast"，强迫自己改成带数字或范围的表达；没有数据就用 `ranging from ... to ...` 造一个合理的区间。
