# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：括号插入语与破折号限定——从气溶胶科普评论学「边写边解释」

学生一写到「需要解释专业概念」的段落就卡壳：写 *Droplets are the main way the virus spreads*，怕读者不懂 droplets，于是停下来补一句 *Droplets are particles in the air*，再继续原来的句子——好好的论证被「解释句」拦腰截断。反过来，敢用术语的同学又常常不解释，读者一头雾水。

英文科普写作的答案很优雅：**用括号把解释塞进句子本身**。*Droplets (defined as particles at least 5-10 microns across) are widely regarded as...*——主句该讲什么讲什么，定义放进括号，解释完还不跳戏。

今天我们读《金融时报》2020年的一篇科学评论——关于新冠病毒是否通过空气传播的争论——学习4种**括号与破折号插入语**。这篇文章术语密集（气溶胶、飞沫、微滴），是学习「边写边解释」的最佳素材；雅思Task 2写环境、科技、健康类话题时，这套技巧能让你的定义段瞬间专业起来。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №58 — *值得重视的气溶胶传播说*（科学评论，2020年）

## 英文原文

> How does coronavirus spread? Scientists are increasingly convinced that airborne transmission plays a role. ... The virus is worryingly contagious, with nearly 13m confirmed cases worldwide. Droplets (defined as particles at least 5-10 microns across) are widely regarded as the master key to respiratory infection.

> But the appearance of large infection clusters at indoor gatherings has led many experts, such as Kimberly Prather, an atmospheric chemist at the University of California, San Diego, to question whether droplets explain everything.

> Airborne spread involves particles that are smaller than droplets, called aerosols (also called droplet nuclei or microdroplets). ... Strong evidence always trumps weak, but, in an extraordinary situation, weak evidence — especially when it converges from multiple disciplines — is arguably better than none.

## 中文对照

> 新型冠状病毒是如何传播的？科学家们越来越确信，空气传播在其中发挥了作用。……新冠病毒具有令人担忧的传染性，全世界已有近1300万确诊病例。外界广泛认为，飞沫（定义为直径至少5-10微米的颗粒）是呼吸道感染的主要原因。

> 但是在室内聚会中出现的大规模聚集性感染使得许多专家——如加利福尼亚大学圣迭戈分校的大气化学家金伯莉•普拉瑟(Kimberly Prather)——开始质疑飞沫是否可以解释一切。

> 空气传播涉及到比飞沫更小的颗粒，称为气溶胶（也称为飞沫核或微滴）。……强有力的证据总是好过不充分的证据，但在特殊的情况下，不充分的证据——特别是当它横跨多个学科时——总比没有证据要好。

## 4个高分结构

### 结构一：括号下定义——"术语 (defined as ...)"

> Droplets **(defined as particles at least 5-10 microns across)** are widely regarded as the master key to respiratory infection.

**为什么好：** 读者可能不知道 droplets 的精确科学定义，但作者不想为此打断论证。他把定义装进括号：*(defined as particles at least 5-10 microns across)*——飞沫是直径5-10微米的颗粒。主句 *Droplets are widely regarded as the master key...* 完整无缺，定义只是顺手补给的弹药。**第一次使用专业术语时，括号定义是最高效的照顾读者的方式。**

**❌ 学生初版**

> Droplets are small particles in the air. They are at least 5 to 10 microns wide. Droplets are the main way the virus spreads.

**✅ 升级版**

> Droplets (defined as particles at least 5-10 microns across) are widely regarded as the master key to respiratory infection.

**💡 效果** 学生爱用 *X is Y* 的完整句解释术语，结果每解释一个词就断一次论证。括号把「定义」降级为插入成分，主句保持推进。雅思写作同理：*Artificial intelligence (defined as computer systems that perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence) is transforming education.*

**模板：** *[术语] (defined as [简洁定义]) + [主句]*

### 结构二：括号别名——"aerosols (also called droplet nuclei or microdroplets)"

> Airborne spread involves particles that are smaller than droplets, called **aerosols (also called droplet nuclei or microdroplets)**.

**为什么好：** 同一个东西可能有多个名字：气溶胶也叫 droplet nuclei（飞沫核），也叫 microdroplets（微滴）。作者在主句里用 aerosols，括号里一次性交代别名——*(also called droplet nuclei or microdroplets)*。读者以后再遇到任何别名都不慌，因为作者已经「对过暗号」。写英文说明文时，遇到同一概念有俗称/学名/缩写之分，这正是括号的用武之地。

**❌ 学生初版**

> These particles are called aerosols. Scientists also call them droplet nuclei. Some people call them microdroplets. They can stay in the air for hours.

**✅ 升级版**

> Airborne spread involves particles that are smaller than droplets, called aerosols (also called droplet nuclei or microdroplets). They linger in the air, where they can be inhaled by others.

**💡 效果** 别名信息收进括号后，后面的句子可以放心只用主名 aerosols，不再需要反复解释。写全球化话题时：*the gig economy (also called the platform economy or the on-demand economy) is reshaping labour markets.*

**模板：** *[主名] (also called [别名1] or [别名2])*

### 结构三：身份同位语引专家——"many experts, such as Kimberly Prather, an atmospheric chemist at ..."

> ...has led many experts, **such as Kimberly Prather, an atmospheric chemist at the University of California, San Diego**, to question whether droplets explain everything.

**为什么好：** 引用专家观点时，英文标准动作是给专家配一个**身份同位语**：*Kimberly Prather, an atmospheric chemist at the University of California, San Diego*——姓名 + 职业 + 机构一次说清。读者不需要认识这个人，也能判断「这个观点有没有分量」。注意这里的结构：*many experts, such as [姓名], [身份同位语], to question...*——姓名和身份都嵌在 such as 列举里，主句骨架 (*has led many experts to question...*) 完全没变形。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Kimberly Prather is a scientist. She studies the atmosphere. She works at a university in California. She thinks droplets cannot explain everything.

**✅ 升级版**

> The appearance of large infection clusters has led many experts, such as Kimberly Prather, an atmospheric chemist at the University of California, San Diego, to question whether droplets explain everything.

**💡 效果** 写议论文需要「权威背书」时，学生只会搬出 *Some scientists think...* 或编一个泛泛的 *According to experts*。身份同位语让每个专家都有名有姓有来头：*experts such as Dr Li Wei, a professor of environmental science at Xiamen University, warn that...* 说服力立刻不同。

**模板：** *[群体], such as [姓名], [a/an 职业 at 机构], [主句]*

### 结构四：破折号让步限定——"weak evidence — especially when it converges from multiple disciplines — is arguably better than none"

> Strong evidence always trumps weak, but, in an extraordinary situation, weak evidence **— especially when it converges from multiple disciplines —** is arguably better than none.

**为什么好：** 作者想表达「证据不足也比没有好」，但他怕读者误解，于是用一对破折号插入限定条件：*— especially when it converges from multiple disciplines —*（尤其是当它横跨多个学科时）。破折号插入语让观点更严谨：主句立论，插入成分负责「打补丁」。这比用括号语气强，比用逗号更醒目，最适合放**需要读者注意的让步与限定**。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Weak evidence is better than no evidence. This is especially true when the evidence comes from many different subjects.

**✅ 升级版**

> In an extraordinary situation, weak evidence — especially when it converges from multiple disciplines — is arguably better than none.

**💡 效果** 学生写议论文最常见的毛病是观点太满、不留余地。破折号让步插入语是天然的「严谨开关」：*Social media — especially when used by teenagers — can both connect and isolate.* 一句话同时做到立论和限定。

**模板：** *[观点主句] — especially when/particularly [限定条件] — [继续主句]*

## 四选一：什么时候用哪种插入语？

| 场景 | 用哪个结构 |
|------|-----------|
| 第一次使用专业术语，要给定义 | 结构一：括号下定义 |
| 一个概念有多个名字/俗称 | 结构二：括号别名 |
| 引用专家观点，要交代来头 | 结构三：身份同位语 |
| 观点太绝对，想加让步限定 | 结构四：破折号限定 |

## 小练习

下面这段写「远程教育」的段落，术语用了但不解释、专家没名没姓、观点不留余地。请用今天的结构升级：

> ❌ Online learning (as a new way of teaching) is becoming popular. It uses the internet to connect teachers and students. During the pandemic, many students studied online. Some scientists think online learning is good. But it also has problems. Students may lose focus. This is especially true when they study at home.

提示：给 online learning 一个更精确的括号定义（不必用我给的）；「有些科学家」换成有名有姓的专家+身份同位语（可以虚构一位，但职业机构要具体）；「学生容易走神」那句用破折号加限定条件。
