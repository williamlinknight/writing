# 读外刊学写作：从「快手财报与股价崩跌」学5个写「商业数据与转折」的高分表达

写财经数据，中文学生最常写两句：*The share price fell. The loss increased.* 语法全对，但像账单，不像报道：没有跌幅、没有蒸发的市值、没有「一边在跌、另一边在涨」的转折。今天我们看《金融时报》(FT) 2021年5月的这篇报道：快手(Kuaishou)公布财报后，香港股价单日下跌11.5%，市值蒸发多达140亿美元。这篇报道的骨架非常清晰——**先交代股价反应和跌幅，再说财报背景和竞争环境，接着用数字对比写「虚拟礼物收入下降 vs 广告业务增长」，最后落到机构下调目标价和经营亏损扩大**。下面5个表达，正好把「商业数据与转折」写扎实。

## 📝 原文对照

**English:**

> Shares in ByteDance's main competitor in China tumbled after the company's losses widened on growing signs that users in the country are spending less on its livestreaming services.

> The company's shares fell 11.5 per cent in Hong Kong on Tuesday, erasing as much as 14bn off Kuaishou's market capitalisation.

> Growth in the company's advertising business helped offset the decline, with revenue in this segment up 161 per cent year on year.

> ... sales for all three of its business lines came in below research group Bernstein's estimates.

> The company's operating loss expanded to Rmb7.3bn (1.14bn) for the quarter, from Rmb5bn a year earlier.

**中文对照：**

> 由于越来越多的迹象表明中国用户在直播服务上的消费减少，字节跳动在中国国内的主要竞争对手快手的股价大幅下跌，此前该公司亏损扩大。

> 周二，快手在香港的股价下跌11.5%，令其市值蒸发多达140亿美元。

> 广告业务的增长帮助抵消了下滑，该分部收入同比增长161%。

> ... 三大业务线的销售额均低于研究集团伯恩斯坦的估计。

> 该公司当季经营亏损扩大至73亿元人民币（合11.4亿美元），而上年同期为50亿元人民币。

## 🔍 重点词汇解析

### 1. tumble —— (股价/数字)暴跌

**原文出处：** Shares in ByteDance's main competitor in China **tumbled** after the company's losses widened ...

**为什么好：** 学生写股价下跌，基本只用 *fall*/*drop* / *go down*。*tumble* 自带「摔跤般突然、陡峭」的画面感，专门用于股价、利润、销售额等数字的快速下跌，比 *fall* 更有新闻色彩。后面常接 *after ...* 交代跌的原因，一句话把「结果+原因」串起来。

**❌ 学生初版**
Kuaishou's share price went down because users spent less on livestreaming.

**✅ 升级版**
Kuaishou's shares tumbled after the company's losses widened on signs that users are spending less on livestreaming services.

**💡 效果** 写「股价/利润/销售额突然下降」时：*Shares tumbled 12 per cent after the earnings warning.* ／ *Profits tumbled as raw material costs rose.* 记住搭配：*tumble after ...*（……之后暴跌）。

### 2. erase ... off market capitalisation —— 使……市值蒸发

**原文出处：** ... erasing as much as 14bn off Kuaishou's market capitalisation.

**为什么好：** *erase* 本意是「擦除」，在财经英语里被借用来写「市值蒸发」——仿佛几十亿美元的价值被一块擦子擦掉了。*erase ... off market capitalisation* 是一个固定的金融搭配，学生绝不会自己拼出来，但读一遍就记住。和 *wipe off* 同义，可互换。

**❌ 学生初版**
Kuaishou lost 14 billion of market value.

**✅ 升级版**
The fall erased as much as 14bn off Kuaishou's market capitalisation.

**💡 效果** 写「市值缩水/资产缩水」时：*The scandal erased 3bn off the company's market capitalisation in a single day.* 把金额放在 erase 之后、off 之前，句式固定，非常像路透社或 FT 的行文。

### 3. help offset the decline —— 抵消下滑

**原文出处：** Growth in the company's advertising business helped offset the decline ...

**为什么好：** 商业写作最常见的结构是「一边在跌，另一边在涨」——*help offset the decline* 就是写这种**让步对比**的标准句：广告业务增长，帮助抵消了虚拟礼物收入下降。*help offset* 语气温和（帮助抵消，而非完全抵消），比 *solve* 或 *fix* 更真实，也更符合事实。

**❌ 学生初版**
Advertising revenue grew, so the decline in gift spending was solved.

**✅ 升级版**
Growth in advertising revenue helped offset the decline in spending on livestreaming gifts.

**💡 效果** 写「正反面共存」的商业现象：*Strong export sales helped offset the decline in domestic demand.* ／ *Cost cuts helped offset the drop in revenue.* 可把 *help offset* 接在好消息后面，坏消息放在 *the decline* 位置。

### 4. come in below estimates —— 不及分析师预期

**原文出处：** ... sales for all three of its business lines came in below research group Bernstein's estimates.

**为什么好：** 财报季写作的必备短语。*come in* 在这里是「（结果）出来，报到」，*come in below/above estimates* = 结果不及/好于预期。学生常写 *The sales were lower than expected*，语气平淡；*come in below estimates* 自带「分析师预期 vs 实际结果」的对比框架，是财经新闻的标准台词。

**❌ 学生初版**
Sales of all three business lines were lower than Bernstein's estimates.

**✅ 升级版**
Sales for all three of its business lines came in below Bernstein's estimates.

**💡 效果** 写「财报/考试成绩/经济数据 vs 预期」时：*GDP growth came in below estimates.* ／ *Quarterly revenue came in above estimates.* 记住搭配：*come in below/above + estimates/expectations*。

### 5. expand from ... to ... —— 从……扩大至……

**原文出处：** The company's operating loss expanded to Rmb7.3bn (1.14bn) for the quarter, from Rmb5bn a year earlier.

**为什么好：** 写「亏损/费用/亏空」变大，学生常写 *the loss became bigger* 或 *increased*。*expand* 配合 *from ... to ...* 是写「数字扩大」的高级搭配，强调规模的膨胀过程；来源还把同期对比（from Rmb5bn a year earlier）接在后面，形成「本期 vs 去年同期」的对照组。

**❌ 学生初版**
The operating loss increased to Rmb7.3bn from Rmb5bn last year.

**✅ 升级版**
The operating loss expanded to Rmb7.3bn (1.14bn) for the quarter, from Rmb5bn a year earlier.

**💡 效果** 写「成本/亏损/开支扩大」时：*Marketing expenses expanded from 55 to 69 per cent of revenue.* ／ *The deficit expanded to 1.14bn.* *expand from ... to ...* 后可继续加 *a year earlier* / *last year* 做同期对比。
