# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：定语从句——从英国毕业生就业数据学「数据限定式定语从句」

## 你的数据句为什么"有数字、没人"？

批改社会现象类、图表类作文时，最常见的写法是这样的：

**❌ 学生初版**
Many graduates cannot find jobs. The number is high. Young people are badly affected.

问题不在数字本身，在**数字和人群没有挂钩**：12% 是"谁的12%"？受影响的是"哪一群年轻人"？母语记者写新闻时，会用定语从句把每一组数据精确钉在对应的人群上——这正是普通学生和数据型写作者的分水岭。

《金融时报》这篇报道讲2020年第三季度英国应届毕业生失业率达12%、接近过去三年平均水平的两倍，全文仅471词，却几乎每段都有一处"数字/人群 + 定语从句"。拆三个最实用的给你。

## FT原文示范

> The Office for National Statistics said 12 per cent of recent graduates were unemployed in the third quarter of 2020, almost double the average rate for this group over the past three years.

> The figures show the divide that is opening up between people who were already established in jobs before Covid-19 hit — many of whom have been shielded from unemployment by the government's furlough scheme — and those struggling to begin their careers while hiring remains depressed and large swaths of the economy closed.

> The proportion of graduates who were overqualified for their job had also fallen sharply over the past year, the ONS said.

中文对照：

> 英国国家统计署表示，2020年第三季度有12%的应届毕业生失业，是这一人群之前三年平均失业率的近两倍。

> 数据显示，在两个人群之间出现了一道鸿沟：一边是新冠疫情爆发前已经在职场站稳脚跟的人们（他们中的许多人因政府的强制休假计划而免于失业），另一边是难以开始职业生涯的人们。

> 国家统计署表示，过去一年里，学历超出职位要求的毕业生比例也急剧下降。

## 技巧1：比例名词 + who 从句——先给"分母"，再切"子群"

**结构公式：** `The [比例/数量名词] of [人群] who [条件] + [趋势/结论]`

**FT例句：**
> The proportion of graduates who were overqualified for their job had also fallen sharply over the past year.

主语不是干巴巴的 a number，而是 The proportion of graduates who were overqualified——"哪一类毕业生、有什么特征"全部用who从句钉在名词后面，主句最后才说变化（had fallen sharply）。数据有了定语，读者才知道这个比例指谁。

**❌ 学生初版**
The number of overqualified workers went down. Many workers have jobs that are too easy for them.

**✅ 升级版**
The proportion of workers who hold degrees far above their job requirements has fallen sharply over the past decade.

**💡 效果**
升级版把"学历远高于岗位要求"这个复杂条件压缩进who从句，主句干净利落。数据作文最忌数字悬空——先给人群定语，再报数字变化。

**考场模板：**
- `The proportion/share of [人群] who [条件] [动词+趋势]`
- 例：The share of teenagers who read paper books has dropped from 70 to 40 per cent in five years.

## 技巧2：鸿沟对比式——between people who…and those…把两群人一次说清

**结构公式：** `[数据/图表] show(s) the divide between [A人群 who 条件] and [B人群 V-ing/who 条件]`

**FT例句：**
> The figures show the divide that is opening up between people who were already established in jobs before Covid-19 hit — many of whom have been shielded from unemployment by the government's furlough scheme — and those struggling to begin their careers.

写"差距"主题（贫富差距、城乡差距、代际差距）的作文，学生只会 The rich are richer, the poor are poorer。FT用一个 between…and… 把鸿沟两侧各撑开：A侧用who从句给条件，B侧用V-ing分词短语（those struggling to begin their careers）给处境，对比一眼看完。

**❌ 学生初版**
Some people have jobs. Other people cannot find jobs. The gap is getting bigger.

**✅ 升级版**
The data show the divide that is widening between workers who joined the labour market before the crisis and those graduating into a frozen job market.

**💡 效果**
"鸿沟两侧各挂一个条件"是雅思大作文对比段的骨架。写城乡教育资源差距、数字鸿沟、代际收入差距时可以直接套，两个人群一次说清。

**考场模板：**
- `The [figure/report] shows the divide between [A who …] and [B who/V-ing …]`
- 例：The survey shows the divide between students who have home internet access and those relying on a shared phone.

## 技巧3：such as + 行业 + which 补注——举完例子再解释一句

**结构公式：** `[现象] reflects [原因] in sectors/industries such as [A] and [B], which [数据/特点]`

**FT例句：**
> The ONS said this could reflect the closure of sectors such as hospitality and recreation, which have a higher proportion of overqualified graduates.

学生举例子只会 for example, restaurants closed。FT把例子嵌进such as里，再用which从句补一句"为什么这个例子能说明问题"——例子不再是孤零零的名词，而是带论证的论据。

**❌ 学生初版**
For example, the hotel industry closed. Many workers there were overqualified.

**✅ 升级版**
The decline reflects the closure of industries such as tourism and retail, which traditionally employ a large share of young workers.

**💡 效果**
which从句补的是"例子与论点之间的连接件"。很多学生举例之后总差一句解释，这个结构直接把解释焊在例子上，论证立刻完整。

**考场模板：**
- `[现象] reflects [原因] in sectors such as [A] and [B], which [数据/特点]`
- 例：The rise of delivery services reflects the growth of platforms such as Meituan and Eleme, which now employ millions of riders.

## 升级锦囊：破折号里塞 many of whom

FT的长句里还有一处进阶操作，值得学有余力的同学拆开看：

> people who were already established in jobs before Covid-19 hit — many of whom have been shielded from unemployment by the government's furlough scheme — and those struggling to begin their careers

在A人群的who从句之后、and B之前，记者用两个破折号夹进一个 many of whom 从句，给A人群补了一条额外信息。句子虽长，层次清楚：先切人群A，再补A的细节，最后切人群B。写对比长句时，可以试着用破折号做一次"中途补充"。

## 针对性练习建议

1. **数据挂钩**：找一句你写过的 There are many…，用"比例名词 + who从句"改写，补上一个真实的百分比。
2. **对比改写**：把"城市学生有课外班，农村学生没有"改写成 between…who…and those… 的鸿沟句式。
3. **举例补注**：写"很多行业受疫情影响"，用 such as + which 补出两个行业，并各解释一句原因。
