# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：句型模仿——写数据只会“increased a lot”？跟FT学4个趋势描述句型

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №184 — 韩国经济第二季度增速创10年新高（2021年7月，452词）

## 为什么学“数据与趋势”

中考、高考、雅思的作文里，数字几乎一定会出现：图表题要描述趋势，议论文要用数字支撑观点，写变化也要交代幅度。学生写数据，通常只有三招：

*The number increased a lot. It is much bigger than before. More and more people are doing this.*

三个问题：第一，数字没有**参照系**——“增长很多”是比谁多、比哪个时期快？第二，数字没有**身份**——谁发布的、什么口径、哪段时间？第三，数字后面没有**判断**——涨了说明什么、接下来会怎样？读者看完只知道“有个数”，不知道“这个数意味着什么”。

今天从《金融时报》一篇报道韩国二季度经济数据的文章里，抽 4 个句型。它们解决一个具体问题：**怎么把数字放进句子，并让数字自己说话**。图表作文、议论文的论证段、任何需要“用事实说话”的段落都能直接移植。

## FT 原文

> South Korea's economy grew **at its fastest pace in a decade** in the second quarter **on the back of** robust exports and rebounding consumption, **but the optimism was damped by** a wave of Covid-19 infections.

> Gross domestic product expanded 5.9 per cent **from a year earlier** in the April-June quarter, **according to** the Bank of Korea, **partly owing to** the low base effect from last year when the country was hit hard by the pandemic.

> Private consumption and government spending rose 3.5 per cent and 3.9 per cent, **respectively**, **quarter on quarter**.

> Daily cases have **topped** 1,000 since early July, **driven by** the highly transmissible Delta variant. Vaccines are also in short supply, **as** just one-third of the population has had at least one dose of vaccine.

> The growth data **are expected to add pressure on** the central bank **to** start tightening monetary policy earlier than expected.

中文大意：在出口强劲和消费反弹的推动下，韩国经济在第二季度创下10年来最快增速，但这一乐观势头被一波新冠感染蒙上阴影。韩国央行的数据显示，今年第二季度 GDP 同比增长 5.9%，部分原因是去年疫情严重冲击造成的低基数效应。私人消费与政府支出环比分别增长 3.5% 和 3.9%。自 7 月初以来单日新增病例已超过 1000 例，由高传染性的 Delta 变种驱动；疫苗也供应不足，只有三分之一的人口接种了至少一剂。预计这组增长数据将给央行带来更大压力，迫使其比预期更早收紧货币政策。

## 句型一：双面数据句 —— on the back of ... but the optimism was damped by ...

**结构公式**

`[主体] grew at its fastest pace in a decade in [时段] on the back of [利好A] and [利好B], but the optimism was damped by [风险]`

**FT 原文**

> South Korea's economy grew at its fastest pace in a decade in the second quarter on the back of robust exports and rebounding consumption, but the optimism was damped by a wave of Covid-19 infections.

**为什么好**

这一句完成了三件事：**给参照系**（at its fastest pace in a decade——不是“很快”，是“十年来最快”）、**给推动力**（on the back of A and B，一个短语带走两个因素，比 because of 更贴合报道体）、**给隐忧**（but the optimism was damped by ...：把“乐观情绪”当成主语让它被“压制”，一个被动句就把转折写成了画面）。学生写“有好有坏”，通常是两句话加一个 But，读起来像清单；FT 把它压成一句，读起来像判断。

**❌ 学生初版**

The economy grew very fast. This is a good thing. But there are also some problems.

**✅ 升级版**

Our school team won at its fastest pace in a decade in the regional contest on the back of daily training and a new coach, but the optimism was damped by two injuries before the final.

**💡 效果**

“增长很快”升级成“十年来最快”，读者立刻有了尺度；“but the optimism was damped by”让转折从“还有问题”变成“乐观被打住”，语气准确得多。

**模板 + 例句**

`[主体] [动词] at its fastest pace in a decade in [时段] on the back of [A] and [B], but the optimism was damped by [风险]`

- 环境话题：The city cut air pollution at its fastest pace in a decade last year on the back of stricter traffic rules and cleaner heating, but the optimism was damped by a rise in construction dust.
- 教育话题：Reading scores improved at their fastest pace in a decade in the district on the back of longer library hours and teacher training, but the optimism was damped by a widening gap between schools.

## 句型二：数据 + 来源 + 限定归因 —— according to ... partly owing to ...

**结构公式**

`[指标] rose / expanded X per cent from a year earlier in [时段], according to [来源], partly owing to [原因]`

**FT 原文**

> Gross domestic product expanded 5.9 per cent from a year earlier in the April-June quarter, according to the Bank of Korea, partly owing to the low base effect from last year when the country was hit hard by the pandemic.

**为什么好**

数字出现在句子的四个位置上，各有分工：紧跟数字的 `from a year earlier` 交代**比较基准**（同比），`according to the Bank of Korea` 给数据**出处**（数据有来源才可信），`partly owing to` 把因果说成**部分原因**——一个 partly 就把话说得克制，不把复杂现象归给单一原因，这正是议论文里最被认可的分寸感。学生常写 The number is 5.9 per cent because of last year，句子没有来源、没有基准、因果说得太满。

**❌ 学生初版**

The GDP grew 5.9 per cent. This is because of last year, when the situation was very bad.

**✅ 升级版**

Online course enrolment rose 42 per cent from a year earlier in the spring term, according to the school's survey, partly owing to the low cost of digital resources when families were cutting spending.

**💡 效果**

一句话里给出数字、基准、来源、限定归因——阅卷老师读到的是“会查资料、会控制语气”的写作者，而不是“只会背数字”的考生。

**模板 + 例句**

`[指标] rose X per cent from a year earlier, according to [来源], partly owing to [原因]`

- 社会话题：Volunteer numbers rose 30 per cent from a year earlier, according to the city's youth centre, partly owing to a shorter training programme.
- 科技话题：Screen time among teenagers rose 18 per cent from a year earlier, according to a national survey, partly owing to the shift of homework on to tablets.

## 句型三：两组数据并列 —— A and B rose X% and Y%, respectively

**结构公式**

`[对象A] and [对象B] rose X per cent and Y per cent, respectively, quarter on quarter / year on year`

**FT 原文**

> Private consumption and government spending rose 3.5 per cent and 3.9 per cent, respectively, quarter on quarter.

**为什么好**

两组数据合成一句，`respectively` 保证顺序一一对应（A 对 X、B 对 Y），`quarter on quarter` 交代比较基准是**环比**——中文里的“同比/环比”，英文就是 `year on year` 和 `quarter on quarter` 这两个短语，写图表作文时几乎必用。学生会写成两句：Consumption rose 3.5%. Government spending rose 3.9%. 意思没错，但节奏散、看不出两者的对比关系。

**❌ 学生初版**

Private consumption rose 3.5 per cent. Government spending rose 3.9 per cent.

**✅ 升级版**

Cycling and walking to school rose 12 per cent and 8 per cent, respectively, year on year, while car use fell for the first time in five years.

**💡 效果**

`respectively` 一句吃掉两组数据，句子因此有了“并列—对照”的骨架，后面接 while 的对比也顺理成章。

**模板 + 例句**

`[A] and [B] rose X per cent and Y per cent, respectively, year on year / quarter on quarter`

- 校园话题：Library visits and club sign-ups rose 20 per cent and 15 per cent, respectively, year on year.
- 健康话题：Fruit and vegetable sales rose 9 per cent and 6 per cent, respectively, quarter on quarter.

## 句型四：趋势的驱动力与下一步 —— driven by ... are expected to add pressure on ... to ...

**结构公式**

`[指标] have topped [数字] since [时间], driven by [原因]` ＋ `[现象], as [分数/比例] ...` ＋ `[数据] are expected to add pressure on [对象] to [动作]`

**FT 原文**

> Daily cases have topped 1,000 since early July, driven by the highly transmissible Delta variant. Vaccines are also in short supply, as just one-third of the population has had at least one dose of vaccine.

> The growth data are expected to add pressure on the central bank to start tightening monetary policy earlier than expected.

**为什么好**

这是“从数据到判断”的三步走。`have topped 1,000 since early July` 用现在完成时表示**持续突破某条线**；`driven by` 用过去分词把原因挂在句尾，比 because of 更像数据报告；`as just one-third of the population ...` 用一个分数交代缺口——写“问题有多严重”用比例，不用形容词。最后一句 `are expected to add pressure on A to do B` 把预测说成“外界预期”，而不是“我认为”，既给出判断又留了余地——学生写结论常是 So the bank will raise rates next month，太满、太绝对。

**❌ 学生初版**

There are more than 1,000 cases every day because of Delta. Vaccines are not enough. So the bank will raise rates next month.

**✅ 升级版**

Late homework has topped 40 per cent since the new term, driven by longer bus journeys, as one-third of students now travel more than an hour each way. The figures are expected to add pressure on the school to adjust the morning timetable earlier than expected.

**💡 效果**

同一条“问题—缺口—压力”的链条，从三个短句升级成两句有层级的句子：数据有驱动力、缺口有比例、判断有出处（expected to），结尾因此不像口号，像分析。

**模板 + 例句**

`[指标] have topped / fallen below [数字] since [时间], driven by [原因]` / `[数据] are expected to add pressure on [对象] to [动作]`

- 环境话题：Plastic waste has topped 100 tonnes a month since spring, driven by online deliveries, as only one in five packaging items is recycled. The trend is expected to add pressure on local stores to charge for bags.
- 科技话题：Screens in classrooms have topped three hours a day since September, driven by digital homework, as half of the lessons now rely on tablets. The findings are expected to add pressure on schools to set screen-free days.

## 练习建议

1. **数据三问**：写下每个数字前先问三件事——参照系（比谁、比哪个时期）、来源（according to）、判断（so what）。三问答不出来的数字，删掉比留着好。
2. **图表改写**：拿一张中考或雅思图表，只用今天的句型写 4 句：第一句双面数据，第二句数字加来源，第三句两组并列，第四句驱动力加预期。写完朗读一遍，检查每句是否有“超过一个信息”。
3. **替换清单**：凡是出现 `a lot`、`more and more`、`very big`、`because of` 的地方，问自己能不能换成 `at its fastest pace in a decade`、`X per cent from a year earlier`、`driven by`、`partly owing to`。
