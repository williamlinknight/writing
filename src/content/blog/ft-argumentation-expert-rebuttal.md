学生议论文最常犯的毛病是什么？不是语法错误，而是**只断言，不论证**。比如写"网络游戏有害"，通篇都是"我认为""我觉得"，没有一个具体证据、没有引用任何权威、更不懂得如何**反驳对立观点**。

真正有说服力的议论文，不是声音大，而是证据硬。今天我们从FT一篇经典报道——美国卫生官员驳斥特朗普关于新冠死亡人数的说法——来学三种**反驳论证**的技巧。

## FT原文示范

> Top US medical officials on Sunday contradicted outgoing President Donald Trump's claims that the country's escalating Covid-19 death figures had been grossly exaggerated.
>
> As the number of fatalities from coronavirus rose above 340,000 over the weekend, Mr Trump dismissed the figures as "fake news", claiming the method used by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention to count cases was out of step with other countries.
>
> In an interview on NBC's Meet the Press, **Anthony Fauci, the top US infectious disease expert**, said the numbers of Covid-19 deaths **"are real"** and that the evidence can be seen in hospitals across the country.
>
> **Speaking on CNN, Dr Jerome Adams, the US surgeon general**, also said there was **"no reason to doubt those numbers"** of Covid-19 deaths.
>
> The US had recorded 341,746 Covid-19 deaths as of January 2, according to the Covid Tracking Project.

> 美国医疗体系高官驳斥了特朗普的说法——美国新冠死亡人数被严重夸大了。特朗普将这些数字斥为"假新闻"。但美国顶级传染病专家福奇表示死亡数字"是真实的"，卫生总署负责人亚当斯也称"没有任何理由怀疑这些数字"。根据"新冠追踪项目"，截至1月2日，美国新冠死亡病例达到341,746例。

## 技巧一：对立观点+反驳的结构公式

很多学生写议论文只知道讲自己的观点，忽略了最关键的一步——**先呈现对立方的主张，再予以反驳**。FT的写法是：

> **结构公式：** \[某人/某方] claimed that \[对立观点] + but/in fact/however \[你的反驳]

看原文是怎么做的：

> Top US medical officials **contradicted** ... Trump's **claims that** the country's escalating Covid-19 death figures had been grossly exaggerated.

先用 `contradicted X's claims that...` 清楚交代对立方的主张（死亡数字被夸大），然后才进入反驳。这个开头的妙处在于：**读者先看到了一方主张，紧接着看到它被推翻，冲击力远比直接说"不对"要大。**

**❌ 学生初版**
Some people think online games are harmful. But I think they are not.

**✅ 升级版**
Some parents have **claimed that** online games serve no educational value. Yet research from top psychologists **has contradicted** this view, showing that strategy games can improve problem-solving skills.

**💡 效果**
升级版先承认"确实有人这么认为"（claimed that），再用权威研究反驳（contradicted），而不是简单地"I think"——读起来更客观、更有力。

> **考场模板：**
> Critics have **claimed that** [对立观点]. However, recent studies **have contradicted** this assumption by showing that [你的证据].

## 技巧二：用权威身份为反驳背书

光说"这是错的"没用，你得告诉读者**谁说的**。FT在引用福奇时，特意加上了身份标签：

> **Anthony Fauci, the top US infectious disease expert**, said the numbers of Covid-19 deaths **"are real"**

注意两个关键细节：

1. **同位语身份标注**：`Anthony Fauci, the top US infectious disease expert`——不是随便一个路人甲，是**顶级传染病专家**。这个身份让他的反驳天然具有分量。
2. **直接引语**：`"are real"`——用引号锁定核心观点，比间接转述更有冲击力。

**❌ 学生初版**
Many experts say that exercise is good for health.

**✅ 升级版**
**Dr Wang, a leading cardiologist at Peking University**, has stated that regular exercise "significantly reduces the risk of heart disease".

**💡 效果**
升级版给出了专家的全名、身份（leading cardiologist）和机构（Peking University），并用直接引语锁定了核心结论。读者一看就知道：这不是随便谁说的，是**这个领域的权威**说的。

> **考场模板：**
> [Expert Name], **[credentials/position] at [institution]**, has stated that [direct quote of key claim].

## 技巧三：多重权威叠加——从"一个人说"到"所有人都说"

FT没有只用一个专家就结束论证。它用了**两个不同的权威机构代表**：

> Anthony Fauci, the top US infectious disease expert, said ... **"are real"**
>
> Speaking on CNN, Dr Jerome Adams, the US surgeon general, also said there was **"no reason to doubt those numbers"**

福奇说了"数字是真实的"，亚当斯说"没有理由怀疑"——两人从不同角度说了同一件事。这就是**多重权威叠加**：不是"一个人说"，而是"两个人都说"。效果是1+1>2。

原文中还有一个隐藏的第三层支持——数据：

> The US had recorded 341,746 Covid-19 deaths as of January 2, according to the Covid Tracking Project.

专家 → 另一专家 → 硬数据。三层叠加，反驳牢不可破。

**❌ 学生初版**
Reading is good for students.

**✅ 升级版**
**Education experts have consistently warned** that declining reading rates among teenagers could have long-term consequences. **Professor Zhang, a literacy researcher at Beijing Normal University**, noted that students who read regularly score "significantly higher" on comprehension tests. **Official statistics from the Ministry of Education** confirm this trend, showing a direct correlation between reading time and academic performance.

**💡 效果**
三段递进：先总说"教育专家警告"，再具体到一位教授和她的直接引语，最后用官方数据收尾。论证层层递进，从"有人说"到"数据证明"。

## 写作建议

用专家反驳论证时，记住三个要点：

1. **先立靶子再打**：用 `claimed that` 先呈现对立观点，再用权威反驳
2. **身份就是武器**：一定要标明专家的头衔和机构——`the top X at Y`
3. **一锤不够加两锤**：两个专家 + 一组数据，论证无懈可击

下次写议论文，别再说"I think"了。学会用专家和证据说话。
