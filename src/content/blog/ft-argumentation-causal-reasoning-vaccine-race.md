# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：论证方法——因果推演与条件假设，让论证滴水不漏

学生议论文最常见的硬伤是什么？不是语法，而是**论证只做一步**：写了"因为A，所以B"就收笔，从不往前推演后果，也从不用假设条件把风险量化。结果整篇文章读起来像喊口号——"环境污染很严重""科技发展很快"，观点有了，推理没有。

今天我们从《金融时报》一篇关于英国阻击变种病毒的报道里，学三种**推演型论证**手法。它们的特点是：不直接下结论，而是把推理过程摆在读者面前，让读者自己得出结论——这样的论证，比十个形容词都有力。

## FT原文

> The UK is in a race to tackle the spread of a coronavirus variant first identified in India through rapid vaccination, ministers declared on Wednesday, as they expressed increasing confidence that jabs were effective against all strains of the disease.
>
> Fast growth in cases of the B.1.617.2 variant in several locations across England, including Bolton and Blackburn with Darwen, has cast doubt on whether the government can stick to its plan to lift all coronavirus restrictions by June 21.
>
> Health secretary Matt Hancock said the number of cases of the variant in England had increased to 2,967, up 27 per cent since he outlined previous figures on Monday.
>
> Professor Neil Ferguson, a leading epidemiologist at Imperial College London, said there was a "glimmer of hope" from initial data on the transmissibility of the B.1.617.2 variant.
>
> "While this variant does still appear to have a significant growth advantage, the magnitude of that advantage seems to have dropped a little bit with the most recent data," he told the BBC.
>
> Ferguson added the level of transmissibility would be critical in deciding when restrictions could be eased. "It's much easier to deal with 20 to 30 per cent than it would be 50 per cent or more," he said, adding the government's plans to lift all restrictions by June 21 were "very much in the balance".

## 中文对照

> 英国的大臣们宣布，英国正加紧通过迅速推广疫苗接种，来阻击一种在印度首次发现的新冠病毒变种。B.1.617.2变种病例在英国多地快速增加，使外界怀疑英国政府还能否坚持6月21日解除一切疫情防控措施的计划。
>
> 卫生大臣汉考克表示，英国受这种变种病毒感染的病例数已升至2967，与周一公布的数据相比增加了27%。
>
> 帝国理工学院流行病学教授尼尔•弗格森表示，从初步数据来看存在"一线希望"。他说："虽然这种变种看起来仍具有显著的增长优势，但根据最新数据，这种优势的程度似乎略有下降。"
>
> 弗格森补充称，传播性水平将是决定何时放松限制的关键。"应对20%到30%（的传播优势）要比应对50%或更高容易得多。"

## 手法一：条件假设推演（if…would）

看这句：

> if the B.1.617.2 variant proved 50 per cent more transmissible than the B.1.1.7 strain that last year took hold in the UK, as some data have suggested, it would cause major disruption to the plans to end all restrictions

记者没有直接断言"这种变种很危险"，而是架设了一个**可检验的假设**：如果传播力高出50%，就会造成什么后果。数字（50%）让假设可以验证，结论（严重扰乱解封计划）让后果清晰可见。读者读完自己就能判断风险有多大——这就是推演的力量。

**❌ 学生初版**

The new variant is very dangerous and will cause serious problems.

**✅ 升级版**

If the new variant proved 50 per cent more transmissible than the previous strain, it would cause major disruption to the government's plans.

**💡 效果**

从"喊口号"变成"可检验的推理"。考官看到的不是情绪，而是逻辑链条。

**模板**

If [X] proved [数字] per cent more [形容词] than [Y], it would [后果]

**例句**

If online learning proved 30 per cent less effective than classroom teaching, it would widen the gap between rich and poor students.

## 手法二：数字 + 变化率 + 时间参照

看这句：

> the number of cases of the variant in England had increased to 2,967, up 27 per cent since he outlined previous figures on Monday

单说"2967例"没有冲击力——读者不知道这算多还是少。FT加了两个参照：**变化率**（up 27 per cent）和**时间跨度**（since Monday）。三天涨27%，读者立刻感受到"快"。这就是把静态数字变成动态证据的三段式。

**❌ 学生初版**

The number of cases is increasing quickly.

**✅ 升级版**

The number of cases had increased to 2,967, up 27 per cent since Monday.

**💡 效果**

"数字 + 变化率 + 时间参照"让数据自己说话，比"quickly"这种模糊副词有力一百倍。

**模板**

[N] had increased to [X], up [Y] per cent since [时间]

**例句**

The number of students joining the reading club had increased to 800, up 15 per cent since last term.

## 手法三：让步修正（While…承认 + 新证据…修正）

看这句：

> While this variant does still appear to have a significant growth advantage, the magnitude of that advantage seems to have dropped a little bit with the most recent data

这是论证中最成熟的一招：**先承认反例，再用新证据修正判断**。弗格森没有全盘否定变种的危险（"仍然有显著增长优势"），而是指出"优势在缩小"（"magnitude…seems to have dropped"）。这样的论证既诚实又坚定——读者会相信一个敢于承认反例的人。

**❌ 学生初版**

The variant is not a big problem now.

**✅ 升级版**

While this variant still appears to have a significant advantage, the magnitude of that advantage seems to have dropped with the most recent data.

**💡 效果**

先退一步再修正，读者感受到的是客观和分寸感，而不是偏执。

**模板**

While [X] does still appear to [正面表述], the [程度] of [X] seems to have [下降/变化] with [新证据]

**例句**

While smartphones do still appear to dominate teenagers' lives, the amount of time spent on them seems to have dropped with the return to school.

## 写作建议

这三种手法的共同点是：**把推理过程写出来，而不是只写结论**。下次写议论文，试着给自己的论点做三次升级——能不能加一个 if 假设？能不能给数字配上变化率和时间？能不能承认一个反例再修正它？每次升级，论证就硬一分。
