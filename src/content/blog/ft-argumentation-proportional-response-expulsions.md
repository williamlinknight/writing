学生写反驳段，最常见的两句话是：This is unfair and wrong. We must strongly oppose it.

这两句话的问题不是不礼貌，而是**没有刻度**。反对到什么程度？按哪一条反对？对方做了什么、我方回应到哪一步为止？读者读完只知道你生气，不知道你对。

《金融时报》这篇 486 词的短稿是一个很好的样本：三国各自驱逐一名俄罗斯外交官，全文没有一句情绪化的形容词，但"反对"的分量写得很清楚——**一换一、按公约、把选择交还对方**。今天我们拆这 4 个动作。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №78 — 《欧洲三国驱逐俄罗斯外交官》

## FT 原文（节选）

> Germany, Poland and Sweden said they would each expel one Russian diplomat in retaliation for Russia's expulsion of three European diplomats last week.

> On Monday, Germany's foreign ministry said it would expel one member of Russia's embassy in Berlin, arguing Moscow's initial expulsions last Friday were not justified.

> "The German diplomat concerned was simply carrying out his duties as set out in the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations to inform himself by lawful means about local developments," a spokesperson said.

> Sweden's foreign minister Ann Linde said on Twitter that the diplomatic expulsion was "a clear response to the unacceptable decision to expel a Swedish diplomat who was only performing his duties".

> "Russia gave us no choice," she told Swedish journalists.

> Russia's foreign ministry criticised the reciprocal expulsions and reiterated their accusation that the EU countries were seeking to meddle in Russian affairs.

## 中文对照（节选）

> 德国、波兰和瑞典表示将各自驱逐一名俄罗斯外交官，以报复俄罗斯上周驱逐三名欧洲外交官。

> 周一，德国外交部表示将驱逐俄罗斯驻德国大使馆的一名成员，并表示莫斯科方面上周五的驱逐没有正当理由。

> 德国外交部发言人表示："相关德国外交人员只是在履行《维也纳外交关系公约》(Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations)规定的职责，通过合法手段了解当地的事态发展。"

> 瑞典外交部长安•林德(Ann Linde)在Twitter上表示，外交驱逐是"对驱逐一名只是在履职的瑞典外交官这一不可接受的决定的明确回应"。

> 她对瑞典记者说："俄罗斯没有给我们选择的余地。"

> 俄罗斯外交部批评了欧洲三国的报复性驱逐，并再次指责欧盟国家试图干涉俄罗斯事务。

## 动作一：一换一——反制的**数字**要先写出来

**FT 原文**

> Germany, Poland and Sweden said they would each expel one Russian diplomat in retaliation for Russia's expulsion of three European diplomats last week.

**为什么好**

- `each expel one ... in retaliation for ... three`：对方驱逐三名，我方**各**驱逐一名。三个国家合起来也是三名，但落笔是 `each one`——分寸感在这个词上；
- `in retaliation for` 把原因用介词短语挂在动词后面，一句话交代了"谁做什么、为什么、为了回应哪一件事"；
- 全句没有 `angry`、`strongly`、`unacceptable` 这类词。**力度不用形容词，用数字。**

**❌ 学生初版**

> These countries were very angry, so they decided to fight back strongly against Russia.

**✅ 升级版**

> Germany, Poland and Sweden said they would each expel one Russian diplomat in retaliation for Russia's expulsion of three European diplomats last week.

**💡 效果**

`each ... one` 与 `three` 对看，读者马上明白这是**对等**而不是升级。凡是写"回应、反击、处罚"，先问一句：对方的动作是几分，我的回应是几分？写得出数字，你的立场就有了刻度。

**模板**

> [主体 A、B、C] said they would each [动作] one [对象] in retaliation for [对方]'s [动作] of [数字] [对象].

**仿写示例**（校园 / 社会话题）

> The school said it would suspend one club activity for a week in retaliation for the repeated misuse of the lab, rather than cancel the whole science fair.

## 动作二：援引成文规则——把「没道理」换成「不合哪一条」

**FT 原文**

> On Monday, Germany's foreign ministry said it would expel one member of Russia's embassy in Berlin, arguing Moscow's initial expulsions last Friday were not justified.

> "The German diplomat concerned was simply carrying out his duties as set out in the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations to inform himself by lawful means about local developments," a spokesperson said.

**为什么好**

- `arguing ... were not justified`：先给**定性**（不正当），再给**依据**（公约）。顺序不能反——先讲道理再下判断，读者才跟得上；
- `was simply carrying out his duties as set out in the Vienna Convention`：`simply` 是这次的关键词，意思是"他只是在做本职"，把对方的指控缩小到"连本职都不许做"的荒谬程度；
- `as set out in` + 公约全名：**把标准的名字写全**。规则一旦有名字，就不是"我觉得"，而是"文件上写着"。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Germany said Russia's decision was totally unreasonable and had no basis.

**✅ 升级版**

> Germany said it would expel one member of Russia's embassy, arguing that Moscow's expulsions were not justified: the diplomat was simply carrying out his duties as set out in the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations.

**💡 效果**

`unreasonable` 是评价，`not justified` + 公约名是论证。考试作文里写"这不合理"，阅卷老师只能给你一个词的分；写出**哪一条规则、怎么被违反**，才拿得到论证的分。

**模板**

> [主体] said [对方]'s [行为] was not justified, arguing that [对象] was simply [本职动作] as set out in [规则 / 公约 / 校规 / 合同第 N 条].

**仿写示例**（校园话题）

> Students argued that the fine was not justified, pointing out that they were simply using the reading room as set out in the library rules.

## 动作三：「别无选择」——四个词把责任交还对方

**FT 原文**

> "Russia gave us no choice," she told Swedish journalists.

**为什么好**

这五个词（`Russia gave us no choice`）做了三件事：**主体是 Russia**（谁造成的）、**动词是 gave**（不是我方挑起）、**结果是 no choice**（我方是被动的）。整句话没有一个形容词，但"我们不是挑衅者"这个立场已经站稳了。

对比一下同篇里瑞典外长的另一句：`a clear response to the unacceptable decision to expel a Swedish diplomat who was only performing his duties`——`unacceptable` 是情绪，`only performing his duties` 才是依据。**两句合起来是一段完整的回应：先定性，再声明被动。**

**❌ 学生初版**

> We had no other way, so we had to do this. It is all their fault.

**✅ 升级版**

> "Russia gave us no choice," the minister said, describing the expulsion as a response to a decision that left a diplomat with no option but to do his job.

**💡 效果**

`We had no other way` 是自我辩解，`Russia gave us no choice` 是**归责**。同一个意思，主语换一下，从"我不得不"变成"你逼的"。写道歉、写处罚、写被迫的决定，这个句式都能用。

**模板**

> "[主体] gave us no choice," [说话人] said, describing [我方动作] as a response to [对方行为].

**仿写示例**（家庭 / 学校话题）

> "The deadline gave us no choice," the organiser said, describing the cancellation as a response to a decision taken without consulting the team.

## 动作四：并置对方版本——以及一条「反面教材」

**FT 原文**

> Russia's foreign ministry criticised the reciprocal expulsions and reiterated their accusation that the EU countries were seeking to meddle in Russian affairs.

> "Today's decisions made by Poland, Germany, and Sweden are baseless and unfriendly and come as a follow-up to the string of steps and actions which have been undertaken by the West against our country and which we qualify as interference in our internal affairs," Maria Zakharova, the ministry's spokeswoman, told state TV.

**为什么好（与怎么用）**

好的部分是**并置**：报道先写完三国的理由（公约、履职），再完整给出俄方的说法（`reiterated their accusation`），让读者自己比较。写作时同理——写完自己的论证，用一句 `Critics, however, argue that ...` 把对方最强的版本摆出来，比假装对方不存在更有说服力。

要小心的是第二句：`baseless and unfriendly` 是**贴标签**，后面用 `string of steps and actions` 含糊带过，没有给出任何可核查的事实。这是一种典型写法，但它**不是论证**。

**⚠️ 反面教材**

Today's decisions made by Poland, Germany, and Sweden are baseless and unfriendly

这类句子在考试作文里几乎拿不到分：`baseless`（没有依据）需要用依据来证明，用形容词宣布"你没有依据"，恰好证明了自己也没有依据。

**✅ 升级版**

> The Russian ministry criticised the expulsions and repeated its accusation that the three countries were meddling in its internal affairs—a claim the EU rejects, since the diplomats were accredited and acting under the Vienna Convention.

**模板**

> [对方] criticised [我方动作] and reiterated [对方的说法]. [我方] rejects the claim, since [可核查的事实].

**仿写示例**（社区话题）

> The company criticised the residents' complaint and repeated its claim that the building work was fully approved—a claim the residents reject, since the approval letter covers only the first three floors.

## 写作建议：回应段四问

写任何"我要反驳 / 我要回应"的段落，先过四道关：

1. 对方做了什么，**数字**是多少？我的回应是几分？（`each expel one ... for three`）
2. 我把对方的行为**定性**了吗？依据是**哪一条**？（`not justified` + `as set out in the Vienna Convention`）
3. 我有没有说清**谁造成的**？（`Russia gave us no choice`）
4. 我有没有把**对方的版本**摆出来，再用事实回应？（而不是用 `baseless` 贴标签）

四问过完，你的反驳就从"我很生气"变成了"我按规则、按对等的分量回应"。这也是雅思 Task 2 的反方段、高考读后续写的人物冲突段最缺的东西：**不是态度不够强，是刻度不够细**。

## 练习建议

- 找一道"该不该处罚"的题目（学生带手机进校园被发现三次），写四句话：① 对方/校方做了什么、几次 ② 我的回应几分（停课一天而不是开除）③ 依据是哪一条校规 ④ 把校方的理由摆出来再回应。
- 把 `unreasonable` 换成 `not justified`，把 `very angry` 换成 `in retaliation for`，把 `we had no choice` 换成 `[对方] gave us no choice`——三处替换，段落立刻从情绪变成分寸。
- 和配套的"权衡论证"一起读：权衡回答**值不值**，立标准回答**合不合规**，对等回应回答**回应到哪一步为止**——一篇高分议论文，三者往往同时出现。
