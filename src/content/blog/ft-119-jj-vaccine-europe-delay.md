很多同学写因果句，只会写 "A led to B" 或 "Because A, B"。句子语法没错，但像连句子，没有论证的厚度。

今天我们用FT一篇关于强生推迟在欧洲推出新冠疫苗的文章，拆解3个可以直接用在作文里的技巧：

1. **因果链写法**：把“事件 → 直接后果 → 背景期望 → 根本原因”串成一条完整的链。
2. **数字比例**：数字不单独出现，要成组出现，建立比例关系，让读者感受规模轻重。
3. **机构表态模板**：承认问题 → 说明立场 → 补足行动，可直接移植到应用文和议论文。

---

## 技巧一：因果链写法 — 不只写“A导致B”

> **原文**：The delay to the planned rollout in Europe is another setback for the EU, which had hoped J&J's vaccine would enable member states to accelerate sluggish vaccination campaigns following months of squeezed supply and logistical problems.

**为什么好**：句子里藏着一个完整的因果链：强生推迟推出（事件） → 欧盟遭遇挫折（直接后果） → 成员国原本想借此加速接种（背景期望） → 月来的供应紧张和物流问题（根本原因）。写作时别只写“A导致B”，要把背景和根因补齐，论证才饱满。

**学生写法**：
> J&J delayed its vaccine in Europe. The EU vaccination campaign slowed down.

**升级写法**：
> The delay to the planned rollout in Europe **is another setback for the EU**, which **had hoped** J&J's vaccine **would enable** member states **to accelerate** sluggish vaccination campaigns following months of squeezed supply and logistical problems.

**模板**：
> [事件或延迟] **is another setback for** [主体]，which **had hoped** [计划] **would enable** [行动] following [背景原因]。

**造句练习**：
> The school's decision to cancel the exchange program **is another setback for** the students, which **had hoped** the trip **would improve** their English following months of pandemic restrictions.
> (学校取消交换生项目的决定对学生们是又一次打击，学生们原本希望这次旅行能在疫情限制数月后提高他们的英语水平。)

## 技巧二：数字比例 — 数字成组出现，建立比例关系

> **原文**：J&J was due to supply 55m of the 360m doses that the EU is expecting to receive in the second quarter. Since J&J is a single-shot vaccine — the other approved Covid-19 vaccines require two doses — 55m shots are enough to immunise just over a quarter of the 207m people the bloc hopes to vaccinate between April and June.

**为什么好**：数字不单独出现，而是成组出现：55m 对 360m；单剂疫苗对双剂疫苗；55m 针对 207m 的四分之一。数字之间建立比例关系，读者才能感受到规模的轻重。作文里的数据也要学会“对着比”写，而不是只罗列。

**学生写法**：
> J&J would supply 55 million doses. The EU expected 360 million doses. 55 million shots can vaccinate more than a quarter of 207 million people.

**升级写法**：
> J&J **was due to supply** 55m of the 360m doses that the EU **is expecting** to receive in the second quarter. Since J&J **is a single-shot vaccine** — the other approved Covid-19 vaccines **require** two doses — 55m shots **are enough to immunise just over a quarter of** the 207m people the bloc hopes to vaccinate between April and June.

**模板**：
> [A] **was due to** [具体数字] of the [总量] that [主体] **is expecting**; since [特殊条件], [数字] **are enough to** [结果]。

**造句练习**：
> The company **was due to donate** 200 of the 1,000 computers that the school **is expecting**; since each student **needs** only one device, 200 computers **are enough for** one fifth of the 1,000 students.
> (该公司本应捐赠学校期望收到的1000台电脑中的200台；由于每个学生只需要一台设备，200台电脑足够覆盖1000名学生的五分之一。)

## 技巧三：机构表态模板 — 承认 → 立场 → 行动

> **原文**："We are aware that thromboembolic events including those with thrombocytopenia have been reported with Covid-19 vaccines," J&J said in a statement. "At present, no clear causal relationship has been established between these rare events and the Janssen [J&J] Covid-19 vaccine," it said, adding that it was working closely with experts and regulators to assess the data.

**为什么好**：机构回应争议时的标准三段式：先承认“我们知道有这类报告”（We are aware that...），再说立场“目前未确定因果关系”（At present, no clear causal relationship has been established...），最后补行动“正在与专家合作评估”（adding that it was working closely with...）。这套表达可以直接移植到应用文（如道歉声明、致歉信）和议论文的反方论述里。

**学生写法**：
> The company said they knew about the problem. They said there is no proof. They are working with experts.

**升级写法**：
> "We **are aware that** [问题] **have been reported**," [机构] **said in a statement**. "**At present**, [否定结论]," it said, **adding that it was working closely with** [合作方] **to** [行动]。

**模板**：
> "We **are aware that** [现象] **have been reported**," [主体] **said**. "**At present**, [立场或结论]."

**造句练习**：
> "We **are aware that** delivery delays **have been reported**," the online store **said**. "**At present**, no system failure **has been identified**," it said, **adding that it was working closely with** the logistics company **to** solve the problem.
> ("我们知道有配送延误的报告，"该网店表示。"目前未发现系统故障，"它表示，并称正在与物流公司密切合作解决问题。)

## 总结

| 技巧 | 适用场景 | 替换或提升对象 |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 因果链写法 | 议论文因果段、社会现象分析 | 只写“A导致B” |
| 数字比例 | 描述规模、政策影响 | 孤立罗列数字 |
| 机构表态模板 | 应用文声明、议论文反方 | 口语化的立场表述 |

下次写关于公共事件、社会问题的文章时，试着用上其中一个。让你的因果论证有厚度，让你的数字有说服力。
