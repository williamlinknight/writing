## 为什么这篇值得学？

一项1.9万亿美元的经济刺激法案——FT记者如何在一篇500字的报道中，既讲清楚法案内容，又呈现各方立场，还做出影响预测？答案是：**精准的动词和名词搭配**。

报道中反复出现的 `seal approval`、`mitigate fallout`、`reboot the economy`、`trigger a jump` 这类搭配，正是雅思大作文、高考议论文中"分析政策影响"场景最需要的表达。它们比学生惯用的 `make the economy better` 或 `help people` 精确得多。

---

## 原文精选

> Joe Biden's 1.9tn coronavirus relief package received final backing from the US House of Representatives on Wednesday, **sealing congressional approval** for a massive fiscal stimulus with far-reaching political and economic consequences that will last for years.

> The final greenlight from Congress marks a big victory for the US president, who took office in January with a mission to **mitigate fallout** from the coronavirus pandemic and **reboot the US economy**.

> The bill reflects a belief among Biden and his top aides that they can rapidly accelerate the recovery without **triggering an unwanted jump** in inflation.

> "This legislation is about **giving the backbone of this nation** — the essential workers, the working people who built this country, the people who keep this country going — **a fighting chance**."

> Republicans attacked the legislation as **excessive spending** that was stuffed with pet projects. "It just throws out money **without accountability**," Kevin McCarthy, the Republican leader in the House, said.

## 中文对照

> 周三，乔•拜登(Joe Biden)的1.9万亿美元新冠疫情救助方案获得了美国众议院(House of Representatives)的最后支持，为这项影响深远的、具有长期政治和经济影响的大规模财政刺激措施扫清了国会批准的障碍。

> 国会最终亮绿灯，标志着这位美国总统的重大胜利。拜登于今年1月就职，肩负着减轻新冠疫情冲击和重启美国经济的使命。

> 该法案反映了一种信念——拜登及其高级助手们相信，他们可以加速经济复苏，而不会引发令人不快的通胀飙升。

> "这项立法是关于给这个国家的脊梁——那些基本工人、建设了这个国家的劳动人民、那些让这个国家继续运转的人——一个奋斗的机会。"

> 共和党人抨击这一立法是充斥着左翼支持的面子项目的过度支出。众议院共和党领袖凯文•麦卡锡(Kevin McCarthy)表示："这只是在浪费钱，没有任何问责。"

## 高分表达解析

### 1. seal (congressional) approval — "锁定（国会）批准"

**原文出处**：*"…**sealing congressional approval** for a massive fiscal stimulus with far-reaching political and economic consequences."*

**为什么好**：学生写"获得批准"只会用 `was approved` 或 `got permission`。`seal` 本意是"封口、密封"，引申为"使某事确定无疑"。`seal approval` 比 `get approval` 多了一层"最终敲定、板上钉钉"的意味。用于议论文中描述一个决定最终落地，非常有力量。

**❌ 学生初版**
The plan was approved by the government and everyone was happy.

**✅ 升级版**
After months of negotiation, the committee finally **sealed approval** for the ambitious infrastructure project.

**💡 效果**
`seal` 暗示谈判过程的艰难和结果的不可逆转——这让一句话就有了叙事张力。

**模拟例句**
The board meeting **sealed approval** for the merger, marking the end of a six-month battle between the two companies.

---

### 2. mitigate fallout — "减轻后果/冲击"

**原文出处**：*"…who took office with a mission to **mitigate fallout** from the coronavirus pandemic and reboot the US economy."*

**为什么好**：`mitigate` 比 `reduce` 正式得多，常用于正式分析和学术写作（雅思大作文的高频词）。`fallout` 原指"放射性尘埃"，引申为"负面后果"。`mitigate fallout` 这个搭配精准到几乎无法被替代——学生用 `reduce the bad effects` 表达同样的意思，但字数翻倍、力度减半。

**❌ 学生初版**
The government should take action to reduce the bad effects of pollution.

**✅ 升级版**
Policymakers must act quickly to **mitigate the economic fallout** of the global recession.

**💡 效果**
一个精准搭配（mitigate + fallout）代替了三个宽泛词（reduce + bad + effects），这就是"用词精准"的本质。

**模拟例句**
Schools introduced mental health programmes to **mitigate the social fallout** of prolonged lockdowns on teenagers.

---

### 3. reboot the economy — "重启经济"

**原文出处**：*"…a mission to mitigate fallout from the coronavirus pandemic and **reboot the US economy**."*

**为什么好**：`reboot` 是一个从电脑术语借用的词（重启电脑），用在经济语境中，暗示经济像一台"死机"的电脑一样需要重新启动。学生写 `make the economy grow again` 虽然没错，但缺乏这种隐喻的新鲜感。雅思写作中，恰当使用一个技术隐喻可以瞬间提升语言层次。

**❌ 学生初版**
The government wants to make the economy grow again after the crisis.

**✅ 升级版**
The stimulus package was designed to **reboot an economy** battered by months of shutdowns and supply chain disruptions.

**💡 效果**
`reboot` 比 `revive` 或 `recover` 更具体——它暗示系统需要从基础层面重新启动，而非自然恢复。

**模拟例句**
The new tourism campaign aims to **reboot** the local economy after the devastating earthquake.

---

### 4. trigger an unwanted jump — "引发不必要的飙升"

**原文出处**：*"…without **triggering an unwanted jump** in inflation."*

**为什么好**：这个表达可以拆成三部分学：
- `trigger` — "引发"（比 `cause` 更强，暗示"扳机扣动"）
- `unwanted` — "不必要的"（比 `bad` 更精确，暗示"本可以避免"）
- `jump` — "飙升"（比 `increase` 更具体）

三个词组合在一起，比学生常用的 `cause a bad increase` 精确了不止一个档次。在分析政策的利弊时，这种"动词+修饰语+名词"的搭配链非常实用。

**❌ 学生初版**
The new policy might cause some bad effects like higher prices.

**✅ 升级版**
Critics warned that the tax cuts could **trigger an unwanted jump** in housing prices, making homes unaffordable for first-time buyers.

**💡 效果**
每个词都承担了特定功能：`trigger` 表因果，`unwanted` 表态度，`jump` 表程度——这是真正成熟的写作。

**模拟例句**
The sudden influx of tourists **triggered an unwanted jump** in rental costs, forcing many local residents to move out.

---

### 5. a fighting chance — "奋斗的机会、一线生机"

**原文出处**：*"This legislation is about giving…**a fighting chance**."*

**为什么好**：学生表达"机会"永远用 `opportunity` 或 `chance`。`a fighting chance` 是一个地道习语，意为"经过努力才能获得的、成败难料的机会"——它不是天上掉下来的好运，而是需要拼搏才能抓住的可能。用在议论文的结论段，比 `a good chance` 有力得多。

**❌ 学生初版**
This policy gives young people an opportunity to succeed.

**✅ 升级版**
By reducing tuition fees, the programme aims to give students from low-income families **a fighting chance** in the competitive job market.

**💡 效果**
`fighting` 一词暗示了"需要努力"和"前景不确定"两层含义——这种复杂性是 `opportunity` 无法传达的。

**模拟例句**
Despite the team's losing streak, the coach believed they still had **a fighting chance** to make the playoffs.

## 写作建议

这五个表达的背后，有一条共同逻辑：**高级英语写作 = 具体动词 + 精准搭配 + 适度隐喻。**

中国学生常犯的错误是"翻译式写作"——先把中文想好，再逐词翻译成英文。结果是每个词都正确，但组合起来不像英语。

读FT这类外刊，最重要的是学习**记者如何搭配词语**：
- 为什么是 `seal approval` 而不是 `get approval`？
- 为什么是 `mitigate fallout` 而不是 `reduce bad results`？
- 为什么是 `reboot the economy` 而不是 `make economy better`？

每发现一个这样的"固定搭配"，记下来，下次写作时主动使用。坚持一个月，你的用词就会从"正确但平淡"变成"地道且有力"。
