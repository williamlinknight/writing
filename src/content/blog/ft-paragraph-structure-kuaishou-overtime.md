英语作文常见的"结构病"有两种：一种是段落之间没有逻辑衔接，读起来像散装句子；另一种是全篇只有一个角度、一种声音，论证单薄得像一面墙。

FT这篇关于快手取消"大小周"工作制的报道，是学习段落结构的素材：584个字，涵盖了**事件宣布、行业背景、政策分析、财务动机、员工反应、行业影响**六个层次，层次分明，环环相扣。

## 原文段落

> China's short-video company Kuaishou has formally cancelled its weekend overtime policy, while its competitor, TikTok's parent ByteDance, is debating internally whether to do the same.
>
> The move by Kuaishou, which raised 5.4bn in a public listing earlier this year, comes as China's tech sector grapples with employees' complaints of overwork and mistreatment. In January, the news of two deaths at ecommerce giant Pinduoduo further stirred nationwide debate over the notorious "996" schedule.
>
> Beijing's regulatory tightening against Big Tech could be a reason behind the change, said Shan Guo, Shanghai-based analyst at consultancy Plenum.ai.
>
> Some Kuaishou staff rejoiced at having their weekends back. "Goodbye, big/small weeks!" posted one employee.
> But many others questioned whether the move was anything more than cosmetic, saying that they expected their teams would demand they "voluntarily" continue working on weekends.
> ...
> At a company-wide meeting last month, ByteDance's incoming chief executive Liang Rubo announced that the company had been reconsidering its big/small week working policy. However, Liang added, a survey of staff found one-third supported continuing the policy, while one-third were against.

## 段落结构技法详解

### 技巧一：事件引出——从个体到行业的纵深

**核心公式：** 具体事件 → 行业对标 → 历史背景

原文用三段递进完成场景构建：

- 第一段：Kuaishou cancels overtime + ByteDance debating（**事件+行业对标**）
- 第二段：5.4bn listing → tech sector overwork → Pinduoduo deaths（**放大到行业+历史**）

这种由近及远的"推镜头"结构，让读者在3句话内理解一个事件的全部背景。

**❌ 学生初版**
Kuaishou cancelled its overtime policy. This is a big change for the company.

**✅ 升级版**
Kuaishou has formally cancelled its weekend overtime policy, while its competitor ByteDance is debating whether to do the same. The move comes as China's tech sector grapples with employees' complaints of overwork, following nationwide debate over the "996" schedule after two deaths at ecommerce giant Pinduoduo.

**💡 效果**
从"Kuaishou做了什么"延伸到"为什么做"（行业背景），再延伸到"什么触发了这个讨论"（Pinduoduo事件）。信息密度高但逻辑清晰，适合作文开头段。

---

### 技巧二：分析段——引用第三方观点增强说服力

**核心公式：** 论断句 + 引语来源 + 引语展开逻辑

报道中引用了分析师Shan Guo的话来解释事件动机：

> Beijing's regulatory tightening against Big Tech could be a reason behind the change, said Shan Guo... "After President Xi called to 'prevent disorderly expansion of capital' last year and 'strengthen protection of workers' in April, it would be difficult for policymakers to keep a blind eye to tech giants' labour practices."

这个段落的精妙之处在于：论断 → 来源 → 引语展开，三层一体。学生写作时常犯的错误是"直接抛观点"而不提供论证支撑。

**❌ 学生初版**
The government's new rules made Kuaishou change its policy. The leader said capital should not expand disorderly.

**✅ 升级版**
Beijing's regulatory tightening against Big Tech could be a key driver behind the change, said Shan Guo, a Shanghai-based industry analyst. As she noted, after President Xi called to "prevent disorderly expansion of capital" and "strengthen protection of workers," regulators could no longer ignore tech giants' labour practices.

**💡 效果**
三种声音交替出现：作者自己的判断（could be a key driver）→ 专家观点（said Shan Guo）→ 直接引语（Xi's words）。这种"作者说→专家说→权威说"的多视角结构，比单一声道有说服力得多。

---

### 技巧三：多方视角交替——让论证立体

**核心公式：** 一方观点 → 转折词 → 对立观点 → 数据/证据

这篇报道最精彩的段落结构是对员工反应的描写：

> Some staff rejoiced at having their weekends back. "Goodbye, big/small weeks!"
> **But** many others questioned whether the move was anything more than cosmetic.

紧接着用"However"引出管理层的困境：

> **However**, Liang added, a survey of staff found one-third supported continuing the policy, while one-third were against.

三段话覆盖了三种视角：支持者（有一些员工很开心）→ 怀疑者（另一些认为只是表面文章）→ 管理层（内部调查显示分裂）。每个视角都有对应的证据（引语+数据）。

**❌ 学生初版**
Many employees were happy. But some were not happy. The company had different opinions.

**✅ 升级版**
Some Kuaishou staff rejoiced at having their weekends back. But many others questioned whether the move was anything more than cosmetic, expecting teams would demand they "voluntarily" continue working. At ByteDance, an internal survey found staff deeply divided: one-third supported ending overtime, one-third opposed it, and the rest remained undecided.

**💡 效果**
先给积极面（Some rejoiced），转折（But many questioned），再加管理层视角（survey showed division）。每个观点都有引语或数据支撑，而不是空洞的"some people think..."

## 写作建议

从快手这篇报道，可以提炼出一个万能段落框架：

**引出段**（事件+背景）→ **分析段**（第三方观点+证据）→ **对比段**（多方视角+转折）

在写议论文的"利与弊"部分时，先把双方观点各用一段写清楚，每段都包含至少一个具体事例或数据。一段只说一个观点，一个观点配一个证据。这样写出来的文章，结构清晰，论证有力。
