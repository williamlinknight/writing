# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：同位语里的「身份打包」——从朝鲜粮食警告报道学一句话交代专家是谁

批改议论文时，最空的一句话往往是引用的那一句：

> Some experts say it is bad. A professor said the problem is serious.

读者读完完全不知道这位专家是谁、在哪个机构、研究什么——**没有坐标的引用，等于没引用**。而学生的困难也很真实：想交代这些信息，中文思维会变成四句独立的话——

> Peter Ward is an expert. He works at the University of Vienna. He is based in Seoul. He studies North Korea.

四句话，读者读到第四句已经忘了第一句。

英文记者的做法是把这四件事**塞进人名后面的同位语**，一句说完。今天我们读《金融时报》2021年6月关于朝鲜粮食短缺的报道，向它学3种「身份打包」结构，外加一个把不确定性写进句子的被动插入语。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №166 — *Kim Jong Un warns of food shortages in North Korea*（Edward White、Kang Buseong 首尔报道，2021年6月）

## 英文原文

> Statistics on the secretive state are difficult to obtain and often unreliable. However, Peter Ward, a Seoul-based expert with the University of Vienna who tracks the North Korean economy, pointed to reports of "unprecedented" price increases for staples such as corn and rice in recent weeks.

> While shipments — believed to be imports of food, fertiliser and fuel — from China, the country's main trading partner, have continued at a much-reduced pace, most cross-border trading activity has declined sharply or stopped completely.

> Rachel Lee, a former US government North Korea analyst who is now with 38 North at the Stimson Center, a Washington think-tank, said Kim might also be acknowledging food shortages to "crack the whip on the farming sector to increase production" as well as justify self-reliance.

## 中文对照

> 这个神秘国家的统计数据很难获取，而且往往不可信。不过，跟踪朝鲜经济的维也纳大学(University of Vienna)驻首尔专家彼得•沃德(Peter Ward)指出，有消息称，玉米和大米等主食最近数周出现"史无前例"的涨价。

> 自疫情爆发以来，朝鲜切断了与外界的联系。虽然货物依然以大大降低的频率从该国的主要贸易伙伴中国那里运送过来（外界普遍认为运来的是进口的粮食、肥料和燃料），但大多数跨境贸易活动都大幅减少，或完全停止。

> 曾在美国政府中分析朝鲜问题、现在为华盛顿智库史汀生中心(Stimson Center)"北纬38度"(38 North)工作的Rachel Lee表示，金正恩承认粮食短缺也可能是为了"鞭策农业部门增加产量"，以及证明自力更生的合理性。

## 4个高分结构

### 结构一：复合前置修饰语 + 机构 + 研究领域——"a Seoul-based expert with the University of Vienna who tracks the North Korean economy"

> However, **Peter Ward, a Seoul-based expert with the University of Vienna who tracks the North Korean economy,** pointed to reports of "unprecedented" price increases...

**为什么好：** 人名后面的同位语由三个模块拼成——

1. **a Seoul-based expert**：用连字符把"驻地"变成形容词，比 *who is based in Seoul* 少四个词；
2. **with the University of Vienna**：介词 with 引出机构（比 of 更常用于"任职于"）；
3. **who tracks the North Korean economy**：who 从句交代研究领域，动词 *track*（持续跟踪）比 *studies* 更有"一直在盯"的画面感。

三个模块合起来，读者立刻知道：这人常驻首尔、在维也纳大学、专门盯朝鲜经济——**可信度是靠这三点建立的**。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Peter Ward is an expert. He is from the University of Vienna. He is based in Seoul. He studies North Korea. He pointed out the price increase.

**✅ 升级版**

> Peter Ward, a Seoul-based expert with the University of Vienna who tracks the North Korean economy, pointed to reports of "unprecedented" price increases.

**💡 效果** 公式可以直接复用：*a [地点]-based [职称] with [机构] who [动词 + 研究内容]*。写"我认识的一位在厦门教了二十年书的老师"：*a Xiamen-based teacher with 20 years of experience who has taught thousands of students.*

**模板：** *[人名], a [地点]-based [职称] with [机构] who [动词 + 领域], [主句谓语]*

### 结构二：前职 + 现任（双层同位语）——"a former US government North Korea analyst who is now with 38 North at the Stimson Center, a Washington think-tank"

> **Rachel Lee, a former US government North Korea analyst who is now with 38 North at the Stimson Center, a Washington think-tank,** said Kim might also be acknowledging food shortages...

**为什么好：** 这是全文密度最高的一处，一个句子做了三层打包：

1. 第一层 **a former US government North Korea analyst**——过去的身份（曾在美国政府分析朝鲜问题）；
2. 第二层 **who is now with 38 North at the Stimson Center**——现在的机构，而且机构名叠了两层（38 North 隶属于 Stimson Center）；
3. 第三层 **a Washington think-tank**——给陌生机构补一个同位语注释（华盛顿智库），读者不必暂停去查。

三层之间没有用 and，也没有另起一句，全靠同位语和 who 从句缝合。**注意第三层：同位语里面还可以再放一个同位语**，这是英文处理"读者不认识这个机构"的标准动作。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Rachel Lee used to work for the US government. Now she works for 38 North. 38 North is part of the Stimson Center. The Stimson Center is a think-tank in Washington. She said Kim might be doing this to increase production.

**✅ 升级版**

> Rachel Lee, a former US government North Korea analyst who is now with 38 North at the Stimson Center, a Washington think-tank, said Kim might also be acknowledging food shortages to "crack the whip on the farming sector".

**💡 效果** 中文写"曾经……如今……"要用两个句子，英文用 **a former X who is now with Y** 就把它缝进一个同位语。写校园作文也用得上：*Ms Lin, a former newspaper editor who is now our writing teacher, said...*

**模板：** *[人名], a former [前职] who is now with [现机构], a [城市] [机构类型], said...*

### 结构三：国家与机构的「职能标签」同位语——"from China, the country's main trading partner,"

> While shipments ... from **China, the country's main trading partner,** have continued at a much-reduced pace...

**为什么好：** China 后面补一个同位语 *the country's main trading partner*（该国的主要贸易伙伴），一句话交代双边关系。两个细节值得学：

- **the country's** 用所有格回指前文的主语 North Korea，不用重复国名；
- 标签本身是"职能/地位"（main trading partner），而不是一句评论（*China is very important to North Korea*），所以能当作名词的附件。

**❌ 学生初版**

> The goods came from China. China is North Korea's main trading partner. The trade has slowed down.

**✅ 升级版**

> Shipments from China, the country's main trading partner, have continued at a much-reduced pace.

**💡 效果** 写家乡、学校、身边人物都能用：*Xiamen, the city where I grew up*、*our school's sister school in Japan*。把"关系"做成同位语，句子立刻少一句。

**模板：** *[专名], the [国家/城市]'s [最高级或职能 + 类别], [主句]*

### 结构四：破折号里的被动说明——"shipments — believed to be imports of food, fertiliser and fuel —"

> While **shipments — believed to be imports of food, fertiliser and fuel —** from China, the country's main trading partner, have continued at a much-reduced pace...

**为什么好：** 记者并不确定船上装的是什么，于是用 **believed to be**（据信是）把"不确定"做进句子——既给了读者信息（粮食、肥料、燃料），又不承担"断言"的责任。这是学术写作里 *It is believed that...* 的压缩版：把 it is 省掉，直接让被动结构跟在名词后面。

学生写议论文说"据说""据估计"时最爱写 *Someone says that...* 或 *It is said by people that...*，其实一个插入语就够。

**❌ 学生初版**

> It is believed by people that the shipments include food, fertiliser and fuel. The shipments came from China.

**✅ 升级版**

> Shipments — believed to be imports of food, fertiliser and fuel — from China have continued at a much-reduced pace.

**💡 效果** 同类公式：*thought to be*（据认为）、*expected to reach*（预计达到）、*estimated at*（估计为）。图表作文里描述预测值特别顺手：*The figure, estimated at 12 per cent, may double by 2030.*

**⚠️ 注意：** *believed to be* 前面不加 it is；要加主语，句子就变成另一个结构了。

**模板：** *[名词] — believed / thought to be [内容] — [主句谓语]*

## 为什么学生的引用总是「没有坐标」？

读者判断一句引用值不值得信，靠三个坐标：**谁（职称）、在哪（机构）、研究什么（领域）**。三个坐标都在同位语里，引用才有重量。

自查三步，写完引用先问自己：

1. 我的引用里有没有**机构**？（没有机构 = 路人甲发言）
2. 有没有**领域或时间**？（研究什么的、做了多久）
3. 读者认识这个机构吗？不认识——**补一个同位语注释**（a Washington think-tank / a Xiamen-based tutoring studio）。

还有一条硬规则：**引用要贴着名词，不要另起一句。** 名字后面挂信息是英文的默认动作，中文的"他……他……他……"到了英文里就是同位语。

## 小练习

把下面这段"四句版"的专家介绍合并成一句话，用今天学的结构：

> ❌ Dr Chen is a teacher at Xiamen No.1 Middle School. She has taught English for 18 years. She studies how students learn writing. She said most students only practise grammar, not writing.

升级提示：① 用 *an Xiamen-based teacher with 18 years of experience* 打前两层；② 用 *who studies...* 交代领域；③ 主语后的同位语用逗号夹住，主句谓语 *said* 不要丢。

参考写法：

> Dr Chen, an Xiamen-based teacher with 18 years of experience who studies how students learn writing, said most students only practise grammar, not writing.

再练一次：给你最喜欢的一位老师，用 *a former [前职] who is now with [现学校]* 写一句话。
