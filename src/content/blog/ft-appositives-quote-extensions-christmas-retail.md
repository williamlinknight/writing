# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：引述之后——一句话写完「谁说的」和「凭什么」

## 「他说」之后，你还有半句话没说

这是高频出现的学生段落：

**❌ 学生初版**
The store manager said business was good. He also said that online orders were three times higher than last year. He is able to sell to customers anywhere.

三句，等于一个引述、一个证据、一个状态。信息没错，但读起来像笔录：一句一个动作，句子之间没有咬合。而在英文报道里，这三件事通常挤在同一句——引述动词后面挂一个分词，或挂一个形容词短语，第二层信息就顺着长出来了。

《金融时报》2020 年底的圣诞购物季报道，写欧洲百货和奢侈品牌怎么在封锁中抢救全年最重要的一个销售季。这篇稿子人物多、引语密，正好能看到"引述之后"的各种接法。今天拆四种。

>

But the 15-year Harrods veteran worried that it would struggle to sell all the merchandise that buyers had chosen in January when they put in Christmas orders.

## 技巧1：引述 + noting 分词，把证据续在后面

**结构公式：** `"[引语]," says [人名], noting (that) [证据句]`

**FT例句：**

> "I think we'll do even better than last year," says Burke, noting the brand's online retail operation was sending out "about 30,000 packages a day this year, compared to 13,000 a day last year."

引语是判断（我们会更好），`noting` 后面接的是支撑这个判断的数据。学生习惯写成两句："他说会更好。他还说每天发 3 万个包裹。" 用 `noting` 一分词，判断和证据变成一句话里的前后半段，因果关系自己成立。

**❌ 学生初版**
The manager said sales would improve. He also said the shop sent out 30,000 packages a day.

**✅ 升级版**
"We will do better than last year," says the manager, noting the shop was sending out 30,000 packages a day, up from 13,000.

**💡 效果**
`noting` 天然表示"补充说明、顺带指出"，比 and he also said 轻得多；后面还能直接接对比数据（up from / compared to），一句话完成"判断—证据—对比"。

**考场模板：** `"[判断]," says [人名], noting (that) [数据/事实].`
例："Demand has not fallen," says the organiser, noting that ticket sales were up 40 per cent on last year.

## 技巧2：引述 + 形容词短语，把"状态/能力"续在后面

**结构公式：** `..., says [人名], able to [做什么], regardless of [条件]`

**FT例句：**

> By giving them access to more information such as live inventory, Louis Vuitton turned "every one into a store manager", says Burke, able to sell whenever and from wherever they are, regardless of whether the brand's 470 worldwide stores are open.

注意逗号后面那一串：`able to sell whenever and from wherever they are` —— 没有主语，没有 is，直接一个形容词短语挂在引述后面，说明"因此他们能做什么"。学生版本必须另起一句 `They are able to sell...`，一句话就变成了两句话。

**❌ 学生初版**
The company gave staff more information. The CEO said this made everyone a manager. They are able to sell anywhere.

**✅ 升级版**
By giving staff live inventory data, the brand turned "every one into a store manager", says its chief executive, able to sell whenever and from wherever they are.

**💡 效果**
形容词短语省掉一个主语和系动词，句子收紧；`able to` 后面接的正是前一句动作的结果，读者顺着读就知道"给数据"和"能随时卖"是因果关系。

**考场模板：** `[做法], says [人名], able to [能力], regardless of [限制条件].`
例：By moving courses online, the school kept every class running, says its principal, able to teach students in five time zones.

## 技巧3：资历标签——第二次提到人，不重复名字

**FT例句：**

> But the 15-year Harrods veteran worried that it would struggle to sell all the merchandise that buyers had chosen in January when they put in Christmas orders.

上一句刚出现 Michael Ward, the managing director of Harrods，下一句就换成 the 15-year Harrods veteran。名字没重复，身份却更具体了：数字（15 年）+ 机构（Harrods）+ 身份名词（veteran）。这就是"资历标签"——用年限给人物加重量。

**❌ 学生初版**
Michael Ward is the managing director. Michael Ward worried that the products would not sell.

**✅ 升级版**
Michael Ward, the managing director, doubted the plan would work. But the 15-year company veteran worried that the stock would not clear in time.

**💡 效果**
同一个人第二次出场，换成一个带年限的称呼，读者立刻知道"这人不是空降的"；同时避免名字重复带来的生硬感。这个写法在人物报道、人物介绍作文里非常好用。

**可套的三种标签：**
- `the 15-year Harrods veteran`（年限 + 机构 + veteran）
- `the three-term mayor`（届数 + 职务）
- `the former teacher turned founder`（前身份 + 现身份）

## 技巧4：引述的两种排布——身份短就前置，身份长就后置

**FT例句：**

> Selfridges does not plan to discount until Boxing Day, says buying and merchandising director Sebastian Manes.
>
> Michael Ward, the managing director of Harrods, says complying with such restrictions was the "right thing to do" for staff and customers.

同一篇文章，两种排法。差别不在风格，在**身份的长度**：

- 身份短（四五个词、无冠词）：`says buying and merchandising director Sebastian Manes` —— 引述动词提前，句子以人名收尾，干净利落。
- 身份长（带 the、带 of 结构、还要加信息）：`Michael Ward, the managing director of Harrods, says ...` —— 名字在前，身份夹在两个逗号里，主句照常推进。

学生最常犯的错是把两种混着用：`Michael Ward, the managing director of Harrods, says buying...` 写成 `Says Michael Ward, the managing director...`，句首大写动词，读起来像翻译腔。

**❌ 学生初版**
Says the manager of the biggest department store in London, we will not discount.

**✅ 升级版**
The store will not discount before Boxing Day, says its manager. / Its manager, who has run the floor for a decade, says the store will not discount before Boxing Day.

**💡 效果**
引述动词放在句中或句尾，句子重心落在"说了什么"上；身份短就跟着动词走，身份长就退回名字后面，读者永远先看到信息，再看到人。

## 三个练习

1. 找一条你写过的引语，把后面补证据的那句删掉，用 `noting` 挂回去。
2. 写一个人物介绍段，让这个人在第二句以"资历标签"（年限+机构+veteran/regular）再次出现，不准重复名字。
3. 同一句引述，写两个版本：身份短语四个词以内（前置），身份短语十个词以上（后置），体会语序的差别。
