# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：论证方法——例证论证，用具体案例让观点落地

学生议论文最常见的硬伤是什么？不是没观点，而是**观点后面没有例子**。写了"环境污染很严重""科技发展很快"，然后就没了——读者不知道"严重"到什么程度，也不知道"快"在哪里。论证就停在喊口号这一层。

今天我们从《金融时报》一篇关于加州居家令的报道里，学四种**例证论证**手法。它们的共同逻辑是：不替读者下判断，而是把具体的规则、数字、人物、措施摆在读者面前，让读者自己得出"确实严重"的结论。

## FT原文

> The new restrictions followed orders announced by California governor Gavin Newsom that would limit travel and commerce if capacity at intensive care units fell below 15 per cent in five regions of the state. As of Saturday, the central San Joaquin Valley area and the broadly defined Southern California region, which includes Los Angeles, had breached the limit.

> California's measures followed a series of record daily highs of coronavirus in the past week, with the US logging 224,831 new cases on Friday and 211,073 on Saturday. More than 101,000 were in hospital on Friday and Saturday, marking another record, according to the Covid Tracking Project.

> Grant Colfax, director of health for San Francisco, said the city was experiencing its "worst surge yet" of the virus. "It is stressing healthcare systems across the state of California and taxing our healthcare workers," he said.

> Under Mr Newsom's orders, retailers and shopping centres will be limited to 20 per cent of their capacity, and restaurants will only be allowed to offer food for delivery or pick-up.

## 中文对照

> 实行新的限制措施之前，加州州长加文·纽瑟姆宣布的抗疫命令意味着，如果该州五个地区的重症监护病房收治能力降至15%以下，就会限制旅行和商业活动。截至上周六，圣华金河谷中部地区和宽泛界定的南加州地区（包括洛杉矶）已突破限制。

> 加州采取严厉措施之前，上周美国新冠病毒每日新增感染病例连创新高，其中上周五新增224831例，上周六新增211073例。上周五和上周六有超过10.1万人因感染新冠在住院，创下又一个纪录。

> 旧金山卫生局局长格兰特·科尔法克斯表示，该市经历着"迄今最严重的疫情爆发"。"（疫情）正在给整个加州的医疗系统造成压力，使我们的医护人员难以应对。"他表示。

> 根据纽瑟姆的命令，零售店和购物中心的客容量将被限制在20%，餐馆只能提供外卖或自取服务。

## 手法一：规则 + 实例法（threshold + concrete cases）

看这句：

> would limit travel and commerce if capacity at intensive care units fell below 15 per cent in five regions of the state. As of Saturday, the central San Joaquin Valley area and the broadly defined Southern California region, which includes Los Angeles, had breached the limit.

记者先给出一个**可验证的标准**（重症监护收治能力低于15%就限制出行），再用**两个具体地点**（圣华金河谷、南加州含洛杉矶）证明标准已被触发。读者看到"15%"和"洛杉矶"，立刻相信"情况确实到了临界点"——这比"加州疫情非常严峻"有力得多。

**❌ 学生初版**

The new rules are very strict in California.

**✅ 升级版**

The new restrictions would limit travel and commerce if ICU capacity fell below 15 per cent. As of Saturday, Southern California, which includes Los Angeles, had breached the limit.

**💡 效果**

先立标准、再给实例，抽象的"strict"变成了可验证的事实。读者自己就能得出"确实严格"的结论。

**模板**

[Order] would [动作] if [条件] fell below [数字] per cent. As of [时间], [具体案例] had breached the limit.

**例句**

The school would cancel outdoor activities if the air quality index fell below 200. As of Thursday, three districts in the city had breached the limit.

## 手法二：数字例证法（precise numbers as evidence）

看这句：

> with the US logging 224,831 new cases on Friday and 211,073 on Saturday. More than 101,000 were in hospital on Friday and Saturday, marking another record.

"病例很多"是观点，224831、211073、101000是**证据**。记者不写"huge numbers"，而是给出精确数字并配上"marking another record"（又破纪录了）这个判断。数字让规模可感知，破纪录让趋势可感知——两句话完成了"规模+趋势"的双重例证。

**❌ 学生初版**

Many people got the virus and hospitals were very crowded.

**✅ 升级版**

The US logged 224,831 new cases on Friday and 211,073 on Saturday. More than 101,000 were in hospital, marking another record.

**💡 效果**

精确数字自带说服力，读者不需要你反复强调"very"——数据已经替他喊了。

**模板**

[主体] logged [N] on [day] and [N] on [day]. More than [N] were [状态], marking another record.

**例句**

The city logged 3,200 cars on the bridge on Monday and 3,050 on Tuesday. More than 80 per cent were single-driver vehicles, marking another record.

## 手法三：人物引语例证法（named official as illustration）

看这句：

> Grant Colfax, director of health for San Francisco, said the city was experiencing its "worst surge yet" of the virus. "It is stressing healthcare systems across the state of California and taxing our healthcare workers," he said.

作者没有自己喊"疫情严重"，而是**借一个具体官员的口**：身份（旧金山卫生局局长）+ 具体判断（"迄今最严重"）+ 具体后果（压垮医疗系统、透支医护人员）。身份给判断背书，判断给观点供血——这就是引语例证的三件套。

**❌ 学生初版**

The situation in the city is very serious.

**✅ 升级版**

"San Francisco was experiencing its worst surge yet of the virus. It is stressing healthcare systems across the state and taxing our healthcare workers," said the director of health.

**💡 效果**

同一句话，自己说是情绪，官员说是证据。读者信的不是形容词，是说话人的身份。

**模板**

[Name], [role], said [place] was experiencing its "worst [X] yet" of [topic]. "[后果]," he said.

**例句**

"The school was experiencing its worst teacher shortage yet. It is putting pressure on every department and taxing our remaining staff," said the principal.

## 手法四：场景拆解法（concrete measures list）

看这句：

> Under Mr Newsom's orders, retailers and shopping centres will be limited to 20 per cent of their capacity, and restaurants will only be allowed to offer food for delivery or pick-up.

"政府采取了严格措施"是抽象表述，读者无感。FT把它拆成**看得见的动作清单**：商场限流20%，餐馆只能外卖自取。读者一读就知道"严不严"——不需要作者下结论。这是例证论证里最实用的一招：把一个大词拆成三个小动作。

**❌ 学生初版**

The government took strict measures to stop the spread of the disease.

**✅ 升级版**

Under the new orders, retailers and shopping centres will be limited to 20 per cent of their capacity, and restaurants will only be allowed to offer food for delivery or pick-up.

**💡 效果**

抽象的"strict measures"变成了具体画面。考官读到这里，脑子里已经有画面了——这就是例证的意义。

**模板**

Under the orders, [A] will be limited to [N] per cent of their capacity, and [B] will only be allowed to [动作].

**例句**

Under the new rules, school libraries will be limited to 30 per cent of their capacity, and canteens will only be allowed to offer take-away meals.

## 写作建议

这四种手法的共同点是：**例证不是装饰，而是让读者自己得出你的结论**。下次写完一个观点，先别急着写下一个，回头问自己四句话——观点后面有没有具体地点？有没有精确数字？有没有具体人物？有没有动作清单？一个观点配一个像样的例子，远胜三个观点配零个例子。例证落地，论证才有重量。
