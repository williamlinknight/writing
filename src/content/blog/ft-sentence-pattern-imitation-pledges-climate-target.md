# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：句型模仿——写“目标”只会 try our best？跟FT学4个承诺与目标句型

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №122 — 英国将推出更雄心勃勃的气候目标（2021年4月，496词）

## 为什么学“承诺与目标”

议论文的“方案段”、活动策划、倡议书的目标句，学生几乎只有一个写法：

*We will try our best to protect the environment. The government wants to reduce pollution a lot. We hope it will be better in the future.*

三句话读下来，读者不知道**目标是多少、什么时候达成、跟原来比进步了多少、靠什么落地**。这正是《金融时报》气候与政策报道最擅长的地方：它写目标，永远带着四个坐标——**幅度（多少个百分点）、目标年、基准年、落实清单**。数字一旦锚定，承诺就从愿望变成计划。

今天从一篇英国宣布 2035 年减排 78% 的报道里，抽 4 个句型：**怎么承诺、怎么量化、怎么定位进步、怎么列落实清单**。

## FT 原文

> The UK will this week commit to steeper cuts in carbon emissions as it prepares to host the UN's COP26 climate summit later this year, according to people briefed on the plan.

> Prime minister Boris Johnson will in coming days announce a new pledge to reduce emissions by 78 per cent by 2035 compared to 1990 levels.

> The UK's new target is a step forward from its previous aim of a 68 per cent emissions reduction by 2030, which was already one of the most ambitious plans among developed economies.

> Hitting the target will require changes to most industrial activity, including: an electricity system that operates without generating carbon emissions; a reduction in meat and dairy consumption across the UK; introducing low-carbon heating systems in homes; and planting more woodland, according to a report by the Climate Change Committee, the government's independent advisory group.

> The UK was one of the first major economies to make a commitment to reaching net zero emissions by 2050. The US is expected to adopt a similar target under the Biden administration.

> Emissions from international aviation and shipping are likely to be included in the new UK target, according to people familiar with the government's plans, representing a major victory for environmental groups.

**中文大意：** 英国本周将承诺更大幅度地削减碳排放，因为该国准备在今年晚些时候主办联合国第 26 届气候变化大会；据听取了相关计划简报的人士透露，英国首相约翰逊将在未来几天宣布一项新的承诺：到 2035 年，碳排放将在 1990 年的水平上减少 78%。这一新目标比它此前到 2030 年减排 68% 的目标又向前迈进了一步，而那已经属于发达经济体中最雄心勃勃的计划之列。要实现目标，需要改变大多数工业活动，包括：建成不产生碳排放的电力系统；减少全英国的肉类和乳制品消费；在家庭中推广低碳供暖系统；种植更多树木——这些来自英国政府的独立咨询机构“气候变化委员会”的报告。英国是首批承诺到 2050 年实现净零排放的主要经济体之一；在拜登政府领导下，美国预计将采取类似目标。国际航空和航运的排放可能也被纳入英国新的目标之中，这代表着环保组织的一场重大胜利。

## 句型一：承诺 + 时机背景 —— will commit to sth as it prepares to do ..., according to ...

**结构公式**

`[主体] will [时间] commit to [更大幅度的动作] as it prepares to [即将发生的场合], according to [消息来源]`

**FT 原文**

> The UK will this week commit to steeper cuts in carbon emissions as it prepares to host the UN's COP26 climate summit later this year, according to people briefed on the plan.

**为什么好**

`commit to` 后面接**名词短语**（steeper cuts），而不是 *promise to cut more*——commit to 是“承诺承担”，语气比 plan to 有约束力。`as it prepares to host ...` 用 as 从句交代**时机与压力**：不是想承诺，而是因为要主办大会、要在台上说话。句尾 `according to people briefed on the plan` 是新闻式**来源限定**——消息尚未官宣，说清楚“据谁所言”，比直接断言更稳。学生写同意思通常是：Our school will have an activity. We want to save water. This is important.

**❌ 学生初版**

Our school will have an environment week. We want to save electricity. We will try our best.

**✅ 升级版**

Our school will this term commit to deeper cuts in electricity use as it prepares to host the city's green campus forum in June, according to the draft plan circulated among class monitors.

**💡 效果**

一句里“谁承诺—承诺到什么程度—因为什么场合—消息出处”全部到位，句子的可信度立刻抬起来，而字数只比原来多几个词。

**模板 + 例句**

`[主体] will [时间] commit to [更大动作] as it prepares to [场合], according to [来源]`

- 环保话题（仿写示例）：The city will next spring commit to steeper cuts in single-use plastic as it prepares to host the provincial coastal clean-up, according to officials briefed on the plan.
- 校园话题（仿写示例）：Our class will this month commit to longer reading sessions as it prepares to take part in the district reading challenge, according to the study plan posted on the board.

## 句型二：量化目标 —— announce a new pledge to do sth by X per cent by YEAR compared to BASE levels

**结构公式**

`[主体] will [时间] announce a new pledge to reduce [指标] by [百分比] by [目标年] compared to [基准年] levels.`

**FT 原文**

> Prime minister Boris Johnson will in coming days announce a new pledge to reduce emissions by 78 per cent by 2035 compared to 1990 levels.

**为什么好**

这一句里有**三个数据锚点**：`by 78 per cent`（幅度）、`by 2035`（目标年）、`compared to 1990 levels`（基准年）。三者缺一，目标就从计划退化成愿望（“减少很多”“以后会更好”）。`announce a new pledge to do` 用名词 pledge 让“承诺”变成可发布的东西，比 *promise* 更正式；`in coming days` 是“未来几天”，比 soon 精确，也避开了具体日期无法确认的尴尬。写作文时，任何涉及数字的目标句，都可以照这三个锚点自查一遍。

**❌ 学生初版**

The school will ask students to waste less food. We want to make it much less than before.

**✅ 升级版**

The canteen will next month announce a new pledge to cut food waste by 40 per cent by the end of the school year compared to last September levels.

**💡 效果**

“减少 40%”“学年末”“与去年 9 月相比”三层坐标把口号变成可以验收的目标；阅卷老师读到的不是决心，而是可衡量的计划。

**模板 + 例句**

`[主体] will [时间] announce a new pledge to cut / reduce [指标] by [百分比] by [目标年] compared to [基准年] levels`

- 社会话题（仿写示例）：The district will in coming months announce a new pledge to cut private-car school runs by 30 per cent by 2028 compared to 2024 levels.
- 校园话题（仿写示例）：The library will this week announce a new pledge to raise borrowing by 25 per cent by June compared to last year's figures.

## 句型三：进步定位 —— is a step forward from its previous aim of ..., which was already one of the most ambitious ...

**结构公式**

`The new [目标] is a step forward from its previous aim of [旧目标], which was already one of the most ambitious [同类] among [比较范围]`

**FT 原文**

> The UK's new target is a step forward from its previous aim of a 68 per cent emissions reduction by 2030, which was already one of the most ambitious plans among developed economies.

**为什么好**

`is a step forward from its previous aim of ...` 是“比原来的目标更进一步”的固定表达——它同时给出**旧目标**和**进步关系**，学生常犯的错是只写新目标，读者无从比较。后半句 `which was already one of the most ambitious plans among developed economies` 用非限制性定语从句追加评价：**旧目标本身已经很强**，于是新目标的含金量不言自明。这种“先立旧高度、再抬新高度”的写法，比直接说 *This is a very ambitious plan* 有效得多。

**❌ 学生初版**

Our new plan is better than the old plan. The old plan was also very good. Our new plan is very ambitious.

**✅ 升级版**

The new target is a step forward from our previous aim of a 20 per cent cut in paper use by last summer, which was already one of the most ambitious plans among the district's schools.

**💡 效果**

一句话交代了“旧目标—新目标—进步幅度—历史地位”，评价不靠形容词（very ambitious）而靠事实对比，语体立刻从学生腔变成报道腔。

**模板 + 例句**

`The new [目标] is a step forward from its previous aim of [旧目标], which was already one of the most ambitious [同类] among [比较范围]`

- 环保话题（仿写示例）：The new target is a step forward from the city's previous aim of planting 10,000 trees by 2023, which was already one of the most ambitious plans among inland cities.
- 教育话题（仿写示例）：The new reading target is a step forward from the school's previous aim of four books a term, which was already one of the most demanding requirements in the province.

## 句型四：落实清单 + 预期 —— Hitting the target will require ..., including: A; B; C; and D / is expected to adopt

**结构公式**

`Hitting the target will require changes to [范围], including: [A]; [B]; [C]; and [D], according to [报告]` ＋ `[主体] is expected to adopt a similar target` ＋ `... are likely to be included in [方案], representing [评价]`

**FT 原文**

> Hitting the target will require changes to most industrial activity, including: an electricity system that operates without generating carbon emissions; a reduction in meat and dairy consumption across the UK; introducing low-carbon heating systems in homes; and planting more woodland, according to a report by the Climate Change Committee, the government's independent advisory group.

> The UK was one of the first major economies to make a commitment to reaching net zero emissions by 2050. The US is expected to adopt a similar target under the Biden administration.

> Emissions from international aviation and shipping are likely to be included in the new UK target, according to people familiar with the government's plans, representing a major victory for environmental groups.

**为什么好**

三处细节值得拆开看。第一，`Hitting the target` 用**动名词短语做主语**，比 *If we want to hit the target, we need to ...* 少一个从句、多一分利落；`including:` 后面用**分号分隔四项并列**（`A; B; C; and D`），句内就能列出清单，不必另起四句。第二，`is expected to adopt a similar target` 用被动 + expected to 表达**预计他人跟进**，比 *The US will also do this* 客观，也避免替别人下结论。第三，结尾 `representing a major victory for environmental groups` 用现在分词收束，把“这意味着什么”挂在句尾，一句话同时给出事实和评价。

**❌ 学生初版**

To reach the goal, we need to do many things. For example, we need to save water, save paper, and recycle. Other schools will also do this. This is good for the environment.

**✅ 升级版**

Hitting the target will require changes to most of our daily habits, including: turning off lights in empty classrooms; printing on both sides of every sheet; sorting waste at the canteen door; and bringing reusable bottles, according to the plan drafted by the student council. Neighbouring schools are expected to adopt a similar target next term, representing a major shift in how the district treats energy use.

**💡 效果**

动名词主语 + 分号清单 + expected to + 分词收尾，四件工具合起来，让“我们要做很多事”这句空话变成一份能执行、能被别人跟进的清单。

**模板 + 例句**

`Hitting the target will require changes to [范围], including: [A]; [B]; [C]; and [D], according to [来源]. [主体] is expected to adopt a similar target.`

- 环保话题（仿写示例）：Hitting the target will require changes to most household habits, including: separating food waste; switching to LED bulbs; taking shorter showers; and using public transport on smoggy days, according to the city's environment bureau.
- 校园话题（仿写示例）：Meeting the target will require changes to most lunchtime routines, including: serving smaller portions; offering a second helping on request; keeping fruit available all afternoon; and recording leftovers daily, according to the canteen committee.

## 练习建议

1. **目标三锚点**：写完任何目标句，自查三样东西——幅度（by X per cent / by a third）、目标年（by 2035 / by the end of term）、基准（compared to 1990 levels / compared to last year）。三样齐全，目标才可验收。
2. **换掉两个动词短语**：把 *try our best* 换成 `commit to [更大幅度的动作]`；把 *want to make it better* 换成 `announce a new pledge to reduce ... by ... by ... compared to ...`。
3. **写完目标就写清单**：目标的下一句用 `Hitting the target will require changes to ..., including: A; B; C; and D` 收尾。分号列清单，比 *and so on* 可信一百倍。
