# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：词汇升级——写"激增"不只会 increase，跟FT学数据趋势词汇

批改雅思小作文和中考图表作文时，最常看到的"数据词汇贫血"长这样：上升就是 **increase**，超过就是 **more than**，最高点就是 **the highest point**，激增就是 **increase a lot**。数据作文拼的不是计算，而是**对同一个趋势的不同说法**——同一个"上升"，FT能写出五六种层次。

今天我们从FT一篇关于美国新冠死亡人数创半年来新高的报道（2020年11月）中学5组**数据趋势词汇**。这篇报道从头到尾都在"报数字"：单日新增1869人、七天平均1162人、总死亡人数241704人……但记者没有一个数字是干巴巴报出来的——每个数字都配了一个精准的动词或名词。写图表作文的考生，这篇是现成的"动词库"。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №27 — *US coronavirus deaths rise to highest level in more than six months*（2020年11月）

## 📖 FT原文选段

> US coronavirus deaths **rose by the most in more than six months** on Wednesday, **propelling** the country's average fatality rate above that of the summer and to its highest level since May.

> The country's **death toll** now stands at 241,704, according to Covid Tracking Project.

> The US has averaged 1,162 deaths a day over the past week, the highest level since late May and **surpassing** the summer **apex** of 1,142 when southern sunbelt states were battling the outbreak.

> Those worrying trends have prompted an increasing number of state and local officials to **impose curbs** on economic and social activity to **head off** a potential **surge** in infections that could follow the Thanksgiving holiday next week.

> ...the nation's largest public school system would close its doors if New York City's seven-day average of positive Covid-19 tests **breached** 3 per cent.

> "You will see a tremendous **spike** after Thanksgiving. Tremendous spike," New York state governor Andrew Cuomo warned on Wednesday.

## 🎯 5组高分词汇解析

### 1️⃣ death toll — 死亡人数

**原文出处：** "The country's **death toll** now stands at 241,704."

**为什么好：** 学生写"死亡人数"只会 **the number of dead people**。**death toll** 是"官方统计的死亡总数"，toll 本义是"（灾难、事故的）代价、伤亡"，是新闻报道和图表作文里的标准说法。它比 the number of dead people 高级在两点：一是名词化、更简洁，二是自带"这场灾难付出了多大代价"的沉重感。

**❌ 学生初版**
The number of dead people in the earthquake was 1,200.

**✅ 升级版**
The death toll from the earthquake now stands at 1,200.

**💡 效果**
death toll 是数据类作文的"定海神针"——凡是写灾难、事故、疫情的伤亡数字，用它准没错；stand at（保持在、达到）也是报数字的高分动词。

**例句：** The death toll in the fire has risen to 67, with rescue teams still searching for survivors.

---

### 2️⃣ surge — 激增

**原文出处：** "...to **head off** a potential **surge** in infections that could follow the Thanksgiving holiday."

**为什么好：** 学生写"激增"只会 **increase a lot**。**surge** 是名词"急剧上升"，自带"来势凶猛、一浪高过一浪"的画面——它不是普通的涨，是"涌上来"。它还有个近亲 **spike**（尖峰）：指单次、突然的暴涨（"You will see a tremendous spike"）。两者搭配使用，数据作文立刻有层次。

**❌ 学生初版**
The number of infections increased a lot after the holiday.

**✅ 升级版**
Officials moved to head off a potential surge in infections after the holiday.

**💡 效果**
surge 比 increase a lot 高级在"名词化+画面感"：一个名词就把"增长的趋势"变成了"有形状、有力度的事件"。

**例句：** The city saw a surge in online orders during the festival, straining delivery services to the limit.

---

### 3️⃣ surpass — 超过（并保持领先）

**原文出处：** "...the highest level since late May and **surpassing** the summer apex of 1,142."

**为什么好：** 学生写"超过"只会 **more than** 或 **over**。**surpass** 是及物动词"超越"，比 exceed 更强调"后来居上、把对方甩在身后"。用 more than 只能报一个数字，用 surpass 能讲一个"新纪录诞生"的故事——这正是图表作文高分和及格分的区别。

**❌ 学生初版**
In 2020, the number of users was more than 80% of the population.

**✅ 升级版**
By 2020, internet users had surpassed 80% of the population, overtaking every previous projection.

**💡 效果**
surpass 让"超过"有了动态感：它是"越过某个标杆"，适合写打破纪录、反超对手的场景。

**例句：** Exports surpassed the ¥10bn mark for the first time in the company's history.

---

### 4️⃣ apex — 峰值、顶点

**原文出处：** "...**surpassing** the summer **apex** of 1,142 when southern sunbelt states were battling the outbreak."

**为什么好：** 学生写"最高点"只会 **the highest point**。**apex**（顶点）和 **peak** 是同义的书面词，一个名词短语 the summer apex 就完成了"夏季达到的最高水平"这个完整信息。写图表作文时，the highest point 满篇都是，换成 apex 或 peak，分数立刻不一样。

**❌ 学生初版**
The highest point of sales was in December.

**✅ 升级版**
Sales reached their annual apex in December, before easing in the new year.

**💡 效果**
apex/peak 自带"曲线图上的尖顶"画面，是趋势作文描述拐点的首选名词。

**例句：** Air travel demand hit its apex during the summer holiday, pushing ticket prices to record highs.

---

### 5️⃣ breach — 突破（阈值、临界点）

**原文出处：** "...the nation's largest public school system would close its doors if New York City's seven-day average of positive Covid-19 tests **breached** 3 per cent."

**为什么好：** 学生写"超过3%"只会 **reach over 3%**。**breach** 原意是"攻破城墙"，引申为"突破某个临界点"——它比 pass、reach 更有力度，暗示"一旦越过这个线，后果很严重"。写分数线、警戒线、阈值时，breach 是专业记者和考生的共同选择。

**❌ 学生初版**
The positive rate reached over 3%, so the city decided to close schools.

**✅ 升级版**
Once the positivity rate breached 3 per cent, the city's policy required schools to close.

**💡 效果**
breach 让"超过"带上了"越界、触发规则"的意味，比 reach over 精准得多——数据不只是数字，还牵动决策。

**例句：** The temperature breached 40°C for the first time in a century, triggering emergency measures.

## ✍️ 写作建议

这5组词的共同点：**它们都在给"数据"做精加工。**

| 学生常写 | 升级表达 | 层次 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| the number of dead people | death toll | 官方统计口径 |
| increase a lot | surge / spike | 来势凶猛的激增 |
| more than | surpass | 后来居上、打破纪录 |
| the highest point | apex | 峰值、拐点 |
| reach over | breach | 突破临界点 |
| make... rise | propel | 推动上升 |
| because of | ascribe to | 归因于 |
| stop... from | impose curbs on | 实施限制 |

**建议你**写图表作文前先列一张"动词清单"：同一个上升，数量上可以用 rise、climb、surge、spike；比较上可以用 surpass、exceed、outstrip；状态上可以用 stand at、reach、breach。一个趋势配三个层次的词，数据作文就不再是"报流水账"。

**今日练习：** 用今天学的表达，把下面这段"学生版"升级成80词的雅思小作文段落：

> ❌ The number of people using the internet increased a lot from 2010 to 2020. In 2020, it was more than 80% in China. The highest point was in 2020.

试着把 increased a lot 换成 surged、把 more than 换成 surpassed、把 the highest point 换成 its apex，感受一下数据作文的浓度变化。
