# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：句型模仿——争端升级（冲突场景的4个句型公式）

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №136 — 英法捕鱼权争端升级 英国派出军舰（2021年5月，632词）

## 为什么学“争端升级”

中高考和雅思都爱考冲突类题材：同学矛盾、家校争议、国际争端、网络论战。学生写这类作文，通常只有一句话的力气：

*The two sides have a disagreement. It is very serious. Now the situation is getting worse.*

三个问题：第一，冲突**没有阶梯**——从不满到抗议，从对峙到反制，在作文里被压扁成一句 getting worse；第二，冲突的**动作**全是 be 动词加形容词，一个真正的动词都没有；第三，双方的**立场与条件**说不清楚，读起来像旁观者的转述，不像当事人在交锋。

今天从《金融时报》一篇报道英法捕鱼权争端的文章里，抽 4 个句型。它们解决一个具体问题：**怎么把一场冲突写出层次——从动作到对峙，从要求到反制**。写校园矛盾、社会争议、国际关系类作文都能直接移植。

## FT 原文

> Two Royal Navy vessels have **begun patrolling** the waters off Jersey **after** Boris Johnson **dispatched** them **in a deepening row** between the UK and France over fishing rights.

> The craft — HMS Tamar and HMS Severn — are in crowded waters **as** more than 50 French fishing boats **converged on** the port of St Helier, Jersey's capital, on Thursday **to protest against** the island's restrictions on French fishermen.

> **Demands that** French ships **be** equipped with monitoring devices and meet other criteria to obtain licences **prompted France to threaten to** cut off its power supply to the Channel island over the dispute.

> Brussels has the option of opening formal dispute settlement procedures with the UK under the terms of the two sides' trade deal **should the EU decide that** Britain is violating the spirit of the agreement.

中文大意：两艘皇家海军舰船已开始在泽西岛附近水域巡逻，此前英国首相鲍里斯·约翰逊在英法之间不断升级的捕鱼权争端中派出它们。这两艘舰船——皇家海军“添马”号与“塞文”号——所处水域十分拥挤，因为周四有超过 50 艘法国渔船聚集到泽西岛首府圣赫利尔港，抗议该岛对法国渔民的限制。泽西岛要求法国船只配备监控设备并满足其他条件才能取得许可证，这些要求促使法方威胁称要对这座海峡岛屿断电。根据双方贸易协议的条款，如果欧盟认定英国违反了协议精神，布鲁塞尔可以选择对英国启动正式争端解决程序。

## 句型一：升级开场 —— has begun ... after ... dispatched ... in a deepening row over

**结构公式**

`[一方] has begun [V-ing 动作], after [决策者] dispatched/sent [手段] in a deepening [冲突名词] between A and B over [议题]`

**FT 原文**

> Two Royal Navy vessels have begun patrolling the waters off Jersey after Boris Johnson dispatched them in a deepening row between the UK and France over fishing rights.

**为什么好**

学生写“冲突开始”，几乎只有 `The problem started` 或 `They began to argue`。FT 这个开场把三件事压进一句：**已经发生的动作**（have begun patrolling）、**触发它的决定**（after Johnson dispatched them）、**争端的性质与级别**（a deepening row over fishing rights）。特别注意 `dispatch`——它比 `send` 多一层“奉命派出”的分量，是媒体报道军事与官方行动时的标准动词；`in a deepening row over X` 则一句话交代了“这是关于什么的、已经到什么程度”。

**❌ 学生初版**

The two sides had a problem about the island. The problem got worse and the army came.

**✅ 升级版**

Volunteers have begun patrolling the school gate after the principal dispatched security guards in a deepening row between parents and the school over homework rules.

**💡 效果**

一句话里读到“动作—起因—争端级别”，读者立刻知道事情已经进入执行阶段，而不是停在“有问题”上。

**模板 + 例句**

`[一方] has begun [V-ing], after [决策者] dispatched [手段] in a deepening row between A and B over [议题]`

- 校园话题：Student unions have begun collecting signatures after the head teacher dispatched a survey in a deepening row between parents and the school over exam schedules.
- 科技话题：Regulators have begun reviewing the app after ministers dispatched a formal inquiry in a deepening row between tech companies and users over data privacy.

## 句型二：聚集抗议 —— as ... converged on ... to protest against ...

**结构公式**

`[主体] — [具体名称] — are in [状态] as [数量] [人群] converged on [地点] to protest against [对象]`

**FT 原文**

> The craft — HMS Tamar and HMS Severn — are in crowded waters as more than 50 French fishing boats converged on the port of St Helier, Jersey's capital, on Thursday to protest against the island's restrictions on French fishermen.

**为什么好**

这句有三处值得抄。第一，**破折号插入具体名称**（the craft — HMS Tamar and HMS Severn —）：报道里点名，读者才会信；学生作文里换成具体的人、时间、地点，可信度立刻上升。第二，`converged on`——不是 went to，而是“从四面八方聚拢过来”，一个动词就写出了数量和动势。第三，`to protest against` 作目的状语，把“聚在一起干什么”说清楚，比另起一句 They did not agree with the rules 干净得多。

**❌ 学生初版**

Many people went to the place. They did not agree with the rules.

**✅ 升级版**

The teachers — three of them from the same department — were in the hall as scores of parents converged on the school gate to protest against the ban on mobile phones.

**💡 效果**

`converged on` 让“很多人去”有了方向感和数量感，破折号插入的具体身份让场景落地——同样是抗议，这句是画面，学生那句是通知。

**模板 + 例句**

`[主体] — [具体名称/身份] — [be] in [状态] as [数量] [人群] converged on [地点] to protest against [对象]`

- 社会话题：Residents — mostly families with young children — are in the square as hundreds of commuters converged on the station to protest against the new fare rise.
- 校园话题：The class — a group of 12 students — stayed in the library as dozens of classmates converged on the noticeboard to protest against the sudden change of timetable.

## 句型三：要求反制 —— Demands that ... be ... prompted X to threaten to ...

**结构公式**

`Demands that [主语] (should) be [动词原形] ... prompted [对象] to threaten to [反制动作] over [争端]`

**FT 原文**

> Demands that French ships be equipped with monitoring devices and meet other criteria to obtain licences prompted France to threaten to cut off its power supply to the Channel island over the dispute.

**为什么好**

这是“因果 + 升级”的教科书句。`Demands that ... be equipped ...` 用了**虚拟语气**——demand 后面的从句用动词原形 be，不加 was/were，这是学生最不敢用、阅卷老师最认的结构之一。接着 `prompted France to threaten to cut off ...`：一个 prompted 承接因果，一个 threaten to 写出反制，最后 `over the dispute` 点明争议对象。学生表达同样的意思，通常要三句：The island asked French boats to follow new rules. France was angry. France said it would cut off the electricity.

**❌ 学生初版**

The island asked French boats to follow new rules. France was angry. France said it would cut off the electricity.

**✅ 升级版**

Demands that all visitors be registered at the gate prompted residents to threaten to close the shared car park over the dispute.

**💡 效果**

`Demands that ... be ...` 一出现，语体立刻从日常口语升到正式书面；`prompted ... to threaten to ...` 让“因果”和“反制”在同一句里完成，文章从此不像在讲故事，而像在分析。

**模板 + 例句**

`Demands that [主语] (should) be [原形] prompted [对象] to threaten to [动作] over [争端]`

- 教育话题：Demands that all club members be tested before joining prompted parents to threaten to withdraw their children over the dispute.
- 职场话题：Demands that staff be available online at weekends prompted the union to threaten to file a formal complaint over the new policy.

## 句型四：条件红线 —— ... should [另一方] decide that ...

**结构公式**

`[一方] has the option of [V-ing 手段] ... should [另一方] decide that [从句]`，以及 `[一方] has warned [另一方] that [V-ing 作主语] breaches [协议]`

**FT 原文**

> Brussels has the option of opening formal dispute settlement procedures with the UK under the terms of the two sides' trade deal ... should the EU decide that Britain is violating the spirit of the agreement.

> Brussels has warned Britain that **applying conditions without prior warning breaches** this trade deal.

**为什么好**

`should + 主语 + 动词原形` 是**省略 if 的倒装条件句**（= if the EU should decide that ...），比 if 从句更正式，几乎就是条约文本的语气。前面用 `has the option of doing` 表示“手上还留着一张牌”，整句于是形成“我还没出手，但随时可以”的压力感。第二句 `X has warned Y that [V-ing 主语] breaches [协议]` 则把“违反”写成动词 breach——在协议语境里比 break、violate 都更精准——并用动名词短语 applying conditions without prior warning 作主语，说明“是这件事本身违反了协议”。

**❌ 学生初版**

If the EU thinks Britain does not follow the deal, the EU can do something. Also Britain broke the deal.

**✅ 升级版**

The union has the option of filing a formal complaint should the school decide that charging for club activities breaches the education agreement. It has warned the school that **introducing fees without prior notice** breaches the same deal.

**💡 效果**

“如果……就……”升级成倒装条件句，“违反协议”升级成 breach 加动名词主语——同样一句话，读出的是懂规则的人才有的分寸感。

**模板 + 例句**

`[一方] has the option of [V-ing] should [另一方] decide that [从句]` / `[一方] has warned [另一方] that [V-ing 主语] breaches [协议]`

- 校园话题：The committee has the option of reporting the case should the school decide that removing the club budget breaches the student charter.
- 环境话题：Campaigners have warned the council that **approving the plan without a new survey** breaches the city's climate pledge.

## 练习建议

1. **先抄后造**：把 4 个句型各抄 5 遍，然后用你自己的话题（不要用 FT 的英法捕鱼权争端）各造 2 句——保留结构，替换内容。
2. **写冲突只写“阶梯”**：重写旧作文时，把冲突段按 升级开场 → 聚集抗议 → 要求反制 → 条件红线 的顺序摆好，一段只写一级。四句读完，冲突自然从“有问题”走到“随时可能爆发”。
3. **对照检查**：凡是写 `It is serious`、`getting worse`、`They are angry` 的地方，问自己：能不能换成今天这 4 个句型之一？
