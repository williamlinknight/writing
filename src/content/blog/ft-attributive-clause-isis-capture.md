定从写来写去只会 `which is...` 和 `who is...`？那你的作文永远停留在"安全区"，拿不到高分。

## 📖 FT原文选段

> Iraqi security forces have captured the man once dubbed "Isis's finance minister" in a cross-border operation, the Iraqi prime minister announced on Monday.
>
> Prime Minister Mustafa al-Kadhimi said on Twitter that Iraqi intelligence forces had apprehended Sami Jassim Mohammad al-Jubouri, **an Iraqi leader of the Islamist terrorist group's so-called caliphate or Islamic state**, in a "complex external operation".
>
> Although the Iraqi government declared Isis had been defeated in December 2017, **the group — which once seized large cities across Syria and Iraq in an area the size of Great Britain —** has not been eradicated.
>
> It remains active in parts of Iraq and eastern Syria but **is a shadow of the force that once ruled its self-declared Islamic state with brutal violence.**
>
> **Jubouri was a veteran member of al-Qaeda in Iraq, the group that predated Isis.**
>
> According to the US state department, **which had offered a 5m reward for information leading to his capture**, Jubouri went on to become "instrumental in managing finances for Isis's terrorist operations".
>
> The US Treasury designated Jubouri a terrorist in 2015, claiming he had "worked to establish a new funding stream for Isil from increased production at oilfields held by the organisation" — **part of a portfolio that made the group financially self-sufficient.**

## 💡 4种定语从句高分结构

### 1. 同位语定语从句——"两段式定义"

**原文:**
> Jubouri was a veteran member of al-Qaeda in Iraq, **the group that predated Isis.**

**结构分析：**
`[A], [the/ a + 名词 + that/who从句]`——先用一个名词性短语（同位语）概括A是什么，再用定语从句进一步解释这个名词。这个"两段式定义"结构比简单写 `Jubouri was a member of a group that predated Isis` 紧凑得多。

**❌ 学生初版**
Alibaba is a company. It changed the way people shop online.

**✅ 升级版**
Alibaba is **the company that revolutionized e-commerce in China** — or, more precisely, **a platform that connects millions of small businesses with consumers.**

但真正高分的写法是同位语式：

**✅ 满分版**
Alibaba, **the company that transformed China's retail landscape**, has expanded into cloud computing and AI.

**💡 效果**
同位语+定语从句的组合，等于"名称→类别定义→具体特征"的三层递进——这是学术写作中引入核心概念的黄金公式。

✅ **模板：** `[专有名词], the [类别名词] that/which [具体特征]`

---

### 2. 插入式非限定性定语从句——"破折号强调"

**原文:**
> **the group — which once seized large cities across Syria and Iraq in an area the size of Great Britain —** has not been eradicated.

**结构分析：**
`[名词] — which [完整从句] — [谓语]`。注意这里用了**破折号**代替逗号——这在新闻报道中用于强调插入的信息量很大、或者带有对比/转折意味的补充说明。破折号的使用让读者在心理上产生"停一下，注意看这里"的效果。

**❌ 学生初版**
The company, which once controlled a large part of the market, is now struggling to survive.

**✅ 升级版**
The company — **which once commanded 40% of the global smartphone market** — is now struggling to maintain even a 10% share.

**💡 效果**
破折号让对比效果更强烈。从"曾经控制40%"到"现在不到10%"，中间插入这个从句就像给了读者一脚刹车，让对比的冲击力更强。

✅ **模板：** `[名词] — which [强调性背景] — [主句谓语 + 出乎意料的结果]`

---

### 3. 限定性定语从句 + "影子化"对比——现在与过去的并置

**原文:**
> It remains active... but **is a shadow of the force that once ruled its self-declared Islamic state with brutal violence.**

**结构分析：**
`[is a shadow of] [the 名词] that [过去的辉煌/强大]`。这里的限定性定语从句 `that once ruled...` 界定的是"过去的那个组织"——不是现在的，是历史上的。这个 "shadow of X that once..." 结构是英语中非常经典的"今昔对比"工具。

**❌ 学生初版**
The team is not as good as it used to be.

**✅ 升级版**
The team is **a shadow of the squad that won three consecutive championships in the 1990s.**

**💡 效果**
"影子"（shadow）这个词本身带有"残留""不复从前"的情感色彩，加上定语从句补充"曾经有多厉害"，对比效果拉满。适用于任何描写"衰落"的议论文主题。

✅ **模板：** `[主语] is a shadow of the [名词] that [过去状态/成就]`

### 4. 介词 + which ——定语从句的"高阶变体"

**原文:**
> the US state department, **which had offered a 5m reward for information leading to his capture**, went on to become...

原文中虽然没有显性的"介词+which"，但我们可以从 `oilfields held by the organisation` 这类表达中，引出最关键的高阶结构：

试想如果把第2句改写成更正式的形式：

> the oilfields **from which the organisation derived its funding**

**结构分析：**
`[名词] + [介词] + which + [完整从句]`
这是雅思/高考写作中真正区分"中级"和"高级"的关键语法点。大多数学生写不出来，但如果能写出来，印象分会大幅提升。

**❌ 学生初版**
The organisation got money from oilfields. These oilfields were controlled by ISIS.

**✅ 中级版**
The oilfields **which the organisation controlled and used to obtain funding** became a key target.

**✅ 高级版**
The oilfields, **from which the organisation derived a substantial portion of its funding**, became a primary target for airstrikes.

**💡 效果**
`from which` 代替了 `which... from`，把介词前置，整个句子瞬间有了正式感和学术感。以下是最常用的几个"介词+which"搭配：

| 介词 | 搭配场景 | 例句 |
|------|---------|------|
| in which | 在某领域/方面 | The field **in which** China has made the most progress is renewable energy. |
| from which | 从某处获得 | The source **from which** the data was collected proved unreliable. |
| for which | 为此目的/原因 | The reason **for which** the policy was implemented remains unclear. |
| through which | 通过某种方式 | The mechanism **through which** the virus spreads is still being studied. |

✅ **模板：** `[名词] [介词] which [从句剩余部分], [主句]`

### 📝 写作建议

把这4种结构按"从易到难"排序，建议的学习路径：

1. **先掌握** 同位语定语从句（第一段）——最容易模仿，且信息密度最高
2. **再练习** 破折号插入式非限定性定语从句——让你的论述有"节奏感"
3. **然后挑战** "shadow of X that"对比结构——在议论文中用于"今昔对比"
4. **最后攻克** "介词+which"——在需要体现学术感的关键段落中使用
