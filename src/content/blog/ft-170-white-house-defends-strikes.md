> 大多数中国学生写议论文立场段，习惯说"I think this is important"，就一句。结尾再补一句"this can solve the problem"。句子短、单一，说服力弱。
> **三词形容词并列 + designed to + 动词原形**是FT新闻和评论中常用的“立场加强组合”：先用三个形容词给行为定性，再用designed to交代目的，一口气说完“做了什么、为什么做、意图是什么”。

今天我们从FT关于白宫辩护叙利亚-伊拉克边境空袭的报道中，学习4种地道用法。

---

## 一、三项并列：用三个形容词给一件事“定性”

**📰 FT原文**

> Jen Psaki, White House press secretary, said President Joe Biden "feels confident that the strikes that he announced yesterday were **necessary, appropriate and deliberate** actions designed to limit the risk of escalation".

**为什么好**

中文学生常写"the strikes were necessary actions"，只用一个形容词。FT用了三项并列：**necessary, appropriate and deliberate**（必要的、适当的、经过深思熟虑的）。三个形容词叠加，语气庄重，读起来像一份经过审慎考量的官方立场表述，而不是一句随心所欲的断言。注意FT风格：最后一项前不加逗号，直接用and连接。

**📊 学生写法 vs 升级写法**

| 学生初版 | 升级版 | 效果 |
|---------|--------|------|
| The strikes were necessary actions. | The strikes were necessary, appropriate and deliberate actions. | 三项并列，语气庄重，像官方立场表述 |
| The policy is important and useful. | The policy is timely, necessary and proportionate. | 适合议论文立场段、建议段 |

**📝 模板 & 例句**

```
[行为] were [形容词1], [形容词2] and [形容词3] actions.
```

- The new regulation is a reasonable, balanced and evidence-based measure.
- This was a careful, principled and proportionate response.

## 二、designed to + 动词原形：写“目的”，不写“为了做”

**📰 FT原文**

> ...actions **designed to limit the risk of escalation**.

**为什么好**

中国学生喜欢写"in order to do"或"for doing"表目的。FT用**designed to limit the risk of escalation**（旨在限制事态升级的风险）。主语是"actions"（行动/措施），designed to自带“经过设计、深思熟虑”的意味，比单纯的目的状语更有说服力。搭配词：**the risk of escalation**（事态升级的风险）。

**📊 学生写法 vs 升级写法**

| 学生初版 | 升级版 | 效果 |
|---------|--------|------|
| The government made rules to reduce pollution. | The government introduced rules designed to reduce air pollution. | designed to = 旨在，主语是措施，更正式 |
| I study English to get a good job. | The training programme is designed to improve students' writing skills. | 适合介绍方案、政策、课程 |

**📝 模板 & 例句**

```
[措施/方案] is designed to + [动词原形] [宾语].
```

- The vaccine certificate system is designed to allow safer international travel.
- The tax increase is designed to discourage high-carbon consumption.

## 三、defensive + a response to：用“防御性反应”框架立场

**📰 FT原文**

> John Kirby, Pentagon press secretary, said operational and weapons storage facilities... were targeted in Syria and Iraq.
> Kirby said late on Sunday that the air strikes were "**defensive**" and a response to an "**ongoing series of attacks**" by Iran-backed groups targeting US troops and facilities.

**为什么好**

立场辩护的核心技巧：先把己方定义为“反应方”而非“挑事方”。**defensive**（防御性的）+ **a response to**（对……的回应）两个词一出，空袭就从“进攻”变成了“自卫”。后面再跟**ongoing series of attacks**（持续的一系列袭击），把对方打成挑衅者。议论文的反驳段、让步段可以直接借这个框架。

**📊 学生写法 vs 升级写法**

| 学生初版 | 升级版 | 效果 |
|---------|--------|------|
| We attacked them because they attacked us first. | Our measures were defensive and a response to the other side's repeated provocations. | 框架转换：从“互相攻击”到“正当防卫” |
| The law is against criminals. | The regulation is a response to rising public concern about safety. | 适合背景交代段 |

**📝 模板 & 例句**

```
[措施] was/were defensive and a response to [对方的行为].
```

- The curfew was defensive and a response to the recent wave of night-time violence.
- These counter-tariffs are a response to protectionist measures by trading partners.

## 四、clear and unambiguous：双形容词修饰，信息更密

**📰 FT原文**

> "The United States took necessary, appropriate and deliberate action designed to limit the risk of escalation — but also to send a **clear and unambiguous deterrent message**," Kirby said.

**为什么好**

**clear and unambiguous**：两个近义形容词并列修饰**deterrent message**（威慑信号），强调“毫不含糊”。学生常只写一个clear；FT双形容词叠加，语气更坚决。破折号后"**but also to send...**"是并列目的，和前面"designed to limit..."呼应，构成“既……也……”的对称结构。

**📊 学生写法 vs 升级写法**

| 学生初版 | 升级版 | 效果 |
|---------|--------|------|
| We need clear rules. | We need clear and enforceable rules. | 双形容词，标准更具体 |
| His explanation is clear. | His explanation was clear and unambiguous. | 适合立场表态、承诺类句子 |

**📝 模板 & 例句**

```
[名词] is/are [形容词1] and [形容词2].
```

- The government's position is firm and unambiguous.
- We need transparent and accountable decision-making.

---

## 总结：立场辩护段的4个工具

| 场景 | 推荐结构 | 例句 |
|------|---------|------|
| 给一项行为“定性” | 三词形容词并列 | The strikes were necessary, appropriate and deliberate. |
| 说明措施目的 | designed to + 动词原形 | The system is designed to protect personal data. |
| 为己方行为正名 | defensive / a response to ... | The curfew was defensive and a response to the violence. |
| 加强表态语气 | 双形容词并列 | a clear and unambiguous message |

**练习建议**：下次写议论文，立场段里故意用1个"designed to"和1个三词并列。这两个结构能让阅卷老师一眼看出“这学生的语言有档次”。
