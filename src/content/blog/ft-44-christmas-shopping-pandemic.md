你是不是经常这样写作文："The shop has many ways to attract customers. Christmas sales are important for the shops." 语法没错，但像流水账，没有画面感，更没有说服力。

好写作的秘诀，往往在于"用准确的动词替换泛泛的动词"，以及"用数据代替模糊的形容词"。

今天我们从FT一篇关于2020年疫情期间圣诞购物季的文章里，提取4个可以直接套用的表达。每一个都来自地道英文原文，适合高考书面表达、雅思小作文/大作文，也适合描写社会现象的议论文。

---

## 表达一：come up with — 想出，提出（方案）

> **原文**：The event planners at UK department store Selfridges **came up with** "Dial-a-Santa" virtual appointments as a way for kids to have a version of the traditional experience while still respecting social distancing.

**为什么好**："come up with"比"have an idea"或"think of"更地道，特别适合描写"某个机构或某个人想出了什么办法"。在写"解决问题"类话题（如环保、教育、校园生活）时，它可以直接替换掉学生作文里最常用的"find a way to"。

**学生写法**：
> The store thought of a new way to attract customers.

**升级写法**：
> The store **came up with** a pop-up Christmas market to tempt shoppers back.

**模板**：
> [Subject] **came up with** [a plan/a way/a solution] **to** [do something].

**造句练习**：
> Schools should **come up with** better ways to reduce students' screen time.
> (学校应该想出更好的办法来减少学生的屏幕使用时间。)

## 表达二：generate — 带来，产生（销售额/收入/影响）

> **原文**：...the shopping period between the Black Friday promotions on November 27 and December 25 **generates** 20 to 50 per cent of annual sales for non-food retailers...

**为什么好**："generate"原义是"产生、生成"，在商业英文中常用来表示"带来销售额/收入"。写图表作文或社会现象类作文时，用"generate sales/revenue/profit"比"have sales"或"make money"专业得多，也更像母语者的商业表达。

**学生写法**：
> Christmas shopping brings a lot of money to the shops every year.

**升级写法**：
> The Christmas shopping season **generates** 20 to 50 per cent of annual sales for non-food retailers.

**模板**：
> [Event/Period/Activity] **generates** [number/per cent] of [sales/revenue/income] for [subject].

**造句练习**：
> The new shopping mall is expected to **generate** millions of yuan in revenue each year.
> (这个新购物中心预计每年将带来数百万元的收入。)

## 表达三：account for — 占（比例）

> **原文**：In luxury, the Christmas holiday season **accounts for** roughly one-third of sales...

**为什么好**："account for"是描写比例的高频地道表达，雅思小作文和高考图表作文里非常管用。比"is"或"makes up"更正式，写"占全年销售的几分之几""占总人口的多少"时都适用。

**学生写法**：
> December sales are two times a normal month.

**升级写法**：
> December sales usually **double** that of a normal month, and the holiday season **accounts for** roughly one-third of annual sales for luxury brands.

**模板**：
> [Part] **accounts for** [fraction/per cent] of [whole].

**造句练习**：
> Online shopping **accounts for** more than a quarter of total retail sales in China.
> (网络购物占中国零售总额的四分之一以上。)

---

## 表达四：[Verb] they must — 倒装强调（必须……）

> **原文**：It is not exactly a recipe for the joyful consumerism that retailers and brands usually seek to stoke at this time of year. But **try they must**: the shopping period between the Black Friday promotions on November 27 and December 25 generates 20 to 50 per cent of annual sales for non-food retailers.

**为什么好**：这是一种修辞倒装，把"they must try"倒过来写，强调"无论如何都得去做"。在议论文结尾或转折段落用一句，能让文章节奏突然紧凑起来，给阅卷老师眼前一亮的感觉。注意这种倒装多用于较正式的书面语。

**学生写法**：
> Retailers must try to save the Christmas season.

**升级写法**：
> But **try they must**: the Christmas shopping period generates up to half of annual sales for many retailers.

**模板**：
> [Verb] [subject] must: [consequence/reason].

**造句练习**：
> **Change they must** if the company wants to survive in the digital age.
> (如果公司想在数字时代生存，就必须改变。)

---

## 总结

| 表达 | 适用场景 | 替换对象 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| come up with | 提出方案、应对措施 | think of / find a way to |
| generate | 带来销售额、收入、影响 | bring / make |
| account for | 描写比例、占比 | is / makes up |
| [Verb] they must | 倒装强调，必须做某事 | subject must [verb] |

下次写关于商业、节日、社会现象的话题时，试着用上其中一两个。准确的动词 + 真实的数据，就是你作文和其他学生作文的分水岭。
