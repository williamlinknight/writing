# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：逻辑衔接——解释链：把「机制」讲清楚的三段

上一篇讲的是把结论说得**准**（证据分级），这一篇讲把机制说得**清**。同一个维度，两面。

批改说明文和议论文时，我最常删的一类句子是这种：

> ❌ Aerosols are very small. They can stay in the air for a long time. Because of this, the virus can spread in a room. So we should open the windows.

四句话都对，但读者读完还是不知道**它到底怎么发生的**：颗粒有多小？为什么能飘？飘着又怎样？每句话都停在半路，用 Because of this / So 硬接下一句。这是典型的**因果标签式写作**——贴了标签，没给机制。

FT 这篇科学评论给了另一种写法：一个术语出现之前先给定义，一个现象出现之后立刻给机制，机制走完再把"所以人们这么做"接上。整段解释只用了三个标点，没有一个 because。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №58 — 值得重视的气溶胶传播说（FT中文网，2020年7月16日，by Anjana Ahuja，科学评论员）

## FT原文

> **How does coronavirus spread? Scientists are increasingly convinced that airborne transmission plays a role.**

> 新型冠状病毒是如何传播的？科学家们越来越确信，空气传播在其中发挥了作用。

> **Droplets (defined as particles at least 5-10 microns across) are widely regarded as the master key to respiratory infection.**

> 外界广泛认为，飞沫（定义为直径至少5-10微米的颗粒）是呼吸道感染的主要原因。

> **Due to gravity, droplets generally fall to the floor within several metres — hence the need for distancing. Infection can occur directly, when virus-laden droplets enter the eyes, mouth or nose of another person, or indirectly, when a person touches a surface on which droplets have fallen and then transfers the virus to their own eyes, mouth or nose, which explains handwashing guidance.**

> 由于重力作用，飞沫一般会在几米内落到地上，这就解释了为何需要保持社交距离。当携带病毒的飞沫进入他人的眼睛、嘴巴或是鼻子时，会造成直接感染；而当携带病毒的飞沫落到物体表面，某人接触这些表面并随即将病毒带入自己的眼睛、嘴巴或鼻子时，会造成间接感染，后者解释了卫生指导意见要求我们洗手的原因。

> **Airborne spread involves particles that are smaller than droplets, called aerosols (also called droplet nuclei or microdroplets).**

> 空气传播涉及比飞沫更小的颗粒，称为气溶胶（也称为飞沫核或微滴）。

## 骨架：定义 → 机制 → 分支 → 落点

四段话其实是一条固定的解释链：

1. **定义先行**：`Droplets (defined as particles at least 5-10 microns across)` —— 先把尺子交出来。
2. **机制落地**：`Due to gravity, … — hence the need for distancing.` —— 一个物理原因，一个必要做法。
3. **路径分支**：`directly, when … or indirectly, when …` —— 同一件事的两条路，对称排开。
4. **解释收尾**：`which explains handwashing guidance.` —— 把前面所有机制落到一个"人们已经在做的事"上。

学生的写法是"结论 → 结论 → 结论"，中间没有机制。下面四个技巧就是把机制这一步补回来。

## 技巧1：called / defined as / also called —— 术语第一次出场，先给一把尺子

**原文出处：** "Droplets (**defined as** particles at least 5-10 microns across)…" ／ "…particles that are smaller than droplets, **called** aerosols (**also called** droplet nuclei or microdroplets)."

**为什么好：** 术语不是装饰，是工具。FT 的做法是：**第一次出现时用括号或分词补一句定义，第二次出现就直接用词，不再解释。** 注意括号里的定义是可量化的——"at least 5-10 microns across"，给了尺子，读者才能自己判断后面说的"更小的颗粒"到底多小。而 called / also called 一次给出三个名字，等于告诉读者"你在别处看到 droplet nuclei 就是它"。

**❌ 学生初版**
Aerosols are very small particles. They are dangerous because they can stay in the air.

**✅ 升级版**
Airborne spread involves particles smaller than droplets, **called aerosols** (**also known as** droplet nuclei or microdroplets).

**💡 效果**
一句里完成了三件事：与已知概念对比（smaller than droplets）、命名（called）、给别名（also known as）。读者不会卡在术语上，后面整段解释才能往下走。

**模板：** `[术语] (defined as [可量化的界定]), …` ／ `…particles that [特征], called [术语] (also called [别名]).`

**仿写示例：** Microplastics (**defined as plastic fragments smaller than 5 millimetres**) are now found in tap water, sea salt and bottled drinks. These particles, **also called** secondary plastics, mostly come from clothing and packaging.

## 技巧2：Due to + 名词 … hence + 名词 —— 名词化因果，用破折号代替 so

**原文出处：** "**Due to gravity**, droplets generally fall to the floor within several metres **— hence the need for distancing**."

**为什么好：** 学生写因果是两句：Because of gravity, droplets fall fast. So we need to keep distance. FT 只用一个破折号。关键差异在词性：**due to 后面接名词（gravity），hence 后面也接名词短语（the need for distancing）**，因果两端都被压成名词，句子中间只留一个事实。hence 比 so 书面，而且它预设"读者会自己完成推理"——hence the need for … 等于"所以（你也能想到）需要……"。

**❌ 学生初版**
Because of gravity, droplets fall down in a few metres. So we need to keep social distance.

**✅ 升级版**
**Due to gravity**, droplets fall to the floor within a few metres **— hence the need for** social distancing.

**💡 效果**
两句变一句，逻辑词从 so 升级成 hence，而且句子只保留一个逗号加一个破折号，读起来像一条物理推导。写说明文、图表作文（原因—结果—措施）时，这一句能救整段。

**模板：** `Due to [名词], [现象] — hence the need for [做法].` ／ `…, hence [结果名词].`

**仿写示例：** **Due to** the way the corridor narrows at the stairwell, queues form within minutes at break **— hence the need for** a second exit on the east side.

## 技巧3：directly, when … or indirectly, when … —— 用副词对举排开两条路径

**原文出处：** "Infection can occur **directly, when** virus-laden droplets enter the eyes, mouth or nose of another person, **or indirectly, when** a person touches a surface…"

**为什么好：** 学生写"两种方式"必然是 First / Second，或者 On the one hand / On the other hand。FT 用的是**一对副词 + 两个 when 从句**：directly / indirectly。好处是读者一眼看出这不是两件事，而是**同一件事的两条路径**——所以后面才能用一句话把两条路一起收掉（which explains handwashing）。用 First/Second 写，两条路会被读成两个并列的观点，收不回来。

**❌ 学生初版**
People can get the virus in two ways. First, droplets go into your eyes or mouth. Second, you touch a surface and then touch your face.

**✅ 升级版**
Infection can occur **directly, when** droplets enter another person's eyes, mouth or nose, **or indirectly, when** someone touches a contaminated surface and then touches their own face.

**💡 效果**
两个 when 从句对称、同一主语、同一时态，路径关系一目了然；句子还省掉了 First/Second 那一层编号。

**模板：** `[过程] can happen directly, when [路径一], or indirectly, when [路径二].`

**仿写示例：** Misinformation spreads **directly, when** a screenshot is forwarded without a source, **or indirectly, when** a joke is repeated often enough to sound like a fact.

## 技巧4：which explains + 既有做法 —— 用解释收尾，把机制落到「人已经在做的事」

**原文出处：** "…**which explains handwashing guidance.**"

**为什么好：** 这个 which 指代的不是前面某个名词，而是**整整一段机制**。前面刚讲完"手会接触被污染的表面，再摸自己的脸"，紧接着一句 which explains handwashing guidance——机制立刻落到一个真实存在的做法上，读者会说"原来洗手建议是这么来的"。学生写到这里通常另起一句 This is why we should wash our hands（也能用，但连着几句 This is why 会非常碎）。用 which explains + 名词短语，是把解释**压成半句**接在机制后面。

**❌ 学生初版**
This is why we should wash our hands often. This is also why the government tells us to wash hands.

**✅ 升级版**
…, **which explains** the official advice to wash hands frequently.

**💡 效果**
前面所有机制，用一个 which 从句统一收口，句子不换气就落地。注意与"回指"的区别：this / these + 概括名词是把上一句**打包**，which explains 是把上一句**解释掉**——功能不同，不能互换。

**模板：** `…, which explains [某个既有做法 / 现象].`

**仿写示例：** Students revise most in the two days before a test but forget most of it within a week, **which explains** the school's new policy of short weekly quizzes instead of one mid-term exam.

## 把四步串成一段

定义 → 机制 → 分支 → 落点，一段完整的解释就这样长出来。示范（仿写，主题换成校园）：

> **How does exam anxiety spread through a class?** Teachers are increasingly convinced that it travels through conversation rather than through marks. Anxiety (**defined here as** a persistent sense that one's results will not be good enough) is widely regarded as contagious in the month before an exam. **Due to** the way students compare scores after every test, worry spreads within a week **— hence the need for** teachers to stop reading marks aloud. It can spread **directly, when** a student announces a ranking in the corridor, **or indirectly, when** a parent's question at dinner turns into a comparison with a cousin, **which explains** why several schools now send scores privately.

五句话：定义、机制、后果、两条路径、落点。整段没有一个 because，也没有一个 First。

## 自检清单

写完一段解释，问自己四个问题：

1. **术语第一次出现，我给了尺子吗？** 括号定义里要能量化（数字、比较基准），不能只写 very small / very important。
2. **因果是一个名词短语，还是两个短句？** 若写成 Because A, B. So C.，试着压成 `Due to A, B — hence C.`
3. **"两种方式"我是不是又用了 First / Second？** 若两者是同一件事的两条路径，改用 directly / indirectly、in some cases / in others、for most people / for a minority。
4. **最后一句有没有落点？** 机制讲完必须落到一个真实存在的东西上（一条建议、一个政策、一个现象），否则读者会觉得"讲完了，然后呢"。

## 针对性练习建议

1. **名词化改写**：把五句 Because… So… 改写成 `Due to + 名词 — hence + 名词`。素材：暴雨与积水、作业量与睡眠、广告与消费、地铁拥挤与迟到。
2. **术语定义**：为"算法推荐""碳足迹""内卷""躺平"各写一句 `X (defined as …), …`，括号里必须出现数字或可观察的标准。
3. **双路径段**：写一段 80 词的说明，主题自选（谣言传播 / 拖延 / 食堂排队），要求只出现一次 directly / indirectly 对举，并以 `which explains …` 收尾，不准用 First / Second。
