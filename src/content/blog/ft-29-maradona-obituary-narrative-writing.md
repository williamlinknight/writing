批改中高考记叙文和雅思小作文时，最常见的问题是“人物没立住”：写“我敬佩的一个人”或“一个著名人物”，全文就是“他是一个优秀的足球运动员，很多人喜欢他，他去世了大家都很难过”。语法没错，但完全没有人物感。

FT 2020年11月26日关于马拉多纳去世的讣告《Footballing world mourns death of Argentine legend Diego Maradona》，用短短几十段写完了一个传奇的一生，同时也写尽了矛盾：球场上几乎无敌，生活中与毒瘾终身苦斗；被政治左派拥抱，又被全体阿根廷人哀悼。今天提炼4个可以直接迁移到中高考、雅思写作的技巧。

## 📖 原文精选

> Diego Maradona, the footballer who led Argentina to victory at the 1986 World Cup, has died at the age of 60, with the sport mourning an iconic player with few peers on the pitch but who struggled with addiction throughout his life.
>
> 中文对照：曾带领阿根廷队在1986年世界杯上夺冠的足球运动员迭戈·马拉多纳去世，享年60岁。足球界悼念一位在球场上几乎没有对手、但终生与毒瘾苦斗的偶像级球员。

> He will be best remembered as a diminutive yet powerful player, who captained Argentina to victory in the 1986 World Cup in Mexico.

> Although Maradona embraced, and was embraced by, the political left around the region — he even died on the anniversary of the death of one of the people he most admired, the late Cuban dictator Fidel Castro — Argentines of all political stripes were distraught by the news.
>
> 中文对照：尽管马拉多纳支持该地区的政治左派，也获得了后者的支持……所有政治派别的阿根廷人仍因他去世的消息而悲恸。

## ✏️ 四个写作技巧

### 1️⃣ 用过去时串起“一生”，用完成时铺底蕴

讣告的叙事骨架几乎全是过去时：led / captained / scored / struggled / was hospitalised / died；开头用现在完成时 has died 引出新闻，过去完成时 had struggled 交代更早的状态。写人物经历时，先用过去时把关键事件串成链，再用完成时点明“一直以来的状态”。

**原文出处：** "Maradona had struggled with his health this year... Media reports suggest he died on Wednesday after a cardiac arrest."

**❌ 学生初版**
Maradona is a good football player. He died in 2020. Many people were sad about his death.

**✅ 升级版**
Diego Maradona, the footballer who led Argentina to victory at the 1986 World Cup, has died at the age of 60. He struggled with addiction throughout his life, yet on the pitch he remained iconic to millions.

**💡 效果**
过去时动词链让“一生”有了起承转合，完成时 has died 把新闻性和历史感写在一起，不再是“我很喜欢他”那样的感想流水账。

### 2️⃣ 用评价形容词替代 good / bad / nice

FT 不是说 Maradona “very good”，而是 iconic（偶像级的）、diminutive yet powerful（身材矮小却强有力）、distraught（悲恸的）、blessed but troubled（幸福却坎坷）。精准形容词本身就带画面感和情绪烈度，比 very + 形容词强十倍。

**原文出处：** "an iconic player with few peers on the pitch but who struggled with addiction throughout his life"；"Argentines of all political stripes were distraught by the news"

**❌ 学生初版**
Maradona was a very good player and people were very sad when he died.

**✅ 升级版**
Diego Maradona was an iconic player with few peers on the pitch; Argentines of all political stripes were distraught by the news.

**💡 效果**
few peers on the pitch（球场上几乎没有对手）比 very good 多一层“几乎无人能及”的比较意味；distraught 比 sad 多一层“震惊、崩溃”的烈度，阅卷老师一眼就能看出词汇量。

### 3️⃣ 用同位语/定语从句把背景信息塞进一句话

不要分两句写“他是谁”+“他做了什么”，FT 把身份、成就、关系全部压进同位语或定语从句，一句话完成交代。

**原文出处：** "Diego Maradona, the footballer who led Argentina to victory at the 1986 World Cup..."；"Gary Lineker, the former England striker who played in that game..."；"Pelé, the former Brazilian forward often cited alongside Maradona as the greatest ever footballer..."

**❌ 学生初版**
Maradona was a football player. He led Argentina to win the 1986 World Cup. He died at the age of 60.

**✅ 升级版**
Diego Maradona, the footballer who led Argentina to victory at the 1986 World Cup, has died at the age of 60, leaving a nation mourning an iconic player with few peers on the pitch.

**💡 效果**
一句话同时回答“是谁、做过什么、发生了什么”，信息密度高，节奏快，非常适合议论文里举例论点的开头句。

### 4️⃣ 用转折让人物立体：矛盾共存才真实

最动人的人物写作从不单面：马拉多纳被左派拥抱，却也被全体阿根廷人哀悼；他人生“幸福却坎坷”。先让步再评价，观点才有可信度。

**原文出处：** "Although Maradona embraced, and was embraced by, the political left around the region ... Argentines of all political stripes were distraught by the news."

**❌ 学生初版**
Everyone in Argentina loved Maradona. He was a national hero and a great player.

**✅ 升级版**
Although Maradona was embraced by the political left around the region, Argentines of all political stripes were distraught by the news — a divided country united in grief for a flawed genius.

**💡 效果**
虽然……但是……的结构承认了复杂性，让人物立体，也让议论文里的人物评价更有分寸，避免“全场最棒/一无是处”的绝对化表达。

## ✍️ 写作建议

写人物纪事，或在议论文里举人物例子，按这个顺序组织：

1. 同位语一句交代“是谁 + 做过什么”；
2. 用过去时串起关键事件，完成时交代长期状态；
3. 挑两到三个精准评价形容词，代替 very good / very bad；
4. 加一个转折，承认矛盾和复杂性。

FT 讣告的秘密是：他们从不直接说“他是一个伟大的球员”，他们写“few peers on the pitch”，写“struggled with addiction throughout his life”——让事实和细节自己说话，人物自然就活了。
