# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：定语从句的对称之美——平行结构与接力长句

批改作文时，我常看到这样的对比句：

> "Small particles have strong forces. Gravity also has a strong force. It works on a large scale. They are actually the same."

四句话，四个句号，逻辑全对，但对比的"对称感"完全消失。**写对比，最忌讳拆成短句**——因为读者看不到"最小"对"最大"的对仗。

而高手处理同一信息时，会用一个结构让对比自己说话：**平行定语从句**。

今天我们从《金融时报》一篇加来道雄《上帝方程式》的书评里，学三个定语从句的高级用法。这篇书评讲的是物理学史上最宏大的命题——万物理论——而它的句子结构本身就是一堂"如何把复杂信息写清楚"的课。

## 📖 FT原文选段

> The latest effort to do better is string theory — an attempt to link **the forces that govern the smallest particles within a nucleus** with **the gravitational force that operates on the largest scale of all**: stretching the length of galaxies and beyond.
>
> Its pages revealed that **the man some judge to be the smartest human being who ever lived** had been working on **a problem that even he had been unable to solve**.
>
> Even mass and energy, **which seemed as far apart as any two things could be**, turned out to be just different aspects of a deeper entity.
>
> Even with the limited tools of Einstein's era it was possible to imagine **wormholes that could stretch from one point in our universe to another**.

> **中文对照**
>
> 在这方面做得更好的最新努力是弦理论，该理论试图将控制原子核内最小粒子的力与作用于最大尺度上的引力——延伸至星系甚至更远——联系起来。
>
> 上面是爱因斯坦一直在研究的一个问题，一个就连这位被誉为有史以来最聪明的人也无法破解的问题。
>
> 即使是看似风马牛不相及的质量和能量，也只是一种更深层存在的不同方面。
>
> 即便是在工具有限的爱因斯坦时代，也能想象出可以从我们宇宙的一个地方延伸到另一个地方的虫洞。

## 三个技巧

### 技巧一：平行定语从句——两个 that 撑起一组对比

**原文：** "an attempt to link **the forces that govern the smallest particles within a nucleus** with **the gravitational force that operates on the largest scale of all**"

**为什么好？** 句子用 link A with B 的框架，A 和 B 各带一个 that 从句：`the forces that govern the smallest particles` 对 `the gravitational force that operates on the largest scale`。**最小 ↔ 最大**，两个从句像两扇对称的窗户，对比不用解释，结构自己会说话。学生写对比通常拆成两句，对仗感就丢了。

**❌ 学生初版**

> String theory tries to connect the forces of small particles. It also connects gravity, which works on a very large scale. They are actually the same force.

**✅ 升级版**

> String theory tries to link the forces that govern the smallest particles with the force that operates on the largest scale.

**💡 效果**

`that govern the smallest` 对 `that operates on the largest`——两个 that 从句结构完全平行，读者一眼看到"最小对最大"的对称。**写对比句的公式：先建好 A with B 的框架，再给 A、B 各配一个 that 从句。**

**考场模板：**

> The program balances the skills that students need for exams with the abilities that they will need for life.
> The city sits between the mountains that protect it from the north wind and the sea that brings it trade.

### 技巧二：接力式长句——从句贴着先行词，长而不乱

**原文：** "**the man some judge to be the smartest human being who ever lived** had been working on **a problem that even he had been unable to solve**"

**为什么好？** 这句话一口气装了四层信息：人物（the man）、评价（some judge to be...）、定语（who ever lived）、事件（had been working on a problem）+ 结果的限定（that even he had been unable to solve）。长，但每一层都贴着自己的名词：who 修饰 human being，that 修饰 problem。**定语从句的"接力"规则：从句永远紧贴先行词，读者按顺序拆包，就不会乱。**

**❌ 学生初版**

> Einstein was the smartest man. He had a problem in his notebook. But he could not solve the problem. The problem was too hard even for him.

（四句碎片，信息没有层级，读者要自己拼装逻辑。）

**✅ 升级版**

> The man widely regarded as the smartest scientist who ever lived had been working on a problem that even he could not solve.

**💡 效果**

一句话装下"人物 + 定语 + 事件 + 结果"，信息密度高，逻辑链完整。长句不可怕，可怕的是从句找不到先行词。**写长句前先问自己：这个从句修饰谁？它贴紧了吗？**

**考场模板：**

> The coach who had led the team for a decade faced a decision that would define his career.

### 技巧三：非限定 which 插入"反差"——主句保留反转力度

**原文：** "Even mass and energy, **which seemed as far apart as any two things could be**, turned out to be just different aspects of a deeper entity."

**为什么好？** which 从句负责塞进"反差背景"（质量和能量看起来风马牛不相及），主句负责给出反转结论（其实同源）。**反差信息进从句，反转力度留给主句**——这是 which 最容易被忽视的用法：不是补充细节，而是制造张力。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Mass and energy seemed very different. But they are actually the same thing.

**✅ 升级版**

> Even mass and energy, which seemed as far apart as any two things could be, turned out to be just different aspects of a deeper entity.

**💡 效果**

`which seemed as far apart as any two things could be` 把"反差"拉到极致，后面的 `turned out to be just different aspects` 反转也就越有力。写"看似无关实则相关"的话题，这个结构百试百灵。

**考场模板：**

> Even the two subjects, which appeared to have nothing in common, turned out to share the same core principle.

## 针对性练习建议

1. **平行对比练习：** 用 `link A that ... with B that ...` 的框架写一组对比（如"应试能力 vs 终身能力""短期利益 vs 长期成长"）。
2. **长句拆包练习：** 把你作文里最长的句子找出来，圈出每个从句，标出先行词。如果哪个从句离先行词太远，就拆出去另起一句。
3. **反差插入练习：** 用 `, which seemed ...` 改写一句"看似不同实则相同"的话，体会主句反转的力度。
