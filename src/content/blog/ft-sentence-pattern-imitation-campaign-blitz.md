## 为什么是这一篇？

描述"某人拼命做某事"或"时间非常紧迫"，是中国学生写作的常见死穴。大多数人的应对方式就是写 `He tried very hard` 或 `Time was very tight`——没错，但无法提分。

FT这篇关于特朗普竞选冲刺的报道，用了3种极其精妙的句型来描述"最后一刻的密集行动"。这些句型不依赖政治背景，**考场上任何需要"抓紧时间做某事"的场景都可以直接用**。

## 句型一：最后一刻的密集行动

**FT 原文：**

> Donald Trump set off on Sunday on an eleventh-hour blitz of five crucial swing states, as polls showed the president continuing to lag behind Democrat Joe Biden with less than 72 hours until polls close.

**句型公式：**

> [Person/Team] set off on an eleventh-hour blitz of [targets], as [situation showing urgency/deadline]

**为什么好？**

**❌ 学生初版**
> He worked very hard in the last few days to finish the project.

**✅ 升级版**
> The team set off on an eleventh-hour blitz of the remaining tasks, as the deadline approached with less than 48 hours to go.

**💡 效果**
`eleventh-hour blitz` 是英语中非常有力的固定搭配——`eleventh-hour` 表示"最后一刻"（源自《圣经》），`blitz` 原意是"闪电战"，合在一起就是"最后关头的密集冲刺"。`set off on` 带出动作的启动感，后面 `as...` 补充紧迫的时间背景。

**核心词汇拆解：**

| 表达 | 含义 | 在考场中的替代品 |
|------|------|------------------|
| eleventh-hour | 最后一刻的 | at the last minute, final-hour |
| blitz | 密集行动 | campaign, push, sprint |
| set off on | 开始（主动、有力） | begin, start (太弱) |
| lag behind | 落后 | fall behind, trail |

**考场仿写：**

> The rescue team set off on an eleventh-hour blitz of the affected villages, as the typhoon approached with less than six hours remaining.

> The student set off on an eleventh-hour blitz of revision, as the final exam was only three days away.

## 句型二：高强度日程的起止结构

**FT 原文：**

> Mr Trump will travel to five of those states on Sunday — Michigan, Iowa, North Carolina, Georgia and Florida — in a packed campaign schedule that began with an 11am rally in Washington, Michigan and will be bookended with a final 11pm rally in Miami.

**句型公式：**

> [Person] did [actions] in a packed [activity] schedule that began with [start] and will be bookended with [end]

**为什么好？**

**❌ 学生初版**
> He had a very busy day. He had a meeting in the morning and another meeting in the evening.

**✅ 升级版**
> The CEO visited three countries in a packed business schedule that began with a keynote speech in Shanghai and was bookended with a final board meeting in New York.

**💡 效果**
`bookended with` 是一个非常精妙的动词——本意是"用书挡夹住"，这里比喻"以……开始和结束"。"packed + schedule" 替代简单的 "busy day"，`that began with...and was bookended with...` 用一次从句覆盖了全天行程，比拆成两句更紧凑、更英语化。

**考场仿写：**

> The conference featured eight speakers in a packed agenda that began with a keynote address on AI ethics and was bookended with a closing panel on sustainability.

> Her weekend was a packed schedule that began with a volunteer event in the morning and was bookended with a family dinner at night.

## 句型三：大数字 + 对比参照

**FT 原文：**

> He predicted up to 160m Americans would vote in the election, compared to 136.5m in 2016.

**句型公式：**

> [Source] predicted up to [large number] [group] [action], compared to [smaller number] [previous period]

**❌ 学生初版**
> Many people went to the concert. Last year less people went.

**✅ 升级版**
> Organisers predicted up to 50,000 people would attend the music festival, compared to 32,000 the previous year.

**💡 效果**
`up to + 数字` 给出一个规模感的上限，`compared to + 对比数据` 在一个句子里完成增长说明。学生常见写法需要两句甚至三句，这个公式一句话搞定。

**考场仿写：**

> The government predicted up to 2 million tourists would visit the city during the holiday, compared to 1.4 million the previous year.

> Analysts estimated up to 80 per cent of students would choose online learning, compared to just 55 per cent before the pandemic.

## 写作建议

这三个句型有一个共同特点：它们描述的都是"紧迫、密集、有压力"的场景——这正是雅思大作文（尤其是"问题解决型"和"利弊分析型"）和高考读后续写的高频情境。

建议你：
1. 把 `eleventh-hour blitz` 和 `bookended with` 这两个固定搭配背下来——它们是最有"高级感"却最容易学会的表达
2. 练习用 `compared to` 把两个数据放在一个句子里，戒掉 `A is big. B is small.` 的拆分写法
3. 下次写"压力情境"（考试前、截止日期前、救援行动）时，刻意使用句型一
