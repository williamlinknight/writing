# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：句型模仿——主语只会 I think / People？跟FT学4个「让事作主语」的句式

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №111 — 英国智库呼吁将远程办公作为默认工作方式（2021年3月，623词）

## 为什么学「让事作主语」

改作文时我做过一个统计：同一篇议论文里，十个句子有六七个的主语是 I、we、people、students。剩下的句子，主语换成了 the government、the school。也就是说，学生笔下只承认两种东西会做事：**人和机构**。

于是句子永远是那个形状：*I think remote working is good. People can save time. The government should make rules.* 意思没错，但读起来像一连串的个人意见，不像分析。

英文书面语（尤其是新闻报道和分析文）里，大量句子的主语是**事情本身**：一项变化、一个需求、一次表决、一种趋势。让它作主语，句子立刻从「谁觉得」变成「什么在发生」。今天的素材是一篇智库报告报道，通篇主语都不是人——正好拆给你看。

上一篇文章教的是「数据怎么进句子」，这一篇教更前面的一步：**句子开头那个位置，放什么**。

## FT 原文

> Remote working should become the default in Britain, with employers legally obliged to demonstrate the need for staff to attend any particular workplace, according to the think-tank Demos.

> Homeworking is a regeneration tool, spreading the spending power of the people with the most disposable income.

> A shift to homeworking would boost affluent areas most because they have a higher share of white-collar professionals.

> The need to attend the workplace could be made clear cut, the report said, in the case of key workers or customer-facing roles, but could also encompass the need for networking, teambuilding, creative collaboration or mentoring younger colleagues.

中文大意：智库 Demos 认为，远程办公应成为英国的默认工作方式，雇主在法律上有义务证明要求员工到某个具体办公地点上班的必要性。居家办公是一种地区振兴工具，它把可支配收入最高那群人的消费力分散到各地。向居家办公转变，对富裕地区的好处最大，因为这些地区的白领比例更高。至于到岗的必要性，报告说，对关键岗位和面向客户的岗位可以界定得很清楚，但也可能包括社交、团建、创意协作或带教年轻同事的需要。

## 句型一：抽象名词作主语 —— A shift to X would boost Y most

**结构公式**

`[变化/趋势/需求] + [强动词] + [对象] + most / least, because [原因]`

**FT 原文**

> A shift to homeworking would boost affluent areas most because they have a higher share of white-collar professionals.

**为什么好**

主语是 `A shift to homeworking`——一个变化，不是一个人。它配的动词是 `would boost ... most`，一口气完成三件事：**给出因果**（因为白领比例高）、**给出程度**（most，不是笼统的 good）、**给出假设语气**（would，说明这是推演，不是断言）。学生写同一件事会说 *If people work at home, rich areas will be better because many people there are white-collar.* 三个短句，全是条件句式的直译，「更好」也没有刻度。

**❌ 学生初版**

If people work at home, rich areas will be better because many people there have good jobs.

**✅ 升级版**

A shift to online classes would hurt rural students most, because they have a lower share of stable internet access at home.

**💡 效果**

主语从 people 换成 A shift to online classes，句子就从「一群人怎么样」变成「一个变化有什么后果」——这正是议论文需要的层级。most 和 because 让判断既有程度又有依据。

**模板 + 例句**

`A shift to [变化] would [动词] [对象] most, because [原因]`

- 环境话题：A shift to public transport would cut emissions most in mid-sized cities, because their bus networks are already dense.
- 教育话题：A shift to project-based assessment would help quieter students most, because their strengths rarely show up in timed exams.

## 句型二：定义 + 分词结果 —— X is a tool, spreading ...

**结构公式**

`[概念] is a [定性名词/比喻], [V-ing 说明它带来的结果]`

**FT 原文**

> Homeworking is a regeneration tool, spreading the spending power of the people with the most disposable income.

**为什么好**

前半句给**定性**：居家办公是「一种振兴工具」——一句话把抽象政策装进一个可操作的概念类别。后半句用 `spreading` 把结果挂在逗号后面，不用 and、不用 so，语法上是伴随，读起来是「它的作用顺着这个动作铺开」。学生写定义只有一种打法：*Remote working is good. It can help poor areas.* 先判断后补充，两句都在原地踏步。

**❌ 学生初版**

Remote working is very good. It can help poor areas and people can spend more money there.

**✅ 升级版**

Reading is a recovery tool, restoring the attention span that short videos have worn down.

**💡 效果**

`is a recovery tool` 把「读书很好」这种空判断，换成「读书属于哪一类东西」；`restoring` 直接把效果演出来，读者不需要你再解释一遍为什么好。

**模板 + 例句**

`[概念] is a [定性名词], [V-ing + 它带来的结果]`

- 科技话题：A school library is an equaliser, giving students without a quiet room at home somewhere to work.
- 社会话题：Volunteering is a bridge, connecting retired residents with teenagers who rarely meet adults outside their families.

## 句型三：无灵主语 + 插入语 —— The need ... could be made clear cut, the report said

**结构公式**

`The need to [动作] could be [形容词/过去分词], [来源] said, in the case of [情形A], but could also [动词] [情形B]`

**FT 原文**

> The need to attend the workplace could be made clear cut, the report said, in the case of key workers or customer-facing roles, but could also encompass the need for networking, teambuilding, creative collaboration or mentoring younger colleagues.

**为什么好**

这句有三个值得偷的动作。第一，主语是 `The need to attend the workplace`——把「需要到岗」这件事放到主语位，而不是 *Employers need staff to come*。第二，`the report said` 插在句子中间，信息来源不抢主语。第三，`in the case of A ... but could also encompass B`：先给出清晰边界，再补上模糊地带——**一个概念先划清、再承认它有弹性**，这是学术写作最被认可的姿态。学生写类似内容只会 *Sometimes it is clear, sometimes it is not clear.* 说了等于没说。

**❌ 学生初版**

Sometimes it is clear why students must go to school. Sometimes it is not clear.

**✅ 升级版**

The need to attend class in person could be made clear cut, the head teacher said, in the case of lab sessions and oral exams, but could also encompass the need for group discussion, sports practice or simply seeing a friend struggle with a problem.

**💡 效果**

同一个「有时清楚、有时不清楚」，升级后变成了：主语是「需要」、来源在句中、边界用 in the case of 划出、弹性用 but could also encompass 承认。读者读到的是判断力，不是犹豫。

**模板 + 例句**

`The need to [动作] could be made clear cut, [来源] said, in the case of [A], but could also encompass [B]`

- 教育话题：The need to ban phones could be made clear cut, the principal said, in the case of exams and late-night study, but could also encompass ordinary lunchtime conversations.
- 环境话题：The need to charge for plastic bags could be made clear cut, the council said, in the case of supermarkets, but could also encompass small shops where alternatives are hard to find.

## 句型四：主张句 + with 挂件 + 来源后置

**结构公式**

`[对象] should become the default in [范围], with [相关方] obliged to [动作], according to [来源]`

**FT 原文**

> Remote working should become the default in Britain, with employers legally obliged to demonstrate the need for staff to attend any particular workplace, according to the think-tank Demos.

**为什么好**

这是「提主张」最完整的一句骨架：`should become the default` 提出主张（不是 should be encouraged 这种软话），`with employers legally obliged to demonstrate ...` 用 with 结构把**执行责任**挂上——谁负责、负责到什么程度（legally obliged，法律义务），`according to the think-tank Demos` 在句尾交代来源，让主张不是「我说的」。学生写建议段是 *The government should encourage people to work at home. This is good for everyone.* 主张软、没人负责、也没有出处。

**❌ 学生初版**

The government should encourage students to read more. This is good for everyone.

**✅ 升级版**

Twenty minutes of reading should become the default homework in primary school, with teachers obliged to set aside time in class rather than assign pages, according to a study by the city's education bureau.

**💡 效果**

主张从「应该鼓励」变成「应该成为默认」，责任从「大家」落到「老师必须留出课堂时间」，末尾再给出处——三句话压缩成一句，而且每一段都有抓手。

**模板 + 例句**

`[做法] should become the default in [范围], with [责任方] obliged to [动作], according to [来源]`

- 校园话题：A ten-minute walk between classes should become the default in senior high, with teachers obliged to end lessons on time, according to the school health office.
- 社会话题：Separating food waste should become the default in canteens, with managers obliged to publish weekly waste figures, according to a local environmental group.

## 练习建议

1. **主语盘点**：把自己最近一篇作文的每句主语写成一列。如果 I / we / people / students 超过一半，挑出三个句子，把主语换成「一个变化、一种需求、一次决定」，动词跟着换强动词（boost、cut、restore、encompass）。
2. **一句一挂件**：今天的四个句型里有三个靠「逗号后面的挂件」撑起信息量（spreading、the report said、with employers obliged to）。练习时先写主句，再强迫自己在逗号后补一个挂件，不允许用 and 连接。
3. **来源后置**：所有 `I think`、`In my opinion`，试着改成句尾的 `according to ...`。判断还是你的，但句子从「我的看法」变成了「有依据的说法」。
