你是不是经常这样写议论文："Many people disagree with the government's decision. The company had to sell TikTok because of the order." 意思表达清楚了，但语气太弱，缺乏论证的力度。

议论文的专业感，往往来自"用挑战、引用、被迫、依据"这样带逻辑的词汇，而不是泛泛的agree/disagree和have to。

今天我们从FT关于字节跳动挑战特朗普TikTok出售令的文章里，提取4个可以直接套用的表达。每一个都来自地道英文原文，适合高考书面表达、雅思大作文、大学英语议论文，也适合读后续写的理由段落。

---

## 表达一：challenge — 挑战，质疑（决定/命令/观点）

> **原文**：ByteDance has filed a legal petition **challenging** a White House order that would force a sale of TikTok's US operations, citing delays from the Trump administration to finalise a deal that would satisfy national security concerns.

**为什么好**：challenge在这里不是"挑战比赛"，而是"对……提出法律上的质疑/挑战"。议论文写"反对某项政策或观点"时，用challenge比disagree或oppose更有力、更正式。

**学生写法**：
> Many people disagree with the government's decision.

**升级写法**：
> Many experts have **challenged** the government's decision on data privacy.

**模板**：
> [Subject] **challenged** [decision/policy/order] **on the grounds that** [clause].

**造句练习**：
> Several consumer groups **challenged** the new regulation **on the grounds that** it violated user privacy.
> (几个消费者团体以侵犯用户隐私为由，挑战了这项新规。)

---

## 表达二：cite — 引用，（以……）为由

> **原文**：...**citing** delays from the Trump administration to finalise a deal that would satisfy national security concerns.

**为什么好**：cite在这里作分词，表示"以……为由，引用……作为理由"，比"because"或"say that"更正式、更精准，适合写因果段落和给出依据的句子。

**学生写法**：
> The company said the delay was the reason for missing the deadline.

**升级写法**：
> The company **cited** delays in the negotiation as the reason for missing the deadline.

**模板**：
> [Subject] **cited** [reason/factor] **as** [the reason for] [result].

**造句练习**：
> The school **cited** safety concerns as the reason for cancelling the trip.
> (学校以安全担忧为由，取消了此次旅行。)

---

## 表达三：be forced to — 被迫（做某事）

> **原文**：ByteDance **would be forced to** sell TikTok's US operations if it failed to do so by Thursday, according to the order.

**为什么好**：be forced to比have to更强调"外部压力、不得不"，带有"被形势或对方逼迫"的意味，写困境、让步、后果时非常合适。

**学生写法**：
> The shop had to close because of the pandemic.

**升级写法**：
> Many small shops **were forced to** close during the pandemic.

**模板**：
> [Subject] **be forced to** [do something] **if** [condition].

**造句练习**：
> If the law passes, tech companies **will be forced to** store user data locally.
> (如果该法律通过，科技公司将被迫将用户数据本地化存储。)

---

## 表达四：on the grounds that — 以……为理由

> **原文**：TikTok is also trying to persuade a judge in Washington to block that same order in a separate lawsuit **on the grounds that** it oversteps the powers of the International Emergency Economic Powers Act.

**为什么好**：on the grounds that = "基于……的理由"，专门用来引出某个行动的法律或逻辑依据，议论文论证段落和读后续写的理由段落用它，立刻显得专业。

**学生写法**：
> The court stopped the order because it went beyond the president's power.

**升级写法**：
> The judge **blocked** the order **on the grounds that** it oversteps the president's legal authority.

**模板**：
> [Subject] [verb] [object] **on the grounds that** [clause].

**造句练习**：
> The committee **rejected** the proposal **on the grounds that** it would increase costs too much.
> (委员会以成本过高为由，驳回了该提案。)

---

## 总结

| 表达 | 适用场景 | 替换对象 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| challenge | 对政策/决定/观点提出质疑 | disagree / oppose |
| cite | 引用原因，以……为由 | say / because |
| be forced to | 外部压力下被迫做某事 | have to |
| on the grounds that | 引出法律/逻辑依据 | because |

下次写关于科技、政策、社会争议的话题时，试着用上其中一两个。带逻辑的词汇，是让议论文从"说明白"升级到"说得透"的关键。
