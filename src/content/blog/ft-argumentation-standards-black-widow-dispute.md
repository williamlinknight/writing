议论文里最没用的一句话是 I think it is unfair. 读者立刻会问：按哪一条不公平？差在哪里？谁来量？

《金融时报》报道斯嘉丽·约翰逊起诉迪士尼的稿子（全文 602 词）里，每一句都挂在**依据**和**标准**上：原告按合同条款说违约，被告按履行情况说没有违约。今天我们借这篇稿子拆 4 个"立标准"的动作，用在任何争议型作文里。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №186 — 《斯嘉丽•约翰逊状告迪士尼在流媒体同步上映〈黑寡妇〉》

## FT 原文（节选）

> Scarlett Johansson has sued Disney, accusing the Marvel owner of breaching her contract by releasing the Black Widow film in cinemas and on its streaming service on the same day, depressing box-office sales.

> Johansson, the star of the superhero film, had agreed a deal to get paid largely based on box-office sales, and her contract promised Black Widow would be a "wide theatrical release", according to the lawsuit filed on Thursday in Los Angeles Superior Court.

> By breaking the "theatrical window" — previously a three-month period of time between a film's release in cinemas and at home — talent contracts that hinged on theatrical revenue were thrown into flux.

> Johansson projects that the decision to release Black Widow on Disney Plus cost her $50m, according to a person familiar with the matter.

> The company said it had "fully complied" with the Johansson's contract and that its release strategy "has significantly enhanced her ability to earn additional compensation on top of the $20m she has received to date."

## 中文对照（节选）

> 斯嘉丽·约翰逊起诉迪士尼，指控这家漫威的母公司违反了她的合同——把《黑寡妇》同一天在影院和流媒体上映，压低了票房。

> 这位超级英雄电影的主演此前的约定是片酬主要与票房挂钩，而她的合同承诺《黑寡妇》将"在影院广泛上映"；上述内容出自上周四提交给洛杉矶高等法院的诉状。

> 打破"影院窗口期"——此前指一部电影从影院上映到可以在家观看之间的三个月——使那些依据影院票房分成的艺人合同陷入不确定。

> 约翰逊估计，在 Disney Plus 上上映《黑寡妇》让她损失了 5000 万美元。

> 迪士尼表示，它"完全遵守"了与约翰逊的合同，并且其发行策略"显著提升了她"在迄今已收到的 2000 万美元之外"获得额外报酬的能力"。

## 动作一：指控三段式——行为 + 依据 + 损害

**FT 原文**

> Scarlett Johansson has sued Disney, accusing the Marvel owner of breaching her contract by releasing the Black Widow film in cinemas and on its streaming service on the same day, depressing box-office sales.

**为什么好**

- `sued ... accusing ... of breaching her contract`：**依据**是合同，可核查；
- `by releasing the film in cinemas and on its streaming service on the same day`：**行为**写到具体到"同一天"这个细节，指控才不是形容词；
- `depressing box-office sales`：**损害**用 V-ing 挂在句尾，成为行为的直接结果，而不是另起一句空话。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Scarlett was unhappy with Disney because Disney did something wrong.

**✅ 升级版**

> Scarlett Johansson has sued Disney, accusing the Marvel owner of breaching her contract by releasing the film in cinemas and online on the same day, depressing box-office sales.

**💡 效果**

一句话交代"谁告谁、告什么、凭什么、伤在哪"。凡是写到"不公平、不对、不合理"，先问自己：**依据**是什么？**具体行为**是什么？**损害**能不能被量？

**模板**

> [A] has sued / criticised [B], accusing [B] of [违反的行为] by [具体动作], [导致的结果].

**仿写示例**（社区话题）

> Residents have taken the developer to court, accusing it of breaking its sales contract by cutting the green space promised in the plan, reducing the value of their flats.

## 动作二：操作性定义——把 "wide theatrical release" 落成「90 到 120 天」

**FT 原文**

> "Both parties, as well as Disney, understood this meant that the [film] would initially be released exclusively in movie theatres, and that it would remain exclusively in movie theatres for a period of between approximately 90 and 120 days," the lawsuit said.

> Johansson interpreted the words "like our other pictures" to mean Black Widow would have a minimum 90 day theatrical window.

**为什么好**

争议的关键词是 "wide theatrical release" 和 "like our other pictures"——**都是含糊话**。双方的对策是各自把它翻译成天数：approximately 90 and 120 days / a minimum 90 day theatrical window。谁先给出可测量的定义，谁的论证就先落地。

**❌ 学生初版**

> The film was supposed to be shown in cinemas first, so Disney broke the rule.

**✅ 升级版**

> Her contract promised a "wide theatrical release", by which both sides understood a window of between approximately 90 and 120 days before the film went online.

**💡 效果**

含糊的词是争论的入口，不是论据。写争议题时，把关键概念（"公平"、"合理"、"上课时间"）先换算成可核查的标准——天数、金额、人数、比例——你的主张立刻变得可检验。

**模板**

> [文件／约定] promised [含糊的说法], by which both sides meant [天数／金额／比例].

**仿写示例**（实习话题）

> The internship agreement promised "flexible working hours", by which both sides understood at least three days a week in the office.

## 动作三：数字对撞——把「双方意见不同」写成两个可比的数

**FT 原文**

> Johansson projects that the decision to release Black Widow on Disney Plus cost her $50m, according to a person familiar with the matter.

> The company said its release strategy "has significantly enhanced her ability to earn additional compensation on top of the $20m she has received to date."

**为什么好**

争的是同一件事（这份合同下她该拿多少），两边都不给对手留空：原告报 **5000 万美元损失**，被告报"**已付 2000 万美元** + 还能再多挣"。`on top of` 这个小短语把"已经给了多少"和"还能给多少"叠起来，反驳的不是情绪，是金额。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Disney said the film made money for her, but she said she lost a lot.

**✅ 升级版**

> She estimates the release cost her $50m. Disney said the strategy had significantly enhanced her ability to earn on top of the $20m already paid to her.

**💡 效果**

争议段的收尾不要停在"双方各执一词"，而要落在**两个数字**上。数字对撞让读者可以自己比较，也让"我觉得不公平"变成"差了多少"。

**模板**

> [A] estimates that [行为] cost [对象] $[N]. [B] said [行为] has "significantly enhanced" [对象]'s ability to earn on top of the $[M] already paid.

**仿写示例**（物业话题）

> Tenants estimate that the new service charge costs each household 3,000 yuan a year. The landlord said the fee has "significantly improved" security, on top of the 200,000 yuan already spent on repairs.

## 动作四：回到依据的反驳——以及一条「反面教材」

**FT 原文**

> Disney said in a statement: "There is no merit whatsoever to this filing. The lawsuit is especially sad and distressing in its callous disregard for the horrific and prolonged global effects of the Covid-19 pandemic."

> The company said it had "fully complied" with the Johansson's contract.

**为什么好（与怎么用）**

注意这两句的**顺序**：迪士尼第一句在骂人（no merit whatsoever / sad and distressing / callous disregard），第二句才回到标准（fully complied with the contract）。

学生要学的是第二句。**反驳先回到依据**——"我按合同履行完了"是可以被核查的；"你太冷血"只是情绪。情绪句可以放在段末做一句加重语气，但不能当作论据。

**⚠️ 反面教材**

> There is no merit whatsoever to this filing. The lawsuit is especially sad and distressing...

这类句子在考试作文里几乎拿不到分：它没有回应对方的**标准**，只是给对方贴标签。真正有力的写法是：

**✅ 升级版**

> The company said it had fully complied with the contract, and that the new release strategy had actually increased her earnings.

**模板**

> [主体] said it had "fully complied" with [依据], and that [可核查的结果].

**仿写示例**（校园话题）

> The school said it had fully met the conditions of the agreement, and that the new timetable had been discussed with parents in writing.

## 写作建议：争议段四问

写任何"这算不算不公平"的段落，先过四道关：

1. 我的指控落到**具体行为**了吗？（by releasing ... on the same day）
2. 有争议的词，我给出**可测量的定义**了吗？（a window of approximately 90 to 120 days）
3. 双方的**数字**都摆出来了吗？（$50m 与 $20m）
4. 我的反驳回到**依据**了吗？（fully complied with the contract）还是只在骂人？

四问过完，你的文章就有了"标准感"：不是你觉得不公平，而是**按某一条、差了某一量**。这正是雅思议论文、高考英语读后续写的评论段，以及语文议论文都通用的底层能力。

## 练习建议

- 拿一道争议题（该不该禁止学生带手机进校园），写四句话：① 谁违反了什么、造成什么结果 ② 给关键词下定义（"上课时间"= 几点到几点）③ 两个数字对撞 ④ 回到规则的反驳。
- 雅思 Task 2 的 "to what extent do you agree" 题目，在开头段先给出你的**判断标准**（例如"衡量一项校规是否合理，看它是否可执行、是否对所有人一致"），主体段的每个论点都回到这条标准，而不是各说各话。
- 和配套的"权衡论证"一起读：权衡回答**值不值**，立标准回答**合不合规**——一篇高分议论文往往两者都要。
