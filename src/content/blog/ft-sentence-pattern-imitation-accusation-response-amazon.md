## 为什么学"控诉与回应"

学生写议论文，一碰到"批评、争议、冲突"就露馅：写批评只会 `People think it is wrong`，写回应只会 `The company said they were wrong`。两个问题：第一，**把冲突写成了情绪，而不是事件**；第二，**每个意思用一个句子，句子之间没有层次**。

今天从 FT 一篇关于华盛顿特区起诉亚马逊的报道里，抽出 4 个句型。它们解决一个具体场景：**怎么把一个"指控—回应"的冲突写得像新闻报道一样清楚**。这套句型不挑话题——写科技、环境、教育、社会问题都能用。

## FT 原文

> Washington DC has sued Amazon for allegedly violating the district's competition laws by illegally tying merchants to restrictive selling rules.
>
> Karl Racine, attorney-general for the District of Columbia, accused the ecommerce giant of charging third-party merchants fees of as much as 40 per cent of a product's price to sell through its website, and then requiring them not to charge less on other platforms.
>
> It comes at a time several large technology companies are facing charges of anti-competitive behaviour.
>
> But it will be seen as a test for whether other states and federal agencies can build their own cases against Amazon.

中文大意：华盛顿特区起诉亚马逊，指控其涉嫌违反竞争法；特区总检察长卡尔·拉辛指责这家电商巨头向第三方商家收取高达商品价格40%的费用，还要求他们不得在其他平台卖得更便宜。此事发生之际，多家大型科技公司正面临反竞争指控。但该案将被视为对其他州和联邦机构能否起诉亚马逊的一次考验。

## 句型一：起诉 + 事由压缩

**结构公式**

`[原告] has sued [被告] for allegedly [违规行为] by [手段]`

**FT 原文**

> Washington DC has sued Amazon for allegedly violating the district's competition laws by illegally tying merchants to restrictive selling rules.

**为什么好**

学生写"谁起诉谁"，通常拆成三句："The government sued the company. The company broke the law. It made sellers do bad things." 而 FT 用一个句子装下三件事：谁起诉（has sued）、以什么名义（for allegedly violating）、用什么手段（by illegally tying）。`for + allegedly + 动名词` 是新闻和法律报道的标准压缩写法，`allegedly` 还保留了"未经证实"的严谨。

**❌ 学生初版**

The government sued Amazon. Amazon broke the competition law. It forced sellers to follow bad rules.

**✅ 升级版**

The government has sued Amazon for allegedly breaking competition law by forcing sellers to follow restrictive rules.

**💡 效果**

一句话讲清"起诉—事由—手段"，逻辑密度直接翻倍，`allegedly` 让论证显得客观严谨。

**模板 + 例句**

`[机构/人] has sued [对象] for allegedly [动名词] by [动名词]`

- 教育话题：Parents have sued the school for allegedly collecting students' personal data by requiring them to install a monitoring app.
- 环境话题：Residents have sued the factory for allegedly polluting the river by dumping waste at night.

## 句型二：身份同位语 + 双动作指控

**结构公式**

`[姓名], [身份], accused [对象] of [动作A], and then [动作B]`

**FT 原文**

> Karl Racine, attorney-general for the District of Columbia, accused the ecommerce giant of charging third-party merchants fees of as much as 40 per cent of a product's price to sell through its website, and then requiring them not to charge less on other platforms.

**为什么好**

`Karl Racine, attorney-general for the District of Columbia` 是"身份同位语"——不用另起一句介绍人，用逗号把职务直接嵌进句子。更妙的是指控本身带两个动作：`of charging ... and then requiring ...`。学生写"他批评公司收费高，还让商家不能降价"要两句，FT 用 `accuse sb of doing A, and then doing B` 一个框架全收。

**❌ 学生初版**

Racine is the attorney-general. He criticized Amazon. Amazon charged sellers a lot. Amazon also did not let them sell cheaply.

**✅ 升级版**

Karl Racine, attorney-general for the District of Columbia, accused Amazon of charging sellers fees of up to 40 per cent, and then requiring them not to charge less elsewhere.

**💡 效果**

身份信息不再单独成句，指控的两个层面用一个 `and then` 串起来，句子有骨架、有递进。

**模板 + 例句**

`[姓名], [身份], accused [对象] of [V-ing A], and then [V-ing B]`

- 校园话题：The coach, a former national player, accused the team of skipping practice, and then blaming their poor result on bad luck.
- 科技话题：Critics accused the app of collecting users' location data, and then selling it to advertisers.

## 句型三：背景框架 It comes at a time

**结构公式**

`It comes at a time [完整从句]`

**FT 原文**

> It comes at a time several large technology companies are facing charges of anti-competitive behaviour.

**为什么好**

学生想把事件放进大背景，只会写 `Now many companies also have this problem.` 或者 `At the same time, ...`。FT 用 `It comes at a time + 从句` 把"当下正是……的时候"这个背景装进一个短语，句子不用另起一段，节奏紧凑。这是新闻写作里最常用的"背景锚点"，也是议论文里连接"个案"和"普遍现象"的桥梁。

**❌ 学生初版**

Now many big technology companies are also facing similar problems.

**✅ 升级版**

It comes at a time more and more technology companies are facing charges of anti-competitive behaviour.

**💡 效果**

`It comes at a time` 是明确的"背景信号"，读者一眼知道你要从个案跳到普遍趋势，段落逻辑立刻清楚。

**模板 + 例句**

`It comes at a time [从句]`

- 教育话题：The school's decision to ban phones comes at a time when many students are distracted by short videos. *(注意：口语里常直接加从句，书面可加 when)*
- 社会话题：The new policy comes at a time when public confidence in online privacy is falling.

## 句型四：事件定性 will be seen as a test

**结构公式**

`[事件] will be seen as a test for whether [从句]`

**FT 原文**

> But it will be seen as a test for whether other states and federal agencies can build their own cases against Amazon.

**为什么好**

写完事件本身，还要给事件"定性"——它意味着什么、将检验什么。学生只会写 `This is very important`，空洞。FT 用 `will be seen as a test for whether + 从句` 把事件的意义变成一个可验证的问题：这件事将检验"是否……"。这个句型是议论文的利器：任何个案都可以用它抬升到"它检验着……"的高度。

**❌ 学生初版**

This case is very important for other places.

**✅ 升级版**

The result will be seen as a test for whether other regions can build their own cases against the company.

**💡 效果**

"被看作对……的检验"直接点出事件的意义，比 "very important" 具体一百倍，还自带论辩方向。

**模板 + 例句**

`[事件] will be seen as a test for whether [从句]`

- 教育话题：The exam reform will be seen as a test for whether schools can really reduce homework pressure.
- 科技话题：The new rule will be seen as a test for whether self-driving cars can be trusted on public roads.

## 练习建议

1. **先抄后造**：把这 4 个句型各抄 5 遍，然后每个句型用你自己的话题（不要用 FT 的亚马逊）各造 2 句——造句时保留结构，替换内容。
2. **一次只加一个**：重写一篇旧作文时，刻意在"批评段"用句型一和二，在"背景段"用句型三，在"结尾段"用句型四，一次只加一个，写到变成肌肉记忆。
3. **对照检查**：写完检查一遍——凡是你写了 `People think ... wrong` 或 `It is very important` 的地方，问自己：能不能换成今天这 4 个句型之一？
