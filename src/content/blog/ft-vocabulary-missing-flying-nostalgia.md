# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：词汇升级——写"怀念"不只会 miss，跟FT学情感浓度词汇

批改记叙文和读后续写时，最常见的"情感词贫血"长这样：学生写"我想念……"就是 **I miss**，写"开心"就是 **happy**，写"难过"就是 **sad**。情感本身很丰富，词汇却只有一个档位——文章自然没有浓度。

今天我们从FT专栏作家迈克尔•斯凯平克(Michael Skapinker)的《你对飞行有什么怀念？》中学5组**情感描写词汇**。这篇散文（2020年11月）写疫情之下人们对飞行的怀念，通篇都在做同一件事：**把一种抽象的"怀念"写出层次感**——有人怀念飞机餐，有人怀念升舱，有人怀念独处，作者自己怀念的是"悬在两地之间的感觉"。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №8 — *Covid nostalgia for the days of taking wing*（2020年11月）

## 📖 FT原文选段

> We will fly again? Probably not as much as we did. But we will have our **fond memories**, even if they are not of the food.
>
> I miss trundling my wheelie bag along the glass walkway from the lift into departures at Heathrow Terminal 5, accompanied by the **light exhilaration** — which never goes away — of heading somewhere else for a bit.
>
> She also misses the solitude that allowed her to **plough through** her work.
>
> She does not miss the breakfasts on overnight flights, a **dispiriting** meal served in the dismal hours before landing.
>
> Above all, I miss flying's sense of **liminality**, of being between places. Flying alone — which, as a business traveller, I often did — is a pleasingly anonymous affair.

## 🎯 5组高分词汇解析

### 1️⃣ fond memories — 美好的回忆

**原文出处：** "But we will have our **fond memories**, even if they are not of the food."

**为什么好：** 学生写"美好回忆"只会 **good memories**。**fond**（深情的、珍爱的）比 good 多了"情感温度"——它不是客观评价"这段回忆质量好"，而是主观表达"我珍爱这段回忆"。一个词，从评价变成了情感。

**❌ 学生初版**
We will have **good memories** of our school days.

**✅ 升级版**
We will always carry **fond memories** of our school days, even the small, ordinary ones.

**💡 效果**
fond memories 是回忆类文章的万能开头词，比 good memories 更能让读者感受到叙述者的温度。

**例句：** Looking back on my childhood, I hold **fond memories** of the summers spent at my grandmother's house.

---

### 2️⃣ light exhilaration — 轻盈的兴奋感

**原文出处：** "...accompanied by the **light exhilaration** — which never goes away — of heading somewhere else."

**为什么好：** 学生写"兴奋"只会 **excited**。**exhilaration** 是"极其兴奋、精神振奋"的名词，比 excitement 更有力量；前面的 **light**（轻盈的）又给这股兴奋加了质感——不是狂喜，而是轻松的愉悦。FT用"形容词+抽象名词"的组合，把一个瞬间的情绪写出了层次。

**❌ 学生初版**
I was **excited** about the trip.

**✅ 升级版**
I set off for the airport with the **light exhilaration** of heading somewhere new.

**💡 效果**
light exhilaration 比 excited 高级在两点：名词化让情绪可被"描述"而非"喊出来"；light 这个修饰词让情绪有了质感。读后续写情绪段立刻有画面。

**例句：** There was a **light exhilaration** in the classroom as the students prepared for their first exchange trip.

---

### 3️⃣ plough through — 埋头推进（大量工作）

**原文出处：** "She also misses the solitude that allowed her to **plough through** her work."

**为什么好：** 学生写"努力做工作"只会 **work hard** 或 **try to finish**。**plough through**（像犁地一样一路推进）是英语里非常生动的动词短语——强调"任务量大，但坚持做完"。它自带画面：一个人埋头干活，像耕牛犁过整片田地。

**❌ 学生初版**
On the plane, she could **work hard on** her emails.

**✅ 升级版**
The long flight gave her the solitude to **plough through** dozens of unanswered emails.

**💡 效果**
plough through 比 work hard 多了"任务量大+专注推进"的双重信息，用在写学习、写工作的段落里非常出彩。

**例句：** During the winter break, I managed to **plough through** all the books on my reading list.

---

### 4️⃣ dispiriting — 令人沮丧的

**原文出处：** "She does not miss the breakfasts on overnight flights, a **dispiriting** meal served in the dismal hours before landing."

**为什么好：** 学生写"令人沮丧"只会 **depressing**。**dispiriting**（令人泄气的）和 depressing 的区别在于：depressing 是"让人心情低落"，dispiriting 是"让人失去斗志"——它强调的是"消耗人的精神"。搭配后面的 **dismal**（惨淡的、昏暗的），FT一句话用了两个高级形容词描述一个糟糕的飞机早餐。

**❌ 学生初版**
The food on the plane was **depressing**.

**✅ 升级版**
The overnight flight was served a **dispiriting** meal in the **dismal** hours before landing.

**💡 效果**
dispiriting 比 depressing 更书面、更精准；dismal 还能用来形容天气、环境、前景。两个词都是雅思写作高频高分词。

**例句：** Failing the exam twice was a **dispiriting** experience, but she refused to give up.

---

### 5️⃣ liminality — 阈限感（处于两者之间）

**原文出处：** "Above all, I miss flying's sense of **liminality**, of being between places."

**为什么好：** **liminality**（阈限）是个学术词，指"处于过渡状态、不属于任何一边"的感觉——坐飞机时你既不在出发地，也不在目的地。学生写这种感受会用 **in-between feeling**，而FT用一个抽象名词就概括了。抽象名词（-ity结尾）是英语写作的"高级感"来源之一，读后续写里描写人物心境转变时尤其好用。

**❌ 学生初版**
Flying made me feel like I was **in between two places**.

**✅ 升级版**
What I miss most about travelling is the sense of **liminality** — the feeling of being between places, belonging to neither.

**💡 效果**
liminality 是"一个词顶一句话"的典范。学会这种抽象名词思维，你的作文会从"描述现象"升级到"概括本质"。

**例句：** The first year of university is a time of **liminality**, when students are no longer children but not yet adults.

## ✍️ 写作建议

这5组表达的共同点：**它们都在给"情绪"做精加工。**

| 学生常写 | 升级表达 | 情绪层次 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| good memories | fond memories | 珍爱感 |
| excited | light exhilaration | 轻盈的兴奋 |
| work hard | plough through | 埋头推进 |
| depressing | dispiriting / dismal | 泄气、惨淡 |
| in-between feeling | liminality | 过渡的悬置感 |

**建议你**在写读后续写和记叙文时，先写"情绪清单"：这段里主角有几种情绪？然后每种情绪配一个"精加工"的词，而不是一个笼统的 happy/sad。情绪的颗粒度，就是作文的分数差。

**今日练习：** 用今天学的5组表达，把下面这段"学生版"升级成80词的记叙文段落：

> ❌ I miss my school days. I was happy when I was with my friends. We studied hard together. Now I am a little sad when I think about it.

试着把 happy 换成 light exhilaration、把 studied hard 换成 ploughed through，看看情绪的浓度变化。
