# 读外刊学写作：从「全民接种」学5个高分表达

写"政府做了什么、民众反应如何"这类社会话题作文时，学生最常见的困境是：动词不够用。推进只会 push forward，排队只会 wait in line，愿意不愿意只会 want / don't want。今天我们读一篇《金融时报》关于阿联酋全民免费接种的报道——713个词，从政府推进写到民众排队，从接种意愿写到推广手段，正好是一条完整的社会行动表达链。

## FT原文

> The United Arab Emirates is offering free Covid-19 vaccinations to all citizens and residents as it pushes ahead in the global race to achieve nationwide immunity against the coronavirus.

> ...has expanded access to all adults across the population of 1m nationals and more than 8m foreign residents.

> Security guards said they admitted more than 1,200 cars a day last week, some of which queued for hours to access the facility.

> Some residents are reluctant to get vaccinated and some western officials reported anxiety among their nationals about potential long-term side effects of the shot.

> Efforts are under way to encourage take-up of the vaccine.

## 中文对照

> 阿联酋正在为所有本国公民和外国居民免费接种新冠疫苗，在实现全民免疫的全球竞赛中继续推进。

> ……接种资格已扩大到所有成年人，覆盖100万本国公民和800多万外国居民。

> 保安表示，上周每天有超过1200辆车入场，其中一些车辆排了几个小时的队才能进入设施。

> 一些居民对接种疫苗感到犹豫，一些西方官员报告称，本国国民对疫苗潜在的长期副作用感到焦虑。

> 鼓励接种疫苗的工作正在进行中。

## 词汇解析

**1. push ahead — 持续推进、坚定前行**

- **原文出处**：as it pushes ahead in the global race to achieve nationwide immunity against the coronavirus.
- **为什么好**：学生写"推进"只会 push forward 或 develop。push ahead 表示"排除阻力继续向前"，比 push forward 更有方向感和决心；后面接 in the global race to...，把"竞赛"的紧迫感也带出来了。
- **考场用法对比**：

**❌ 学生初版**
The country is pushing forward with its plan.

**✅ 升级版**
The city pushes ahead with its plan to build more charging stations.

**💡 效果**
push ahead 自带"不被困难阻挡"的语感，写政策执行、计划落实都很贴切。

- **例句**：Despite the economic downturn, the government pushed ahead with its reforms.

**2. expand access to — 扩大……的获取渠道**

- **原文出处**：has expanded access to all adults across the population.
- **为什么好**：学生写"让更多人能用"只会 let more people use / provide。expand access to 是政策文本里的高频搭配——access 表示"获取权、使用渠道"，比 provide 更正式，也更准确。
- **考场用法对比**：

**❌ 学生初版**
The government lets more students use the library.

**✅ 升级版**
The library has expanded access to digital resources for students.

**💡 效果**
expand access to 一个短语就讲清了"覆盖范围扩大"，比 let more people use 书面得多。

- **例句**：The programme aims to expand access to quality education in rural areas.

**3. queue（作动词）— 排队**

- **原文出处**：some of which queued for hours to access the facility.
- **为什么好**：学生写"排队"只会 wait in line / stand in a queue。外刊里 queue 可以直接作动词，一个词就完成"排队"的动作，简洁地道，新闻体最常用。
- **考场用法对比**：

**❌ 学生初版**
People waited in line for a long time to get the tickets.

**✅ 升级版**
Hundreds of fans queued for hours to get the tickets.

**💡 效果**
queued for hours 比 waited in line for a long time 短一半，画面感却更强。

- **例句**：Students queued outside the auditorium for the guest lecture.

**4. reluctant — 勉强的、不情愿的**

- **原文出处**：Some residents are reluctant to get vaccinated.
- **为什么好**：学生写"不愿意"只会 don't want to / are unwilling to。reluctant 精确传达"并非反对、而是顾虑重重的不情愿"，remain reluctant 还能表示"持续犹豫"，写民意反应时非常实用。
- **考场用法对比**：

**❌ 学生初版**
Some people don't want to take the vaccine.

**✅ 升级版**
Some residents remain reluctant to be vaccinated.

**💡 效果**
reluctant 比 unwilling 更书面，remain reluctant 还能带出"犹豫还在持续"的意味。

- **例句**：Many employees remain reluctant to return to the office full-time.

**5. take-up — 参与率、使用率**

- **原文出处**：Efforts are under way to encourage take-up of the vaccine.
- **为什么好**：学生写"使用率、参与率"只会 usage rate 或 how many people use。take-up 是外刊高频名词，指"一项服务被接受和采用的程度"，写推广、普及类话题是神器。
- **考场用法对比**：

**❌ 学生初版**
The government wants to increase how many people use the service.

**✅ 升级版**
The campaign aims to boost take-up of the free service.

**💡 效果**
boost take-up 一个名词搭配，胜过一整句绕口的 how many people use。

- **例句**：Low take-up of the scheme has forced officials to rethink the policy.

## 写作建议

这5个表达串起来，就是一条"社会行动"表达链：

**宏观推进**（push ahead）→ **扩大覆盖**（expand access to）→ **具体场景**（people queued for hours）→ **意愿反馈**（reluctant）→ **推广目标**（boost take-up）

写"一项政策如何落地"时，从政府的推进决心写到民众的具体反应，再到最终的目标衡量——层次一下子就出来了。尤其记住：写"人数/规模"别堆数字，用 expand access to；写"民众态度"别只会 like / dislike，用 reluctant 和 take-up。这样一篇社会类作文，语言和结构同时加分。
