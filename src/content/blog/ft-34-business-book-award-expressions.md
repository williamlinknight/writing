# 读外刊学写作：从「2020年度FT最佳商业图书奖公布」学5个写「评价」与「变化」的高分表达

写「评价类」作文时，学生的句子几乎长同一张脸：*The book is very good. It is very popular. Many people like it.* 而一旦要写「变化」——一家公司、一个人、一个行业怎么从 A 走到 B——就只剩 *In the beginning... Now...* 今天我们看《金融时报》(FT) 2020年12月的一篇报道：彭博社记者 Sarah Frier 凭《无滤镜》(No Filter) 一书拿下当年的 FT/麦肯锡年度最佳商业图书奖。全文649词，没有一句「这本书很好看」，却把三件学生最怕写的事写得清清楚楚：**一笔当年被骂「出价离谱」的收购，如何变成丰厚回报；创始人为什么从不爽到离开；这本书为什么在疫情之后又变得切题。** 下面这5个表达，就是这篇报道里最值得搬进作文的部分——它们分别对应「让步反转」「情绪递进」「作品评价」「意义变化」「监管压力」。

## 📝 原文对照

**English:**

> Attacked at the time as a wild overvaluation, the purchase has paid off royally.

> Ms Frier recounts how Instagram's founders grew increasingly disgruntled and ultimately quit Facebook, after their creation became entangled in "a corporate struggle over personality, pride and priorities".

> The FT review of No Filter described it as "a compelling tale of jealousy", which also tracks the malign impact of an app that sets unattainable standards of beauty and lifestyle, fuelled by "alarming behavioural incentives".

> It has since taken on new relevance as technology companies have reported increased revenues and antitrust authorities have subjected Big Tech to tighter scrutiny.

**中文对照：**

> 这笔收购当时被抨击为出价高得离谱，结果却获得了丰厚的回报。

> 弗赖尔讲述了Instagram创始人何以在公司陷入"一场围绕个性、自豪感和优先权的企业斗争"之后，日益不满并最终离开了Facebook。

> 英国《金融时报》对《无滤镜》的评论称其为"一个扣人心弦的、关于忌妒的故事"，因为它还追踪了这款应用带来的有害影响——受"扰乱人心的行为激励"的激发，它为美和生活方式设置了难以企及的标准。

> 随着科技公司报告的收入增加，以及反垄断机构对大型科技公司进行更严格的审查，这本书显得更加切合当下。

## 🔍 重点词汇解析

### 1. pay off royally —— 一句话写完「当初被看衰，如今大赚」

**原文出处：** "Attacked at the time as a wild overvaluation, the purchase **has paid off royally**."

**为什么好：** 学生写这笔收购，会拆成三句：*Facebook bought Instagram in 2012. People said the price was too high. But the deal was very successful.* 三句都没错，但读者感受不到「反转」。FT 把「被骂」压成一个过去分词短语放在句首（Attacked at the time as...），主句只留结果——**pay off** 是「有了回报」，**royally**（像国王那样地）把回报的份量一次加满。整句读起来是「当年那一巴掌，如今变成了掌声」。公式：**Attacked / Dismissed / Written off at the time as + 负面评价, [主语] has paid off royally.**

**❌ 学生初版**

Facebook bought Instagram in 2012. People said the price was too high. But the deal was very successful.

**✅ 升级版**

Attacked at the time as a wild overvaluation, the purchase has paid off royally.

**💡 效果**

「very successful」是给结论，「has paid off royally」是把当年与今天摆在同一句里对照。写「坚持学一门特长」「一项被质疑的政策后来见效」，都能直接套：*Dismissed at the time as a waste of money, the programme has paid off royally.*

### 2. grow increasingly disgruntled → ultimately quit —— 情绪递进要「两级台阶」

**原文出处：** "Ms Frier recounts how Instagram's founders **grew increasingly disgruntled and ultimately quit** Facebook."

**为什么好：** 学生写人物离场，往往是一步到位：*The founders were not happy. In the end, they left the company.* 中间那段「越来越……」的心理过程全丢了。FT 用两个词搭出两级台阶：**grow increasingly** 表示渐变（不是某天突然爆发），**ultimately** 表示最终结果。人物从「有点不满」到「忍不下去」，句子自己会走。这个结构在**读后续写**里是硬通货——人物情绪转折最忌讳的正是「突然就不高兴了」。

**❌ 学生初版**

The founders were not happy. In the end, they left the company.

**✅ 升级版**

The founders grew increasingly disgruntled and ultimately quit the company they had created.

**💡 效果**

increasingly 把时间的重量加进来，ultimately 给结局盖章。写「学生从讨厌到喜欢一门课」「居民从怀疑到支持一项工程」，都可以改成 *grew increasingly sceptical and ultimately refused to co-operate* 这类两级递进。

### 3. a compelling tale of X / track the malign impact of —— 评价作品，不能只会 good

**原文出处：** "The FT review of No Filter described it as **a compelling tale of** jealousy, which also **tracks the malign impact of** an app that **sets unattainable standards of** beauty and lifestyle."

**为什么好：** 写读后感、影评、书评，学生只有 *The book is very good and it tells a story about jealousy. It shows the app is bad for people.* 两句都在说「好」和「坏」，但没有一处指出好在哪、坏在谁身上。FT 的评论是三步走：先**定调**（a compelling tale of jealousy——一句话给出作品的题材和气质），再用 which 从句**说内容**（tracks the malign impact of...——跟踪、记录某事物的有害影响，比 shows 精确得多），最后用一个 that 从句**落到具体**（sets unattainable standards of beauty——设置了无法企及的标准，替读者把「哪里有害」说清楚）。

**❌ 学生初版**

The book is very good. It tells a story about jealousy and shows that this app is bad for people.

**✅ 升级版**

The FT described the book as a compelling tale of jealousy, which also tracks the malign impact of an app that sets unattainable standards of beauty.

**💡 效果**

三步骨架可以直接搬到任何「作品/现象评价」题：定调 → 它记录了什么问题 → 问题落在谁身上。写短视频、写选秀、写消费主义：*a compelling tale of ambition, which also tracks the malign impact of a platform that sets unattainable standards of success.*

### 4. take on new relevance —— 写「时机变了，意义也变了」

**原文出处：** "It has since **taken on new relevance** as technology companies have reported increased revenues."

**为什么好：** 学生想说「疫情之后这本书更值得看了」，会写 *Because of the pandemic, this book became more important.* 语法没问题，但把「意义」当成了「重要性」在称重。**take on** 的意思是「获得（某种性质）」——relevance 不是变重了，而是**变得切题了**：书中写的事，恰好对准了当下正在发生的事。后半句用 as 引导背景（科技公司收入上升、反垄断机构收紧审查），时间和逻辑同时到位。

**❌ 学生初版**

Because of the pandemic, this book became more important to readers.

**✅ 升级版**

The book has taken on new relevance as more people live and shop online.

**💡 效果**

写议论文结尾最好用的一句：把老话题接到新现实上。*The debate has taken on new relevance as AI writes more of what students read.* 一个词的改动，就把「旧观点」变成了「当下必须回答的问题」。

### 5. subject A to tighter scrutiny —— 「被更严格地审查」的正式说法

**原文出处：** "antitrust authorities have **subjected Big Tech to tighter scrutiny**."

**为什么好：** 学生写监管，永远是 *The government is checking these big companies more carefully now.* 意思对，但「check」太日常，「more carefully」太含糊。**subject A to B** 是英语里表达「使A遭受B」的标准动词短语：B 换成 scrutiny（审查）、pressure（压力）、criticism（批评）、cuts（削减），句子立刻进入正式语域。注意两个细节：scrutiny 前加比较级 tighter（更严的），说明监管在收紧；主语是 authorities（主管部门），责任主体明确。被动形式同样好用：**be subjected to tighter scrutiny**。

**❌ 学生初版**

The government is checking these big companies more carefully now.

**✅ 升级版**

Antitrust authorities have subjected Big Tech to tighter scrutiny.

**💡 效果**

「政府更严格地管了」这句，在雅思大作文和高考议论文里出现频率极高。记住公式 **主语 + subject + 对象 + to + 名词**，以后写平台、算法、直播带货都能一句话收住：*Regulators have subjected livestreaming platforms to tighter scrutiny.*

## ✍️ 写作建议

这5个表达其实是一条完整的**「评价链」**：先用 **Attacked at the time as...** 把当年的质疑摆出来，再用 **has paid off royally** 给结果，用 **grew increasingly disgruntled and ultimately quit** 写人的反应，用 **a compelling tale of..., which also tracks the malign impact of...** 给作品/现象做判断，最后用 **has taken on new relevance** 与 **subject... to tighter scrutiny** 把话题接到当下。连起来就是一段有始有终的评论：

> Dismissed at the time as a wild overvaluation, the purchase has paid off royally. The founders, however, grew increasingly disgruntled and ultimately quit the company they had built. The book that tells their story is a compelling tale of ambition, which also tracks the malign impact of an app that sets unattainable standards of success. It has since taken on new relevance as regulators have subjected Big Tech to tighter scrutiny.

另外三个「配角」表达，写书评、奖项、竞赛类题目时特别占优势：**a deeply sourced inside story of X**（材料扎实的内幕故事，形容一本书/一部纪录片）、**a wild overvaluation**（出价高得离谱，写「被高估」）、**make the final shortlist**（进入最终入围名单，写竞赛/评选）。最后一个是学生在写「比赛类」应用文时最缺的一句：

> More than 400 entries were submitted, and only six titles made the final shortlist.

最后提醒一句：**评价类作文的分差，不在形容词的多寡，而在「有没有把时间和结果装进同一个句子里」。**FT 这篇报道里出现的评价词（compelling, vital, malign）都算不上生僻，真正拉开差距的是它让「当年的批评」和「今天的回报」出现在同一句中——这才是阅卷人记住的句子。
