# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：段落结构——跨段编号推进：把清单拆成段，每讲一课就落一次地

学生作文里的"三点建议"几乎只有两种死法。

第一种，全塞进一段：`First, we should... Second, we should... Third, we should...` 一段四行，读者一条也记不住。第二种，拆成三段，但三段长得一模一样：每段都是 Firstly 开头 + 一句建议 + 一句 because + 一句 so。结构上合格，读起来像三份复印件。

《金融时报》2021年6月16日的专栏《飞行员教我们的重启经验》（by David Bodanis，全文约1,040词，28个自然段）给了第三种写法：作者把飞行员的四条重启经验写成**四堂课**，用 first / next / third / finally 四个不同的序号句型推进，而且**每一课后面都跟一段"落地段"**——把驾驶舱里的操作翻译成普通人能用在自己生活里的动作。

这篇最值得学的，不是序号词，而是"编号"如何承担段落之间的关系：一个序号开头，读者立刻知道上一课结束了；一个落地段，读者立刻知道这一课跟自己有什么关系。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №165 — 飞行员教我们的重启经验

## FT原文（示范素材）

> Pilots don't use restarts like that very often, but when there's absolutely no alternative, the methods they use to make it work are instructive for the rest of us.

> 飞行员不会经常那样重启，但是当彻底别无选择时，他们恢复工作的方法对我们其他人是有启发的。

> Their first lesson is not to rush, but take the time to properly disengage.

> 他们的第一个教训是不要着急，而是花时间适当地脱离。

> Next is to understand limbo.

> 接下来是了解即将到来的不确定状态。

> The third lesson is about acceleration.

> 第三个教训与加速度有关。

> Finally there's the technique of spot-testing, which is used very widely.

> 最后是被广泛使用的现场测试技术。

> In business or government, having different units get practice in recovering from sudden shocks can match that.

> 在企业或政府中，让不同的部门练习从突然的冲击中恢复可能与此类似。

## 技法一：承诺段——先用一句话把"接下来要分几课讲"交代掉

**结构公式：** `[主体] don't [动作] very often, but when there's absolutely no alternative, the methods they use to [目的] are instructive for the rest of us.`

FT写法：

> Pilots don't use restarts like that very often, but when there's absolutely no alternative, the methods they use to make it work are instructive for the rest of us.

一句话做了三件事：**限定频率**（don't ... very often，不是天天用）、**限定条件**（when there's absolutely no alternative）、**宣布价值**（are instructive for the rest of us）。最后半句是"目录预告"——读者知道接下来要开始讲课了。

学生的清单文通常没有这一段：上一段刚讲完个案，下一段直接 `Firstly, we should...`。读者没有被通知，只被推着走。

**❌ 学生初版**

> English speech contests are very important. Here are some suggestions for you. Firstly, you should prepare early.

**✅ 升级版**

> Few students rehearse a speech contest more than twice, but when there is absolutely no second chance on stage, the methods experienced speakers use to prepare are instructive for the rest of us.

**💡 效果**

承诺段不是寒暄，它是**给读者一张目录**。有了它，后面的 first / next / third / finally 才不显得突然。

## 技法二：序号词换句型——四个序号，四种句法骨架

**结构公式（四种轮换）：**

- `Their first lesson is not to [动作], but [替代动作]`
- `Next is to [动词原形] [名词]`
- `The third lesson is about [抽象名词]`
- `Finally there's the technique of [动名词], which [补充说明]`

FT写法：

> Their first lesson is not to rush, but take the time to properly disengage.

> Next is to understand limbo.

> The third lesson is about acceleration.

> Finally there's the technique of spot-testing, which is used very widely.

四句话句子长度差得很远（最短六个词 `Next is to understand limbo.`），句型也各不相同：`is not to ... but ...`、`Next is to ...`、`is about ...`、`there's the technique of ...`。**序号词负责宣告"换课了"，句型负责让每节课有不同的开场姿势。**

学生常犯的错正好相反：Firstly/Secondly/Thirdly/Last but not least 四个序号 + 四句同样的 `we should`，读者读到第三段就开始跳。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Firstly, we should practise more. Secondly, we should listen to others. Thirdly, we should not be nervous. Last but not least, we should sleep well.

**✅ 升级版**

> The first lesson is not to memorise the whole script, but to mark the three sentences you cannot afford to lose.

> Next is to rehearse under a clock.

> The third lesson is about your hands, which will do something strange in front of a crowd.

> Finally there's the technique of recording yourself once and listening to it only at the end of the week.

**💡 效果**

四条建议内容差不多，但**段落之间的"接口"变了**：每段一小换，读者的注意力就重新上线一次。

## 技法三：每一课 = 专业段 + 落地段，两段一组

**结构公式：** `In [你的领域], [对应做法] can match that.`

FT 讲第三课（加速度）时，先用一整段写涡轮叶片和 27,000 磅推力的发动机——全是专业场景，读者完全插不上话。然后紧接着一段：

> In business or government, having different units get practice in recovering from sudden shocks can match that.

一个 `In business or government` 把前面的机械细节整体搬到读者熟悉的场景里，`can match that` 负责搭桥。再补一个括号 `(Getting enough rest ahead of time, avoiding "personal" fatigue, wouldn't hurt either.)`，加一条人人用得上的小建议。

**这就是编号推进里最容易被学生漏掉的一环：专业段后面必须有落地段。** 没有落地段，读者只会觉得"你说得对，但跟我没关系"。

**❌ 学生初版**

> The third lesson is about acceleration. When a rocket takes off, it must bear great pressure. This is very dangerous.

**✅ 升级版**

> The third lesson is about acceleration. When a rocket lifts off, every bolt is asked to carry a load it never meets on the ground. In a school term, the same thing happens in the last three weeks: a debate final, a mid-term and a sports meeting land on the same Thursday, and none of them can be moved. Getting one of them finished a week early can match that.

**💡 效果**

专业段负责**可信**，落地段负责**可用**。两段一组，一个编号点才算写完。

## 技法四：课与课之间插一段"难度提醒"，别让方法显得轻松

**结构公式：** `[动作] is hard. Yet if [主体] keeps [错误做法] — like [日常比喻] — [系统] will [后果], and never [预期结果].`

FT写法：

> Waiting out limbo periods is hard. Yet if a pilot keeps nervously commanding further restarts — like someone anxiously stabbing a lift button — systems keep being sent back to the start, and never build up the fixes in time.

一句 `Waiting out limbo periods is hard.` 独立成句，先承认难。接着用 `Yet if ...` 摆出一个具体的错误动作，再用破折号塞一个生活比喻（`like someone anxiously stabbing a lift button`），最后才给后果。

学生写建议最爱直线推进：第一条好，第二条更好，第三条最好。**没有任何一段告诉读者"你大概会在这里做错"**，于是文章没有重量。这一段就是重量。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Rehearsing under a clock is useful. You should do it every day.

**✅ 升级版**

> Rehearsing under a clock is uncomfortable. Yet if a speaker keeps restarting the same paragraph every time a word slips — like someone dragging a video back to the beginning — the last two minutes never get rehearsed at all.

**💡 效果**

承认难 → 指出错误做法 → 给后果。三步写完，一段就够。

## 技法五：结尾把四课压成破折号清单

**结构公式：** `[判断句] that are most likely to [结果]: [主体] have the useful experience that clarifies the necessary steps:` + 四条 `— [动词原形] ...`

FT 的最后几段先把全篇判断收成一句：

> It's our moderate restarts that are most likely to do better: going back not as far as we might dream, yet clearing away enough entanglements so that changes which have wide appeal can work. Pilots and engineers have the useful experience that clarifies the necessary steps:

然后四条破折号：

> — configure for a safe pause

> — prepare for the limbo you'll be in

> — toughen yourself for dizzying acceleration when you restart

这个结尾有三个条件：**前面已经逐课展开过**（不是新信息）、**每条五到十个词**、**全部动词开头**。所以它读起来是回顾，不是补充。

学生的结尾是反的：In conclusion 一开，接着把前文四句建议原样再说一遍，字数翻倍，信息为零。

**❌ 学生初版**

> In conclusion, we should prepare early, practise more, not be nervous and sleep well. Only in this way can we do better in the speech contest.

**✅ 升级版**

> It is the medium-sized preparations that tend to work: not the script you rewrite from scratch, but the three sentences you refuse to lose. Speakers who have done it before can name the steps:

> — mark the lines you cannot cut

> — rehearse once under a clock

> — record yourself and listen late

**💡 效果**

一样的建议，一次是"再总结一遍"，一次是"再点一遍"。前者让读者疲劳，后者让读者带走。

## 写作建议：编号清单五步落地

1. **先写承诺段**：一句限定频率、一句限定条件、一句"对你有用"，再开始第一点。
2. **数一数你打算讲几点**，然后给每个点换一个序号句型——不要四个一样。
3. **每个点后面问一句"这在普通人生活里对应什么"**，答不出来就补一个落地段。
4. **在第二点和第三点之间插一段难度提醒**：承认难、指出常见错误、给后果。
5. **结尾改清单**：动词开头、五到十个词、只点不解释。

一篇"三点建议"的作文，只要做到第 3 步和第 5 步，就已经比绝大多数同龄人清楚。

## 小练习

下面这段是最典型的"清单塞一段 + 总结式结尾"，请按上面的结构拆开：补一个承诺段、给三点各换一个序号句型、每点后面加一个落地段、把结尾改成动词开头的破折号清单。

> In conclusion, if we want to improve our English writing, we should do three things. Firstly, we should read more. Secondly, we should write more. Thirdly, we should ask our teacher for help. Only in this way can we improve our writing.
