突发新闻（breaking news）的写作风格有一个鲜明特点：**信息密集但不断裂**。记者需要在极短篇幅内传递事件、伤亡、地点、发言人、背景——这些信息如果平铺直叙，读起来就像流水账。

今天这篇FT关于喀布尔机场爆炸的报道只有479个词，却包含了**至少7个同位语结构**。我们从中挑出三个进阶技巧，让你的写作从「把信息列出来」升级为「把信息织进去」。

> General Kenneth McKenzie, head of the Pentagon's central command, said he expected Isis to strike again. "We believe it is their desire to continue those attacks … and we're doing everything we can to be prepared," he said. "If we can find who's associated with this, we will go after them."
>
> 五角大楼中央司令部司令肯尼思·麦肯齐上将表示，他预计伊斯兰国会再次袭击。"我们认为，他们想继续这种袭击……我们正在尽一切努力为此做准备。"他表示，"如果我们能找出谁与袭击有关，我们会对付他们。"

> Rita Katz, director of the Site Intelligence Group, which monitors Islamist groups' communications, said Isis had claimed responsibility for the attacks through its Amaq news agency.
>
> 监控伊斯兰极端组织通讯的Site Intelligence Group的主管丽塔·卡茨表示，伊斯兰国已通过其阿马克通讯社宣布为两起袭击负责。

> At least 13 members of the US military have died along with dozens of civilians after two bombs exploded near Kabul airport on Thursday while western countries were scrambling to complete their final evacuation flights from Afghanistan.
>
> 喀布尔机场附近发生两起炸弹爆炸事件，造成至少13名美国军人和数十名平民丧生。眼下西方国家正在争分夺秒完成最后阶段的撤退飞行任务。

## 技巧一：缩写全称同位语（Acronym, Full Name, rest...）

**公式：** `[Acronym], the [full expanded form], + predicate`

这是新闻英语中最「隐形」但最实用的同位语。记者在第一次提到缩写机构时，必须交代全称——英文中通常用同位语结构来实现。

**❌ 学生初版**
The Pentagon said Isis claimed responsibility for the attack. The Islamic State group carried out suicide bombing at Kabul airport.

**✅ 升级版**
General Kenneth McKenzie, head of the Pentagon's central command, said he expected **Isis, the Islamic State militant group**, to strike again.

**💡 效果**
学生版的缺陷是：两个句子之间的逻辑关系是隐含的（Isis = Islamic State group），但读者需要自己推断。升级版用一个同位语就把缩写和全称的关系直接交代了——句子更紧凑，读者不需要「靠自己连接」。在雅思写作或高考议论文中，第一次提到 WTO、WHO、UNESCO 等缩写时，用同位语展开能让考官觉得你写得专业。

**句型模板：**
> [Subject] [verb] that [Acronym], the [full expanded description], [action/clause].

**仿写例句：**
> The report highlights the role of **OECD**, the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development, in setting global tax standards.

## 技巧二：嵌套式同位语（Appositive within Appositive）

**公式：** `[Name], [Title], which/who [nested clause], + rest...`

这是一个进阶技巧——在已有的「姓名 + 头衔」同位语内部，再嵌入一个定语从句。原文中的 `Rita Katz, director of the Site Intelligence Group, which monitors Islamist groups' communications` 就是经典案例。

**❌ 学生初版**
Rita Katz is the director of the Site Intelligence Group. It monitors Islamist groups' communications. She said Isis had claimed responsibility.

**✅ 升级版**
Rita Katz, director of the Site Intelligence Group, which monitors Islamist groups' communications, said Isis had claimed responsibility for the attacks through its Amaq news agency.

**💡 效果**
学生版用三个短句塞了三层信息：谁、什么职位、做了什么事。升级版用一个「嵌套式同位语」把三层信息压缩进一个句子——第一层是 `Name, Title`（Rita Katz + 职位），第二层是 `Title, which + clause`（对职位的展开说明）。这种结构在阅读时非常流畅，在写作中则展示了高超的从句控制能力。

**⚠️ 使用注意：** 嵌套式同位语对从句控制力的要求较高，适合高中作文或雅思6.5+水平。如果连基本定语从句还不熟练，建议先掌握本系列的「定语从句技巧」篇再来用这个进阶结构。

**句型模板：**
> [Person's Name], [Title/Position], which/who [additional explanation], [main verb + rest].

**仿写例句：**
> Satya Nadella, CEO of Microsoft, which employs more than 220,000 people worldwide, announced a 10 billion investment in AI infrastructure.

## 技巧三：数字同位语（Number + noun appositive）

**公式：** `[Numerical fact], [precise figure with unit], + continuing clause...`

在新闻报道中，数字信息通常不会单独成句，而是以同位语的形式「贴」在主干句子上。原文第一句话就是一个典型例子。

**❌ 学生初版**
At least 13 members of the US military have died. Dozens of civilians also died. Two bombs exploded near Kabul airport.

**✅ 升级版**
At least 13 members of the US military have died **along with dozens of civilians** after two bombs exploded near Kabul airport on Thursday while western countries were scrambling to complete their final evacuation flights from Afghanistan.

**💡 效果**
学生版的缺陷不是语法错误——而是节奏。三个短句把读者「切」成了三段叙述，读起来像在念清单。升级版用 `along with dozens of civilians` 这个介词短语把两个伤亡数字合并，再用 `after...` 和 `while...` 两个从句交代原因和背景——一句话就建立了一个「事件全景」。

其实 FT 原文中还有另一个数字同位语值得学习：

> The Associated Press reported that **at least 60 Afghan civilians died and more than 140 were wounded**, citing US and Afghan officials.

这里的 `citing US and Afghan officials` 是一个典型的「信息源同位语」——在数据后面补充信源，让数据有据可查。这在议论文中是极具说服力的写法。

**句型模板（数据 + 信源同位语）：**
> [Source] reported that [numerical fact], [citing/according to/based on] [source details].

**仿写例句：**
> The UN estimated that more than 300,000 people had been displaced, citing data from local humanitarian agencies.

## 练习建议

1. **缩写展开练习：** 写出以下缩写的全称同位语形式，各造一个句：UNESCO, IMF, NASA, WHO。

2. **嵌套式改写：** 把下面三个短句合并成一个含嵌套式同位语的句子：
   - Dr Sarah Chen is the lead researcher at the Center for Climate Studies.
   - The Center for Climate Studies published a landmark report on Arctic ice loss.
   - The report warns that Arctic summer ice could disappear by 2040.

3. **数字同位语练习：** 用以下素材，写一个含数字同位语和信息源同位语的句子：
   - 数据：150万人失业
   - 信源：政府统计局
   - 时间范围：2025年第二季度
