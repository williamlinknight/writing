# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：词汇升级——写"政策出台、承压、收尾、试探反应"只会 start 和 stop，跟FT学5个政策动作动词

批改"政府出台一个计划来帮助经济"这类议论文，学生的段落几乎年年一样：*The government made a plan to help the economy. It started in 2020. Later the problem became better. Now they stop it. If the market has problems, they will think of a new way.*——观点都对，但**动作只有 make、start、stop**，整段读起来像会议纪要。

其实一件工具、一项政策是有"一生"的：**出台 → 承压 → 见效 → 收尾 → 试探下一步**。FT 报道央行退出紧急工具时，用的就是一组精准动词，把每一拍都写成可验证的动作。今天我们从这篇报道里学 5 个**政策动作动词**。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №157 — *美联储将逐步出售疫情期间所购公司债券*（2021年6月，记者 Colby Smith 发自纽约，567词）

## 📖 FT原文选段

> The aim is to wrap up the process by the end of the year, according to a Fed official.

> It was rolled out in April last year alongside 12 other facilities aimed at supporting a range of debt markets that had come under severe pressure as the US economy shut down.

> Corporate bond prices rallied and companies hamstrung by the pandemic remained able to access capital markets.

> SMCCF portfolio sales will be gradual and orderly, and will aim to minimise the potential for any adverse impact on market functioning by taking into account daily liquidity and trading conditions for exchange traded funds and corporate bonds.

> "This is one little step and a good way to test the waters in terms of what the market reaction is going to be," said Leary.

**中文对照**

> 一名美联储官员表示，目标是在今年年底前了结这一过程。
>
> 该工具去年4月与另外12项工具一同推出，旨在支持因美国经济关停而承受巨大压力的一系列债券市场。
>
> 公司债券价格上涨，而被疫情束缚住手脚的企业仍能进入资本市场。
>
> SMCCF投资组合的出售将是循序渐进、有条不紊的，目标是把对市场运行的潜在不利影响降到最低——具体会考虑ETF和企业债券的日流动性和交易情况。
>
> 利里说："这只是一小步，也是试探市场将作何反应的一个好办法。"

## 🎯 5个高分动词解析

### 1️⃣ hamstrung by — 被……束缚住手脚

**原文出处：** "companies **hamstrung by** the pandemic remained able to access capital markets"

**为什么好：** 学生写"受……影响"永远只有 *affected by* 或 *because of*。hamstring 本义是"割断腿筋"，所以 **be hamstrung by** 的画面是"被绑住手脚、想动动不了"：**hamstrung by a lack of funding**（被资金不足捆住手脚）、**hamstrung by regulation**（受制于监管）、**hamstrung by debt**（被债务拖住）。

**❌ 学生初版**
Many companies were affected by the pandemic and could not borrow money.

**✅ 升级版**
Companies hamstrung by the pandemic could still access capital markets.

**💡 效果**
affected by 只说明"有关系"，hamstrung by 说明"想动却动不了"——写企业困境、政策效果、个人处境时，它一句话就能交代"为什么需要帮助"。

**例句：** Small firms are hamstrung by rising energy costs.

---

### 2️⃣ come under severe pressure — 承受巨大压力

**原文出处：** "a range of debt markets that had **come under severe pressure** as the US economy shut down"

**为什么好：** 学生写"压力很大"只会 *have big pressure*（中式搭配）。英语里压力是"压上来"的：**come under pressure**（承受压力）、**come under severe/intense pressure**（承受巨大压力）、**put pressure on sb**（对某方施压）。关键：come under 的主语是**承压的一方**，不是施压的一方。

**❌ 学生初版**
The bond market had big pressure when the economy stopped.

**✅ 升级版**
A range of debt markets came under severe pressure as the US economy shut down.

**💡 效果**
方向分清楚了，因果就不会写反：come under 是"被压上"，put ... on 是"施压于"。写行业危机、机构处境、公共系统吃紧都用得上。

**例句：** The currency came under severe pressure after the election.

---

### 3️⃣ wrap up — 了结、收尾（把一个过程走完）

**原文出处：** "The aim is to **wrap up the process** by the end of the year, according to a Fed official."

**为什么好：** 学生写"结束"永远只有一个 stop，但 stop 是"中断"，不是"做完"。**wrap up** 是"把一件事完整包好、了结"，自带"按计划走完"的意味：**wrap up the process**（了结流程）、**wrap up a meeting**（结束会议）、**wrap up the year**（收尾这一年）。

**❌ 学生初版**
The government decided to stop the plan at the end of the year.

**✅ 升级版**
The aim is to wrap up the process by the end of the year.

**💡 效果**
stop 让读者以为"出问题了"，wrap up 让读者明白"完成了"——写项目周期、活动尾声、学期收尾都能用，还顺手交代了时间点。

**例句：** We should wrap up the discussion before noon.

---

### 4️⃣ test the waters — 试探（市场、对方的反应）

**原文出处：** "a good way to **test the waters** in terms of what the market reaction is going to be"

**为什么好：** 学生写"先试试看"只会 *try first*。**test the waters**（也作 test the water）来自"先伸脚试试水温"，指**正式行动前小规模试探反应**：**test the waters with a small change**（先小改一下试试）、**test the waters before launching**（推出前先试水）。

**❌ 学生初版**
They want to try first and see how people will react.

**✅ 升级版**
The small sale is a good way to test the waters before a bigger move.

**💡 效果**
try 是"对结果没把握"，test the waters 是**有策略地收集反馈**——写新政策、新产品、新做法时，它把"谨慎推进"这个态度写出来了。

**例句：** The company is testing the waters with a single city before a national launch.

---

### 5️⃣ minimise the potential for any adverse impact on — 把对……的不利影响降到最低

**原文出处：** "will aim to **minimise the potential for any adverse impact on** market functioning"

**为什么好：** 学生写"减少不好的影响"只会 *reduce the bad effects*。FT 用的是公式化的正式表达：**minimise the potential for any adverse impact on [对象]**——minimise（降到最低）+ the potential for（……的可能性）+ adverse impact on（对……的不利影响）。adverse 比 bad 正式得多，是政策语言和雅思大作文的高频词：**adverse impact/effect on**、**adverse conditions**。

**❌ 学生初版**
The plan will reduce the bad effects on the market.

**✅ 升级版**
The plan will aim to minimise the potential for any adverse impact on the market.

**💡 效果**
三个抽象名词叠起来，句子立刻有了"官方声明"的正式感；而且它精确到"降低的是可能性，不是已经发生的影响"——这种精度就是高分作文和普通作文的分界线。

**例句：** New rules aim to minimise the potential for any adverse impact on small businesses.

## ✍️ 写作建议

写政策、项目、改革类话题时，别再用 make / start / stop 三件套，按**出台—承压—见效—收尾—试探**五拍组织动词：

| 段落功能 | 学生常写 | 升级表达 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 行业/群体承压 | had big pressure | came under severe pressure |
| 一方受困、施展不开 | affected by the problem | hamstrung by the problem |
| 项目按计划收尾 | stopped the plan | wrapped up the process |
| 先小规模试探反应 | tried first | tested the waters |
| 把负面影响降到最低 | reduced the bad effects | minimised the potential for any adverse impact on |

同样是写"一项紧急政策的全过程"，学生版是 *The government made a plan. It started in 2020. The market had big pressure. Later the problem became better and they stopped it.*，FT版是 *The facility was rolled out as debt markets came under severe pressure; firms hamstrung by the pandemic stayed able to borrow; officials will wrap up the process gradually, and use the sale to test the waters*——**差别不在观点，而在每个动作都有主语、有阶段、有分寸**。

顺带积累同篇里的另外几个表达：**gradual and orderly**（循序渐进、有条不紊）、**be shut to new purchases**（停止接受新的买入）、**pledge its support**（承诺提供支持）、**the eventual removal of policy accommodation**（政策支持的最终取消）。还有一个数据落差公式值得抄下来：**totalled less than 140亿美元, under 2 per cent of the 750亿美元 available, according to Fed data**——先给绝对值，再用 under X per cent of Y 说明"相对规模有多小"，according to 交代来源。

**今日练习：** 用今天的表达，把这段"学生版"升级成90词的评论段：

> ❌ The city government made a plan to help small shops in 2020. At that time many shops were affected by the virus and they had big pressure. The plan worked, and the shops were better. Now the government wants to stop the plan. Next year they will start a new one and try first.

试着把 made a plan 换成 was rolled out、were affected by the virus and had big pressure 换成 were hamstrung by the virus and came under severe pressure、wants to stop the plan 换成 will wrap up the scheme、try first 换成 test the waters，看看整段是不是从"流水账"变成了"有阶段的政策叙述"。
