> 本文精选自FT双语文章《滴滴App遭中国网信办下架处罚》，学习科技监管/政策新闻语境下的高频写作表达。

---

中国学生写议论文时，一写到"政府监管\"\"企业\"\"科技\"这类话题，往往只会用 `control`、`stop`、`bad for` 这类基础动词，缺乏新闻英文的正式感和精准度，导致文章显得幼稚。

今天这篇FT报道讲的是中国网信办在滴滴赴美上市后下架其App，属于典型的科技监管新闻。文中包含大量政策新闻写作中的高频搭配，对中高考议论文和雅思大作文的"政府与企业\"\"科技与监管\"议题非常实用。

---

## 📖 原文段落

> China's cyber security regulator has ordered that Didi be taken off domestic app stores just days after the ride-hailing giant raised 4.4bn in the biggest Chinese listing in the US since Alibaba in 2014.
>
> The move marks **a fresh regulatory offensive on China's tech groups**, whose shares have suffered in recent months after Beijing's market regulators intensified their antitrust campaign.
>
> It also deals another blow to foreign investors in Chinese tech companies after China's financial regulators halted Ant Group's IPO.
>
> "This is another signal that, at the very least, Beijing will not let tech companies' financing plans **stand in the way of** regulating them," wrote Xiaomeng Lu.
>
> Didi said it would **resolutely implement** authorities' demands, and would remove the app from stores **for rectification**.

---

## 🔑 五个必学表达

### 1️⃣ regulatory offensive — 监管攻势；监管收紧行动

**原文：** `a fresh regulatory offensive on China's tech groups`

**中文解释：** `regulatory offensive` 指监管机构对行业的"收紧攻势、整治行动"，常用于科技、金融等领域的政策新闻。

**❌ 学生初版**
> The government started to control tech companies more strictly.

**✅ 升级版**
> Beijing launched a fresh regulatory offensive on China's tech groups.

**💡 效果** `regulatory offensive` 把"监管\"与"攻势\"结合，带有强烈的政策力度感，适合描述政府对某一行业的集中整治。

**例句：** The new data privacy law marks a regulatory offensive against internet platforms.

---

### 2️⃣ deal … a blow to — 给……造成打击；重创

**原文：** `deals another blow to foreign investors in Chinese tech companies`

**中文解释：** `deal a blow to` 是"给……造成打击\"，`another blow` 表示"又一次打击\"，常用于新闻英文描述政策或事件对某方的负面影响。

**❌ 学生初版**
> The policy is bad for foreign investors in Chinese tech.

**✅ 升级版**
> The move dealt another blow to foreign investors in Chinese tech companies.

**💡 效果** 比 `is bad for` 更有新闻感和力度，适合在议论文论证段里描述政策后果。

**例句：** The new tariffs deal a blow to export-oriented manufacturers.

---

### 3️⃣ stand in the way of — 妨碍；挡……的路

**原文：** `will not let tech companies' financing plans stand in the way of regulating them`

**中文解释：** `stand in the way of` 指"挡……的路、妨碍……进行"，一个高频固定搭配，比 `block` 或 `stop` 更地道、更有书面语气。

**❌ 学生初版**
> The government will not let financing plans stop the regulation.

**✅ 升级版**
> Beijing will not let financing plans stand in the way of regulating tech companies.

**💡 效果** 这个短语能精准表达"某事不应成为另一事的障碍\"，在议论文章论证让步或反驳时非常实用。

**例句：** We should not let short-term profits stand in the way of environmental protection.

---

### 4️⃣ for rectification — 为了整改；进行整改

**原文：** `remove the app from stores "for rectification"`

**中文解释：** `rectification` 是"整改\"，`for rectification` 指"为了进行整改\"，常见于中国公司面对监管处罚时的表述，体现正式公文语体。

**❌ 学生初版**
> The company removed the app to correct its mistakes.

**✅ 升级版**
> Didi removed the app from stores for rectification.

**💡 效果** `for rectification` 是一个典型的正式短语，适合描述企业或机构主动配合监管、进行问题整改的场景。

**例句：** The factory suspended production for rectification after the safety inspection.

---

### 5️⃣ resolutely implement — 坚决贯彻；坚决落实

**原文：** `Didi said it would resolutely implement authorities' demands`

**中文解释：** `resolutely implement` 是"坚决贯彻执行\"，`resolutely` 表示"坚决地\"，`implement` 指"执行、落实\"，搭配使用语气坚定，带有官方声明色彩。

**❌ 学生初版**
> Didi said it would surely do what the government asked.

**✅ 升级版**
> Didi said it would resolutely implement the authorities' demands.

**💡 效果** `resolutely implement` 比 `surely do` 正式得多，适合用于引用公司声明、政府文件等正式场合。

**例句：** Schools should resolutely implement the new safety regulations.

---

## 📝 写作建议

这5个表达都来自**政策新闻**，共享一个写作启示：**描述政府与企业的博弈时，要用"动词 + 名词\"的固定搭配，而不是笼统的 `control` / `stop`。** 

建议：
- **中高考议论文**：在"科技与监管\"\"政府与企业\"类议论文里，用 `regulatory offensive`、`stand in the way of` 等短语提升语体。
- **雅思大作文**：`deal a blow to`、`resolutely implement` 这类搭配可用于 Task 2 政策类话题的论证段。

积累新闻搭配，胜过背孤立单词。用了才是你的。
