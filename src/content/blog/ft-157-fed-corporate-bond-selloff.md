> 本文精选自FT双语文章《美联储将逐步出售疫情期间所购公司债券》，学习经济/货币政策语境下的高频写作表达。

---

中国学生写议论文时，最容易掉进的一个陷阱是：**用口语词表达宏观议题**。一写到"经济\"\"政策\"\"市场\"，大多数同学就只会用 `help`、`bad`、`make better` 这类基础动词，导致文章缺乏正式感，得分上不去。

今天这篇FT报道讲的是美联储如何逐步出售疫情期间紧急购入的公司债券，撤回紧急货币政策。文中密集出现大量正式文体中的经济政策词汇，对高中议论文和雅思大作文的议题句与论证段都非常实用。

---

## 📖 原文段落

> The Federal Reserve said that it would start selling the corporate bonds and fixed income funds that it bought to stabilise the financial system last year, **unwinding** unprecedented emergency measures that electrified markets and brought down borrowing costs for companies **reeling from** the pandemic.
>
> The Fed touted the limited usage as **testament to** its success in restoring market functioning quickly, simply by pledging its support.
>
> Corporate bond prices rallied and companies **hamstrung by** the pandemic remained able to access capital markets.
>
> "This is one little step and a good way to **test the waters** in terms of what the market reaction is going to be," said Leary.
>
> Leary said the SMCCF announcement **paved the way for** that discussion and the eventual removal of policy accommodation.

---

## 🔑 五个必学表达

### 1️⃣ unwind — （逐步）撤回；解绑

**原文：** `unwinding unprecedented emergency measures`

**中文解释：** `unwind` 在金融/政策语境中指"逐步撤回、解除（之前采取的措施）"，比简单的 `stop` 或 `end` 更正式，带有"把复杂局面慢慢理顺"的过程感。

**❌ 学生初版**
> The government stopped the emergency policy last year.

**✅ 升级版**
> The government began unwinding the emergency measures step by step.

**💡 效果** `unwind` 带有"循序渐进、有序撤退"的含义，特别适合描述央行收缩货币政策、政府退出紧急干预等场景。

**例句：** The central bank is unwinding its bond-buying programme as inflation rises.

---

### 2️⃣ testament to — …的证明；证明了……的成功

**原文：** `touted the limited usage as testament to its success`

**中文解释：** `testament to` 是"……的证明、证明……的证据"，常用于正式文体，表示某个事实能证明某个结论。比 `show` 或 `prove` 更有书面感。

**❌ 学生初版**
> The low usage shows that the policy was successful.

**✅ 升级版**
> The limited usage is testament to the success of the policy.

**💡 效果** `be testament to` 是一个固定搭配，适合在议论文论证段里用来引出"事实证明"的论点，语气坚定且正式。

**例句：** The rapid recovery of the market is testament to the effectiveness of government intervention.

---

### 3️⃣ hamstrung — 使……束手束脚；严重限制

**原文：** `companies hamstrung by the pandemic`

**中文解释：** `hamstring` 原意是"割掉腿筋"，比喻"使……失去行动能力、束手束脚"。作形容词用时 `hamstrung by` 表示"被……严重限制"，比 `affected by` 更具体、更生动。

**❌ 学生初版**
> Many companies were affected by the pandemic and could not get loans.

**✅ 升级版**
> Many companies hamstrung by the pandemic still managed to access capital markets.

**💡 效果** `hamstrung by` 能精准表达"被外部环境束缚、行动受限"，在描述企业、个人或国家面对危机时非常有画面感。

**例句：** Students hamstrung by poor English foundations often struggle with academic writing.

---

### 4️⃣ test the waters — 试探；试探反应

**原文：** `a good way to test the waters in terms of what the market reaction is going to be`

**中文解释：** `test the waters` 本义"试探水深"，引申为"在正式行动前先小范围尝试、观察反应"。在商业和政策写作中非常常用。

**❌ 学生初版**
> The bank wants to see how the market will react before doing more.

**✅ 升级版**
> The sale is one small step to test the waters before the central bank moves further.

**💡 效果** 这个短语比 `see how people react` 更地道、更简洁，常用于描述试探性的政策举措或商业决策。

**例句：** The company launched a pilot project to test the waters before expanding nationwide.

---

### 5️⃣ pave the way for — 为……铺平道路；为……创造条件

**原文：** `paved the way for that discussion and the eventual removal of policy accommodation`

**中文解释：** `pave the way for` 是"为……铺路、创造条件"，一个固定搭配，常用于描述某项举措为后续更大变化打基础。

**❌ 学生初版**
> The announcement made it possible to discuss other measures later.

**✅ 升级版**
> The announcement paved the way for discussions on scaling back other emergency measures.

**💡 效果** `pave the way for` 简洁有力，是议论文因果段的高频表达，能替代笨拙的 `make it possible for`。

**例句：** This policy will pave the way for more foreign investment in the education sector.

---

## 📝 写作建议

这5个表达共享一个共同特点：**它们都不是孤立的"大词"，而是在特定语境里有固定搭配的"语块"（chunk）。**

中国学生普遍的问题是背单词不背搭配，写作时只能拼单词，造不出地道的论证句。建议：
- **高中议论文**：在"经济/社会/科技\"类议论文的议题句里，刻意用上 `unwind`、`hamstrung by`、`pave the way for` 中的 1-2 个。
- **雅思大作文**：`testament to`、`test the waters` 这类正式搭配可用于 Task 2 的论证段，帮助提升语体分。

记住：搭配比单词更重要，用了才是你的。
