# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：开头技巧——用「压力+行动」式开头把两难写进第一句

## 你的开头为什么总被跳过？

批改作文时，最常见的开头是这两句：

**❌ 学生初版**
Facing the serious problem, we should take action to solve it.

**❌ 学生初版**
Recently, more and more people are paying attention to this issue.

这两句的问题不是语法，是**信息量**：第一句没说清是什么问题、谁该行动、压力从哪来；第二句的 Recently 更是等于什么都没说。阅卷老师看到这样的开头，心里已经给你打了六折。

《金融时报》这篇关于东京奥运会是否闭门举办的报道，第一句就把**局势、压力、动作、背景**全部装进了一句话。它写的是日本政府在疫情反弹下被迫考虑闭门办奥运——一个典型的"两难局面"，新闻记者用三个不同层次的开头，把这种压力写得清清楚楚。今天拆给你。

## FT原文示范

> The Japanese government is under pressure to reverse course and hold the Tokyo Olympics behind closed doors as Covid-19 cases rise in the capital.
>
> After bottoming out at fewer than 400 cases a day in mid-June, the number of new diagnoses has climbed to almost 600 a day, raising fears of a full-blown surge when the games are held between July 23 and August 8.
>
> The increasing cases count has highlighted the risk taken by Yoshihide Suga, Japan's prime minister, in choosing to ignore his medical advisers and allow the games to go ahead with up to 10,000 fans in stadiums.

中文对照：

> 随着东京的新冠肺炎确诊病例数量不断攀升，日本政府面临压力，要求其改变做法、闭门举办东京奥运会。
>
> 在经历6月中旬单日新增确诊病例数量不到400例的低点后，目前单日新增确诊病例数量已攀升至近600例。这让人们担心7月23日至8月8日东京奥运会举办期间感染人数会全面激增。
>
> 不断攀升的病例数突显了日本首相菅义伟所冒的风险。菅义伟选择无视他的医疗顾问，决定每场比赛允许不超过1万名观众进入场馆观看。

## 技巧1：压力+行动式开头

**结构公式：** `[主体] is under pressure to [动作] as [背景变化]`

**FT例句：**
> The Japanese government is under pressure to reverse course and hold the Tokyo Olympics behind closed doors as Covid-19 cases rise in the capital.

这句话的妙处在于：`under pressure to` 直接点出"被迫感"，`as` 给出压力的来源，一句话里同时有**主体、动作、原因**三个要素。学生写"两难"时只会 I think it is difficult to choose，而记者用 under pressure to 就把"不得不"写了出来。

**❌ 学生初版**
Our school has a problem about whether to have the sports meeting.

**✅ 升级版**
The school is under pressure to cancel the sports meeting as Covid-19 cases rise again in our city.

**💡 效果**
升级版第一句就同时交代了决策主体（school）、被迫动作（cancel）、压力来源（cases rise）——三个信息点一个不落，还自带"两难"的紧张感。

**考场模板：**
- `[主体] is under pressure to [动作] as [背景/问题]`
- 例：The government is under pressure to lower food prices as inflation hits record levels.

## 技巧2：数据拐点式开头

**结构公式：** `After bottoming out at [低点], [指标] has climbed to [高点], raising fears of [后果]`

**FT例句：**
> After bottoming out at fewer than 400 cases a day in mid-June, the number of new diagnoses has climbed to almost 600 a day, raising fears of a full-blown surge when the games are held between July 23 and August 8.

学生写数据只会 "The number is increasing" 或 "There are more and more cases"。FT记者用**拐点叙事**：先给低点（bottoming out at fewer than 400），再给高点（climbed to almost 600），最后给后果（raising fears of...）。数据不再是静止的，而是一条有方向的曲线。

**❌ 学生初版**
The number of cases increased a lot, so people were worried.

**✅ 升级版**
After bottoming out at fewer than 400 cases a day in June, the number of new diagnoses has climbed to almost 600 a day, raising fears of a full-blown surge during the holiday.

**💡 效果**
升级版用"低点→高点→后果"三步走，数字有了参照系（400 vs 600），担心有了具体对象（full-blown surge），比 so people were worried 有说服力得多。

**考场模板：**
- `After bottoming out at [低点], [指标] has climbed to [高点], raising fears of [后果]`
- 例：After bottoming out at 20 per cent in May, the unemployment rate has climbed to 35 per cent, raising fears of a social crisis.

## 技巧3：风险点名式开头

**结构公式：** `The increasing [问题] has highlighted the risk taken by [人物], [身份], in choosing to [决定]`

**FT例句：**
> The increasing cases count has highlighted the risk taken by Yoshihide Suga, Japan's prime minister, in choosing to ignore his medical advisers and allow the games to go ahead with up to 10,000 fans in stadiums.

学生写"某人做了个冒险的决定"只会 His decision was risky。FT记者把**风险挂到具体的人身上**：`highlighted the risk taken by [人名], [身份], in choosing to [决定]`——批评一个人时不直接说"他错了"，而是说"他的决定把风险暴露了出来"，客观又锋利。

**❌ 学生初版**
The headmaster made a bad decision. He was wrong.

**✅ 升级版**
The rising infection rate has highlighted the risk taken by the headmaster in choosing to keep the school open against doctors' advice.

**💡 效果**
升级版保留了批判性，但把矛头从"人"转向"决定本身"（the risk taken by...in choosing to...），语气更客观，在议论文里显得更成熟。

**考场模板：**
- `The increasing [问题] has highlighted the risk taken by [人物] in choosing to [决定]`
- 例：The growing traffic jams have highlighted the risk taken by the city in choosing to close the main bridge for repair.

## 针对性练习建议

1. **改写练习**：用压力+行动式，把"学生面临是否参加课外辅导的选择"写成一句英文开头（提示：主体是 students，压力来源可以是 exam pressure，动作可以是 give up/keep）。
2. **数据拐点仿写**：找一组你自己作文里的数据（比如成绩、时长、人数），用 After bottoming out at... has climbed to... raising fears of... 改写成拐点叙事。
3. **风险点名应用**：从本周新闻里挑一个"有争议的决定"（任何国家、任何领域），用 highlighted the risk taken by... in choosing to... 写一句评论式开头。
