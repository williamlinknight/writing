学生写「影响」那一段，最常写成这样：This policy caused many problems. It affected the whole economy. —— 说了「大」，却没交代「怎么大到那里去」。读者凭什么相信一件事能波及整个市场？

《金融时报》处理「一轮政策冲击」的报道时，用的是**连锁外推论证**：先把事件放进「动作 → 可核查的反应 → 更大范围的担忧」这条链里，再用数字把量级钉住，最后用连续前例把单次事件升级成「模式」。今天我们以 FT 报道中国整顿教培行业引发股市大跌的稿子为例（全文 562 词），拆 4 个动作，再加一个收口写法。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №182 — 《中国重拳整顿培训产业触发股市大跌》

## FT 原文（节选）

> Global investors sold almost $2bn of Chinese stocks on Monday as Beijing's crackdown on education companies raised fears of more regulatory tightening across the world's second-biggest economy.

> The market tumult was sparked by a move by Beijing over the weekend to ban academic tuition groups from making profits, raising capital or going public. News of the measures, which were unveiled via a leaked memo and were later confirmed, wiped about $16bn from the value of three of the sector's biggest companies on Friday.

> Investors said the crackdown, which followed regulatory moves to rein in financial and technology companies including ride-hailing app Didi Chuxing and ecommerce group Alibaba, raised concerns that no sector would avoid more stringent policing.

> "The growing speed and intensity of these crackdowns is unprecedented," said Frank Benzimra, head of equity strategy at Société Générale.

> The Hong Kong-listed shares of New Oriental Education, one of the sector's heavyweights, plunged almost 40 per cent on Monday, taking the stock's losses to about 60 per cent over two sessions.

> "We can't say what sector will be the next that the Chinese government wants more control over," said Dickie Wong, head of research at Hong Kong brokerage Kingston Securities. "This will create fear and selling pressure in the near term."

## 中文对照（节选）

> 国际投资者周一卖出20亿美元的中国股票，原因是，中国政府打击教育企业的行动，引发了外界对这个全球第二大经济体将进一步收紧监管的担忧。

> 引发市场动荡的是中国政府上周末出台的一项举措，该举措禁止学科类培训机构盈利、融资或上市。有关这些措施的消息是通过一份外泄的备忘录披露的，并于随后得到了证实。上周五，这些举措令该行业三家最大企业的市值蒸发了约160亿美元。

> 投资者表示，继中国监管部门采取行动约束金融和科技企业——包括网约车应用滴滴出行(Didi Chuxing)和电子商务集团阿里巴巴(Alibaba)——后，此次打击行动引发了人们的担忧，即没有哪个行业能够避免更严格的监管。

> 法国兴业银行(Société Générale)亚洲股票策略主管弗朗克•本齐姆拉(Frank Benzimra)表示："这些打击行动的速度越来越快，力度也越来越大，这是前所未有的。"

> 周一，在香港上市的新东方(New Oriental Education)股价暴跌逾40%，导致该股在两个交易日内累计下跌约65%。新东方是业内的重量级企业之一。

> "我们说不好中国政府接下来想对哪个行业加强管控。"香港金利丰证券(Kingston Securities)研究主管黄德几(Dickie Wong)表示，"这将在短期内造成恐慌和抛售压力。"

## 动作一：一句三层——反应、导火索、外推

**FT 原文**

> Global investors sold almost $2bn of Chinese stocks on Monday as Beijing's crackdown on education companies raised fears of more regulatory tightening across the world's second-biggest economy.

**为什么好**

- 结果摆句首（`sold almost $2bn of Chinese stocks`），读者第一眼就看到可核查的动作；
- `as` 一句话同时承担「时间」和「原因」，不必另起一句 because；
- 真正的落点在句尾：`raised fears of more regulatory tightening across the world's second-biggest economy` —— 从一次抛售推到整个经济体的监管预期，这就是**外推**。

**❌ 学生初版**

> The new policy made investors worried, and it may affect the whole economy.

**✅ 升级版**

> Global investors sold almost $2bn of Chinese stocks as the crackdown on education companies raised fears of more regulatory tightening across the economy.

**💡 效果**

学生版只有「担心」和「可能」；升级版先给一个可核查的动作（卖掉多少钱的股票），再让 `raised fears of more ... across ...` 把影响范围摊开——外推之前，先把锚点钉住。

**模板**

> [主体] [动作 + 数字] as [导火索] raised fears of more [趋势] across [范围].

**仿写示例**（校园话题）

> Parents withdrew about 300 applications in one week as the new admission rules raised fears of more restrictions across the district.

## 动作二：动因压缩成一句，量级清点成数字

**FT 原文**

> The market tumult was sparked by a move by Beijing over the weekend to ban academic tuition groups from making profits, raising capital or going public. News of the measures, which were unveiled via a leaked memo and were later confirmed, wiped about $16bn from the value of three of the sector's biggest companies on Friday.

**为什么好**

- `was sparked by a move ... to ban [对象] from doing A, B or C` 把「谁做了什么」压进一句，`from making profits, raising capital or going public` 顺手交代政策的三条边界；
- `which were unveiled via a leaked memo and were later confirmed` 交代消息的来源与可信度（泄密备忘录 → 随后证实）——读者要先用这个数字，你就得先证明数字站得住；
- `wiped about $16bn from the value of three of the sector's biggest companies` 用「抹掉多少 + 涉及几家」把打击量级钉死。

**❌ 学生初版**

> The government announced some new rules about tutoring companies, and the companies lost a lot of money.

**✅ 升级版**

> The market tumult was sparked by a move to ban academic tuition groups from making profits, raising capital or going public. The measures wiped about $16bn from the value of three of the sector's biggest companies.

**💡 效果**

`some new rules` 和 `a lot of money` 读者无法量化；`ban ... from doing A, B or C` 与 `wiped about $16bn from the value of three ... companies` 让「打击力度」和「损失规模」都变成可核查的事实。

**模板**

> [现象] was sparked by a move to ban [对象] from [A], [B] or [C]. The measures wiped about [金额] from the value of [N] of the sector's biggest companies.

**仿写示例**（校园话题）

> The protests were sparked by a move to ban students from using phones, taking notes on tablets or joining online clubs. The rules wiped out about 40 per cent of the club's membership in one term.

## 动作三：把影响的范围推到边界（用连续前例支撑）

**FT 原文**

> Investors said the crackdown, which followed regulatory moves to rein in financial and technology companies including ride-hailing app Didi Chuxing and ecommerce group Alibaba, raised concerns that no sector would avoid more stringent policing.

> Beijing has launched successive crackdowns on fast-growing sectors over the past year. Regulators in November pulled the plug on the record $37bn initial public offering of Ant Group, a fintech company controlled by billionaire Jack Ma, at the last minute. That was followed by a broader clampdown on technology groups ... Shortly after Didi's New York IPO this month, new rules were introduced governing listings abroad for Chinese companies whose data-gathering activity covers more than 1m users.

**为什么好**

- `which followed [前例清单]` 把单个事件挂到一串已发生的事件后面：这不是意外，而是模式；
- `raised concerns that no sector would avoid more stringent policing` 是全篇的外推极点——从三个行业一路推到「没有行业能幸免」；
- 后段用 `successive` → `That was followed by` → `Shortly after` 三个时间标记铺出连续动作的节奏，前例越多、越具体，「这是模式」这个判断就越站得住。

**❌ 学生初版**

> The government has also done similar things to other companies before, so everyone is afraid now.

**✅ 升级版**

> The crackdown, which followed regulatory moves to rein in financial and technology companies, raised concerns that no sector would avoid more stringent policing.

**💡 效果**

`also done similar things before` 是一句模糊的历史；`which followed [具体清单]` 是可核查的序列。要写「整个行业都在紧张」，先交出三到四个前例——范围敢推多远，取决于前例有多实。

**模板**

> [事件], which followed [前例 A] and [前例 B], raised concerns that no [范围] would avoid [后果].

**仿写示例**（社会话题）

> The fine, which followed warnings to delivery platforms and ride-hailing apps, raised concerns that no online business would escape closer scrutiny.

## 动作四：单日数字 + 累计数字的两段式写法

**FT 原文**

> The Hong Kong-listed shares of New Oriental Education, one of the sector's heavyweights, plunged almost 40 per cent on Monday, taking the stock's losses to about 60 per cent over two sessions.

**为什么好**

- 先给**单日**跌幅（`plunged almost 40 per cent on Monday`），再用 `taking ... to ... over two sessions` 补**累计**跌幅——同一个事实被读了两次；
- `one of the sector's heavyweights` 用同位语交代这家公司有多少分量，跌幅才有意义；
- `plunged` / `tumbled` / `shed` 这一组动词，比 went down 更能传达幅度。

**❌ 学生初版**

> The share price went down a lot, and it was a very bad day for the company.

**✅ 升级版**

> Shares of New Oriental Education, one of the sector's heavyweights, plunged almost 40 per cent, taking the company's losses to about 60 per cent over two sessions.

**💡 效果**

数据段落别只写一个数字。单日加累计两层，读者的「严重」感是自己算出来的。提醒一句：这只适用于数据类段落，别为了堆数字而写数字。

**模板**

> [主体], one of [范围]'s heavyweights, [涨跌动词] almost [X] per cent on [时间], taking [其损失／涨幅] to about [Y] per cent over [时段].

**仿写示例**（校园话题）

> The club, one of the region's biggest, lost almost 40 members in September, taking its losses to about 60 over two terms.

## 动作五：收口——承认不知道「下一个是谁」，但给出短期结论

**FT 原文**

> "The growing speed and intensity of these crackdowns is unprecedented," said Frank Benzimra, head of equity strategy at Société Générale.

> "We can't say what sector will be the next that the Chinese government wants more control over," said Dickie Wong, head of research at Hong Kong brokerage Kingston Securities. "This will create fear and selling pressure in the near term."

**为什么好**

- 引语加职称，再加一个趋势名词短语（`The growing speed and intensity of these crackdowns`）：一句话概括整段事实，比 very serious 精确得多；
- 第二位分析师先说**不知道**（`We can't say what sector will be the next`），再说**知道什么**（`This will create fear and selling pressure in the near term`）——承认不确定，短期结论反而更可信；
- `in the near term` 给结论加上了时间边界，避免「永远如何如何」这种撑不住的判断。

**❌ 学生初版**

> Nobody knows what will happen next, so it is really dangerous.

**✅ 升级版**

> Analysts said they could not say which sector would be next, but warned that this would create fear and selling pressure in the near term.

**💡 效果**

`Nobody knows what will happen next` 是放弃论证；「不知道什么 + 知道什么 + 时间边界」才是判断。外推的范围可以很大，结论的有效期一定要写小。

**模板**

> "[趋势判断]," said [姓名], [职称] at [机构]. "[我们不知道什么]. [但短期内会怎样]."

**仿写示例**（教育话题）

> "The pace of these inspections is unprecedented," said Li Wei, a researcher at a local policy institute. "We cannot say which school will be next. But this will create uncertainty in the near term."

## 区分小节：别和这三种写法混用

- **与「数据锚定」的区别**：数据锚定用一个对比数字支撑判断（A 比 B 高多少）；连锁外推是先用数字交代**量级**，再把影响**推到更大的范围**。
- **与「例证论证」的区别**：例证是「规则 → 例子」（先立观点再举例）；连锁外推里的连续前例是「例子 → 模式」（先摆一串已发生的事，再推出「这不是意外」）。
- **与「历史实例链」的区别**：历史实例链按年份排进步（in 2011 ... by 2018 ...），落点是「一路向上」；连锁外推按事件排重复（successive ... That was followed by ...），落点是「还会波及谁」。
- **与「权衡论证」的区别**：权衡必须同时给出代价与对策；连锁外推只处理「影响有多大、扩到哪里」这一段，通常用在支撑「必须重视／必须监管」这类结论的段落里。

## 写作建议：外推段三问

写任何「这件事影响很大」的段落，落笔前问三句话：

1. **有没有一个可核查的动作？**（卖掉 20 亿美元股票 / 下架 App / 罚款）而不是「很多人反对」；
2. **影响有没有范围词？**（across the economy / no sector / the whole district）范围越大，越要先给前例和数字；
3. **结论有没有时间边界？**（in the near term / within one year）把话说满，反而丢掉分数。

一段完整的连锁外推＝**动作（带数字）+ 直接反应 + 连续前例 + 范围外推 + 时间边界**。

## 练习建议

- 挑一条你熟悉的政策或校规，写一段五句话的连锁外推：① 谁做了什么（动作 + 范围）② 直接反应（数字）③ 为什么（背景因果）④ 相似前例（which followed ...）⑤ 短期后果（in the near term）。
- 雅思 Task 2 里 technology / regulation / environment 的题目，凡是写 it will have a big impact on society 的地方，都可以按「动作 → 可量化反应 → 范围 → 时间边界」重写一遍。
- 注意分寸：没有前例和数字支撑的「会波及整个社会」是夸张；有连续前例的「没有行业能幸免」才是判断。
