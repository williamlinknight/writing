# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：词汇升级——写"代价、别无选择、难以适应、退回老路"只会 bad 和 have no choice，跟FT学5个描述与评价表达

批改"某个变化带来的影响"这类作文，学生的段落高度雷同：*Working from home is a big change. It has good points and bad points. Some workers have no choice because their companies decided it. Some people cannot adapt to it. If it is not good, companies will go back to the old way.*——观点站得住，但**评价只有 good / bad 两级**，动作停在 go back，读起来像没有立场的新闻摘要。其实写"变化与适应"最见功力的地方就两个：**把"代价"写成能摸到的东西，把"选择"写成一个有前提的权利**。FT 专栏作者 Andrew Hill 用一组名词化表达和形容词短语，把工作和生活的变化写得非常具体，今天就从这篇里学 5 个**描述与评价表达**。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №84 — *后疫情时代如何办公？*（2021年2月，Andrew Hill 专栏，649词）

## 📖 FT原文选段

> Some NWFH staff will currently be on short hours, furlough, or some awkward, temporary version of remote work, if they haven't lost their jobs altogether.

> The longed for exit from the tunnel of lockdown is an opportunity to end casual assumptions that our job title determines where and even how we carry out the role.

> Writing in the Financial Times last week, Theo Nicolaou, who runs a security services company, brought home the human cost of some NWFH jobs.

> Emerging economies, and rural areas, are less amenable to remote work.

> Some companies will claim they do not have the luxury of choice, given the commercial pressures they now face. The temptation is strong to revert to old job descriptions... But businesses that let staff take advantage of the flexibility they will soon regain will thrive. Those that revert to the old rigid job boundaries will not.

**中文对照**

> 一些无法居家办公的员工，目前正遭遇工时缩短、无薪休假，或某种尴尬的、临时的远程工作安排——如果他们还没有彻底失业的话。
>
> 期盼已久的解封，是一个终结"想当然"的机会——终结那种认为职位名称决定了我们在哪里、甚至如何履行工作的默认假设。
>
> 运营一家安保服务公司的西奥•尼科劳(Theo Nicolaou)上周在英国《金融时报》撰文，让我们真切看到一些无法居家办公岗位的人力代价。
>
> 新兴经济体和农村地区对远程工作更不买账。
>
> 一些公司会声称，考虑到眼下面对的商业压力，它们并没有挑三拣四的余地。退回旧职位说明的诱惑很强。但那些让员工利用即将重新获得的灵活性的企业会兴旺发达；而那些退回僵化岗位边界的，则不会。

## 🎯 5个高分表达解析

### 1️⃣ bring home the human cost of — 让人真切体会到……的人力代价

**原文出处：** "Theo Nicolaou... **brought home the human cost of** some NWFH jobs."

**为什么好：** 学生写"让人意识到问题有多严重"只会 *let people know it is very serious*。**bring home** 原义是"把东西带回家"，引申为"使某件事被真切地感受到"：**bring home the reality of sth**（让人看清现实）、**bring home the human cost of X**（让人真切体会到 X 的人力代价）。宾语常是 cost、reality、danger 这类抽象名词。

**❌ 学生初版**
The report let people know that the change is very serious.

**✅ 升级版**
The report brought home the human cost of the change.

**💡 效果**
bring home 的力点在"从抽象到切身"——议论文里刚举完一个例子，用它接一句评价，比 make people realize 更有画面，也顺势把例证收口到论点。

**例句：** The pandemic brought home the value of frontline workers.

---

### 2️⃣ do not have the luxury of choice — 没有挑三拣四的余地

**原文出处：** "Some companies will claim they **do not have the luxury of choice**, given the commercial pressures they now face."

**为什么好：** 学生写"没有选择"永远只有 *have no choice*。luxury 是"奢侈品"，在这里指"多余的享受"：**not have the luxury of choice**（没资格挑）、**not have the luxury of doing sth**（没条件去做某事）、**cannot afford the luxury of**（奢侈不起）。

**❌ 学生初版**
Small companies have no choice because they face big pressure.

**✅ 升级版**
Small companies do not have the luxury of choice, given the commercial pressures they face.

**💡 效果**
luxury 一词给"被迫"垫了一个台阶：不是不想选，而是没资格选——写弱势一方、写紧迫处境时，它比 have no choice 更冷静，也更准确。given 交代前提，一句话就把"为什么没得选"说清了。

**例句：** With the deadline approaching, we do not have the luxury of another meeting.

---

### 3️⃣ be less amenable to — 对……更不买账、更难以适用

**原文出处：** "Emerging economies, and rural areas, are **less amenable to** remote work."

**为什么好：** 学生写"不适应、不接受"只会 *cannot accept* 或 *are not used to*。**amenable to** 表示"愿意接受、可被引导"——**amenable to change**（愿意改变）、**amenable to reason**（讲得通道理）。写成 **less amenable to** 就是"更不买账、更不适用"。

**❌ 学生初版**
Rural areas are not used to remote work and cannot accept it.

**✅ 升级版**
Rural areas, where jobs are tied to physical sites, are less amenable to remote work.

**💡 效果**
用 amenable 时，读者看到的是"意愿和条件"，而不是"能力不足"——写文化差异、政策落地、群体接受度时，它比 cannot 更少评判、更像分析。

**例句：** Older consumers are less amenable to shopping on a phone.

---

### 4️⃣ end casual assumptions — 终结想当然的默认假设

**原文出处：** "an opportunity to **end casual assumptions** that our job title determines where and even how we carry out the role"

**为什么好：** 学生写"很多人想当然地认为"只会 *many people always think*。casual 除了"随意"，还指"未经考察、随手得出的"；**casual assumptions** 就是"没验证过的想当然"。句式 **end casual assumptions that + 从句** 可以直接套用。

**❌ 学生初版**
Many people always think that a person's job decides where he works.

**✅ 升级版**
The change is an opportunity to end casual assumptions that a job title determines where we work.

**💡 效果**
casual 和 assumption 的组合是评论写作的利器：它批评的不是某一个人的看法，而是"大家随手就这么以为"的默认设定——写教育观念、性别偏见、职业偏见都能用。

**例句：** Data can help end casual assumptions about which jobs are productive.

---

### 5️⃣ revert to — 又退回（老做法、旧状态）

**原文出处：** "The temptation is strong to **revert to** old job descriptions... Those that **revert to** the old rigid job boundaries will not."

**为什么好：** 学生写"回到原来的样子"只会 *go back to* 或 *become like before*。**revert to** 是正式用语，强调"退回先前（往往不太好的）状态"：**revert to old habits**（故态复萌）、**revert to the previous system**（退回原制度）。**the temptation is strong to revert to X** 更是现成的"警惕回潮"句式。

**❌ 学生初版**
After the pandemic, many companies will go back to the old way.

**✅ 升级版**
The temptation is strong for companies to revert to old job descriptions.

**💡 效果**
go back 只是叙述方向，revert to 带着"退步"的判断——写改革出现反复、习惯难以改变时用它，观点就自己冒出来了。FT 还用 It will thrive / will not 的对照收尾，值得整句背下来。

**例句：** Without practice, learners quickly revert to old errors.

## ✍️ 写作建议

写"变化与适应"类作文，把"影响"拆成四层：**代价 → 选择 → 接受度 → 回潮**，每层配一个精准表达，段落就不会停在 good / bad：

| 段落功能 | 学生常写 | 升级表达 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 让人真切看到代价 | make people know it is serious | bring home the human cost of |
| 不是不想选，是没资格选 | have no choice | not have the luxury of choice |
| 接受度低、难以适用 | cannot accept / cannot adapt | be less amenable to |
| 打破想当然的默认假设 | many people always think | end casual assumptions that |
| 又退回老做法 | go back to the old way | revert to |

同样是写"居家办公的影响"，学生版是 *It has good points and bad points. Some workers have no choice. Some people cannot adapt to it. If it is not good, companies will go back.*，FT版是 *Remote work has brought home the human cost of some jobs; some regions are less amenable to it; the temptation to revert to old boundaries is strong, but companies that let staff use their new flexibility will thrive*——**差别不在观点，而在评价有没有前提、判断有没有落点**。

顺带积累同篇里的其他高分表达：**the longed for exit from the tunnel of lockdown**（期盼已久的解封）、**for all the words spent on the pros and cons of X**（尽管围绕 X 的利弊已经讨论了这么多）、**the monotony of WFH**（居家办公的单调乏味）、**always-on stress**（随时待命的压力）、**on short hours, furlough**（工时缩短、无薪休假）、**the optimum mix of A and B**（A 与 B 的最佳组合）。

**今日练习：** 用今天的表达，把这段"学生版"升级成90词的评论段：

> ❌ Remote working is a big change for many companies. It has good points and bad points. Some workers have no choice because their companies decided it. Some workers cannot adapt to it. If the change has too many problems, companies will go back to the old way.

提示：have good points and bad points → bring home the human cost of；have no choice → not have the luxury of choice；cannot adapt to it → be less amenable to it；go back to the old way → revert to the old way。写完再检查一遍：你的段落里，评价词是"两级"还是"有前提、有落点"？
