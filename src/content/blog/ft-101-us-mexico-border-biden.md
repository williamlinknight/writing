在英语议论文中写移民、边境管控等社会热点话题时，很多同学习惯于 *increased by 28 percent* 或 *the government faced opposition* 这样的基础表达。今天的素材来自《金融时报》(Financial Times)关于拜登政府面对美墨边境闯关者激增的双语新闻，其中关于“百分比增长”“让步与两难”“习语化比喻”“遭到反对”的表达，非常适合用于中考、高考或雅思议论文的背景数据段与论证段。

> **FT原文**
> The US Customs and Border Protection announced on Wednesday that 100,441 people had attempted entry along the US southern border in February — a 28 per cent increase from January. The agency reported that 9,457 of those individuals were unaccompanied minors — a 62 per cent increase from January.
>
> While it has placed blame for the current border crisis on the previous White House, it has struggled to simultaneously tout a promising message to migrants while deterring people from crossing into the country outside the proper legal channels.
>
> “The border is not open,” Jacobson, the former US ambassador to Mexico, added.

> **中文对照**
> 美国海关及边境保护局周三宣布，2月有100,441人试图从美国南部边境入境，较1月增长28%。其中9,457人为无人陪同的未成年人，较1月增长62%。
>
> 虽然拜登政府把当前边境危机归咎于上一届政府，但它很难一边向移民释放有盼头的信息，一边阻止他们通过非正规途径入境。
>
> “边境不是敞开的，”前美国驻墨西哥大使雅各布森补充道。

### 💡 高分表达解析

**1. a 28 per cent increase / a 62 per cent increase**
- **原文出处**：*100,441 people had attempted entry ... — a 28 per cent increase from January.*
- **中文解释**：……较……增长 28% / 62%（百分比增长表达）
- **为什么好**：*a + 数字 + per cent increase from + 时间* 比 *increased by 28 percent* 更书面、更新闻化，并且自带时间基准（from January），让数据对比更精确、更有说服力。
- **考场用法对比**：
  **❌ 学生初版**
  The number of crossings increased by 28 percent.
  **✅ 升级版**
  The number of attempted crossings saw **a 28 per cent increase from January**.
  **💡 效果**：带“较上月”的对照基准，数据论证更扎实，适合议论文背景段。

**2. While ... it has struggled to simultaneously ...**
- **原文出处**：*While it has placed blame for the current border crisis on the previous White House, it has struggled to simultaneously tout a promising message to migrants while deterring people from crossing ...*
- **中文解释**：虽然……，但它很难同时……(让步 + 并行矛盾)
- **为什么好**：*While* 引导让步状语从句，先承认一方面（归咎前政府），主句再用 *simultaneously* 表“同时”，并嵌套 *while deterring...* 表另一个互斥动作，构成典型的“两难”论证结构——一方面安抚，另一方面遏制，两者难以兼得。
- **考场用法对比**：
  **❌ 学生初版**
  The government blames the previous administration. It also wants to stop illegal crossings.
  **✅ 升级版**
  While the government **has placed blame for the crisis on the previous administration, it has struggled to simultaneously** send a welcoming message **while deterring** illegal crossings.
  **💡 效果**：用一个长句把“承认”“矛盾”“两难”都带出来，逻辑层次远强于两句拆开的简单陈述。

**3. walk and chew gum at the same time**
- **原文出处**：Jacobson 承认这像是 *trying to “walk and chew gum at the same time”*
- **中文解释**：一边走路一边嚼口香糖 = 同时处理两件事 / 兼顾两个目标
- **为什么好**：这是地道英语习语，用日常动作比喻“同时兼顾两个目标的困难”，比 *deal with two things at once* 更生动、更有画面感，适合写政策执行中的矛盾与两难。
- **考场用法对比**：
  **❌ 学生初版**
  It is hard to manage the economy and control the virus at the same time.
  **✅ 升级版**
  Managing the economic recovery and controlling the virus is like *trying to walk and chew gum at the same time*.
  **💡 效果**：习语化比喻瞬间提升语言地道度，适合议论文现象引入或论证段。

**4. be subject to push back from**
- **原文出处**：*the White House has also been subject to push back from some Democrats*
- **中文解释**：遭到……的反对 / 受到……的抵制
- **为什么好**：*be subject to* 表“易受……影响”，*push back* 表“反对、抵制”，整个被动结构比 *some Democrats opposed the White House* 更客观、更正式，适合议论文呈现多方博弈、利益冲突。
- **考场用法对比**：
  **❌ 学生初版**
  Some parents opposed the new school rule.
  **✅ 升级版**
  The new school rule **has been subject to push back from some parents**.
  **💡 效果**：被动化处理把焦点放在“政策本身”而非“谁反对”，语气更中肯、更学术。

### ✍️ 写作建议

写移民、边境、公共政策类议论文时，可套用以下三个句型：

**[数据段]** [主语] + saw / registered + a + [比例] + per cent increase from + [时间]。
例如：*The number of illegal crossings registered a 35 per cent increase from last year.*

**[两难段]** While [主语] + has/have + [动作A]，[主语] + has/have struggled to simultaneously + [动作B] + while + [动作C]。
例如：*While the city has tightened border controls, it has struggled to simultaneously protect human rights while deterring illegal entry.*

**[博弈段]** [政策] + has been subject to push back from + [群体]。
例如：*The stricter immigration policy has been subject to push back from human rights groups.*

这四个表达都直接摘自真实的英文新闻，套用到中考、高考或雅思议论文中，都能让你的语言更具新闻感和逻辑深度。
