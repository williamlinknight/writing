# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：词汇升级——写"裁决与处罚"不只会 punish 和 stop，跟FT学5个裁决类动作动词

批改学生写"违规—被处罚—上诉—有结果"这类段落时，最常见的一段是这样：*He broke the rule. The school punished him. At first the punishment was one year, but later it became half a year. He said he did not do it. But the school found he changed the paper, and other students said it was true.*——句子没错，但动词只有 punish 和 say 两个，整段像流水账。

写"裁决"，英文真正的高级感来自**动作动词的层级**：处罚是被"维持"还是被"推翻"、期限是被"减半"还是"撤销"、证据是被"篡改"还是被"多方印证"——每一步都是不同的动词。今天我们从《金融时报》2020年12月关于俄罗斯全球体育禁赛期被减半的报道中，学5个**裁决类动作动词**。这篇报道是一场"制裁—抗辩—裁决—证据"的完整演示，写规则、处罚、争议类话题（中高考议论文、雅思 Task 2、读后续写的冲突段落）都能直接借用。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №47 — *俄罗斯全球体育禁赛期被减半*（2020年12月，国际体育仲裁法庭裁定）

## 📖 FT原文选段

> Russia's four-year ban from global sporting events **has been halved**, in a move that will affect athletes competing at the next two Olympic Games, but could end the long-running fallout from its state-sponsored doping scandal.

> The Court of Arbitration for Sport, a Swiss body considered the ultimate arbiter for global sports disputes, ruled on Thursday that wide-ranging sanctions applied on Russia by the World Anti-Doping Agency **should be upheld**. However, CAS also decided to reduce the original four-year ban to two years.

> This decision means that, although some Russian athletes will be allowed to compete at big sporting events, they are able to do so only as "neutral athletes", while the country's flag and anthem are **barred**.

> Wada's latest sanction is based on allegations that Russian authorities **tampered with** a database from its Moscow testing laboratory before handing it over to anti-doping investigators last year.

> Many international bodies, including the International Olympic Committee, the games organisers, have independently **corroborated** Mr Rodchenkov's evidence.

> In its ruling on Thursday, CAS said: "This panel has **imposed** consequences to reflect the nature and seriousness of the non-compliance [to doping controls] and to ensure that the integrity of sport against the scourge of doping is maintained. ..."

**中文对照**

> 俄罗斯为期四年的全球体育禁赛令被减半，此举将会影响参加未来两届奥运会的运动员，但也可能结束该国政府支持的兴奋剂丑闻带来的长期影响。
>
> 被视为全球体育争端最终仲裁机构、位于瑞士的国际体育仲裁法庭(CAS)裁定，维持世界反兴奋剂机构(WADA)对俄罗斯实施的广泛制裁。然而，该法庭还决定将原来的四年禁赛期缩短为两年。
>
> 这一决定意味着，尽管一些俄罗斯运动员将获准参加大型体育赛事，但他们只能以"中立运动员"身份参赛，禁止升俄罗斯国旗和奏俄罗斯国歌。
>
> 世界反兴奋剂机构的最新制裁是基于相关指控，即俄罗斯当局在去年将莫斯科检测实验室的数据库交给反兴奋剂调查人员之前，篡改了该数据库。
>
> 许多国际机构，包括奥运会的组织者国际奥委会(IOC)，都独立证实了罗琴科夫的证词。
>
> 国际体育仲裁法庭表示："本仲裁庭的仲裁结果反映了违反兴奋剂管制的行为的性质和严重性，并且是为了确保体育运动的诚信免受兴奋剂的祸害。"

## 🎯 5个高分词汇解析

### 1️⃣ uphold — 维持（裁决、处罚、决定）

**原文出处：** "...wide-ranging sanctions applied on Russia by the World Anti-Doping Agency **should be upheld**."

**为什么好：** 学生写"法院支持了这个处罚"，只会 *agreed with the punishment* 或 *said the punishment was right*——两个都是"表态"，不是"裁决"。**uphold** 是法律与规则语境里的核心动词，"经审查后依然认定有效"：**uphold a ruling**（维持裁决）、**uphold a ban**（维持禁令）、**uphold sanctions**（维持制裁）。它的反义动作才是 overturn（推翻）。

**❌ 学生初版**
The court agreed with the punishment from the anti-doping body.

**✅ 升级版**
The Court of Arbitration for Sport upheld the sanctions imposed by the World Anti-Doping Agency.

**💡 效果**
uphold 一词就交代了"有人挑战 → 机构审查 → 处罚依然成立"的完整过程。写规则与处罚类话题，"维持"和"推翻"是两个必须区分开的动作。

**例句：** The Supreme Court upheld a lower court's ruling on data privacy.

---

### 2️⃣ halve — 把……减半

**原文出处：** "Russia's four-year ban from global sporting events **has been halved**..."

**为什么好：** 学生写"从四年变成两年"，标准写法是 *the ban was reduced from four years to two years*——把数字重复念一遍。**halve** 一个动词就说完了"减半"：**halve the ban**（把禁赛期减半）、**halve the penalty**（把处罚减半）、**the deficit has halved**（赤字减半）。它的同位词还有 double（翻倍）、triple（增至三倍）。

**❌ 学生初版**
The ban was reduced from four years to two years.

**✅ 升级版**
Russia's four-year ban from global sport has been halved.

**💡 效果**
写数字变化时，一个动词能顶一整句数字改写。阅卷老师看到 halved，立刻知道你掌握了数量表达的"动词化"写法。

**例句：** The company halved its marketing budget during the downturn.

---

### 3️⃣ impose ... on — 对……施加（处罚、限制、规定）

**原文出处：** "This panel has **imposed** consequences to reflect the nature and seriousness of the non-compliance..." / "Limited curbs were previously **imposed on** Russian athletes at the past two Olympics."

**为什么好：** 学生表达"惩罚他们"永远是 *punish them*。但正式写作要说出"施加的是什么"：**impose sanctions on sb**（对某方施加制裁）、**impose a ban on sth**（对某事施加禁令）、**impose limits on sth**（施加限制）。被动式 **be imposed on** 是政策类文章的高频结构——主语是"处罚"，执行者反而退到句尾。

**❌ 学生初版**
The court punished the country with a two-year ban.

**✅ 升级版**
The court imposed a two-year ban on Russian athletes.

**💡 效果**
impose ... on 把笼统的"惩罚"变成"施加某项具体制裁"，这正是议论文需要的精确度：谁施加、施加给谁、施加的是什么，一句话交代清楚。

**例句：** The government imposed stricter limits on single-use plastics.

---

### 4️⃣ tamper with — 篡改、擅自改动（数据、证据、文件）

**原文出处：** "...Russian authorities **tampered with** a database from its Moscow testing laboratory before handing it over to anti-doping investigators last year."

**为什么好：** 学生写"偷偷改了数据"只会 *changed the data secretly*——副词硬撑。**tamper with** 精准表达"未经授权地动手脚"，且自带贬义：**tamper with evidence**（篡改证据）、**tamper with the results**（篡改结果）、**tamper with a database**（篡改数据库）。

**❌ 学生初版**
They changed the data secretly.

**✅ 升级版**
Investigators found that the authorities had tampered with the testing database.

**💡 效果**
tamper with 是学术诚信、比赛作弊、数据造假类话题的考点词——一个短语同时表达"动手脚"和"违规"，比 secretly change 有力得多。

**例句：** Any student caught tampering with exam records will face expulsion.

---

### 5️⃣ corroborate — 印证、证实（某人的说法/证据）

**原文出处：** "Many international bodies, including the International Olympic Committee, the games organisers, have independently **corroborated** Mr Rodchenkov's evidence."

**为什么好：** 学生写"很多人都说这是真的"只会 *many people said it was true*。**corroborate** 的含义更精确——用**独立的证据**去印证某人的说法：**corroborate a claim**（印证某说法）、**corroborate evidence**（印证证词）、**independently corroborated**（经独立印证）。它强调的是"证据之间对得上"，不是"人多"。

**❌ 学生初版**
Many people said his words were true.

**✅ 升级版**
Several international bodies independently corroborated his evidence.

**💡 效果**
写"证据链"的段落，corroborate 几乎是不可替代的动词。它让论证从"大家都说"升级到"多方独立印证"，这是雅思 Task 2 和高中议论文都吃分的地方。

**例句：** The witness's account was corroborated by security footage.

## ✍️ 写作建议

写"违规—处理—争议—结论"时，不要停留在 punish + say 的空转上。按**裁决、期限、处罚、证据**四个动作层级组织词汇，段落立刻有专业感：

| 段落功能 | 学生常写 | 升级表达 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 处罚被审查后成立 | agreed with the punishment | upheld the sanctions |
| 期限减少一半 | reduced from four years to two years | halved the ban |
| 施加某项制裁 | punished ... with a ban | imposed a ban on ... |
| 数据被偷偷改动 | changed the data secretly | tampered with the database |
| 说法被独立印证 | many people said it was true | independently corroborated the evidence |

同样是写这场裁决，学生版是 *The court agreed with the punishment, but the ban became two years. Russia said it did not cheat. But many people said the evidence was true.*，FT版是 *The CAS upheld the sanctions but halved the four-year ban, after Russian authorities tampered with a testing database which international bodies later corroborated*——**高下之分不在立场，而在动词是否落在动作的层级上**。

顺带积累同篇里的另外几个高分表达：**be barred**（被禁止：the country's flag and anthem are barred）、**be prevented from doing**（被阻止做：Russia will be prevented from bidding to host global events）、**turn whistleblower**（成为举报人：the former director has since turned whistleblower）、**tarnish the country's prestige**（玷污国家声望）、**subvert the system**（颠覆、破坏体系）、**curbs imposed on**（对……施加的限制）。

**今日练习：** 用今天的动词链，把这段"学生版"升级成90词的处罚与证据段：

> ❌ A student cheated in an exam. The school punished him. At first the school said he must leave school for one year, but later the time became half. The student said he did not cheat. But the school found he changed the exam paper, and other students said it was true.

试着把 punished him 换成 imposed a one-year suspension on him、the time became half 换成 the penalty was halved、changed the exam paper 换成 tampered with the exam paper、other students said it was true 换成 his classmates' accounts corroborated the evidence，再补一句 The school's decision was upheld after a review，看看整段是不是从"流水账"变成了"处分通告"。
