# 读外刊学写作：从「牛津疫苗70%有效率」学5个写数据对比与百分比的高分表达

学生一碰到“数据”和“对比”，句子往往变成简单陈述：*The efficacy was 90 per cent. The price went down.* 语法没错，但读不出“高与低”“差了多少”“反过来也成立”的层次。今天看《金融时报》(FT) 2020年11月关于牛津-阿斯利康疫苗三期试验的报道：同一款疫苗，半剂量+全剂量方案有效率90%，两全剂量方案只有62%，平均70%；与辉瑞、莫德纳接近95%对比又逊色不少。记者用5个表达，把“数字本身—数字间的对比—对比的方向—结果的伴随—因果的让步”讲清楚了。这5个表达可以直接搬进高考图表作文、雅思小作文的趋势描述里。

## 📝 原文对照

**English:**

> The coronavirus vaccine developed by Oxford university and AstraZeneca has proved effective in late-stage trials, **giving another boost to hopes** that the pandemic can be defeated.
>
> Under one dosing regime, the Oxford-AstraZeneca vaccine achieved **90 per cent efficacy**. That required a half dose of the vaccine to be given followed by a full dose at least a month later. When the jab was given as two full doses at least one month apart, efficacy was **only 62 per cent**. The average efficacy, during trials in the UK and Brazil, was **70 per cent**.
>
> The results for the new treatment were better than the **50 per cent effectiveness** sought by both the US Food and Drug Administration and the European Medicines Agency, which approve vaccines. But they were **less impressive than** trials of Covid-19 vaccines from Pfizer/BioNTech and Moderna, which showed effectiveness of **close to 95 per cent** in their interim results.
>
> Before the trials, we'd have expected an efficacy **around 60 to 70 per cent**. Plus it could be 90 per cent with one of the dosing regimens.
>
> **By contrast**, AstraZeneca, along with Johnson & Johnson, has said it will sell its inoculation at cost to the world during the pandemic.
>
> The jab is priced at about 3 to 4 dollars a dose, supply deals suggest, **a fraction of the price** of other vaccines. The vaccine can be stored long-term at normal fridge temperature, **between 2C and 8C**; other treatments require a storage temperature **as low as minus 70C**.

**中文对照：**

> 牛津大学和阿斯利康合作研发的新冠疫苗在后期试验中被证明有效，为战胜疫情的希望再添一把助力。
>
> 在一种剂量方案下，牛津-阿斯利康疫苗达到90%的有效率。该方案要求先注射半剂量疫苗，至少一个月后再注射全剂量。如果至少间隔一个月注射两次全剂量，有效率仅为62%。英国和巴西试验显示平均有效率为70%。
>
> 该疫苗结果优于美国FDA和欧洲药品管理局要求的50%有效率。但与辉瑞、莫德纳中期结果接近95%的有效率相比，又逊色不少。
>
> 试验前，人们本来预计有效率大约在60%到70%之间。再说，其中一种方案可达90%。
>
> 相比之下，阿斯利康——还有强生公司——表示疫情期间将以成本价向全世界销售疫苗。
>
> 供应协议显示，该疫苗约每剂3至4美元，仅为其他疫苗价格的一个零头。疫苗可在普通冰箱温度下（2至8摄氏度）长期储存；其他疫苗要求的储存温度低至零下70摄氏度。

## 🔍 重点词汇解析

### 1. giving another boost to hopes that —— 给……希望再添助力（结果伴随）

**原文出处：** "The coronavirus vaccine developed by Oxford university and AstraZeneca has proved effective in late-stage trials, **giving another boost to hopes** that the pandemic can be defeated."

**为什么好：** giving... 是现在分词作伴随状语，指出主句事件（疫苗有效）带来的直接结果（希望被提振），而不用另起一句 "This gave another boost..." 学生写结果常爱用 so、therefore 引导新句，句子显得碎；分词短语把“事实”和“结果”拧成一根绳，信息更紧凑。

**❌ 学生初版**
The vaccine proved effective. So people had more hope that the pandemic could be defeated.

**✅ 升级版**
The new policy has reduced emissions, giving fresh hope that the city can meet its climate target.

**💡 效果**
从“两句平铺”变成“一句带结果”——写政策影响、写措施效果，分词结尾最能体现因果的连贯感。

### 2. only 62 per cent / close to 95 per cent —— 写百分比，别只会 exactly/approximately

**原文出处：** "When the jab was given as two full doses at least one month apart, efficacy was **only 62 per cent**." ... "effectiveness of **close to 95 per cent** in their interim results."

**为什么好：** only 强调“偏低，未达预期”，close to 强调“接近但未达到”。学生写百分比总用 exactly、approximately，语气平平；FT记者用 only 表达失望，用 close to 表达“差一点就……”，态度和数字一起写进去了。

**❌ 学生初版**
The effectiveness was approximately 62 per cent. The other vaccine was approximately 95 per cent.

**✅ 升级版**
Efficacy with two full doses was only 62 per cent, while the rival vaccines showed effectiveness close to 95 per cent in their interim results.

**💡 效果**
同一个数字，加上 only 或 close to，读者立刻知道你是“觉得低”还是“觉得高”——图表作文趋势段最需要的就是这种态度。

### 3. By contrast —— 相反地，相比之下（强硬转折）

**原文出处：** "**By contrast**, AstraZeneca, along with Johnson & Johnson, has said it will sell its inoculation at cost to the world during the pandemic."

**为什么好：** 前文刚说辉瑞、莫德纳以盈利方式销售、富国抢购供应，By contrast 一出，话题立刻掉头到“成本价、卖给全世界”。它比 however 强硬，比 on the other hand 更直接指向“完全相反的另一种做法”。写中英对比、政策对比、企业对比，这是最高频的转折词之一。

**❌ 学生初版**
Pfizer sells at profit. However, AstraZeneca sells at cost.

**✅ 升级版**
Rich countries have secured most of the supply of the expensive vaccines. By contrast, AstraZeneca will sell its inoculation at cost to the world during the pandemic.

**💡 效果**
“然而”变成了“相比之下”——对比双方不再是并列陈述，而是形成明确的价值取向差异。

### 4. a fraction of the price / between 2C and 8C / as low as minus 70C —— 数量级与区间表达

**原文出处：** "The jab is priced at about 3 to 4 dollars a dose, supply deals suggest, **a fraction of the price** of other vaccines." ... "The vaccine can be stored long-term at normal fridge temperature, **between 2C and 8C**; other treatments require a storage temperature **as low as minus 70C**."

**为什么好：** a fraction of the price = 价格的零头，用来写“远低于”；between X and Y 写区间，as low as 写极值（强调多低）。学生写对比只会 "lower than"，写区间只会 "from...to..."；FT表达更精准：fraction 强调比例关系，as low as 强调惊人的低温。

**❌ 学生初版**
The price is lower than other vaccines. The temperature is from 2 to 8 degrees.

**✅ 升级版**
At about 3 to 4 dollars a dose, the vaccine costs a fraction of the price of other products, and it can be stored between 2C and 8C, while other treatments require temperatures as low as minus 70C.

**💡 效果**
“更便宜”变成了“零头价”，“2到8度”变成了可储存区间，对比两边的温差也立刻有了画面感。

### 5. around 60 to 70 per cent / Plus —— 区间估算与补充递进

**原文出处：** "Before the trials, we'd have expected an efficacy **around 60 to 70 per cent**. **Plus** it could be 90 per cent with one of the dosing regimens."

**为什么好：** around 60 to 70 per cent 把“约数”和“区间”叠在一起，写预测性数据最自然；Plus 在这里不是数学加法，而是口语化的“再说／此外”，用来补充一个有利条件，语气比 Furthermore 轻松、比 Moreover 更像报道口吻。

**❌ 学生初版**
We expected the efficacy to be about 60 to 70 per cent. Moreover, it could be 90 per cent with one dosing regimen.

**✅ 升级版**
Before the launch, analysts expected turnout to be around 60 to 70 per cent. Plus, the new subsidy could push participation even higher.

**💡 效果**
“约60到70%”比精确数字更符合预测语境；Plus 这一句一接，论证从“预估”滑向“乐观补充”，节奏自然。

## ✍️ 写作建议

这5个表达连起来，就是一套完整的**“数据对比写作链”**：先用 giving another boost to hopes that 写数据带来的结果，再用 only / close to 给百分比加态度，用 By contrast 掉头写反面做法，用 a fraction of / between / as low as 写数量级与区间，最后用 around ... per cent 和 Plus 写预测与补充。写高考图表作文或雅思小作文，可以直接套：

> The recycling rate rose to 68 per cent, giving fresh hope that the city can meet its 2030 target. By contrast, landfill use remains close to 90 per cent of total waste. The cost of the new bins is a fraction of the price of building a new plant, and they can be placed between residential areas and schools. Officials expected participation to be around 50 to 60 per cent. Plus, the education campaign should push the figure higher.

另外提醒一句：**数据作文的分不在“数字列得多全”，而在“数字之间是什么关系”**。学生最爱写 The number was 90. The other number was 62，而FT记者的做法是：谁高谁低、差多少、反过来怎么样、预期是多少。图表类、议论类作文，都可以用这条链。
