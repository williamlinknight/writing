学英语写作的同学，写议论文论证观点时，要么反复用 "important"，要么句子结构单调。今天我们从FT一篇剖析特朗普“用人不察”的英文评论中，提炼4个可直接迁移到议论文的高分表达，让你的观点论证更有深度、更有说服力。

## 原文选段

> Populist movements face a Catch-22. They take power arguing that a governing class or even a "deep state", insulated from the democratic will of the people, has grown snobby, opaque and self-serving.

> 民粹主义运动面临一个两难境地。他们主张统治阶级甚至“深层政府”与人民的民主意志隔绝，已变得势利、不透明和自私自利，从而夺取权力。

> But even an anti-technocratic movement, once in power, needs enough technocratic knowhow to run the government and locate the abuses it has been complaining about.

> 但即便是反技术官僚运动，一旦掌权，也需要足够的技术官僚知识来运转政府，并找出其一直抱怨的弊病。

> Seen in this light, the suspicion that Mr Trump was a budding autocrat was always off-base.

> 从这个角度看，特朗普正逐渐成为独裁者的怀疑一直是站不住脚的。

## 高分表达解析

### 1. Catch-22

**原文：** "Populist movements face a Catch-22."

**中文解释：** 两难困境、循环困境。源自美国作家约瑟夫·海勒的同名小说，指一种相互制约、无法摆脱的窘境。

**为什么好：** 学生写"两难"或"困境"只会用 *difficult situation* 或 *problem*。*Catch-22* 自带"规则互相卡死、进退维谷"的意象，写社会议题、教育议题的议论文时极具说服力。

| 学生写法 | 升级写法 |
|---------|---------|
| Students face a difficult situation when choosing a major. | Students face a Catch-22 when choosing a major: no experience, no job; no job, no experience. |
| It is a serious problem for young people. | Young people are trapped in a Catch-22 of housing prices and wages. |

**例句：** Graduates are caught in a Catch-22 — employers demand experience, but no one is willing to hire them without it.

---

### 2. at the tail-end of

**原文：** "...usually at the tail-end of a long tenure in the White House for his party."

**中文解释：** ……的末期/尾声。tail-end 指一段时间或事件的最后阶段。

**为什么好：** 学生写"在……的最后"只会用 *at the end of* 或 *in the last years of*。*at the tail-end of* 更正式、更有书面语感，适合写历史回顾、政策变迁或趋势判断类议论文。

| 学生写法 | 升级写法 |
|---------|---------|
| The policy was introduced at the end of his presidency. | The policy was introduced at the tail-end of his presidency. |
| In the last years of the 20th century, the internet changed everything. | At the tail-end of the 20th century, the internet changed everything. |

**例句：** At the tail-end of the pandemic, many industries are still struggling to recover.

---

### 3. technocratic knowhow

**原文：** "...needs enough technocratic knowhow to run the government..."

**中文解释：** 技术官僚知识、专业技术能力。technocratic 指技术官僚的/技术政治的，knowhow 指诀窍、技术能力。

**为什么好：** 学生写"专业能力"只会用 *professional ability* 或 *skill*。*technocratic knowhow* 是一个地道的搭配，写政府治理、公共政策、教育改革等议题时非常精准。

| 学生写法 | 升级写法 |
|---------|---------|
| The government needs professional skills to solve the problem. | The government needs enough technocratic knowhow to solve the problem. |
| Teachers should have strong teaching ability. | Teachers should have strong pedagogical knowhow. |

**例句：** Running a modern city requires not only political will but also technocratic knowhow in urban planning and public health.

---

### 4. off-base

**原文：** "...the suspicion that Mr Trump was a budding autocrat was always off-base."

**中文解释：** 站不住脚的、错误的、偏离目标的。base 原指棒球中的垒包，off-base 即"离开垒包"，引申为观点不准确、立场不站得住。

**为什么好：** 学生写"错误的"只会用 *wrong* 或 *incorrect*。*off-base* 语气更柔和、更地道，特别适合在议论文中反驳对方观点，显得客观理性。

| 学生写法 | 升级写法 |
|---------|---------|
| The idea that technology is bad for students is wrong. | The suspicion that technology harms students' learning is off-base. |
| His opinion is incorrect. | His claim that social media only brings harm is off-base. |

**例句：** The claim that remote learning has no benefits at all is off-base — many students thrive in flexible learning environments.

## 给同学们的外刊阅读建议

这篇FT评论的精髓不在于特朗普，而在于它如何**做让步与反驳**：先承认民粹主义对“深层政府”腐败的批评有时是对的（让步），随即转折指出反技术官僚运动一旦掌权仍需技术官僚知识（反驳）。这种“承认局部合理性，再整体推翻”的结构，是雅思/高考议论文拿高分的核心技巧。

建议每次读外刊时，标注出文章里用来**让步**（even though, admittedly）和**反驳**（however, yet, in fact）的句子，套到自己的作文中练习。坚持两周，你的议论文逻辑会立刻上一个档次。
