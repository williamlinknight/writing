## 为什么是这一篇？

很多同学写英语作文，遇到"数据变化"或"重大新闻宣布"时，只会用 very big / a lot of people / the price went up 这种初中级表达。FT这篇关于辉瑞/BioNTech疫苗90%有效的报道，恰好蕴含了3个考场高频句型——它们不是冷门词，而是**结构公式**，学会就能套用到几乎所有议论文和数据描述类写作中。

我们来看看原文中最精华的三个句子结构。

## 句型一：突破性成果宣布

**FT 原文：**

> A Covid-19 vaccine being developed by Pfizer and Germany's BioNTech has been found to be more than 90 per cent effective, in a breakthrough that could make the shot available for use by the end of the year if drug authorities give it the green light.

**句型公式：**

> [Subject] has been found to be [adj/prep], in a breakthrough that could [result]

**为什么好？**

大多数学生写"重要发现"时会写：

**❌ 学生初版**
> Scientists found a new vaccine. It is very effective. It can help many people.

**✅ 升级版**
> A new vaccine has been found to be more than 90 per cent effective, in a breakthrough that could protect millions of people by the end of the year.

**💡 效果**
`has been found to be` 比 `Scientists found` 更客观、更新闻化；`in a breakthrough that could + 结果` 这个同位语结构，用一个逗号就交代了突破的影响，比另起一句简洁得多。

**考场仿写：**

> A new study on climate change has been found to be highly alarming, in a breakthrough that could reshape global environmental policies.

> Regular reading has been found to be effective in improving writing skills, in a breakthrough that could change how students prepare for exams.

## 句型二：市场数据联动描述

**FT 原文：**

> Pfizer shares jumped almost 15 per cent in pre-market trading in New York, while those of Nasdaq-listed BioNTech rose 25 per cent.

> British Airways parent IAG soared by a third in London, while Air France-KLM jumped 30 per cent in Paris and Ryanair climbed 16 per cent in Dublin.

**句型公式：**

> [Entity A] [strong verb] by [X%], while [Entity B] [verb] by [Y%]

**动词替换库（考场上直接取用）：**

| 动词 | 程度 | 适用场景 |
|------|------|----------|
| jump | 大幅上升 | 股价、销量、人数 |
| soar | 猛涨（幅度更大） | 数据爆发式增长 |
| climb | 稳步上升 | 温和增长 |
| rally | 反弹上涨 | 先跌后涨 |
| add to earlier gains | 在原有涨势上继续涨 | 连续增长 |

**❌ 学生初版**
> The price of Apple shares went up a lot. Samsung shares also went up.

**✅ 升级版**
> Apple shares jumped nearly 8 per cent in after-hours trading, while Samsung shares climbed 3 per cent in Seoul.

**💡 效果**
用 `jumped` / `climbed` 替代 `went up a lot`，数字精度（nearly 8 per cent）替代模糊描述，`while` 连接两个市场信息，一句话传递双倍信息量。

**考场仿写：**

> Sales of electric cars soared by 45 per cent in Europe last year, while traditional petrol car sales fell by 12 per cent.

> The number of students studying abroad jumped by 20 per cent in 2025, while those choosing domestic universities climbed only 3 per cent.

## 句型三：标准对比 + 补充说明

**FT 原文：**

> The Pfizer results far exceed the US Food and Drug Administration's criteria for approving a Covid-19 vaccine, which mandates an efficacy of at least 50 per cent in placebo-controlled trials.

**句型公式：**

> [X] far exceed [Y]'s standards for [Z], which mandates/requires [condition]

**为什么好？**

当你想说"某个结果远超标准"时，大多数学生会写：

**❌ 学生初版**
> The results are much better than the standard. The standard says you need at least 50 per cent.

**✅ 升级版**
> The test scores far exceed the university's admission criteria, which requires a minimum of 85 per cent in English proficiency.

**💡 效果**
`far exceed` 比 `much better than` 正式精准；非限制性定语从句 `which mandates/requires` 优雅地补充标准细节，不用另起一句。

**考试替代品：**
- `far exceed` 可替换为 `far surpass` / `significantly outperform`
- `mandates` 可替换为 `stipulates` / `sets a threshold of`

**考场仿写：**

> The company's quarterly revenue far exceeded market expectations, which had predicted only modest growth of 5 per cent.

> The student's final project far surpassed the course requirements, which mandated at least 10,000 words of original research.

## 写作建议

这3个句型有一个共同特点：它们都不是"单词"的胜利，而是**结构**的胜利。你不必背 `jumped` 还是 `soared`——真正拉开分数差距的，是 `has been found to be...in a breakthrough that could...` 这种复合句结构的能力。

建议：
1. 把这三个句型公式抄在笔记本上
2. 每个公式自己写2个仿写句（用不同话题）
3. 下次写作文时，刻意用至少一个替换掉原来的简单句
