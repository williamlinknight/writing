# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：词汇升级——写"值不值得、代价与失望"不只会 important 和 sad，跟FT学5个评价与心理表达

批改学生写"父母为教育花钱值不值""要不要为了别人牺牲"这类议论时，最常见的段落是：*Parents pay a lot of money for their children's education. They think it is important. They prepare for a hard life. Sometimes there are bad results that they did not think of. Some parents feel they made their children sad. But maybe the old habit will become less and less.*——观点都对，但评价词只有 important 和 bad，情绪词只有 sad，整段没有一个**有画面、有判断力**的表达。

写"投入与代价"这类话题，英文的高级感来自两组词：一组是**评价**（值不值、后果严不严重），一组是**心理**（准备承受、内疚、逐渐释怀）。今天我们从《金融时报》2021年9月关于英国私校费用与家庭选择的专栏中，学5个**评价与心理表达**。这篇是一位财务规划师的现身说法，544个词里装着"算账—警惕—内疚—放手"的完整心理过程，正好对应中高考和雅思里最常见的"教育投入与家庭选择"话题。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №205 — *你能承受日益昂贵的英国私校吗？（下）*（2021年9月，作者为财务规划师 Charles Calkin）

## 📖 FT原文选段

> The obvious question is this: does private education **deliver value for money**? Many families undoubtedly think it still does. But success is not guaranteed.

> **Beware of unintended consequences.** Your children may find themselves in the company of much richer families, with children chauffeur-driven to school... It can be hard keeping up, and it can **distort children's expectations**.

> Until then many will be **braced for** the sacrifices they consider necessary to pay for good schooling.

> But those who cannot afford what they enjoyed themselves should not feel they are **letting their own children down**.

> We put all five of our children through private education. Would we do it again? I don't think it would be affordable now, given the fee increases. It was a struggle then. We have no expectation that our grandchildren will attend private school. I am happy for this family tradition to **wither**.

**中文对照**

> 显而易见的问题是：私校教育是否物有所值？许多家庭无疑认为它仍然如此。但成功是没有保证的。
>
> 要提防各种意外后果。你的孩子可能会发现同学来自富得多的家庭，那些孩子可能有司机开车送到学校……你可能会觉得很难跟上，而且这些会扭曲孩子们的期望。
>
> 在那之前，许多人将准备为子女获得优良教育买单，认为这种牺牲是必要的。
>
> 但是，那些负担不起自己曾经享受的私校教育的人士，不应该觉得他们对不起自己的孩子。
>
> 我们让五个孩子都接受了私校教育。如果有机会，我们会再来一遍吗？鉴于学费上涨，我不认为这么做在当今是可负担的。我们不期待孙子孙女会上私校。我乐意让这个家庭传统自行消亡。

## 🎯 5个高分词汇解析

### 1️⃣ deliver value for money — 物有所值、值这个价

**原文出处：** "The obvious question is this: does private education **deliver value for money**?"

**为什么好：** 学生写"值不值这个钱"，只会 *it is good for the price* 或 *is it worth it*——前者是中式搭配，后者太口语。**value for money** 是英语里评价"钱花得值不值"的固定说法：**deliver value for money**（提供物有所值的价值）、**offer good value for money**（性价比高）、**get value for money**（花钱花得值）。搭配动词 deliver / offer / get 都成立。

**❌ 学生初版**
Parents want to know if the school is good for the price.

**✅ 升级版**
Parents want to know whether private schooling delivers value for money.

**💡 效果**
value for money 把"贵不贵"和"值不值"分开说清楚了——价格高不等于不值，价格低也不等于划算。写教育投入、消费选择、旅游住宿类话题，这个词组是现成的评价尺子。

**例句：** At that price, the laptop offers excellent value for money.

---

### 2️⃣ be braced for — 绷紧身体、做好准备迎接（坏消息/代价）

**原文出处：** "...many will be **braced for** the sacrifices they consider necessary to pay for good schooling."

**为什么好：** 学生写"准备面对困难"只会 *prepare for the hard life*。**be braced for**（也作 **brace yourself for**）暗示"知道冲击要来，绷紧身体去承受"——原是身体动作（brace 绷紧），用在心理上自带画面感：**be braced for a backlash**（准备迎接反弹）、**be braced for criticism**（准备挨批）、**be braced for the worst**（做最坏打算）。

**❌ 学生初版**
Parents prepare for the hard life they must live.

**✅ 升级版**
Many families are braced for the sacrifices that good schooling demands.

**💡 效果**
prepare for 是中性动作，be braced for 是心理状态——写"明知要付出代价但仍选择付出"的家庭，这个短语比 prepare 高出一个层次。读后续写里写人物迎接坏消息，也可以直接用它。

**例句：** The industry is braced for a wave of job cuts.

---

### 3️⃣ unintended consequences — 意想不到的后果（政策的副作用）

**原文出处：** "**Beware of unintended consequences.**"

**为什么好：** 学生写"会有大家没想到的坏结果"只会 *there will be bad results that people did not think of*——从句绕一圈。**unintended consequences** 是社会、政策、教育类写作的固定表达，专指"意图之外、意料之外"的结果：**the unintended consequences of a policy**（某项政策的意外后果）、**have unintended consequences for**（对……产生意外影响）。前面加 **Beware of**（要提防）就是一个高分祈使句开头。

**❌ 学生初版**
The plan may bring bad results that people did not expect.

**✅ 升级版**
The plan may carry unintended consequences for poorer families.

**💡 效果**
这个词组自带"我做过利弊分析"的专业感。写政策评价、教育政策、科技发展类议论文，用它开头或结尾，论证立刻从"好或坏"升级到"有好有坏，要注意副作用"。

**例句：** Raising tuition fees had the unintended consequence of driving away talented students.

---

### 4️⃣ let sb down — 让（信任你、指望你的人）失望

**原文出处：** "...those who cannot afford what they enjoyed themselves should not feel they are **letting their own children down**."

**为什么好：** 学生表达内疚只会 *made my parents sad* 或 *disappointed my parents*。**let sb down** 更贴近英语里"辜负了别人对你的期待"那层意思，是口语与书面通用短语动词：**let your parents down**（让父母失望）、**let the team down**（拖累团队）、**feel you are letting sb down**（觉得自己辜负了某人）。它的力量在于——失望的人不是被"伤害"，而是"期待落空"。

**❌ 学生初版**
They think they disappointed their children.

**✅ 升级版**
They should not feel they are letting their own children down.

**💡 效果**
写家庭关系、责任、承诺类话题，"内疚"需要准确的落点。let sb down 把内疚指向"辜负期待"，比 disappoint 更有情感重量，也比 be sorry 具体得多。

**例句：** He trained every morning because he did not want to let his coach down.

---

### 5️⃣ wither — 逐渐凋零、自行消亡

**原文出处：** "I am happy for this family tradition to **wither**."

**为什么好：** 学生写"慢慢没有了"只会 *becomes less and less* 或 *disappears slowly*。**wither** 本义是植物枯萎，用在传统、关系、产业、期待上时，自带"缓慢、不可逆、无人挽救"的画面：**traditions wither**（传统凋零）、**the industry withered**（产业衰落）、**their friendship withered**（友谊渐渐淡了）。

**❌ 学生初版**
The tradition becomes less and less in their family.

**✅ 升级版**
They are happy to let the family tradition wither.

**💡 效果**
一个 wither 就完成了"缓慢消失"的时间感和情绪感。读后续写里写感情变淡、热闹散去、老店关门，用它比 slowly disappear 高一个量级；议论文里写传统文化保护，wither 的反面正是 revive（复兴）。

**例句：** Without new readers, the print magazine slowly withered.

## ✍️ 写作建议

写"投入与选择"时，不要停在 important + sad 的空转上。按**评价—代价—后果—情绪—结局**的顺序组织词汇，段落立刻立体：

| 段落功能 | 学生常写 | 升级表达 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 评价钱花得值不值 | is it good for the price | deliver value for money |
| 准备承受代价 | prepare for the hard life | be braced for the sacrifices |
| 政策的意外副作用 | bad results people did not expect | unintended consequences |
| 觉得自己辜负了孩子 | disappointed their children | letting their children down |
| 传统慢慢消失 | becomes less and less | wither |

同样是写"家庭为教育投入"，学生版是 *Parents spend a lot of money and think it is important. Sometimes there are bad results. Some parents feel sad.*，FT版是 *Parents must ask whether schooling delivers value for money, be braced for the sacrifices, and watch out for the unintended consequences — but those who cannot afford it should not feel they are letting their children down*——**高下之分不在观点对错，而在评价有没有尺度、情绪有没有落点**。

顺带积累同篇里的另外几个高分表达：**be not to be ignored**（不容忽视：the state school option is not to be ignored）、**opt to do sth**（选择做：they opted to save money）、**in the company of**（与……为伴：find themselves in the company of richer families）、**distort children's expectations**（扭曲孩子的期望）、**indulge in negative behaviour**（沉溺于消极行为）、**it was a struggle**（那是一段挣扎的日子）。

**今日练习：** 用今天的表达，把这段"学生版"升级成90词的评论段：

> ❌ Many parents pay a lot of money for extra classes. They think it is important. They prepare for a hard life. But classes sometimes have bad results that parents did not expect, because children compare themselves with richer classmates. Some parents feel they made their children sad. But maybe the old habit of extra classes will become less and less.

试着把 think it is important 换成 ask whether it delivers value for money、prepare for a hard life 换成 be braced for the sacrifices、bad results that parents did not expect 换成 unintended consequences、made their children sad 换成 let their children down、become less and less 换成 wither，看看整段是不是从"意见"变成了"分析"。
