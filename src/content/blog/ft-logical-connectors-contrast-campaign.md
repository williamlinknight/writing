英语写作中，最常被忽视的一项基本功，不是词汇量，而是**逻辑衔接**。

两段话之间怎么过渡？对比关系怎么表达？原因和结果怎么串联？很多学生只会用"but"和"because"走天下，写出来的文章像没有接缝的积木——虽然堆在一起，但随时会散。

今天我们从FT一篇选举现场报道中，学习5种**对比让步型逻辑衔接**结构。

## FT原文

> **来源：** FT双语阅读 #11 — 《特朗普展开密集竞选活动 力求追赶拜登》

> Donald Trump set off on Sunday on an eleventh-hour blitz of five crucial swing states, **as** polls showed the president continuing to lag behind Democrat Joe Biden with less than 72 hours until polls close.
>
> **As of** Sunday morning, more than 92m ballots had been cast in the election, according to the US Elections Project.
>
> **While** the president won all five of the states in 2016, some polls suggest he faces a more difficult path this time. A Saturday Des Moines Register poll, **meanwhile**, gave Mr Trump a seven percentage point lead in Iowa.
>
> **Amid** the surge in early voting, many state officials are warning Americans to expect a delayed result.
>
> Some competitive states have begun counting ballots and are expected to report results on election night. **But** others such as Michigan and Pennsylvania are not allowed to start counting ballots until election day, **meaning** it could take several days for states to report their totals, **given** the expected volume of mail-in ballots.

## 五个逻辑衔接结构

### 1. While + 对比句：先让步，后转折

**原文出处：**
> *While* the president won all five of the states in 2016, some polls suggest he faces a more difficult path this time.

**为什么好：** "While" 引导的让步状语从句，先承认一个事实（特朗普2016年赢了这五个州），再引出对比信息（今年选情更艰难）。这种"先让步、后转折"的结构，比直接说"but"更含蓄、更有说服力。

**结构公式：**
> While + [一方事实], [另一方却不同]

**❌ 学生初版**
> Although Trump won five states in 2016, but he was behind in polls this time.

**✅ 升级版**
> While the president won all five states in 2016, polls suggest he faces a more difficult path this time.

**💡 效果**
去掉重复的"although... but"，用"while"简洁引出对比���像记者一样客观陈述。

**模板：**
> While [普遍认知/过去情况], [当前情况却不同].

> *While online learning offers flexibility, many students struggle to maintain self-discipline without in-person supervision.*

### 2. Amid + 名词：用大背景引出具体事件

**原文出处：**
> *Amid* the surge in early voting, many state officials are warning Americans to expect a delayed result.

**为什么好：** "Amid"（在...之中）把一个大的社会背景（投票率飙升）和具体事件（官员警告）联系起来。比"Because there was a surge in early voting"简洁得多。

**结构公式：**
> Amid + [大背景/趋势], [具体事件/反应]

**❌ 学生初版**
> Because there was a surge in early voting, many state officials warned people to expect a delayed result.

**✅ 升级版**
> Amid the surge in early voting, many state officials warned that a delayed result should be expected.

**💡 效果**
Amid把原因融入背景状语，让句子从\"为什么\"变成\"在什么背景下\"，更符合英文写作习惯。

**模板：**
> Amid [当前趋势/争议/增长], [主体] [行动/反应].

> *Amid growing concerns about screen time, many schools are rethinking their digital learning policies.*

### 3. Given + 名词短语：引出合理理由

**原文出处：**
> ...it could take several days for states to report their totals, *given* the expected volume of mail-in ballots.

**为什么好：** "Given" 表示"考虑到...的情况"，用在结论后面引出合理理由。比"because"更正式，更适合议论文。

**结构公式：**
> [结论], given + [理由/考虑因素]

**❌ 学生初版**
> It may take several days to count because there will be many mail-in ballots.

**✅ 升级版**
> It could take several days to count the ballots, given the unprecedented volume of mail-in votes.

**💡 效果**
Given 引出的理由逻辑更紧密，且不破坏主句结构。

**模板：**
> [某事很困难/需要时间], given [客观限制因素].

> *Reducing carbon emissions by 50% within a decade is ambitious, given the current pace of technological adoption.*

### 4. But + 具体对比：突然转折的力量

**原文出处：**
> Some competitive states have begun counting ballots... *But* others such as Michigan and Pennsylvania are not allowed to start counting ballots until election day.

**为什么好：** 简单的一个"but"在段中制造了清晰的对比关系。先讲一些州已经开始计票，再用but引出另一些州不能提前计票，对比一目了然。

**❌ 学生初版**
> Some states can count. But Pennsylvania can't.

**✅ 升级版**
> Some competitive states have begun counting ballots. But others such as Michigan and Pennsylvania are not allowed to start until election day.

**💡 效果**
在but后补充具体信息（州名、时间限制），让对比有血有肉。

### 5. As / As of：引出时间/原因背景

**原文出处（两个用法）：**
> Donald Trump set off... *as* polls showed the president continuing to lag behind...
> **As of** Sunday morning, more than 92m ballots had been cast...

**为什么好：** "As" 既可以表示"当...时"（时间），也可以表示"正如...所显示的"（伴随原因）。"As of" 表示"截至某个时间点"，是新闻报道中引出数据时间范围的标配。

**❌ 学生初版**
> Trump started campaigning. At the same time, polls showed he was behind.

**✅ 升级版**
> Trump set off on an eleventh-hour blitz of crucial swing states, as polls showed the president continuing to lag behind.

**💡 效果**
"As"把两个动作无缝衔接进同一句，比and或at the same time更自然。

**模板（as用于原因/伴随）：**
> [主要动作], as [伴随情况/原因].

> *The company announced layoffs, as quarterly profits fell by 30%.*

**模板（as of用于数据）：**
> As of [时间], [数据] had [状态].

> *As of last month, over 2 million users had signed up for the platform.*

## 写作建议

对比让步型逻辑衔接的核心作用，是在论证中**承认对立面，但守住自己的立场**。中式议论文喜欢直接说"但是我认为..."，而英文更习惯先承认对方有道理（while），再给出自己的理由（but/amid/given）。

练习方法：下次写议论文时，在每一个"but"之前，尝试加一个"while"让步句。
