# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：开头技巧——用「动词+因果链」式开头一句话讲清全局

## 你的开头为什么总被跳过？

批改作文时，最常见的开头是这两句：

> "Recently, the vaccine delivery is not enough. Many countries are in trouble."

问题不在语法，而在**信息密度**——第一句没有动词力度，第二句没有具体数字，两句加起来等于什么都没说。

**好开头的任务不是"铺垫"，而是"交代全局"：** 谁、发生了什么、变化多大、为什么。今天我们从《金融时报》一篇关于COVAX下调疫苗交付预测的报道，拆解记者的三种高密度开头技法。这篇报道第一句就同时塞进了**强动词 + 精确数字 + 三原因因果链**，正是中国学生最缺的"开头信息量"。

## FT原文（示范素材）

> **Covax has slashed its forecasts for coronavirus vaccine deliveries to the developing world by about 25 per cent this year, after India's export ban, manufacturing problems and delays in approvals of new shots knocked the programme off track.**
>
> Deliveries under the scheme to provide vaccines to low- and middle-income countries are ramping up, **with 1.1bn doses set to be available for the rest of the year. But Covax will fall short of its target of delivering 2bn vaccines this year, with a total of 1.4bn shots.**
>
> **Soumya Swaminathan, chief medical officer of the World Health Organization, which backs Covax, said there are still about 10,000 deaths a day from Covid-19, which she called "entirely preventable", if vaccines were shared more equally.**

> 新冠疫苗全球获取机制(COVAX)已将其对今年向发展中国家提供的疫苗数量预测下调约25%。印度的出口禁令、制造问题以及新疫苗批准延迟导致该计划偏离轨道。根据该机制向中低收入国家提供疫苗的交付量正在增加，今年剩余时间将交付11亿剂疫苗。但全年交付14亿剂疫苗意味着COVAX将无法实现今年交付20亿剂疫苗的目标。支持COVAX的世界卫生组织(WHO)首席医疗官苏米娅•斯瓦米纳坦(Soumya Swaminathan)指出，目前每天仍有大约1万人死于新型冠状病毒肺炎；她表示，如果疫苗能够更平等地共享，这种局面是"完全可以防止的"。

## 三个开头技巧

### 技巧一：动词+因果链式开头

**结构公式：** `[主体] has [强动词] [对象] by [数字] this year, after [原因1]、[原因2] and [原因3] [动词-ed] [X] off track`

FT第一句只有一个句子，却装了四层信息：动作（`slashed` 砍）、幅度（`by about 25 per cent`）、对象（疫苗交付预测）、原因链（出口禁令、制造问题、审批延迟 `knocked the programme off track`）。**一个强动词 + 一串原因，开头就自带因果逻辑。**

**❌ 学生初版**

> Recently, the vaccine delivery is not enough. Many countries are in trouble.

**✅ 升级版**

> Covax has slashed its forecasts for vaccine deliveries to developing countries by about 25 per cent this year, after an export ban, manufacturing problems and delays in approvals knocked the programme off track.

**💡 效果**

`slashed` 比 `cut` 更狠、比 `reduced` 更形象——一刀砍下去的感觉。原因用 `after` 一口气带出三个，读者第一句就知道"发生了什么、为什么"。写"读书人数下降""共享单车被破坏"这类话题时，同样的公式：`[主体] has [强动词] [对象] by [数字] in recent years, after [原因1]、[原因2] and [原因3] [动词-ed] [X] off track`。

**考场模板：**

> The number of students reading for pleasure has slumped by nearly a third this year, after heavy homework, online gaming and short-video apps knocked the habit off track.

### 技巧二：数字落差式开头

**结构公式：** `[A] are ramping up, with [数字1] set to be available ... But [主体] will fall short of its target of [目标], with a total of [数字2]`

FT第二段先扬后抑：交付量在增加（`ramping up` + 11亿剂），但 `But` 一转——离20亿剂目标还差得远（只有14亿剂）。**好消息和坏消息都用数字说，落差自己会说话。**

**❌ 学生初版**

> The plan is not successful. We can not achieve our goal.

**✅ 升级版**

> Deliveries of the scheme are ramping up, with 1.1bn doses set to be available for the rest of the year. But the programme will fall short of its target of delivering 2bn vaccines this year, with a total of 1.4bn shots.

**💡 效果**

`fall short of its target` 是写作里的提分短语——比 `cannot achieve` 地道得多。更重要的是：**"不足"不再是一个形容词，而是一组数字的落差**。写"减塑计划效果不佳"时：`Recycling rates are climbing, with 200 tonnes collected this month. But the city will fall short of its target of 500 tonnes this year, with a projected total of just 260.`

**考场模板：**

> Volunteers are signing up fast, with 300 new members joining this month. But the campaign will fall short of its goal of 1,000 participants, with a projected total of 600.

### 技巧三：权威引语点题式开头

**结构公式：** `[专家+身份], said there are still about [数字] [问题], which [he/she] called "[定性词]", if [条件]`

FT用世卫组织首席医疗官的引语点题：身份同位语（`chief medical officer of the World Health Organization`）+ 数字（`10,000 deaths a day`）+ 定性词（`"entirely preventable" 完全可以防止的`）+ 条件（`if vaccines were shared more equally`）。**四个元素叠加，一句引语就完成了"问题的严重性"论证。**

**❌ 学生初版**

> Some people say the situation is very bad. We should do something.

**✅ 升级版**

> Li Wei, chief engineer of the city's water company, said there are still about 3,000 tonnes of plastic waste entering the river every year, which he called "entirely avoidable", if recycling were taken more seriously.

**💡 效果**

引用不是"某人说了一句话"，而是**身份背书 + 量化问题 + 权威判断**的组合拳。`which she called "entirely preventable"` 这种"非限定从句 + 引号定性词"结构，比 `very bad` 有力一百倍。

**考场模板：**

> Professor Zhang, director of the university's environmental lab, said there are still about 200 species at risk in the region, which he called "entirely recoverable", if protections were enforced more strictly.

## 针对性练习建议

1. **强动词替换练习：** 列出 `cut`、`reduce`、`decrease` 三个词，每个找两个比它们更有画面感的动词（参考 `slashed`、`slumped`、`plunged`）。
2. **因果链改写：** 写一句关于"学校图书馆借阅量下降"的开头，必须用 `after [原因1]、[原因2] and [原因3] [动词-ed] ... off track` 结构。
3. **数字落差练习：** 把"活动不太成功"改写成先扬后抑的两句话，两句都要有具体数字（参考技巧二的 `1.1bn vs 2bn`）。
