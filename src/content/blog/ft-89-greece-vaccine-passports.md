> 中国学生议论文的通病：观点全，证据空。写"tourism is important to Greece"就停了，不敢接数据。
> **account for + 比例**、**slump by + 比例**、**battered by** —— FT新闻里的数据表达现成可用，把“重要”变成“占GDP五分之一”，把“受影响”变成“收入锐减三分之二”。

今天我们从FT关于希腊推动疫苗护照的报道中，学习4种数据与趋势表达。

---

## 一、account for：说“占比”，比 "is X% of" 更地道

**📰 FT原文**

> Tourism is vital to Greece, **accounting for about a fifth of GDP and employment**, according to the Greek Tourism Confederation.

**为什么好**

**account for** = 占……的比例。主语可以是旅游业，后面接a fifth of GDP and employment（GDP和就业的五分之一）。中文学生常写"tourism is 20% of Greece's GDP"，语法没错，但读起来像翻译腔；account for是FT和经济类报道的高频搭配，适合议论文背景段或图表作文。

**📊 学生写法 vs 升级写法**

| 学生初版 | 升级版 | 效果 |
|---------|--------|------|
| Tourism is 20% of Greece's GDP. | Tourism accounts for about a fifth of Greece's GDP and employment. | account for，表达比例更地道，可并列两个对象 |
| English is important in school. | English accounts for a large part of the gaokao score. | 适合写考试分值、课程占比 |

**📝 模板 & 例句**

```
[主语] accounts for [比例/分数] of [对象].
```

- The service sector accounts for more than half of national employment.
- Renewable energy accounts for about a third of the country's electricity.

## 二、battered by：说“遭受重创”，有画面感

**📰 FT原文**

> ...a holiday industry that has been **battered by the coronavirus pandemic**.

**为什么好**

batter原义“反复殴打”，新闻里常用**be battered by** = 遭……重创。搭配：an industry / a region / an economy battered by the pandemic。比被动的"be affected by"有力得多，画面感更强，适合写议论文的问题段。

**📊 学生写法 vs 升级写法**

| 学生初版 | 升级版 | 效果 |
|---------|--------|------|
| The tourism industry was affected by COVID-19. | The holiday industry has been battered by the coronavirus pandemic. | battered by，语气强，画面感强 |
| Students are affected by stress. | Students are battered by pressure from parents and schools. | 稍带感情色彩，适合议论文开头 |

**📝 模板 & 例句**

```
[行业/地区] has been battered by [事件/危机].
```

- High street shops have been battered by the rise of online shopping.
- The region was battered by three typhoons in two months.

## 三、wipe ... from ... 与 slump by ...：写损失，要有数字

**📰 FT原文**

> ...the restrictions on international travel **wiped 4 per cent from Greece's GDP**, UN estimates suggest, as hotel and accommodation revenues **slumped by two-thirds**。

**为什么好**

**wipe ... from ...** = 抹去、削减：travel restrictions wiped 4% off Greece's GDP。**slump by two-thirds** = 锐减三分之二，比"decrease a lot"具体可信得多。议论文数据段最该学的两个动词：wipe ... from + 经济指标；slump by + 比例/分数。

**📊 学生写法 vs 升级写法**

| 学生初版 | 升级版 | 效果 |
|---------|--------|------|
| The pandemic reduced GDP a lot. | The pandemic wiped 4 per cent from Greece's GDP. | wipe ... from，有数字，主语是事件 |
| Hotel income went down a lot. | Hotel revenues slumped by two-thirds. | slump by，比 go down 剧烈得多 |

**📝 模板 & 例句**

```
[事件] wiped [数字] from [经济指标].
[收入/销售] slumped by [比例].
```

- The lockdown wiped 2 per cent from quarterly GDP.
- Online sales slumped by 40 per cent after the fee increase.

## 四、reliant on：说“依赖”，更正式

**📰 FT原文**

> The Greek sector is almost entirely **reliant on international tourism** with the domestic population too small ... to make up for a fall in external visitors.

**为什么好**

**be reliant on** / **be dependent on** = 依赖…… almost entirely reliant on = 几乎完全依赖。中文学生常写"depend too much on"；reliant on更正式，适合议论文分析段。可用almost entirely / heavily / largely修饰程度。

**📊 学生写法 vs 升级写法**

| 学生初版 | 升级版 | 效果 |
|---------|--------|------|
| Greece depends too much on tourism. | Greece's tourism sector is almost entirely reliant on international visitors. | reliant on，正式，可加程度副词 |
| Students rely on the Internet. | Modern education is heavily reliant on digital tools. | 适合写趋势、现象分析 |

**📝 模板 & 例句**

```
[行业/经济] is [almost entirely/heavily] reliant on [对象].
```

- The local economy is heavily reliant on a single car factory.
- The city's budget is largely reliant on property taxes.

---

## 总结：数据与趋势表达速查

| 意图 | 推荐表达 | 例句 |
|------|---------|------|
| 说占比 | account for ... of ... | Tourism accounts for a fifth of GDP. |
| 说遭重创 | be battered by ... | The industry was battered by the pandemic. |
| 说抹掉/削减 | wipe ... from ... | The crisis wiped 4 per cent from GDP. |
| 说锐减 | slump by ... | Revenues slumped by two-thirds. |
| 说依赖 | be reliant on ... | The sector is reliant on international travel. |

**练习建议**：找一篇你之前写过的议论文，把里面所有"is important""went down""depends on"逐句替换成上面5个结构之一，再对照读一遍——论点立刻有了“肉”。
