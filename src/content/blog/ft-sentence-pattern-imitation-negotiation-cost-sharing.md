# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：句型模仿——僵局与和解（谈判场景的4个句型公式）

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №97 — 美韩就驻韩美军军费分担达成一致（2021年3月，547词）

## 为什么学“谈判与和解”

写“解决问题”的作文，学生几乎都是同一个结尾：双方讨论了一下，最后同意了，问题解决了。

*The two sides talked about the problem. At last they agreed. So the problem was solved.*

问题在于：第一，**没有“之前的僵局”**——没有僵局，和解就没有分量；第二，**“同意”只有一个 agreed**，看不出是原则性同意、暂时性安排，还是正式签署；第三，**让步与代价一个字都没有**——谈判之所以值得写，正因为双方都要动一点自己的底线。

今天从《金融时报》一篇报道美韩达成防卫费分担协议的文章里，抽 4 个句型。它们解决一个具体问题：**怎么把“从僵局到达成”写得有过程、有分寸、有立场**。写合作、协商、矛盾化解、团队分工类作文都能直接移植。

## FT 原文

> The US and South Korea have **reached a deal to** boost Seoul's financial support for America's military presence in the country, **in a bid to resolve** a big source of friction in the alliance during Donald Trump's presidency.

> The **tentative** pact still has to be finalised and signed but was **reached in principle** on Sunday, **according to officials involved in the talks**.

> The state department said it **reflected** the Biden administration's "commitment to reinvigorating and modernising our democratic alliances around the world".

> The deal **follows a tense impasse** under the Trump administration which had **strained ties** between Washington and Seoul.

中文大意：美国与韩国已达成协议，增加首尔方面对驻韩美军的财政支持，以解决特朗普总统任内美韩同盟的一个重大摩擦点。参与谈判的官员表示，这一初步协议仍需最终敲定并签署，但已于上周日原则上达成。美国国务院表示，这反映出拜登政府“致力于重振和更新我们在世界各地的民主同盟”。这份协议达成前，美韩关系在特朗普政府时期一度陷入紧张的僵局。

## 句型一：达成协议 + 目的 —— have reached a deal to ..., in a bid to ...

**结构公式**

`A and B have reached a deal to [动作], in a bid to [目的]`

**FT 原文**

> The US and South Korea have reached a deal to boost Seoul's financial support for America's military presence in the country, in a bid to resolve a big source of friction in the alliance during Donald Trump's presidency.

**为什么好**

学生写“达成一致”，只有 `They agreed`。FT 用的是 `have reached a deal to do`——是“谈成了”，而不是“口头同意”，一个动词就交代了中间有过程、有来回。`in a bid to` 是英语媒体高频的“为了”，比 in order to 更紧凑，还暗含“这是一次争取”。后半句 `resolve a big source of friction in the alliance` 用名词化把矛盾点出来：`a source of friction`（摩擦点）是写人际、团队、国际关系时最好用的名词短语之一，比 `a problem between them` 具体得多。

**❌ 学生初版**

The two sides talked and agreed to do something about the problem between them.

**✅ 升级版**

The two clubs have reached a deal to share the sports field, in a bid to resolve a long source of friction between them.

**💡 效果**

“同意”变成“谈成”，“为了”变成 in a bid to，“矛盾”变成 a source of friction——一句话就有新闻的分量，也把“为什么谈”说明白了。

**模板 + 例句**

`A and B have reached a deal to [动作], in a bid to [目的]`

- 校园话题：The school and the parents association have reached a deal to rebuild the playground, in a bid to resolve a long source of friction over safety.
- 社会话题：City officials and residents have reached a deal to keep the market open at weekends, in a bid to end months of protest.

## 句型二：谨慎待定 —— tentative ... still has to be finalised ... was reached in principle

**结构公式**

`The tentative [文件/协议] still has to be finalised and signed but was reached in principle on [时间], according to [消息来源]`

**FT 原文**

> The tentative pact still has to be finalised and signed but was reached in principle on Sunday, according to officials involved in the talks.

**为什么好**

学生想表达“还没完全确定”，只会写 `Maybe it is not true` 或者 `It is not sure`（后者还是错的）。FT 用三个层次把“确定到什么程度”说得清清楚楚：`tentative`（初步的）、`still has to be finalised and signed`（还待敲定与签署）、`was reached in principle`（原则上已经达成）。结尾 `according to officials involved in the talks`（据参与谈判的官员）交代消息来源，让陈述有依据——这正是英语新闻里最典型的“有保留的确认”，也是雅思大作文里区分 5.5 与 7 分的那种分寸感。

**❌ 学生初版**

Maybe they will agree, but we do not know. Someone said it.

**✅ 升级版**

The tentative plan still has to be approved by the board but was agreed in principle on Friday, according to two people involved in the talks.

**💡 效果**

从“也许吧”升级成“初步、待批准、原则上已同意”三层信息，再补一个消息来源——不确定的表述反而变得可信。

**模板 + 例句**

`The tentative [协议] still has to be [V-ed] but was [V-ed] in principle on [时间], according to [消息来源]`

- 教育话题：The tentative rule still has to be approved by the district office but was accepted in principle last week, according to teachers involved in the talks.
- 科技话题：The tentative agreement still has to be signed by both sides but was reached in principle on Monday, according to people familiar with the matter.

## 句型三：官方表态 —— it reflected ... commitment to ...ing

**结构公式**

`[机构/发言人] said it reflected [一方]'s commitment to [V-ing] and [V-ing] [范围]`

**FT 原文**

> The state department said it reflected the Biden administration's "commitment to reinvigorating and modernising our democratic alliances around the world".

**为什么好**

`reflect` 在这里不是“反射”，而是“反映出”——用来解释一个动作背后的态度，比 `show` 正式得多，也比 `This means they want to ...` 高级。`commitment to` 后面必须接动名词（reinvigorating、modernising），这是学生最容易写错、也最容易被加分的地方。两个 -ing 并列，还自带节奏感：reinvigorating and modernising，一动词承接“重振”，一动词承接“更新”。

**❌ 学生初版**

They said this shows they want to help their friends and make things better.

**✅ 升级版**

The school said it reflected the board's commitment to rebuilding and modernising its ties with parents.

**💡 效果**

“他们想帮忙”变成了“这反映出某方致力于重建并更新某关系”——同一件事，一个是感想，一个是定性。

**模板 + 例句**

`[机构] said it reflected [一方]'s commitment to [V-ing] and [V-ing] [范围]`

- 校园话题：The head teacher said it reflected the staff's commitment to supporting and encouraging students beyond the classroom.
- 环保话题：Ministers said it reflected the city's commitment to cutting and recycling waste across all districts.

## 句型四：僵局根源 —— The deal follows a tense impasse which had strained ties

**结构公式**

`The deal follows a tense impasse [时期/背景] which had strained ties between A and B`，以及 `[一方]'s refusal to budge in the negotiations caused concern among [相关方]`

**FT 原文**

> The deal **follows a tense impasse** under the Trump administration which had **strained ties** between Washington and Seoul.

> The Trump administration's **refusal to budge** in the negotiations caused concern among other longstanding US defence partners.

**为什么好**

先看时态：`had strained` 用过去完成时，说明“关系紧张”是协议达成**之前**就已经发生并持续的状态——一句话把时间层次写出来了。再看三个表达：`impasse`（僵局）、`strain ties`（使关系紧张）、`refusal to budge`（拒绝让步），它们把“谈判为什么会拖”变成一个可以被分析的对象。学生写“他们之前吵了很久”，FT 写的是“这份协议终结了一段令双方关系紧张的僵局”——差别在于，前者是情绪，后者是判断。

**❌ 学生初版**

Before this, they argued for a long time and the relationship was bad.

**✅ 升级版**

The deal follows a tense impasse which had strained ties between the two departments. The manager's refusal to budge in the negotiations caused concern among other teams.

**💡 效果**

`impasse` 和 `refusal to budge` 让“吵很久”有了准确名字；过去完成时把因果关系锁在同一句里，和解的分量也就出来了。

**模板 + 例句**

`The deal follows a tense impasse which had strained ties between A and B` / `[一方]'s refusal to budge caused concern among [相关方]`

- 校园话题：The agreement follows a tense impasse which had strained ties between the two classes. Both sides' refusal to budge caused concern among teachers.
- 职场话题：The settlement follows a tense impasse which had strained ties between the union and the management. The company's refusal to budge on pay caused concern among other staff.

## 练习建议

1. **先抄后造**：把 4 个句型各抄 5 遍，然后用你自己的话题（不要用 FT 的美韩军费谈判）各造 2 句——保留结构，替换内容。
2. **先写僵局，再写和解**：任何“问题解决”类作文，都先补一句句型四（僵局与根源），再用句型一、句型二写达成与分寸，最后用句型三写表态。顺序固定，段落自动成形。
3. **对照检查**：写完圈出所有的 `agreed`、`they talked`、`it is not sure`，逐个问自己——能不能换成 today 的 4 个句型之一？
