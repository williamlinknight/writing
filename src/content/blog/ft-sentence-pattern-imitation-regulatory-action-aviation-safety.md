# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：句型模仿——写措施只会“The government should”？跟FT学4个监管与执行句型

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №87 — 美国联邦航空管理局发布紧急适航指令 检查普惠引擎裂纹（2021年2月，567词）

## 为什么学“监管与执行”

“提建议、写措施”是议论文的必考动作：环保题要写政策，科技题要写监管，校园题要写校规。学生写措施，几乎只有两个句式：

*The government should do something. People must not do this. It is very important.*

四个问题：**谁执行**（谁下命令、谁被要求）？**什么时候生效**（立刻、还是先检查再放行）？**依据和标准是什么**（查什么、多久查一次）？**后续怎么调整**（如果情况变了怎么办）？这四问不回答，措施就只是一句口号。

今天从《金融时报》一篇报道美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）发布紧急适航指令的文章里，抽 4 个句型。它们解决一个具体问题：**怎么把一条措施写得像真的会被执行**——有主体、有时限、有标准、有跟进。

## FT 原文

> US aviation regulators **have ordered** airlines **to inspect** certain Pratt & Whitney engines on Boeing jets for invisible cracks **before the planes are flown again**.

> The US Federal Aviation Administration **issued an emergency airworthiness directive** on Tuesday related to the PW4000-112, an engine used in about 8 per cent of Boeing's 777 aircraft. **The order came three days after** an engine on a 777 broke apart four minutes after take-off, **scattering** debris over a Denver suburb.

> The order **called for** aircraft operators **to conduct** a thermal acoustic image inspection of the titanium fan blades at the front of each engine.

> Regulations currently **require the engine to be inspected** every 6,500 flight cycles — **defined as** one take-off and one landing. The FAA said it **might establish a shorter inspection interval based on** the results from airlines' initial engine checks.

> P&W has advised that it **was working to correct** those issues.

中文大意：美国航空监管机构已下令，要求各航空公司检查波音飞机上部分普惠引擎是否存在肉眼难以发现的裂纹，检查之后这些飞机才能重新执飞。美国联邦航空管理局周二发布了关于 PW4000-112 引擎的紧急适航指令，约 8% 的波音 777 飞机使用该引擎。这道命令下达于事发三天之后：一架 777 飞机起飞 4 分钟后引擎破裂，碎片散落在丹佛郊区。命令要求各航空公司对每个引擎前端的钛合金扇叶进行热声成像检查。现行规定要求该引擎每 6500 个飞行周期检查一次——一个飞行周期指一次起飞和一次着陆。FAA 表示，可能会根据航空公司的初步检查结果缩短检查间隔。普惠公司表示，正在努力纠正这些问题。

## 句型一：下令 + 前置条件 —— have ordered sb to do ... before ...

**结构公式**

`[监管方] have ordered [执行方] to inspect / review [对象] for [问题] before [对象] are [过去分词] again`

**FT 原文**

> US aviation regulators have ordered airlines to inspect certain Pratt & Whitney engines on Boeing jets for invisible cracks before the planes are flown again.

**为什么好**

`order sb to do` 是有强制力的“下令”，比 ask、tell、suggest 都硬气——写政策、写校规、写公司规定时，动词选对了，句子的权威感就到位了。中间 `for invisible cracks` 用介词短语交代**查什么**，不另起一句；句尾 `before the planes are flown again` 用被动的一般现在时表达“复飞之前”这个时间条件，把“先检查、后放行”的顺序绑在一句里。学生写同样意思要三句：The FAA asked airlines to check the engines. The engines may have cracks. The planes should not fly first.

**❌ 学生初版**

The government should ask the companies to check the food. The food may be bad. They should not sell it first.

**✅ 升级版**

City regulators have ordered supermarkets to test packaged salads for bacteria before the products are put on shelves again.

**💡 效果**

一句里读到了“主体—动作—查什么—什么时候才能放行”四个信息，措施从此有了执行顺序，而不是一句“应该管一管”。

**模板 + 例句**

`[监管方] have ordered [执行方] to inspect [对象] for [问题] before [对象] are [过去分词] again`

- 校园话题：The district has ordered schools to test drinking water for lead before the taps are opened again.
- 科技话题：Regulators have ordered platforms to review recommendation algorithms for addictive design before the new term starts.

## 句型二：措施 + 事件时序 —— The order came three days after ..., -ing ...

**结构公式**

`[机构] issued an emergency [措施名词] on [时间] related to [对象]. The order came [N days] after [事件], [V-ing 结果]`

**FT 原文**

> The US Federal Aviation Administration issued an emergency airworthiness directive on Tuesday related to the PW4000-112, an engine used in about 8 per cent of Boeing's 777 aircraft. The order came three days after an engine on a 777 broke apart four minutes after take-off, scattering debris over a Denver suburb.

**为什么好**

先给**措施名词**（an emergency airworthiness directive）当主语，再用 `The order came three days after ...` 把触发它的事件补上——措施在前、原因在后，是新闻写作的常规顺序，读起来像“先有行动，再有解释”。句尾 `scattering debris over a Denver suburb` 用现在分词把**后果**挂在逗号后面，不必另起一句 As a result, debris was scattered ...。学生写这类句子，往往是先讲事故再讲规定，逻辑没问题，但重心落在事故上；FT 的重心落在**措施**上。

**❌ 学生初版**

Three days ago an engine broke and pieces fell on a suburb. Then the FAA made a new rule on Tuesday.

**✅ 升级版**

The education bureau issued an emergency notice on Friday on after-school tutoring, an industry worth about 8 per cent of the city's private tuition market. The notice came three days after a training centre closed overnight, leaving hundreds of families without refunds.

**💡 效果**

措施先行、事件后置，重心从“发生了什么”移到“做了什么”；句尾的分词结构顺手交代后果，句子因此多了一层而没变长。

**模板 + 例句**

`[机构] issued an emergency [措施] on [时间] related to [对象]. The [措施] came [N days] after [事件], [V-ing 结果]`

- 环境话题：The city issued an emergency ban on fireworks last week. The ban came two days after a dry spell, pushing air quality to its worst level of the year.
- 交通话题：The transport authority issued a temporary speed limit on the ring road. The limit came four days after a lorry overturned, blocking two lanes.

## 句型三：措施内容 + 明文规定 —— called for sb to do ... / require sth to be done every ...

**结构公式**

`The order called for [执行方] to conduct / carry out [动作] of [对象]` ＋ `Regulations currently require [对象] to be [过去分词] every [频次] — defined as [解释]`

**FT 原文**

> The order called for aircraft operators to conduct a thermal acoustic image inspection of the titanium fan blades at the front of each engine.

> Regulations currently require the engine to be inspected every 6,500 flight cycles — defined as one take-off and one landing.

**为什么好**

`called for sb to do` 把命令的**内容**说成“要求做某事”，比 must 更书面、更像制度语言；`require sth to be inspected every ...` 用被动加频次表达规定，一出现就有“条款感”。最值得学的是破折号后面的 `defined as one take-off and one landing`——专业术语（flight cycles）后面顺手给读者一个解释，术语立刻从障碍变成可信度。学生写专业名词，常常默认读者都懂；加一个 defined as，专业感立刻不一样。

**❌ 学生初版**

The rule says airlines must check the blades. The rule says check every 6,500 flights.

**✅ 升级版**

The new policy calls for schools to conduct a monthly review of canteen menus, and it requires the menus to be published every two weeks — defined as one full cycle of dishes served to each grade.

**💡 效果**

同样的规定，一句用 called for 写“要求做什么”，一句用 require ... to be published 写“多久一次”，中间再用破折号解释术语——制度的三个层次（做什么、频率、定义）各就各位。

**模板 + 例句**

`The order called for [执行方] to conduct [动作] of [对象]` / `Regulations require [对象] to be [过去分词] every [频次] — defined as [解释]`

- 健康话题：The notice called for restaurants to carry out a weekly check of frying oil, and it requires the results to be logged every seven days — defined as one working week including weekends.
- 科技话题：The rule called for platforms to conduct a yearly audit of data use, and it requires the report to be filed every 12 months — defined as one full subscription cycle.

## 句型四：弹性调整 + 整改承诺 —— might ... based on ... / was working to correct ...

**结构公式**

`[机构] said it might [调整动作] based on [依据]` ＋ `[责任方] has advised / said that it was working to [整改动作]`

**FT 原文**

> The FAA said it might establish a shorter inspection interval based on the results from airlines' initial engine checks.

> P&W has advised that it was working to correct those issues.

**为什么好**

措施不是一次写完的。`might establish a shorter inspection interval based on ...` 用 might 加 based on 表达“看结果再定”，既给出了下一步，也不把话说死——这是监管语言里最常被模仿的弹性表达。第二句 `has advised that it was working to correct those issues`：用过去进行时 was working 表示“一直在改”，比 will correct 更可信，因为它回避了“什么时候改完”这个无法保证的承诺。学生写整改常是 The company promised to solve the problem，语气太轻。

**❌ 学生初版**

The FAA may change the rule later. The company said it will fix the problem.

**✅ 升级版**

The school said it might shorten the review cycle based on the results of the first round of inspections, while the supplier has advised that it was working to correct the labelling errors.

**💡 效果**

`might ... based on ...` 让跟进措施有依据、有分寸；`was working to correct` 让承诺听起来正在进行中——同一个意思，语气从“表态”变成“进度”。

**模板 + 例句**

`[机构] said it might [调整动作] based on [依据]` / `[责任方] has advised that it was working to [整改动作]`

- 社会话题：The council said it might extend the ban on street stalls based on the results of the summer inspection, and vendors have advised that they were working to meet the new hygiene rules.
- 环境话题：The ministry said it might shorten the reporting cycle based on the first year of emissions data, while the plants have advised that they were working to correct faulty meters.

## 练习建议

1. **措施四问**：写下任何一条建议后，补上四样东西——执行主体（谁下令）、时间或前置条件（before / came N days after）、标准或频率（every ...）、后续调整（might ... based on ...）。四问补齐，口号就变成了方案。
2. **两个动词升级**：把作文里的 `should` 换成 `have ordered ... to do`，把 `promised to solve` 换成 `has advised that it was working to correct`，再读一遍，感受语体的变化。
3. **对照检查**：凡是写 `The government should do something`、`It is very important` 的地方，问自己：这句能不能落在具体的主体、期限和标准上？能落地的句子，才算写完了措施。
