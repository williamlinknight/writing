# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：段落结构——因果链段落：让每一步都有来处

"为什么"是学生作文里最写不好的句子之一。最常见的写法是：`Because the environment is polluted, so we must protect it.` ——原因空、结果也空，因果之间没有证据、没有过程，只有两个空话被 because 和 so 硬绑在一起。

好的因果段落，读起来像一条**链子**：决定有理由，理由有证据，证据有后果，后果又引发新问题。每一步都有来处，读者才会信。

今天我们拆解《金融时报》2021年3月一篇报道——德国批准65岁以上人群接种阿斯利康疫苗（全文仅286词）。这篇短报道是一个完美的因果链样本：**政策反转 → 医学证据 → 舆论连锁反应**。

## FT原文（示范素材）

> Germany has decided to approve the Oxford/AstraZeneca Covid-19 jab for people aged 65 and over, in a move that could help speed up the country's sluggish vaccination campaign.
>
> Germany's standing commission on vaccinations, known as Stiko, had previously recommended the vaccine only be used on those aged 18 to 64, arguing that there was a lack of evidence from clinical trials showing its effectiveness in older people.
>
> Spahn also said Stiko had recommended extending the period between the first and second doses of the AstraZeneca shot to 12 weeks maximum, based on studies showing a longer gap improved its efficacy.
>
> The decision to restrict the use of the AstraZeneca jab had come under mounting criticism in Germany in recent weeks, with experts saying it had undermined the vaccine's credibility with the general public. There have been numerous cases of people not turning up for appointments for the AstraZeneca shot, leading to a massive backlog of unused doses.

> 德国已决定批准65岁及以上人群接种牛津/阿斯利康(Oxford/AstraZeneca)的新型冠状病毒肺炎（COVID-19，即2019冠状病毒病）疫苗，此举可能有助于加快该国进展缓慢的疫苗接种运动。
>
> 德国疫苗接种常设委员会(STIKO)此前建议该疫苗仅面向18至64岁人群，认为缺少证明其对老年人有效性的临床试验证据。
>
> 施潘还表示，德国疫苗接种常设委员会建议将阿斯利康疫苗第一剂和第二剂的接种间隔延长至最长12周，原因是研究表明较长的接种间隔可以提高其有效性。
>
> 最近几周，限制阿斯利康疫苗使用的决定在德国遭到了越来越多的批评，专家表示这削弱了疫苗在公众中的信誉。许多人在预约接种阿斯利康疫苗后没有现身，导致未使用的疫苗大量积压。

## 技法一：决定 + 理由并置——一句话装下"做了什么"和"为什么"

**结构公式：** `[主语] has decided to [动作], in a move that [影响]` / `[主语] [动作], arguing that [理由]`

FT第一句：

> Germany has decided to approve the Oxford/AstraZeneca Covid-19 jab for people aged 65 and over, **in a move that could help speed up** the country's sluggish vaccination campaign.

决定（approve）+ 潜在影响（speed up）压进一句话。再看第二句的"过去决定 + 理由"：

> Stiko had previously recommended the vaccine only be used on those aged 18 to 64, **arguing that** there was a lack of evidence from clinical trials.

过去式 had recommended 表示"旧政策"，arguing that 直接给出当时决策的依据。新旧两个决定各自带着理由出场，因果链第一环就扣上了。

**❌ 学生初版**

> The government changed the policy because of the vaccine. It is very important.

**✅ 升级版**

> Germany has decided to widen the use of the vaccine, in a move that could speed up the country's sluggish campaign. Officials had previously restricted the jab, arguing that there was a lack of evidence from clinical trials.

**💡 效果**

`in a move that` 和 `arguing that` 都是"原因附体"结构：动作和理由同时给出，句子密度翻倍，学生作文里"单句单意思"的松散感立刻消失。

## 技法二：数据支撑的归因——based on 把"凭什么"说清楚

**结构公式：** `[建议/决定], based on [证据来源] showing [结论]`

FT写法：

> Stiko had recommended extending the period between the first and second doses ... to 12 weeks maximum, **based on studies showing a longer gap improved its efficacy**.

很多学生写归因：`We should do this because it is good.` 到底怎么"good"，说不出来。based on 后面跟的是**具体的证据形态**：studies（研究）、data（数据）、analysis（分析）——归因立刻从"我觉得"升级为"有据可查"。

**❌ 学生初版**

> We should extend the interval because it is useful.

**✅ 升级版**

> Officials recommended extending the interval to 12 weeks, based on studies showing that a longer gap improved the vaccine's efficacy.

**💡 效果**

归因三件套 `based on + 证据形态 + showing + 具体结论`，是考场作文"论证有据"的最低成本写法。议论文里"延长休息时间""增加阅读量"都能套。

## 技法三：连锁后果——leading to 把因果推成链条

**结构公式：** `[事件] had come under [批评], with [专家] saying [影响]. There have been [现象], leading to [最终结果].`

FT最后一段：

> The decision ... had come under **mounting criticism** in Germany in recent weeks, **with experts saying** it had **undermined** the vaccine's credibility ... There have been numerous cases of people not turning up for appointments ..., **leading to a massive backlog** of unused doses.

注意这条链：限制使用 → 招致批评 → 削弱公信力 → 人们爽约 → 疫苗积压。五环相扣，每一环都是上一环的直接后果。学生作文里最常见的断链写法是"because... so..."两步就停，这条链示范了如何**连续推五步**。

**❌ 学生初版**

> People don't like the policy. So the vaccine is wasted.

**✅ 升级版**

> The decision came under mounting criticism, with experts saying it had undermined the vaccine's credibility. Many people did not turn up for appointments, leading to a massive backlog of unused doses.

**💡 效果**

`with experts saying` 引入"谁说"，`leading to` 推进"结果"，因果链条在句与句之间咬合推进。连续两个后果句，比一个 because 句有说服力得多。

## 写作建议：因果链的三层检查

写"为什么"段落时，按这三层自查：

1. **有没有证据？** 原因不能只有观点，`based on studies showing...` 比 because 有力。
2. **链条推了几步？** 两步（因为A所以B）只是起点，试着像 FT 一样推四到五步（A → 批评 → 公信力 → 爽约 → 积压）。
3. **每一步之间有没有"咬合词"？** `in a move that` / `arguing that` / `based on` / `with experts saying` / `leading to`，这些词就是链条的扣环。

## 小练习

用今天学的三个结构（in a move that / based on studies showing / leading to），把下面这个"断链"升级成因果链段落：

> The school changed the rule. Because of this, students are unhappy. So the school should change it back.
