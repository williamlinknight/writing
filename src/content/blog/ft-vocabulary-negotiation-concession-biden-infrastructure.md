# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：词汇升级——谈判让步场景，从 give up 到 drop demands

批改议论文时，"放弃"和"反对"是出现频率最高的两个动作。学生写出来几乎千篇一律：**give up、stop、disagree with、be against**。意思没错，但考官读完十篇作文，十篇都在用同一批词——分数自然上不去。

今天我们从FT一篇关于拜登基建谈判的报道中，学5组**谈判让步场景**的高阶表达。这篇报道（2021年6月）讲的是拜登为了两党能达成基建协议，愿意放弃提高企业税率——整篇都是"坚持、让步、反对、妥协"的动作，简直是议论文词汇的天然素材库。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №158 — *Biden open to dropping corporation tax rise in infrastructure talks*（2021年6月）

## 📖 FT原文选段

> Joe Biden is prepared **to drop his demands** for an increase in the US corporation tax rate if enough Republicans agree to support a surge in infrastructure spending, according to people familiar with the negotiations.
>
> **The concession by the US president is aimed at catalysing** a bipartisan agreement on his bid to fund more than $1tn in additional spending over the next decade.
>
> Republicans are **adamantly opposed** to it **on the grounds that** it would hurt the economy and partly unwind Donald Trump's signature tax cuts.
>
> Biden's move came ahead of a self-imposed deadline of next week to proceed with negotiations on infrastructure, or **to ditch** them and try to pass the package using the wafer-thin Democratic majorities in Congress.
>
> If the US president ends up negotiating an additional spending package with Democrats alone, a corporate income tax increase is likely **to be back on the table**.

## 🎯 5组高分词汇解析

### 1️⃣ drop one's demands — 放弃（某方面的）要求

**原文出处：** "Joe Biden is prepared to **drop his demands** for an increase in the US corporation tax rate."

**为什么好：** 学生写"放弃"只会 **give up**。**drop** 在这里比 give up 精准得多——它强调"放下原本坚持的某个具体要求"，而不是"整体放弃努力"。谈判语境下，drop demands 的意思是"我还在谈，只是这一条不坚持了"，比 give up 少了挫败感，多了策略性。

**❌ 学生初版**
The two sides had to **give up** their original plans.

**✅ 升级版**
After hours of negotiation, the two sides agreed to **drop their demands** on the most controversial points.

**💡 效果**
drop demands 暗示"这是有策略的让步，不是认输"——用在议论文里描述双方博弈，立刻有新闻腔。

**例句：** Environmental groups are unlikely to **drop their demands** for stricter emission standards.

---

### 2️⃣ a concession aimed at — 旨在……的让步

**原文出处：** "The concession by the US president is **aimed at catalysing** a bipartisan agreement."

**为什么好：** 学生写"让步"只会 **compromise**。**concession** 是"单方面做出的让步"，比 compromise（双方各让一步）更精确——它强调"我让了，为了换取对方的配合"。搭配 **be aimed at doing**（旨在）和 **catalyse**（催化、推动），一句话就把"让步的目的"说清楚了。

**❌ 学生初版**
The government made a **compromise** to **make** the two parties agree.

**✅ 升级版**
The government's **concession was aimed at catalysing** an agreement between the two parties.

**💡 效果**
concession + be aimed at + catalyse 三个词连用，把一个常见的"妥协"动作写出了目的感和因果链，是雅思大作文高分段的典型搭配。

**例句：** The company's offer to lower prices **was aimed at catalysing** a quick settlement of the dispute.

---

### 3️⃣ adamantly opposed, on the grounds that — 坚决反对，理由是

**原文出处：** "Republicans are **adamantly opposed** to it **on the grounds that** it would hurt the economy."

**为什么好：** 学生写"反对"只会 **strongly disagree** 或 **be against**。**adamantly opposed** 是"坚决反对"，语气比 strongly against 强硬得多。更关键的是后面的 **on the grounds that**（理由是）——它比 because 正式，且自带"这个理由是站得住脚的立场"的意味，是议论文论证反对观点的首选句式。

**❌ 学生初版**
Many people **are against** the new policy **because** it costs too much.

**✅ 升级版**
Many residents are **adamantly opposed** to the new policy **on the grounds that** it places a heavy burden on low-income families.

**💡 效果**
adamantly opposed 表态度，on the grounds that 给理由——态度和理由一次到位，比 be against because 高了两个等级。

**例句：** The committee was **adamantly opposed** to the proposal **on the grounds that** it would threaten public health.

---

### 4️⃣ ditch — 果断放弃（原有方案）

**原文出处：** "...or **to ditch** them and try to pass the package using the wafer-thin Democratic majorities."

**为什么好：** 学生写"放弃某个方案"用 **abandon** 或 **stop**。**ditch**（抛弃、甩掉）是个口语感极强的动词，在新闻文体里表示"果断抛弃原计划、另走捷径"。它比 abandon 更有画面感——像把没用的东西扔进沟里，暗示"这个方案不值得再耗时间"。

**❌ 学生初版**
The company decided to **abandon** the old plan and try a new one.

**✅ 升级版**
Frustrated by the slow talks, the company decided to **ditch** the partnership and pursue an independent strategy.

**💡 效果**
ditch 自带"果断、不拖泥带水"的态度，用在描述转折决策的段落里，比 abandon 更有力度。

**例句：** After months of failed negotiations, the mayor threatened to **ditch** the plan and fund the project directly.

---

### 5️⃣ back on the table — （重新）摆上台面

**原文出处：** "a corporate income tax increase is likely to **be back on the table**."

**为什么好：** 学生写"重新考虑某事"用 **be considered again**。**on the table**（摆在桌面上）是英语谈判的经典隐喻——桌面上=正在讨论的选项。**back on the table** 表示"之前被搁置的方案又重新进入讨论范围"，一个短语就完成了"时间+状态"的转换。

**❌ 学生初版**
The tax increase may **be considered again** if the talks fail.

**✅ 升级版**
If the two sides fail to reach an agreement, the controversial proposal could **be back on the table**.

**💡 效果**
on the table / off the table / back on the table 是一整套谈判隐喻，学会一个就能扩展出一组。写作里用它替代 be considered again，立刻有成熟感。

**例句：** The issue of raising tuition fees is likely to **be back on the table** after the budget review.

## ✍️ 写作建议

这5组表达的共同点：**它们都是"谈判场景词汇"——描述两方或多方博弈时使用的精确动词。**

| 学生常写 | 升级表达 | 使用场景 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| give up | drop one's demands | 放弃某个具体条件 |
| compromise | a concession aimed at | 单方面让步以换取进展 |
| strongly disagree / be against | adamantly opposed | 强硬反对 |
| abandon / stop | ditch | 果断抛弃原方案 |
| be considered again | back on the table | 方案重新进入讨论 |

**建议你**按场景积累词汇，而不是按字母表背单词。写议论文前先问自己：这篇文章里有没有"两方博弈"？如果有，就从"谈判词汇"这个抽屉里取词——drop demands、concession、adamantly opposed、ditch、back on the table——比你临时想 give up 高级得多。

**今日练习：** 用今天学的5组表达，把下面这段话升级成80词的议论文段落：

> ❌ The government wanted to raise taxes. Many people were against it. The government gave up. But if the problem gets worse, they may consider it again.

试着把"against"换成 adamantly opposed、把"gave up"换成 drop their demands，看看整段的语气变化。
