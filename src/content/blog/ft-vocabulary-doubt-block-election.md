# 读《金融时报》学英文写作：词汇升级——写"质疑阻挠"不只会 doubt 和 stop，跟FT学博弈较量词汇

批改议论文和读后续写时，学生写"质疑"就是 **doubt**，写"反对"就是 **disagree**，写"破坏计划"就是 **stop**——三个词用遍全场，冲突感荡然无存。博弈类场景（竞争、辩论、维权、比赛）的写法，高手和平庸者的差距就在**动词的力度**。

今天我们从FT一篇关于特朗普试图阻挠美国各州认证选举结果的报道（2020年11月）中学5组**博弈与较量词汇**。这篇报道写的是"最后的机会"：各州陆续认证选举结果，特朗普用法律行动拖延、质疑、阻挠，即便希望渺茫。整篇文章就是一个大型"反对现场"——正是学习"怎么写质疑、怎么写阻挠"的最佳教材。

> **📖 素材来源：** FT №28 — *Trump's last chance: blocking states from certifying election results*（2020年11月）

## 📖 FT原文选段

> As Donald Trump continues to **dispute** his election loss, the outgoing US president is approaching a series of deadlines that will further **cement** Joe Biden's victory.

> The lengthy timeline provides space for Mr Trump to **cast further doubt on** the clear verdict of the American people and to **block** the transition process to Mr Biden's administration, even if he has little hope of **overturning** the result.

> In Pennsylvania, where Mr Trump has **mounted** most of his lawsuits, the state has a November 23 deadline for counties to submit their final vote totals.

> In Michigan, the results are certified by a four-person board that has two Democrats and two Republicans, **raising the prospect of a deadlock**.

> In a portent of that possibility, the board of canvassers in Wayne County **deadlocked** on certifying the results on Tuesday afternoon following a 2-2 split.

> The process is more idiosyncratic elsewhere, **offering Mr Trump opportunities to throw a spanner in the gears**.

## 🎯 5组高分词汇解析

### 1️⃣ cast doubt on — 公开质疑

**原文出处：** "The lengthy timeline provides space for Mr Trump to **cast further doubt on** the clear verdict of the American people."

**为什么好：** 学生写"质疑"只会 **doubt** / **question**。**cast doubt on**（往……上投下怀疑）和 doubt 的区别在于：doubt 只是"心里怀疑"，cast doubt on 是"公开地、有动作地让别人也开始怀疑"——它自带"传播怀疑"的意味。写议论文里反驳对方观点时，cast doubt on 比 I doubt 正式得多、有力得多。

**❌ 学生初版**
Some people doubt the safety of the new drug.

**✅ 升级版**
The leaked report cast doubt on the safety of the new drug.

**💡 效果**
cast doubt on 让"质疑"从心理活动变成了公开行动：主语可以是人，也可以是报告、数据、事件——一个动作同时交代了"谁在质疑"和"质疑什么"。

**例句：** The company's delayed financial report cast doubt on its claims of rapid growth.

---

### 2️⃣ cement — 巩固、夯实

**原文出处：** "...a series of deadlines that will further **cement** Joe Biden's victory."

**为什么好：** 学生写"巩固"只会 **make... stronger** 或 **make sure**。**cement** 本义是"水泥"，作动词就是"用水泥固定"，引申为"巩固、夯实"。写"巩固成果""夯实基础""坐实优势"时，cement 比 strengthen 更有画面感——像砌墙一样把胜利一层层砌牢。

**❌ 学生初版**
The win made their position stronger.

**✅ 升级版**
The election result cemented their position as the region's leading party.

**💡 效果**
cement 是"结果导向"的动词：它暗示"一旦巩固，就很难动摇"。用在竞争、比赛、谈判的收尾段特别有力。

**例句：** A string of strong results cemented her reputation as the school's best debater.

---

### 3️⃣ mount — 发起（行动、诉讼）

**原文出处：** "In Pennsylvania, where Mr Trump has **mounted** most of his lawsuits..."

**为什么好：** 学生写"发起"只会 **start** / **bring**。**mount** 是正式新闻语体里"发动一场行动"的标准动词：mount a lawsuit（发起诉讼）、mount a campaign（发起运动）、mount a challenge（发起挑战）、mount an attack（发动进攻）。它比 start 正式，比 bring 有力——自带"组织、部署、推进"的规模感。

**❌ 学生初版**
They started a campaign against the new policy.

**✅ 升级版**
Students mounted a campaign against the new policy, collecting thousands of signatures.

**💡 效果**
mount 让"发起"有了分量：它暗示这件事不是一时兴起，而是经过组织的大动作。写社会运动、维权行动、商业竞争时必备。

**例句：** The union mounted a legal challenge to the company's dismissal policy.

---

### 4️⃣ deadlock — 僵局

**原文出处：** "...raising the prospect of a **deadlock**." / "...the board **deadlocked** on certifying the results following a 2-2 split."

**为什么好：** 学生写"谈不拢"只会 **cannot agree**。**deadlock** 是"僵局"——双方僵持不下、谁也无法推进，比 cannot agree 精准得多：它描述的不是"没谈成"，而是"卡死了"。作名词是 a deadlock，作动词是 deadlock on something。写谈判、比赛、冲突的顶点时，deadlock 是王牌词。

**❌ 学生初版**
The two sides could not agree, so the meeting ended.

**✅ 升级版**
The talks ended in deadlock, with neither side willing to compromise.

**💡 效果**
deadlock 自带"卡住"的画面，还能搭配 break the deadlock（打破僵局）、end in deadlock（以僵局告终）——一个词撑起整个冲突段的高潮。

**例句：** The negotiation deadlocked over the issue of pay, delaying the deal for weeks.

---

### 5️⃣ overturn — 推翻（结果、判决）

**原文出处：** "...even if he has little hope of **overturning** the result."

**为什么好：** 学生写"推翻结果"只会 **change the result**。**overturn** 专指"推翻（决定、判决、结果）"，比 change 正式得多——它暗示"原来的结果已经确立，要把它整个翻过来"。写"推翻判决""取消决定""逆转结果"时，overturn 是法律和新闻语体的标准动词。

**❌ 学生初版**
The team wanted to change the referee's decision.

**✅ 升级版**
The club appealed, hoping to overturn the referee's decision.

**💡 效果**
overturn 让"改变"有了"翻案"的力度——它是针对既有裁决的正式挑战，比 change 精准一个量级。

**例句：** The court overturned the previous ruling after new evidence emerged.

## ✍️ 写作建议

写"反对"不是只有 disagree。从轻到重，可以排成一条完整的**反对光谱**：

| 学生常写 | 升级表达 | 层次 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| doubt | cast doubt on | 公开质疑 |
| make... stronger | cement | 巩固、坐实 |
| start / bring | mount | 发起行动 |
| cannot agree | deadlock | 陷入僵局 |
| change the result | overturn | 推翻结果 |
| stop | block | 阻挠 |
| mess things up | throw a spanner in the gears | 从中作梗 |

**建议你**写议论文里的对立双方时，先确定"冲突到哪一步了"：是刚提出疑问（question/cast doubt on），还是已经正面交锋（dispute/mount），还是彻底卡死（deadlock），还是要把对方的结果整个翻过来（overturn）？按这条光谱选词，冲突就有了层次，读后续写的矛盾段也会更有张力。

**今日练习：** 用今天学的表达，把下面这段"学生版"升级成80词的议论文段落：

> ❌ Some people disagree with the new rule. They say it is not fair. They want to change the decision. The two sides cannot agree.

试着把 disagree 换成 cast doubt on、把 change the decision 换成 overturn the decision、把 cannot agree 换成 deadlock，看看冲突的力度差了多少。
